An old noble family whose estate lies out in the desert inland from [[Greenstone]], past [[North East Quarry Village Four]]. The Vanes keep to themselves.

## What players know

The Vane estate is a manor with a hedge maze and gardens kept green in the desert by climate-control magic. The family rarely comes to the city. Servants who leave the estate's employment do not talk about it.

## Founders

The house was founded by **Ambrose Vane**, a Silver-rank adventurer who found a spring in the hills beyond the quarries and built a caravan inn beside it, on the inland road that runs from the coast into the desert. The inn became a manor, the trade became a fortune, and the Vanes became nobility without ever really becoming part of the city.

## Key people

- [[Cressida Vane]], Lady of the house. She looks like a woman in her early thirties, with olive skin and dark hair, and has looked that way for longer than anyone in the city can remember.
- [[Landemere Vane]] (Bronze), her son, a scholar of magic who sometimes visits the [[Magic Society]].
- [[Alicia Vane]], her daughter.

## Interests and income

- **The inland road.** The family still owns the caravan inns and wells along the road into the desert, and every caravan that uses them pays.
- **Salt and glass sand.** Salt pans and fine white sand from the desert, sold to glassworks and alchemists in the city.
- **Desert herbs.** Rare plants grown in the estate's magical gardens and sold to alchemists at high prices.
- **Interests.** Privacy. The Vanes take little part in the city's politics and turn down most invitations.

## DM notes

**The truth.** The Vanes are the heart of a cannibal blood cult called [[The Red Table]]. They hold rituals on the estate, sacrifice captives in a chamber under the manor, and eat the dead. Some of the family's workers are cultists. Some are simply too frightened to leave. The caravan inns are how they choose travellers nobody will miss.

- **Cressida Vane** leads the cult. She is far older than she looks: the Red Table's rites keep her young. She may be Ambrose Vane's own daughter, and every "late Lord Vane" in the family history was hers.
- **Landemere Vane** (Bronze), a slender, olive-skinned scholar with dark wavy hair and an umbral mountain snake familiar, has drifted to the edge of the Red Table because he venerates great astral beings, especially [[The Builder]]. He has been attempting summoning rituals of his own. He has no idea what he might bring through.
- **Alicia Vane**, his sister, is as dangerous as her mother and more impatient.

**The ritual.** The cult is trying to summon an apocalypse beast, a monster that eats worlds. Its sacrifices are travellers, workers nobody will miss, and, if they can manage it, a team of adventurers sent to investigate.

**Hooks.**
- A church posts a contract to look into missing people near the Vane estate, and a Bronze-rank team takes it. They don't come back.
- A stranger walks out of the desert, half-dressed and confused, claiming to have woken up in a hedge maze.
