**Bronze rank.** Leonid man, rank assessor and yard master at the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] of the [[Adventure Society]].

- **Looks:** A huge Leonid with a greying mane, a missing ear and a chest of scars he never covers. Smells of sand and liniment. Moves very quietly for something so big.
- **Personality:** Gruff and blunt, with no patience for showing off. Fair to a fault: he has failed a noble's heir and passed a street orphan in the same afternoon.
- **Wants:** Adventurers who live. Every one who dies on their first Bronze contract feels like his failure.
- **Essences:** Might, Valour and Shield, forming the **Warlord** confluence. He leads from the front, and his voice carries over any fight.
- **Ties:** He trained half the Silver-rankers in Greenstone, including [[Captain Reyna Holt]], who still calls him sir.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Rank assessment, training in the yard, and hard, honest feedback.
:::

## What people say

Ulric in the yard. He assesses every rank-up in the branch, and he doesn't care who your parents are. Fail his assessment and you go back to Iron contracts, no arguments.

:::dm
## DM notes

- He was demoted from Silver-rank contracts after a team under his command was lost. He knows exactly how much luck had to do with his own survival, and he assesses everyone against that.
- **Assessment.** He runs rank assessments as live drills: a fight against yard-trained monsters, a scenario he improvises, and a long talk afterwards. He cares more about how a team retreats than how it wins.
- **The lost team.** His Silver team died in an astral space that collapsed. He got out because he was on the wrong side of a door. He has never told anyone that it was luck.
- **Training.** A party that trains with him for a week gets one good habit: once per long rest, when a teammate within 30 feet fails a saving throw, a character can use their reaction to call a warning and let them reroll it.
- **A scene.** The party walks into the yard for their first assessment. A huge Leonid with half an ear looks them over, says "You'll do, maybe," and throws a wooden sword at the one who looks most confident.

## Playing Ulric

**Gruff yard master.** Blunt, fair and protective, and much more afraid for his students than he lets them see.

- **Speech:** Low, rough growl in short bursts
- **How to play:** Point out the party's worst habit before they've done anything
- **Tell:** His tail lashes when he's worried
- **Toward the party:** Gruff and testing, quietly invested
- **Wants from the party:** For them to live through their next rank
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Again. And this time, watch your back.
>
> I don't care who your father is.
>
> Retreat is not failure. Dying is failure.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** That no team he passes dies on its first contract at the new rank
- **Driven by:** Guilt, and a soldier's sense of duty
- **Fears:** Losing another team
- **Bond:** His students, and the memory of his old team
- **Flaw:** Trusts no one's luck, including his own

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Might** (common, Might): Raw strength and crushing force. Grants Proficiency in Athletics or Intimidation. The essence of sheer physical power. Bearers' shoulders broaden and their grip grows crushing, their blows land with a dull thunderclap, and flagstones crack where they plant their feet.
- **Valour** (uncommon, Might): Courage in the face of danger. Grants **Rallying Cry**. Valour is the courage to stand when others run. Bearers have steady eyes and an easy, confident stance, and their magic burns with a clear, unwavering light.
- **Shield** (common, Might): Protection and warding others. Grants Proficiency with shields or in Medicine. The essence of the shield and the one who stands in front. Bearers instinctively place themselves between danger and their companions, and a faint translucent barrier flickers around them when they cast.
- **Confluence: Warlord** (Might + Valour + Shield): Command amid the clash of battle. Grants **Battle Order**. Warlord is the instinct to lead soldiers through blood and chaos and bring them home victorious. Bearers stand as if surveying a field from a hilltop, their voices cut through clamour like a war horn, and their spells flare in the shapes of banners and spearpoints.
