The church of **the Healer**, the god who embodies healing: the closing of wounds and the mending of the sick, and everything that makes healing necessary.

- **Standing:** The most trusted church in Greenstone.
- **Symbol:** an open hand holding a leaf.
- **Colours:** green and white.

## The god

The Healer is healing itself: the closing of wounds, the mending of bone and the recovery of the sick. The Healer's priests can close wounds that would kill anyone else.

- **What the god gives:** The Healer offers mercy for anyone who is hurt, rich or poor, friend or enemy, and a church that runs the best clinics in the world.
- **Offended by:** Wounds that can never heal, and the undead, who cannot be healed at all.

:::dm
### The dark face

The Healer is untroubled by suffering, because suffering is what he exists to answer. He is content for wounds to be made if it means more of them will be healed, and his followers may seek out war, plague or disaster to be where the healing is.

- **Will accept:** Injury, sickness and pain, as long as they lead to healing.

### Secret motives

- The Healer is content for corruption to thrive in his church: fake cures, diluted potions and treatments that keep patients coming back. More people needing healing for longer is more healing.
- The Healer is quietly pleased by war, plague and monster surges. His priests are always first to arrive at a disaster, and some are suspiciously well prepared.
- The Healer's clinics make everyone, from street orphans to noble houses, dependent on the church. That dependence is the church's real political power, and it is not shy about using it.
:::

## Divine essence

The Healer grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers have warm, steady hands, a faint green light shows when they touch a wound, and they smell of clean herbs.*

- **Essence trait:** Proficiency in Medicine or with herbalism kits.
- **Sense: Sense Wounds.** As an action, you learn how hurt every creature within 60 feet is, and whether any of them is poisoned or diseased, until the end of your next turn.
- **Channel: Lay on Hands.** You have a pool of healing equal to five times your level, which refills when you finish a long rest. As an action, you touch a creature and restore any number of hit points from the pool, or spend 5 points to end one disease or poison.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Mending** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Whenever a blessed creature regains hit points, it regains extra hit points equal to your proficiency bonus. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *cure wounds*, *healing word*, *inflict wounds*; level 2: *aid*, *lesser restoration*, *prayer of healing*; level 3: *revivify*, *mass healing word*, *aura of vitality*; level 4: *death ward*, *aura of life*; level 5: *mass cure wounds*, *greater restoration*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Life.

**Divine confluence: Saint.** A Saint of the Healer closes wounds that should not close. Bearers are surrounded by a faint green warmth, flowers turn toward them, and the sick feel better just for being near them. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Healer (divine) + Mercy + Renewal; Healer (divine) + Growth + Soul; Healer (divine) + Bread + Shepherd. It grants **Miracle**: Once per long rest, you can restore a creature that died within the last minute to life with 1 hit point, as long as its body is mostly whole.

**What the god asks.** Heal whoever comes to you. Give the church its share of what your healing earns. Go where the wounded are.

**What makes the god take it back.** Letting someone die in front of you when you could have healed them. Making a wound that can never heal.

## What players know

The healing church runs clinics and is generally welcome everywhere. Its priests will patch up an adventurer who staggers in at midnight, and most do not ask how the wound happened. Paying the suggested donation is considered good manners.

## Beliefs

- Heal first. Ask questions afterwards, if at all.
- No wound is beneath notice and no patient is beneath care.
- A healer who turns someone away has stopped being a healer.

## Clergy

Healer-priests, herbalists and field medics. Many travel with adventuring parties for a share of the contract. Its hospices take in the sick, the old and the abandoned.

## In Greenstone

Clinics throughout [[The Old City]] and a large hospice by the river. The healing church is welcome everywhere, including places no other church would set foot.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of Death]], [[Church of the Hearth]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Destruction]], [[Church of Undeath]]

The Healer's church has few true enemies, but it quietly disapproves of churches that make wounds faster than it can close them.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A clinic in the Old City needs an escort for a load of rare herbs coming in from the delta.
- A healer has started refusing to treat patients from one noble house, and will not say why.
- A sickness is moving through a farming village, and the healing church's magic is not stopping it.

## DM notes

- Healers hear everything: wounds tell stories, and patients talk. A healer-priest is one of the best information sources in Greenstone, if the party can earn their trust.
- Not every clinic is what it seems. An NPC posing as a healer is a natural fit for the twist system in the NPC generator.
:::
