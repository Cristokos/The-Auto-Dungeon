*The classes of 5e as Pallimustus uses them: the 2024 rules, with the artificer from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Each page has the class's core traits, its level table, what every feature does, its subclasses and its spell list.*

Essences, awakening stones and ranks sit on top of a class (see [[Essences]] and [[Ranks]]); they never replace a class feature.
