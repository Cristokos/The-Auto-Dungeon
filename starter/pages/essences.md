Essences sit on top of a normal 5e character: the class does the work, and essences give it a theme, a rank, and a few small extras. Nothing here changes class features, spell slots, or the action economy.

## Gaining essences

Essences are physical items: magical objects, usually taken from slain monsters or bought at great cost, that a person absorbs to bond them for life. Characters level up through normal 5e advancement, and essences are never granted by leveling. They are acquired in play: found as loot, bought, earned as rewards, or taken from monsters. Pace rewards to this guideline:

| Level | Essences held |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | None, one or two; the character is still Normal rank |
| 2 | All three, plus the confluence, which forms at once (reach Iron) |

**Iron rank means a full set.** A person becomes Iron rank the moment all their essence slots are filled and the confluence forms. Until then they are Normal rank, however many essences they hold, and plenty of people in the city live their whole lives with one or two. From Iron onward, a character grows by levelling and by feeding their essences awakening stones, not by adding essences.

Level 1 is the climb: players start as ordinary people in a world of ranked power, perhaps already holding an essence or two, and completing their set is the moment they become real adventurers.

A character can hold at most three essences, plus the [[Confluence]]. Ranks above Iron come from growing those essences, not from adding more.

**Absorbing an essence.** Finding or buying an essence doesn't bond it. Until it is absorbed it is just a carried object, and it can be sold, stolen or lost like any other treasure. Absorbing it takes a ritual in a safe place over a long rest. When the rest ends, the essence is bonded for life and fills an empty slot, and its trait and any awakening stones used on it work from then on. A character can't absorb an essence with no empty slot, and can't absorb the same essence twice. Two *different* essences can share an affinity, though (Fire, Smoke and Spark are all Fire-affinity essences), so a set can hold two or even three essences of one affinity; see [[Confluence]].

**Divine essences.** A god can also grant a worshipper a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]], which fills one of the three slots at once, with no ritual. It costs nothing but faith, which makes it the most common way for poor folk to gain an essence, but the god can take it back at any time. See [[Divine Essences]].

## What an essence gives

Each essence grants two things, both deliberately small:

- **Essence trait.** A flavour effect plus one minor benefit: proficiency in one skill or tool tied to the theme, *or* one cantrip from any class list that fits the theme (your casting ability is your highest mental score).
- **Visual and damage flavour.** A character's existing abilities may take on the essence's look. A Dark-essence rogue's Sneak Attack leaves shadow wisps. Changing a spell's damage type is allowed only between types of equal value (for example cold to necrotic) and with DM approval.

[[Awakening Stones]] unlock further abilities.
