**Landmark.** A mile-wide curtain of falling water whose spray rises like smoke and can be seen a day's walk away. *The south-central continent.*

## What players know

The locals say the Mist Gate is a doorway, and that on certain nights the spray parts to show a land on the other side. Rainbows in its spray never fade, even at night.

## What mana has made of it

- The spray holds a permanent moonbow, and anyone who walks under it at midnight can see invisible things until dawn.
- Leaping from the brink into the mist does not always end in the gorge below.
- The roar of the falls drowns out spoken spells within a hundred yards; casters must use gestures alone.

**Dangers.** The drop, the river, and the guardians of whatever lies beyond.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A family asks the team to bring back a son who jumped into the mist a year ago and has been seen in it since.
- **Secret:** The gate leads to a pocket world where time has not moved since the falls first formed.
- **On Earth this would be** Victoria Falls. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
