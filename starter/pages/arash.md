**Normal rank.** [[Peoples of Pallimustus|Runic]] man who sells smoothies from a stall near the Adventure Society campus in [[The Guild District]] of [[Greenstone]].

- **Looks:** Short and stocky and completely bald, with deep blue skin so dark it is nearly black, marked all over with natural runes that glow faintly.
- **Personality:** Friendly, chatty, practical and self-assured. He is a devout follower of the Hero (see [[Church of the Hero]]) and talks about his faith with the same easy certainty he uses to talk about fruit.
- **Family:** His nephew **Jamar** helps on the stall and wants to be an adventurer more than anything.

:::dm
**Offers players:** An easy first encounter for a new arrival, good smoothies, and a stall where news, rumours and messages all seem to pass through.
:::

## What people say

A Runic who sells smoothies near the Society. Everyone knows Arash. Arash, it seems, knows everyone.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **An information hub.** Adventurers stop at his stall before and after contracts, and they talk. Arash hears which teams are in trouble, which contracts are bad and who is new in town.
- **A safe place to leave a message.** Over time his stall can become the party's message drop and meeting point.
- **Faith.** He will ask new arrivals what kind of hero they mean to be, and he is genuinely interested in the answer.

## Playing Arash

**Neighbourhood fixture.** Knows every regular by name and every rumour by noon. Faithful, practical and impossible to dislike.

- **Speech:** Warm and unhurried, full of names and small news
- **How to play:** Hand over a smoothie before anyone orders, then ask what kind of hero they mean to be
- **Tell:** His runes glow brighter when he is excited or moved
- **Toward the party:** Welcoming; new arrivals are his favourite customers
- **Wants from the party:** Their stories, and for someone to talk his nephew Jamar out of doing anything stupid
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Mango and redfruit. Trust me, it's what heroes drink.
>
> The Hero doesn't ask if you're ready. Nobody is.
>
> Team with the blue cloaks? Came back short a man yesterday. Be careful with that contract.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To see Jamar grow up safe, and maybe as a real adventurer
- **Driven by:** His faith in the Hero, and plain kindness
- **Fears:** Outliving another young adventurer he served that morning
- **Bond:** His nephew Jamar, and the regulars at his stall
- **Flaw:** Repeats everything he hears, including things he should not

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: None. He has never held an essence.
