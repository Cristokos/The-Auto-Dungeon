#### Traits

***Uncrowned Royalty (Crown essence).*** Guards, servants and common folk treat her as a person of noble rank, and she can always secure an audience with a local lord or magistrate within a day.

***Voice of Command (Master essence).*** She has advantage on Charisma checks to give orders to people under her authority or to restore order to a panicked crowd.

***Broken Vow (Oath essence).*** She always knows when a creature breaks a promise it made directly to her.

***Hold Court (Sovereign confluence).*** Once per long rest, she calls for silence. Each non-hostile humanoid within 60 feet that can understand her stops talking and listens for up to 1 minute, unless harmed or threatened.

***Fey Ancestry.*** She has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put her to sleep.

***Split Enchantment.*** When she casts an enchantment spell that targets one creature, she can have it target a second creature.

***Spellcasting.*** Intelligence-based (spell save DC 17, +9 to hit). #### Actions

***Hypnotic Gaze.*** One creature she can see within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom save or be charmed by her until the end of her next turn, with a speed of 0 and incapacitated. She can keep it up each turn as her action.

***Fire Bolt (Cantrip).*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +9 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 16 (3d10) fire damage.

***Dagger.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.

#### Reactions

***Instinctive Charm.*** When a creature she can see within 30 feet attacks her, she forces it to make a DC 17 Wisdom save. On a failure, it must target the nearest other creature instead, or the attack is wasted.

**Silver rank.** Elf woman, director of the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] of the [[Adventure Society]].

- **Looks:** Chestnut hair framing an elf's tapered ears, an elegant, unhurried bearing, and an amused glint in her eyes, as if she finds the world more interesting than its rules allow.
- **Essences:** Crown, Master and Oath, forming the **Sovereign** confluence. When she asks a room for silence, it goes quiet.
- **Personality:** Poised, politically sharp and ruthless when she needs to be. She carries authority easily, like someone who earned every room she stands in.
- **Wants:** To curb the great houses' influence over the branch, and to keep her own past exactly where it is.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Contracts that matter, protection on Society business, and her attention, which is a mixed blessing.
:::

## What people say

A self-made elf who climbed to the director's chair without a great house behind her. The houses don't like her, which most adventurers count in her favour.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Her secret.** She is the daughter of Adris Dorgan, one of [[The Big Three]]. She has spent her career making sure nobody connects them, and the connection would end her.
- **Compromises.** Her reforms need allies, and some of her allies are bad people. She has made at least one deal with [[Lucian Lamprey]] of the Magic Society that she would rather not explain, and it involves [[Sophie Wexler]].
- **The white pins.** She suspects someone inside the branch is sending teams on contracts that never passed the counter. She doesn't trust anyone on her staff to look into it, except perhaps an Iron-rank team nobody would suspect.

## Playing Elspeth

**Reforming director.** Elegant, amused and three conversations ahead. She wants the branch clean and the houses out of it, and she will use dirty hands to get there.

- **Speech:** Light, precise and politely dangerous
- **How to play:** Smile, compliment one thing the party did, then ask the question they least want to answer
- **Tell:** Taps one finger on the desk when she has already decided
- **Toward the party:** Curious; an unconnected Iron team is a useful tool
- **Wants from the party:** Discreet work on the white-pin contracts, and people the houses do not own
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> Sit. You are not in trouble. Yet.
>
> The houses think the Society works for them. I am correcting that.
>
> Nobody needs to know you were here. That is rather the point.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** A branch that answers to its own rules, not to the great houses
- **Driven by:** Ambition, and the need to keep her past buried
- **Fears:** Anyone connecting her to Adris Dorgan
- **Bond:** The [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch]] she rebuilt
- **Flaw:** Believes any compromise is acceptable if the goal is right

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user. She rose without a great house behind her.

- **Crown** (legendary, Might): Sovereignty, rightful rule and command. Grants **Uncrowned Royalty**. Crown is rule itself, the weight of the right to command that kingdoms are built around. When the bearer speaks with authority a faint circlet of golden light seems to rest on their brow, and courtiers and commoners alike recognise them as someone born, or chosen, to lead.
- **Master** (uncommon, Might): Command, control and leadership. Grants **Voice of Command**. Master is the essence of authority, the right and the will to direct others. Bearers stand straighter than those around them, and their voice holds a weight that makes people stop and listen.
- **Oath** (uncommon, Might): Promises, loyalty and binding words. Grants **Broken Vow**. Oath is the force of a sworn word. Bearers speak carefully and keep promises to the letter, and when they make a vow a faint ring of light briefly circles their wrist.
- **Confluence: Sovereign** (Crown + Master + Oath): Rightful rule and unbending dignity. Grants **Hold Court**. Sovereign is command, pride and authority fused into the bearing of one born to rule. Bearers stand as though crowned even when bareheaded, servants step aside for them unasked, and their spells unfold with a stately purple-gold gleam.
