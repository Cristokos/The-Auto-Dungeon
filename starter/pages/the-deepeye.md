**Landmark.** The oldest and deepest lake in the world, clear as glass, frozen for half the year. *Far north.*

## What players know

Fishermen on the Deepeye see a vast pale shape under the ice on still nights, and the lake's seals are said to be wiser than most people. Ice roads cross it in winter, and every year the lake takes a sledge or two.

## What mana has made of it

- Water drawn from the lake shows, for a moment, whatever the drinker most wants to find.
- The ice cracks in long straight lines that spell words in an alphabet no one has deciphered.
- Its seals carry quintessence-rich blubber that alchemists prize.

**Dangers.** Freezing water, sudden cracks in the ice, and things that come up through the holes ice fishers cut.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A lake spirit's seal-mother asks for help: something is eating her young from below.
- **Secret:** The pale shape is an eye. Something the size of a mountain lies under the lakebed, asleep, and the lake is the lid on its astral space.
- **On Earth this would be** Lake Baikal. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
