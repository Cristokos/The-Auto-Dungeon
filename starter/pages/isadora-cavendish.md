#### Traits

***Glimpse of the Past (Epoch essence).*** Once per long rest, when you enter a building or ruin, you can see it for 1 minute as it looked when it was new: its furnishings, colours and layout, but no creatures.

***Reflected Memory (Memory essence).*** Once per long rest, you can look into a reflective surface and ask your reflection about something you saw or heard in the past 24 hours. It truthfully describes one detail you failed to notice.

***Unaging (Eternity essence).*** You age at one tenth of the normal rate, and magic and curses that would age you have no effect on you.

***Stone Ring (Henge confluence).*** Once per long rest, you can spend 10 minutes setting stones in a ring up to 30 feet across. Until you next finish a long rest, you know whenever a creature larger than Tiny crosses the ring while you are within 1 mile of it, and a faint chime in your head wakes you if you are asleep.

**Gold rank.** Elf woman, founder and head of [[House Cavendish]], and one of the original settlers of Greenstone.

- **Looks:** Silver-haired and fine-boned, with skin like old parchment and green eyes that miss nothing. She dresses in fashions three centuries out of date and makes them look deliberate.
- **Personality:** Dry, patient and unsentimental. She has outlived most of her enemies and expects to outlive the rest.
- **Essences:** Epoch, Memory and Eternity, forming the **Henge** confluence. Her power is time and remembrance, and she has had a very long time to master it.
- **Ties:** Mother of [[Ephraim Cavendish]] and grandmother of [[Beth Cavendish]] and [[Mose Cavendish]]. She rarely leaves the Cavendish townhouse.

:::dm
**Offers players:** History nobody else remembers, and the backing of the Old City's landlord, at a price.

Isadora remembers the city's founding: who paid for what, who was buried under which wall, and which bargains the founding families made that are still in force. She is the best source of secrets about the Gellers and the Mercers in the campaign, if the party can make her interested in them.
:::

## What people say

The oldest person in Greenstone, people say, and the richest. She was here before the walls. Nobody has seen her outside the townhouse in twenty years, and everybody in the Old City still pays her rent.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **What she remembers.** The founding bargains: who paid Aldous Mercer's debts, who was buried under the north wall and why, and what the Gellers agreed to in exchange for the land the estate stands on. Each is a weapon in the right hands.
- **An audience.** She sees the party only if they bring her something she doesn't know. New stories interest her more than gold.
- **Her fear.** She does not fear death, but she fears her family is not ready. She is watching Beth closely, and may test the party to see if they are worthy friends for her granddaughter.
- **A scene.** The party is shown into a sitting room where every clock has stopped at a different time. Isadora sits by the window, and says without turning round that they are late, and that she has been expecting them for about forty years.

## Playing Isadora

**Ancient matriarch.** Dry, patient and unsentimental, with a memory that reaches back further than the city.

- **Speech:** Slow, precise and wry, in an accent nobody uses any more
- **How to play:** Correct a historical detail the party gets wrong, and then wait
- **Tell:** Touches the stopped clock beside her before she says something true
- **Toward the party:** Amused and assessing
- **Wants from the party:** Something she doesn't already know
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> I remember when this was a marsh. It was better company.
>
> Tell me something new. I shall know if it isn't.
>
> The Gellers built the walls. The Mercers bought them. I own the ground they stand on.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To leave behind a family strong enough to need her no longer
- **Driven by:** Endurance, curiosity and an old grudge or two
- **Fears:** Her family failing the moment she is gone
- **Bond:** Beth, and the Old City
- **Flaw:** Treats everyone, including her own children, as tenants

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Epoch** (legendary, Earth): The great ages of history. Grants **Glimpse of the Past**. Epoch is the great ages of the world, laid down one atop another like layers of rock. Faint bands of colour like sediment show in the bearer's eyes and they speak of centuries as others speak of days, and historians recognise them as living archives.
- **Memory** (legendary, Light): Reflections that remember everything. Grants **Reflected Memory**. Memory is the world behind every reflection, where everything seen is kept. The bearer's reflection lags a heartbeat behind them, and anyone who notices recognises them by the way that reflection sometimes glances at things the bearer missed.
- **Eternity** (legendary, Magic): Timeless endurance beyond all ages. Grants **Unaging**. Eternity is the stillness at the end of all clocks, the part of the world that outlasts everything else. The bearer's eyes fade to the grey of old marble and their face settles into a calm, statuesque cast, and acquaintances recognise them years later because they never look a day older.
- **Confluence: Henge** (Epoch + Memory + Eternity): Stone circles aligned to the sky. Grants **Stone Ring**. Henge is the ring of standing stones raised to catch the sunrise on the longest day, where earth, sky and old magic meet. A bearer has an instinct for arranging things in circles and habitually turns to face the dawn, and their magic flows in a slow ring of pale light.
