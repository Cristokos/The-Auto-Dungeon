The ducal family of [[Greenstone]]. The head of the house holds the title of [[The Duke of Greenstone|Duke]], which makes the Mercers the most politically powerful house in the city: land, guards, contracts, and a family with a hand in everything.

| Wants | Holds power through | Default view of a new Iron-ranker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| To keep the title, and the city, in the family | The dukedom, land, money, and political schemes | Useful, or in the way |

**What players know.** The Mercers are the Duke's family, and nobody in Greenstone forgets it. Lady Thalia is respected. Cassandra is admired. Thadwick is avoided.

## Founders

The house was founded by **Aldous Mercer**, a Gold-rank adventurer who never fought for the city's walls but paid for its charter. When the founding families argued over who should rule the new port, Aldous bought out the quarries, the docks and the debts of half the council, and was named the first Duke. The Gellers built the walls; the Mercers own what is behind them. Neither family has ever let the other forget it.

## Key people

- [[The Duke of Greenstone|The Duke]], head of the house and ruler of the city. Rarely seen.
- [[Beaufort Mercer]], the Duke's brother, who runs the family's interests while the Duke runs the city.
- [[Thalia Mercer]] (Silver), Beaufort's wife and the face of the family in society.
- [[Cassandra Mercer]] (Bronze), their daughter. Elegant, observant and sharp.
- [[Thadwick Mercer]] (Iron), their son. Arrogant, petty and dangerous to embarrass.
- **Retainers.** Minor houses attach their sons to the Mercers. [[Dustin Kettering]] of the Kettering family serves on Thadwick's team.

## Interests and income

- **The green marble.** The Mercers own the quarry company that cuts the green stone at [[North East Quarry Village Four]], the stone that gives Greenstone its name and its finest buildings.
- **The port.** Tariffs and docking fees at the Old City port and [[The Marinas]] go to the ducal treasury, and the family owns many of the warehouses beside them.
- **Land.** The family buys land through agents, quietly, and always seems to own the fields beside a new road or a new astral space entrance.
- **Patronage.** Every licence, permit and charter in the city passes through Mercer hands, and favours flow back the other way.
- **Interests.** Keeping the title, keeping the other houses smaller than the Mercers, and keeping the [[Adventure Society]] from becoming a power that answers to nobody.

## DM notes

- **Thadwick** treats anyone who embarrasses him as an enemy for life. He is exactly the kind of young noble a [[The Builder Cult|Builder cultist]] can recruit with promises of power.
- **The Mercers and the Gellers** are rivals in politics and friends in society, which is the most dangerous kind of rivalry.
- **Land schemes.** Some of the fields being bought around [[Harrowfield]] are Mercer purchases, through agents. The family is buying near astral space entrances because it knows something about them.
- **The Duke's heir.** The Duke has no children of his own. Beaufort's line is next, which makes Thadwick the family's problem in more ways than one.
:::
