Greenstone stands on three pillars: the [[Adventure Society]], the [[Noble and Adventuring Houses|noble houses]] and [[The Churches]]. Each does something different, and none rules the others. No king sits above them. The city is ruled by [[The Duke of Greenstone|its Duke]], who must answer to the pressure the three pillars bring, and the rest runs on the deals, alliances and rivalries between them.

- **The Adventure Society** keeps the monsters in check. It licenses adventurers, posts contracts, judges rank and disciplines its members.
- **The noble houses** hold money and power. They own land, fund expeditions, train the next generation and protect their own.
- **The churches** hold faith and healing. They tend the sick, bless the living and dead, and speak with the gods' real authority.

Around them are other powers: the [[Magic Society]], the hidden [[Cults]], the distant [[Astral Powers]], foreign realms such as [[The Storm Kingdom]] and its [[The Crown|crown]], and the tribes beyond the cities: [[The Drift Hosts]] of the steppe and [[The Cloud Clans]] of the southern mountains.
