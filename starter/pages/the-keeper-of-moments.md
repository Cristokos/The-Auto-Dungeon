The Keeper of Moments is the [[Astral Powers|great astral being]] that governs the flow of time, keeping it moving smoothly between every world. It claims no single name and prefers not to be discussed, so it collects several: the Keeper, the Keeper of Sands, the Underclock.

Even the Keeper cannot change the past. Scholars who dream of time magic find that a hard sentence to read.

## Raythe

The Keeper's prime avatar is **Raythe**, a Diamond-rank elf with brown skin and green-flecked hair a shade lighter than her skin. She favours the name Keeper of Sands, for the hourglass.

## What it wants

The Keeper wants time to flow: smoothly, in one direction, at the same pace in every world. It has no interest in what people do with their moments, only that the moments come in order. It is the most impersonal of the great astral beings, and the most patient.

What troubles it is anything that bends the flow: astral spaces where time runs fast or slow, magic that stops a moment or skips one, and above all anyone who tries to undo what has been done.

## How it touches mortals

- **Time magic.** Every essence power that slows, freezes or hastens time works because the Keeper allows it, and stops at the line it draws. People who use such powers sometimes report a sensation of being watched by something very old and very calm.
- **Lost moments.** Travellers who come back from an astral space and find more time has passed, or less, than they lived. The Keeper sends no one to fix these; it simply notes them.
- **Raythe.** When time is truly at risk, the Keeper sends its avatar. She appears, speaks very little, and leaves when the problem is solved, often in a way the people involved didn't like.

## Cultists and bargains

There is no church of the Keeper. Clockmakers, hourglass makers and some old elves keep its sign, an hourglass with no sand, and say a word to it at midnight. A secretive circle of chronomancers called the Sand Society studies time magic and believes the Keeper can be petitioned. It cannot, but it does notice them.

The Keeper does not make bargains. It does, very rarely, give warnings.

:::dm
**Using the Keeper.** It explains why no essence power reverses time and why time-flavoured abilities slow, freeze or skip moments but never undo them. If two worlds' clocks ever drift apart, the Keeper is the one who notices. Raythe is quiet and precise and has no interest in Pallimustus unless time itself is at risk.

**Hook.** An astral space near the Greenstone quarries is running time faster than the world outside: a team who went in for a week came back aged ten years. Someone has been using the space to grow things, monsters or crops or children, and the Keeper has noticed. Raythe arrives at the branch to ask, very politely, who is responsible.
:::
