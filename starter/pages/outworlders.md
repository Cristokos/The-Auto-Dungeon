title: "Outworlders"

---

Any player may choose a species not native to Pallimustus, such as a dwarf, halfling, gnome, tiefling or orc, by playing an **outworlder**: someone who arrived from another world. Each such character is a one-off, the only one of their kind anyone has seen. Players of native species may also choose to be outworlders.

- **In the world**, outworlders arrive from other worlds, often after dying in their own, and their bodies are made of magic.
- **At the table**, an outworlder gains one bonus language, starts with no local reputation, and stands out to anyone who knows what to look for. To a sensitive nose, outworlders smell faintly of springtime.
- **Socially**, how outworlders are treated follows the *Outworlders* dial in [[Political Dials]].

**Celestines.** When two outworlders have a child on Pallimustus, the child is always a Celestine. It is the only way new Celestines come into the world, which is why they are rare. See [[Peoples of Pallimustus]].

There are no dwarves, gnomes or halflings native to Pallimustus. If players meet one, they have met an outworlder.

> *Outworlders do not arrive by accident.* (a [[Whispers|whisper]])
