**Bronze rank.** Runic woman, valuer of cores and monster parts at the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] of the [[Adventure Society]].

- **Looks:** Compact and quick, with glowing runic lines along her forearms, silver-rimmed lenses pushed up on her forehead and gloves she changes between every item.
- **Personality:** Sharp, fast and done talking before you are. She never haggles: the price is the price.
- **Wants:** Clean goods, honest reports, and the Magic Society kept at arm's length.
- **Essences:** Rune, Coin and Glass, forming the **Karmic** confluence. She can read a core's rank and history at a glance and knows when it has been tampered with.
- **Ties:** She trained in the [[Magic Society]] and left after an argument she won't discuss. She has not been back.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Fair prices for cores and parts, and a quiet word when something an adventurer brings in should not exist.
:::

## What people say

Magda in valuations. She'll tell you what your loot is worth in about three seconds, and she's never wrong. Don't argue. Don't ever argue.

:::dm
## DM notes

- She has been asked twice this season to value cores that came from monsters of the wrong rank for where they were killed. Someone is farming spawns in an astral space the branch doesn't know about.
- **Prices.** Her valuations are the branch's official rates. A party that wants more has to sell elsewhere, and she knows every buyer in the city who pays more and why.
- **The farmed cores.** The wrong-rank cores came through two different teams. She has kept a sample of each, and she will hand them to anyone she trusts to find the astral space they came from.
- **The Society.** She left after reporting a researcher for testing on people. Nothing happened to him. If the party ever brings her proof against [[Lucian Lamprey]], she will help them for free.
- **A scene.** The party tips their loot onto Magda's counter. She sorts it in seconds, writes a figure, then picks up one core, frowns, holds it to the light, and asks them very precisely where they killed the thing that dropped it.

## Playing Magda

**Brisk valuer.** Fast, sharp and honest, with zero patience and a long memory.

- **Speech:** Short, quick and certain
- **How to play:** Finish the party's sentence and give them the price
- **Tell:** Pulls her lenses down when she's interested
- **Toward the party:** Brisk and businesslike, warmer to honest sellers
- **Wants from the party:** Clean goods, and someone to look into the farmed cores
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> Forty silver. Next.
>
> No. The price is the price.
>
> Where did you get this? Exactly where.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To expose whoever is farming spawns, and the researcher who got away
- **Driven by:** Honesty, and an old grudge against the Magic Society
- **Fears:** Being ignored again
- **Bond:** Her counter and her ledgers
- **Flaw:** Assumes everyone is lying until they prove otherwise

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Rune** (uncommon, Magic): Inscription, glyphs and structured magic. Grants **Glyph Sight**. Rune is magic given shape through writing and symbol. Faint script traces itself across the bearer's hands as they work, and their spells leave brief glowing glyphs hanging in the air.
- **Coin** (common, Sin): Trade, value and the haggle. Grants Proficiency in Persuasion, or one language of your choice. The essence of coin and commerce. Bearers can weigh a purse by eye, their voices take on a trader's easy patter, and their spells glint like spinning gold.
- **Glass** (rare, Light): Clarity, fragility and shaped transparency. Grants **Glassworker's Touch**. The essence of sand made clear by fire. A bearer's skin takes on a faint glossy sheen, their eyes become clear as polished marbles, and their spells give off a brittle, chiming glitter like sunlight through a window.
- **Confluence: Karmic** (Rune + Coin + Glass): Every deed weighed and returned. Grants **Returned in Kind**. Karmic is the world's ledger written in glyphs of power, where every deed is weighed and paid back in kind. Bearers feel a small, satisfied tug whenever a wrong is repaid, and their spells show as interlocking runes that tip like scales before they settle.
