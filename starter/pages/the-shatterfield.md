**Landmark.** A forest of trees flattened outward in a perfect ring, around a crater that still hums. *Far north.*

## What players know

Something fell here long ago and knocked down every tree for days in every direction. The trunks still point away from the centre like the spokes of a wheel. Trappers from the north say compasses spin near the crater and that the snow there never settles.

## What mana has made of it

- Every fallen trunk has turned to a dark, glassy wood that rings like a bell when struck; carvers pay well for it.
- Within a mile of the crater, spells that move things (*thunderwave*, *gust of wind*, *telekinesis*) push outward from the centre, whatever the caster intends.
- The snow falls upward for an hour at dawn.

**Dangers.** Monsters spawned here are often a rank above their kind. Packs of glass-furred wolves hunt the ring.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A Magic Society survey team went in to find what fell, and only their sledge dogs came back.
- **Secret:** Whatever fell is still in the crater, and it is not finished falling: it is slowly sinking toward an astral space beneath the ice.
- **On Earth this would be** the Tunguska blast site in central Siberia. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
