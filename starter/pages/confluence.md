The confluence is a fourth essence that a character's first three create together. It forms the moment the third essence is absorbed, so absorbing a final essence grants two at once.

The DM chooses the confluence to fit the set. For example, [[Dark essence|Dark]], [[Blood essence|Blood]] and [[Sin essence|Sin]] together can produce **Doom**. Players can pick essences freely, or roll on the monster that dropped them for flavour.

**Affinities.** Every essence has an affinity, and when three essences have no exact recipe, their affinities decide the confluence. Many confluences call for the same affinity twice or even three times. That never means the same essence twice, which no one can absorb: it means different essences that share an affinity. Fire, Smoke and Spark are all Fire-affinity essences, so a character holding Fire and Smoke has two Fire affinities.

A [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] counts by its affinity like any other essence, and each god also has a divine confluence of its own that forms only from one of its exact recipes. See [[Divine Essences]].

:::dm
The Confluences browser lists every confluence with its recipes and affinity trio. The `confluences` generator list holds suggested combinations; only Dark + Blood + Sin = Doom is fixed, and the rest are yours to keep or replace.
:::

## How a confluence is found

1. **Look for an exact recipe.** Some confluences have one or two recipes: three specific essences that always make them. If the character's three essences match one, that is their confluence.
2. **Otherwise, use the affinities.** Take the affinity of each essence, put the three in alphabetical order, and look up the trio. Every possible trio has a confluence, so there is always an answer.
3. **The order of absorption doesn't matter.** Only the final set of three counts.

## Worked examples

- **Exact recipe.** Magic, Balance and Rune is a recipe for **Karmic**. Their affinities are Magic, Light and Magic, which on their own would point somewhere else, but the recipe comes first.
- **Affinities only.** Fire, Smoke and Swift have no recipe. Their affinities are Fire, Fire and Swift. The Fire + Fire + Swift trio gives **Wildfire**: flames racing faster than horses.
- **Two roads to one place.** Blood, Hunger and Moon is a recipe for **Vampire**. Wolf, Hunt and Moon is not, but Wolf and Hunt are both Blood-affinity and Moon is Dark, and the Blood + Blood + Dark trio is also Vampire. Different essences, same confluence.
- **Same essences, different idea.** Sword, Shield and Light have no recipe; their affinities, Might + Might + Light, give **Justice**. A sworn sword is what the set adds up to, even though none of the three is about law.

## What a confluence gives

- A fourth essence in the character's set, with its own theme and look.
- A quirk or feature of its own, listed on its page in the Confluences browser.
- The moment it forms, the character is Iron rank.

## Questions and answers

- **Can I choose my confluence?** Only by choosing your third essence. Many players plan their set around the confluence they want.
- **Can a confluence change?** No. Essences are bonded for life, so the confluence is too.
- **Is the same confluence always the same?** The name and the feature are, but how it shows depends on the person. Two Vampire confluences can look very different.
- **What about divine essences?** They count by affinity like any other essence, and each god has a divine confluence that forms only from one of its exact recipes. See [[Divine Essences]].
- **Who decides?** The DM has the final say. The tables give an answer for every set, but if a different confluence fits the story better, use it.

> *A confluence is not random. Somewhere, someone understands exactly how it is chosen.* (a [[Whispers|whisper]])
