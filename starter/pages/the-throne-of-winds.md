**Landmark.** The highest mountain in the world, rising above a range of white peaks into air too thin to breathe. *The eastern lands.*

## What players know

A monastery clings to the Throne's shoulder, where monks breathe magic instead of air and train adventurers who can survive the climb. The summit is said to touch the astral, and nobody who has reached it has come down the same.

## What mana has made of it

- Above the snow line, essence abilities cost more to use, but their effects are stronger.
- The monastery runs a spirit coin farm that mints only in the coldest month.
- Prayer flags here carry messages on the wind to anyone named on them, anywhere in the world.

**Dangers.** Cold, thin air, avalanches, and the yetis and wind spirits of the high passes.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** The monastery asks for a team to recover a monk who reached the summit and has been sitting there, unmoving, for a year.
- **Secret:** The summit is where a god once stood to look at the world, and the footprint is still warm.
- **On Earth this would be** Mount Everest and the Himalaya. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
