#### Traits

***Kindle (Fire essence).*** At will, as an action, you can light or snuff out a nonmagical flame no larger than a campfire within 10 feet of you.

***Quick Mending (Renewal essence).*** Once per long rest, when you spend a Hit Die to regain hit points, you regain the maximum amount for that die instead of rolling it.

***Featherfall (Wing essence).*** Once per long rest, when you fall, you can descend at a rate of 60 feet per round and take no damage from that fall.

***Rekindling Breath (Phoenix confluence).*** As an action, you can breathe warmly on a plant that is wilted, frostbitten or dying, but not dead for more than a day, and restore it to full health. This has no effect on plant creatures.

***Wild Shape.*** Twice per short rest, becomes a beast of CR 1 or lower for up to 4 hours.

**Bronze rank.** Human woman, daughter of [[Danielle Geller]] and older sister of [[Humphrey Geller]].

- **Looks:** Statuesque and handsome, with short-cropped dark hair. Her eyes turn bright purple when her eye familiar rests inside them.
- **Essences:** Fire, Renewal and Wing, forming the **Phoenix** confluence.
- **Fighting style:** A summoner. She has a bonded phoenix with feathers like living fire, and three summoned familiars: a dervish of ash and cinders, a huge floating eye on leathery wings whose beam can burn, and a green lantern that heals.
- **Personality:** Confident, dry and protective of her little brother, which she shows by teasing him.
- **Ties:** She leads her own Bronze team, and trains in the Geller mirage chamber with [[Cassandra Mercer]] when neither of their families is watching.

:::dm
**Offers players:** A capable Bronze-rank ally, and a view of what a Geller looks like a few years further on.

Henrietta is close friends with Cassandra Mercer of [[House Mercer]], which neither family entirely approves of.
:::

## What people say

Danielle Geller's daughter, the one with the burning bird. When Henrietta's phoenix flies over the delta, the farmers say it means the season will be good.

## DM notes

- **Her familiars.** The eye is the one she uses outside a fight: it can rest in her own eyes, turning them purple, and let her see what it saw. A guard who meets her gaze may not realise how much she is reading.
- **Big sister.** She is hard on Humphrey because she knows exactly how dangerous the next rank will be. If he is ever in real trouble she will arrive, and she will be very angry about it afterwards.
- **Cassandra.** Their friendship is real, and it is also the one channel of trust between the Gellers and the Mercers. If a house crisis needs talking down, they are the ones who can do it.
- **A scene.** The phoenix lands on the branch roof and every Iron-ranker in the yard stops to stare. Henrietta walks in below, ignores all of them, and asks the party whether they're the ones her little brother keeps talking about.

## Playing Henrietta

**Dry older sister.** Confident, capable and teasing, with a protective streak she hides behind sarcasm.

- **Speech:** Dry, amused and a little bored
- **How to play:** Tease a party member the way she teases Humphrey, then quietly help them
- **Tell:** Her eyes flicker purple when the eye familiar is watching
- **Toward the party:** Amused, and protective of anyone Humphrey likes
- **Wants from the party:** People she can trust to watch Humphrey's back
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> You're the ones Humphrey won't stop talking about. You don't look like much either.
>
> Don't touch the bird. It's fine, he likes you. Don't touch him anyway.
>
> If my brother gets hurt with you, I'll know.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To reach Gold before her mother
- **Driven by:** Pride, and love for her brother she would never admit
- **Fears:** Burying Humphrey
- **Bond:** Humphrey, Cassandra and her phoenix
- **Flaw:** Mocks the people she cares about until they believe she doesn't

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user. The Phoenix confluence uses the compendium's Firebird entry; rename it if the compendium gains its own.

- **Fire** (common, Fire): Heat, flame and fierce energy. Grants **Kindle**. The essence of living flame. Bearers run warm to the touch, ember glints flicker in their hair, and their spells crackle and smell of woodsmoke.
- **Renewal** (common, Life): Healing, recovery and second chances. Grants **Quick Mending**. The essence of renewal and fresh starts. Bearers' cuts close with a faint green shimmer, they wake from any rest looking refreshed, and their magic leaves behind the smell of rain.
- **Wing** (common, Wind): Feathers, flight and open sky. Grants **Featherfall**. The essence of the wing. Bearers feel lighter on their feet, stray feathers drift down where they have stood, and spectral wings briefly flare behind them when they cast.
- **Confluence: Phoenix** (Fire + Renewal + Wing): Glowing bird whose cry brings morning. Grants **Rekindling Breath**. The firebird, whose glowing plumage lights the orchard at night and whose warm breath brings frostbitten trees back into blossom. Bearers have a few streaks of flame-coloured hair and are always warm to the touch, and their magic trails sparks like drifting plumes.
