The church of **Journey**, the god who embodies travel, roads and the open way, and the change that every journey brings.

- **Standing:** Beloved by travellers and traders.
- **Symbol:** a winding road beneath a star.
- **Colours:** earth brown and pale green.

## The god

Journey is the act of travelling and the change it brings. Roads are one of its faces. Journey cares that people move, explore and are changed by what they find.

- **What the god gives:** Journey brings the open road, discovery, trade between distant peoples, and the growth that comes from leaving home.
- **Offended by:** Closed roads, borders, prisons, and anything that holds people in place.

:::dm
### The dark face

Journey brings exile, wandering without end, and refugees driven from their homes. Journey is as pleased by a forced march as by a pilgrimage.

- **Will accept:** Exile, migration and banishment, and anything else that sets people moving.

### Secret motives

- Journey wants borders open and people moving, and its road-wardens smuggle anything and anyone across them.
- Journey is content for a war or a famine to drive whole peoples from their homes. Displacement is still a journey.
:::

## Divine essence

Journey grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers never seem dusty from the road, always know which way is north, and grow restless indoors.*

- **Essence trait:** Proficiency in Survival or with navigator's tools.
- **Sense: Sense the Way.** As an action, you learn the safest route to any place you have been before and the direction of the nearest road or path, and you always know which way is north.
- **Channel: Swift Feet.** As a bonus action, for 1 minute your speed increases by 20 feet and you ignore difficult terrain. Friendly creatures within 10 feet of you when you use this gain 10 feet of speed for the same time.
- **Blessing: Blessing of the Road** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures ignore nonmagical difficult terrain and their speed increases by 10 feet. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *longstrider*, *expeditious retreat*, *goodberry*; level 2: *find steed*, *misty step*, *pass without trace*; level 3: *leomund's tiny hut*, *haste*, *water walk*; level 4: *dimension door*, *freedom of movement*; level 5: *teleportation circle*, *commune with nature*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Swift.

**Divine confluence: Wayfarer.** A Wayfarer has walked further than anyone. Bearers are never lost and never tired, roads seem shorter under their feet, and their magic carries them from place to place. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Journey (divine) + Pilgrim + Crossroads; Journey (divine) + Horse + Wind; Journey (divine) + Swift + Herald. It grants **Road Never Ends**: You and up to six companions travelling with you cover twice the normal distance when travelling overland, without becoming exhausted.

**What the god asks.** Shelter travellers who ask. Walk a pilgrimage at least once a year. Never close a road.

**What makes the god take it back.** Refusing shelter to a traveller in need. Closing or blocking a road.

## What players know

Priests of Journey bless caravans and keep the waystations along the roads. A traveller in trouble can almost always find shelter at a Journey waystation, and a meal, even without coin. Many adventurers carry a Journey charm for luck on the road.

## Beliefs

- Every road is sacred, and every traveller is a guest.
- Those who never leave never change.
- Share the road, share the fire, share the way.

## Aspects

Journey has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Roads:** The face of Journey that builds and keeps the ways: road-wardens, bridge-builders and waystation keepers.

## Clergy

Wandering priests, road-wardens and keepers of waystations. Pilgrims of Journey walk the roads for a year and a day to be ordained.

## In Greenstone

A roadside temple at the main gate where caravans are blessed before leaving, and a string of waystations along the road to the coast, where pilgrims take ship for [[Rimaros]].

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of Trade]], [[Church of Liberty]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Destruction]]

Journey's priests dislike anything that closes a road, whether it is a monster, a bandit or a noble's toll gate.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A waystation on the coast road has gone silent, and the church wants to know why.
- A pilgrim of Journey wants an escort through the marshes on the last leg of their year-and-a-day walk.
- A noble house has built a toll gate across an old pilgrim road, and the road-wardens are about to tear it down by force.

## DM notes

- Waystations are ideal places for chance meetings, odd guests and secrets. They also make good "safe" locations that turn out not to be.
:::
