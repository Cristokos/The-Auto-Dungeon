**Silver rank.** Human woman, wife of [[Beaufort Mercer]] and mother of [[Cassandra Mercer|Cassandra]] and [[Thadwick Mercer|Thadwick]].

- **Looks:** Olive skin and delicate features, with an air of almost impossible vitality. She looks younger than her children.
- **Essences:** Might, Potent and Swift, forming the **Onslaught** confluence.
- **Role:** A former adventurer who now leads the family's expeditions and negotiations. She is the face of the house in society.
- **Personality:** Gracious, decisive and tired of cleaning up after her son.
- **Fighting style:** A heavy lancer. In her adventuring days she charged through monster lines on a warhorse, and she still rides out with every expedition the family sends.
- **Ties:** The house's expedition captains report to her, not to Beaufort.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Expedition contracts, and a fair hearing even from a Mercer.

Thalia is on friendly terms with [[Danielle Geller]], and the two women manage the rivalry between their houses more than their husbands realise. She loves Thadwick and has no illusions about him.
:::

## What people say

Lady Thalia, the one Mercer everyone respects. People say she was the best adventurer of her generation, gave it up to marry Beaufort, and has regretted only the marriage.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Expeditions.** She hires adventurers to scout new astral space entrances and survey land the family is buying. She pays well and expects silence about where they went.
- **The land schemes.** She knows what Beaufort is buying and why, and she does not like it. A party that brings her proof of harm can get her to stop it, at a cost to her marriage.
- **Thadwick.** She is the first to accept that her son may be lost. If the party has to fight him, she will not stand in their way, and she will never forgive them either.
- **A scene.** Thalia arrives at the branch on a grey warhorse in full scale armour, back from an expedition, and asks the desk for the best Iron team in the building for a survey job. Then she looks at the party and asks who they are.

## Playing Thalia

**Gracious commander.** Courteous, decisive and very tired, with a soldier's eye under the society manners.

- **Speech:** Clear, polite and commanding
- **How to play:** Give the party a choice and make it plain which one she expects
- **Tell:** Sits like a rider even in a chair
- **Toward the party:** Courteous and fair, interested in useful people
- **Wants from the party:** Reliable scouts, and someone honest about her son
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> You'll be paid well. You'll tell no one where you went.
>
> My husband handles money. I handle results.
>
> Thadwick is my son. That isn't the same as being right.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To see her daughter free and her son saved
- **Driven by:** Duty to the house, and to her children above it
- **Fears:** Losing Thadwick to whatever he's reaching for
- **Bond:** Cassandra, Thadwick and Danielle Geller
- **Flaw:** Keeps the peace long after she should have broken it

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user. The Onslaught confluence uses the compendium's Cataphract entry; rename it if the compendium gains its own.

- **Might** (common, Might): Raw strength and crushing force. Grants Proficiency in Athletics or Intimidation. The essence of sheer physical power. Bearers' shoulders broaden and their grip grows crushing, their blows land with a dull thunderclap, and flagstones crack where they plant their feet.
- **Potent** (uncommon, Might): Raw power and amplified force. Grants **Surge**. Potent is power pushed past its usual bounds. A pressure builds in the air before the bearer acts, and their spells and blows flare brighter and land harder than they should.
- **Swift** (common, Swift): Speed, reflexes and quick feet. Grants **Fleet**. The essence of speed. Bearers fidget whenever they are made to wait, leave faint afterimages when they move suddenly, and rarely seem out of breath.
- **Confluence: Onslaught** (Might + Potent + Swift): The armoured rider and the charge. Grants **Quick Buckle**. The cataphract is the heaviest of horsemen, rider and mount alike clad in scale and mail, who can be armed and in the saddle before the trumpet stops sounding. Bearers sit tall and walk with a rider's rolling gait, never seem weighed down by their gear, and their magic comes with the drumming of distant hooves.
