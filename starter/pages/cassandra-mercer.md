**Bronze rank.** Human woman, daughter of [[Beaufort Mercer]] and [[Thalia Mercer]], and older sister of [[Thadwick Mercer]].

- **Looks:** Elegant and self-possessed, with dark hair and watchful eyes.
- **Personality:** Observant, witty and sharp: the knife to a blunter person's hammer. She wants to be an adventurer on her own terms, not the family's.
- **Essences:** Dagger, Secret and Mirror, forming the **Moth** confluence. She works alone, unseen and from behind, and is far more dangerous than her party gowns suggest.
- **Ties:** She has no fixed team. She takes contracts with whoever interests her, which annoys her father and delights her.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Information, a sharp ally inside the ducal family, and a very interesting evening.

Cassandra seeks out interesting newcomers to learn more about them, often at concerts or parties. She may start a relationship with a player character, and her family may order it ended. She is close friends with [[Henrietta Geller]].
:::

## What people say

The Mercer daughter who goes to every concert in the city and talks to everyone. Society says she is the best catch in Greenstone; adventurers say she is the scariest Bronze-ranker they've seen in a dark room.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Collector of people.** She learns the party's names, essences and ambitions within a week of their arrival, and she will share them with Beaufort if she is cornered. She would rather not.
- **Her real ambition.** To leave Greenstone and adventure somewhere nobody knows her name. A player character who offers her that is the most interesting person she has ever met.
- **Thadwick.** She is the one who sees him sliding toward the cult before anyone else. She will come to the party with it, quietly, if she trusts them.
- **A scene.** At a recital in the Mercer music room Cassandra slides into the empty seat beside a party member, compliments their badge, and asks them three questions about their essences before the second movement starts.

## Playing Cassandra

**Curious blade.** Witty, watchful and restless, and always one question ahead.

- **Speech:** Light, clever and full of questions
- **How to play:** Ask something personal as if it were small talk
- **Tell:** Turns her rings when she has learned something useful
- **Toward the party:** Fascinated, and possibly more
- **Wants from the party:** An adventure that isn't the family's, and someone to have it with
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> You're new. I like new.
>
> My father would hate you. That's a compliment.
>
> Tell me something about yourself you haven't told anyone in this city.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To make her own name, far from the dukedom
- **Driven by:** Curiosity, and a need to be her own person
- **Fears:** Ending up as her mother: gracious, useful, and trapped
- **Bond:** Henrietta Geller, and her brother, despite everything
- **Flaw:** Plays with people as if they were puzzles

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Dagger** (common, Swift): Knives, close work and hidden blades. Grants Proficiency in Sleight of Hand or Intimidation. The essence of the dagger. Bearers develop quick, deft fingers and a habit of keeping a blade within reach, and their magic arrives as a sudden glint at close quarters.
- **Secret** (uncommon, Dark): Hidden things and kept confidences. Grants **Hidden Ways**. Secret is the essence of what is hidden and kept. Bearers speak softly and smile knowingly, and they feel a tug of curiosity toward locked drawers and walls that sound hollow.
- **Mirror** (uncommon, Light): Reflection, truth and doubles. Grants **True Reflection**. Mirror is the reflection that shows exactly what stands before it, nothing more and nothing less. Bearers' eyes take on a faint silvery sheen, and they catch themselves studying reflections as if checking that the world is honest.
- **Confluence: Moth** (Dagger + Secret + Mirror): Night wings drawn to the flame. Grants **Drawn to Light**. The moth flits through the dark, restless and quick, always pulled toward the light. Bearers have soft, dusty-looking hair and large dark eyes, they fidget near candles, and their magic scatters like powder from beating wings.
:::
