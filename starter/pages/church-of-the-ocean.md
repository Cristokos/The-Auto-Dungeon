The church of **Ocean**, the god who embodies the sea and every water that flows to it, from the smallest stream to the deepest trench.

- **Standing:** Powerful along the coasts and rivers.
- **Symbol:** a wave curling over a shell.
- **Colours:** sea green and deep blue.

## The god

Ocean is the sea and every water that runs to it. Rivers are its inland face. It is vast, deep and indifferent to everything that lives on its surface.

- **What the god gives:** Ocean brings food, trade routes, the life of the rivers and the delta, and a way to anywhere.
- **Offended by:** Dams and anything else that holds the water back from flowing where it will.

:::dm
### The dark face

Ocean brings drowning, floods, shipwreck and the crushing deep. It takes what it wants, and gives nothing back.

- **Will accept:** Drownings, floods and the loss of ships and sailors.

### Secret motives

- Ocean wants its waters free and its claims respected. Its church takes a share of every cargo that crosses the sea, and quietly sinks ships that refuse to pay.
- Ocean speaks with the merfolk more than with the crown, and would side with the merfolk if it came to a choice.
:::

## Divine essence

Ocean grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers smell of salt, their hair is always slightly damp, and their voices sound like the tide.*

- **Essence trait:** Child of the Water. You have a swimming speed equal to your walking speed, and you can hold your breath ten times longer than normal.
- **Sense: Sense the Current.** As an action, you learn the depth, currents and dangers of any water within 300 feet, and sense every creature in it, until the end of your next turn.
- **Channel: Tidal Surge.** As an action, a wave bursts from you in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in it must succeed on a Strength save against your stone spell DC or take 2d8 bludgeoning damage (3d8 from Silver rank) and be pushed 15 feet. The wave puts out nonmagical fires.
- **Blessing: Blessing of the Deep** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures can breathe water and have resistance to cold damage. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *create or destroy water*, *fog cloud*, *ice knife*; level 2: *misty step*, *blur*, *hold person*; level 3: *water breathing*, *water walk*, *tidal wave*; level 4: *control water*, *watery sphere*; level 5: *maelstrom*, *cone of cold*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Water.

**Divine confluence: Tidelord.** A Tidelord carries the ocean with them. Bearers smell of salt and storms, water rises to meet their hands, and their magic comes in waves that do not stop. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Ocean (divine) + Leviathan + Brine; Ocean (divine) + Water + Pearl; Ocean (divine) + Ferryman + Harbour. It grants **Master of Tides**: You can breathe water, have a swimming speed of 60 feet, and once per short rest can cast *tidal wave* without a spell slot.

**What the god asks.** Give the water its due: a coin at every crossing. Never foul a river or a well. Save the drowning when you can.

**What makes the god take it back.** Fouling water on purpose. Damming a river.

## What players know

The Church of the Ocean blesses ships, ferries and anyone who works the water. In Greenstone, its priests tend the river shrines across the delta. Most ferrymen will not cross until they have dropped a coin at the shrine.

## Beliefs

- All water returns to the sea. So do all things.
- Respect the deep, and it may spare you.
- Give the water its due, and it will carry you.

## Aspects

Ocean has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Rivers:** The Ocean's inland face: rivers, streams and the delta. River-folk and ferrymen pray to it, and its shrines mark every crossing.

## Clergy

Ship's priests, ferry-blessers and tide-readers. Its priests are welcome on every ship, and few captains sail without one. [[Merfolk]] worship the Ocean in their own way.

## In Greenstone

A river temple on stilts above the water, and small shrines at every ferry crossing in [[The Delta and Marshes|the delta]].

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of the Storm]], [[Church of Trade]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of the Earth]]

Ocean and Storm are uneasy partners: sailors pray to both, and hope they are not arguing that day.

## Adventure hooks

- The river shrines have started overflowing with water that tastes of salt.
- A ship's priest of the Ocean has been thrown overboard by a crew who claim the god demanded it.
- The church asks the party to escort a merfolk envoy through the delta to a meeting no one else may know about.
