**Arena.** A massive, ancient stone stronghold in the [[The Old City|Old City]]'s warehouse district, older than the city walls and built to hold out against [[Monster Surges|monster surges]]. Now it holds Greenstone's fighting pits.

## What players know

- **The pits.** Several levels of fighting pits, from sand rings for Normal-rank brawlers to warded cages where Iron and Bronze-rankers fight with their powers. Crowds pack the stone galleries and shout down into the pits.
- **The boxes.** Private boxes look over the best pits. Nobles, Society officials and crime lords watch from them, and some of them own fighters.
- **The fighters.** Some fight for the money, some for the love of it, and some because they owe someone. Preparation rooms below the pits are where the deals are made.
- **The book.** Betting runs through Hubert Bertinelli, who keeps the books and never forgets a debt.
- **Nightingale.** The current crowd favourite is a fast, silver-haired fighter called the Nightingale. See [[Sophie Wexler]].

> *Fights are to submission. Mostly.* (a sign over the fighters' stairs, partly scratched out)

## DM notes

**Who runs it.** The pits are Old City business, and [[The Big Three]] each have a stake. Cole Silva wants the best fighters for himself. Clarissa Ventress shelters fighters to spite him. [[Lucian Lamprey]] of the Magic Society is a regular in the best box.

**Running a bout.** A pit fight is a 5e duel with house rules: no killing blows, no outside help, and a bell when a fighter yields or drops. Purses for an Iron-rank bout run 1 to 5 iron spirit coins. An upset win makes the party known in the Old City overnight.

**Hooks.**
- A fighter asks the party to throw their bout, because the alternative is that someone kills her brother.
- The Fortress was built to hold against surges. The old surge tunnels under it are still there, and someone is using them.
:::
