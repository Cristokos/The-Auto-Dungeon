**Settled tribe.** The peoples of the great western mountains of the southern continent, living high above the jungle among the clouds. They are settled but scattered: villages on terraced slopes days apart, linked by rope bridges and mountain paths. They have no noble houses and no kingdom, only chieftains and the people who choose to stand with them.

## Life among the clouds

- **The terraces.** Every village is cut into the mountainside in steps, each one a field. The magic in the mountains makes them absurdly fertile: a family can live on a terrace the size of a large room.
- **The clouds.** The high peaks are wrapped in cloud most of the year, and the Clans have learned to live in them. Clan-crafters can thicken a patch of cloud enough to walk on, and their oldest villages are partly built of it. The cloud-shaping done in great cities elsewhere is a cruder cousin of their craft.
- **The jungle below.** Far beneath the terraces lies [[The Verdant Deep]]. The Clans trade with the jungle's edge for wood, medicine and monster parts, and go down into it only when they must.
- **The beasts.** Long-necked, woolly mountain heidels carry loads along the paths, and huge condor-like monsters circle the peaks. Taming one of those birds is the Clans' greatest honour.

## Rallying to the strong

A village belongs to its **chieftain**, and a chieftain is whoever the village rallies around: usually the strongest person in it, sometimes the wisest, always someone who can keep the monsters off the terraces. When a stronger person climbs the path, a village may simply decide to follow them instead. Nobody calls this treason; it is how things work.

| The chieftain's rank | What rallies to them |
| --- | --- |
| Iron | A household or a single terrace |
| Bronze | A village of a few dozen families |
| Silver | A valley: several villages and the bridges between them |
| Gold | A mountain: a dozen valleys that answer when called |
| Diamond | All the Clans, in war or in crisis, and only then |

The strong are expected to earn it. A chieftain who cannot protect their people, or who takes too much, wakes one morning to find the village following someone else.

## What players know

- The Clans are polite, proud and very hard to impress. They measure a visitor by what they can do for a village, not by where they come from.
- Clan-crafted cloud goods (cloudsilk, cloud-glass, walkable cloud matting) sell for high prices in the great cities.
- Travellers who climb uninvited usually meet a chieftain on a narrow bridge, and that conversation decides the rest of the visit.

## Notable chieftains

- **Huaman of the Seven Bridges**, a Silver-rank chieftain who holds the busiest pass in the mountains and charges every caravan a toll in news.
- **Old Ayra Cloudhand**, a Gold-rank crafter whose mountain follows her because she built half its bridges herself. She is very old, and her valleys are quietly choosing sides for when she is gone.
- **The Condor Riders**, a band of young fighters who follow no chieftain and ride tamed birds. Villages call on them in trouble, and pay them however the riders ask.

## DM notes

**Rallying the players.** If the party protects a Clan village, they can win its loyalty. A village rallies to the strongest member of the party once that character meets the rank in the table above and has defended it at least once. Followers give lodging, guides, food, and a small band of Iron-rank helpers, but expect the party to come when called.

**Travel.** Mountain paths and rope bridges halve travel speed. A Clan guide restores normal speed and avoids most monster nests.

**Hooks.**
- Two chieftains claim the same village after it rallied to one and then the other in the same week. Both ask the party to settle it.
- A tamed condor has come home without its rider, carrying a monster core no one in the mountains recognises.
- Something in [[The Verdant Deep]] has started climbing, and the lowest terraces have gone quiet.
:::
