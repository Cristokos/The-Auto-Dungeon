The guild that licenses adventurers, posts contracts, assesses rank and polices misconduct. Every city of note has a branch with a director, administrators and a contract board. It is meant to be apolitical and service-minded, and in practice each branch carries the politics of the city around it. For players, it is the quest board, the rulebook and the first source of institutional friction.

## What players know

The Society licenses adventurers, posts contracts, judges rank, and disciplines those who break its rules. Every adventurer answers to it, at least officially. It claims to stand above politics and serve only the common good. Most people believe it, mostly. Getting your first license here is the start of every adventuring career, and the Society never forgets a name once it has one on file.

## Tensions

- Versus [[Noble and Adventuring Houses|the houses]]: the Society claims authority over all adventurers; elite families expect their children to be exempt.
- Versus the [[Magic Society]]: one controls the fighters, the other the knowledge and crafting; each needs the other and resents it.

## Greenstone branch

The [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] stands on the square where three of the city's main roads meet: a long hall of pale green stone with the contract board, the counters, the assessment wing and the infirmary under one roof.

## Contracts

Contracts are posted on the branch's board in five tiers, Iron to Diamond. A team may take a contract of its own rank or lower, never higher, and a mixed team counts as its lowest member's rank. The Society takes a tenth of every reward. Generate a board with the **Contracts** generator.
