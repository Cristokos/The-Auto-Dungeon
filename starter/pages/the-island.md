**District.** An artificial island off the shore of [[Greenstone]], raised long ago as a breakwater to shelter the harbour and since built over by everyone who could afford to move off the mainland. It is paved in green marble and coloured tile, and it is where the city's money, guilds and temples live.

## What players know

- **Getting there.** A long toll bridge runs out from the [[The Old City|Old City]], and everyone pays to cross it. The faster way is **the Loop**, a two-lane subway in a tunnel under the water, with terminals across the Island and on the mainland.
- **Green and gold.** The streets are wide, clean and lit all night. Buildings are faced in green marble and decorated with bright tile. Some of the grandest are shaped from solid cloud.
- **[[The Guild District]]**, in the north-west, holds the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Adventure Society]] campus, the [[Magic Society]] and the compounds of the other guilds.
- **[[The Temple District]]** is built around Divine Square, with a temple for every god who can afford one.
- **[[The Ducal Palace]]** is the seat of [[The Duke of Greenstone|the Duke]].
- **[[Sailor's Watch Inn]]** sits at the Island's seaward edge, with the best ocean views in the city.
- **The north marina** and its transit terminal handle passenger boats, yachts and floating cloud houses. See [[The Marinas]].

> *Robert sells fruit on the Island at three times the price Herbert charges in the Old City. It is the same fruit. People still buy it.*

## Who lives here

Great-house townhouses, rich merchants, successful adventurers, Society officials and senior clergy. Normal-rank servants cross from the Old City every morning and go back every night. An Iron-ranker with no money is tolerated here, as long as they look like they are on their way somewhere.

## DM notes

**Fixed points.** An artificial breakwater island for the rich, green marble and coloured tiles, a bridge with a toll, the Loop under the water, and the Guild District in the north-west.

**Hooks.**
- The Loop stops dead between two stations with the party aboard, in the dark, under the sea. The lights come back on to show a passenger missing.
- A great house wants a minor house's townhouse on the Island, and the minor house wants an adventurer to persuade it not to sell.
- The toll on the bridge goes up for adventurers only, overnight, and nobody will say who ordered it.
:::
