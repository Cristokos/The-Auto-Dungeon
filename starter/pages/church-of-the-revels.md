The church of **Revels**, the god who embodies celebration, wine, joy and every pleasure of the flesh.

- **Standing:** Popular, loud and rarely taken seriously.
- **Symbol:** a cup overflowing with grapes.
- **Colours:** wine red and green.

## The god

Revels is joy taken in the senses: feasting, wine, music, dancing and desire. Vineyard and Lust are faces of Revels. It cares that pleasure is enjoyed, not what it costs.

- **What the god gives:** Revels brings celebration, friendship, festivals, and relief from hard lives.
- **Offended by:** Joyless denial, and those who would forbid pleasure to others. The Church of Purity is its rival.

:::dm
### The dark face

Revels brings excess, addiction and ruin. A feast while the city starves, or a pleasure that consumes the person enjoying it, is still a revel.

- **Will accept:** Drunkenness, debauchery, waste and indulgence without limit.

### Secret motives

- Revels wants pleasure, and the church's pleasure houses and festivals are where the city's powerful drink, gamble and talk too much. The church listens, and remembers.
- Revels is content for addiction to ruin people. Someone who cannot stop is a perfect worshipper.
:::

## Divine essence

Revels grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers have flushed cheeks and easy laughs, music seems to start near them, and they smell faintly of wine.*

- **Essence trait:** Proficiency in Performance or with brewer's supplies.
- **Sense: Sense Desire.** As an action, you learn what each creature you can see within 30 feet wants most right now, such as food, rest, love, a drink, or escape.
- **Channel: Revel.** As an action, choose any number of creatures within 30 feet: each gains temporary hit points equal to 1d8 plus your level and is no longer frightened. Alternatively, one hostile creature must succeed on a Wisdom save against your stone spell DC or fall into helpless laughter until the end of its next turn.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Merriment** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures have advantage on saving throws against being frightened and against despair. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *tasha's hideous laughter*, *charm person*, *purify food and drink*; level 2: *calm emotions*, *enthrall*, *suggestion*; level 3: *hypnotic pattern*, *create food and water*, *beacon of hope*; level 4: *confusion*, *charm monster*; level 5: *modify memory*, *dream*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Life.

**Divine confluence: Bacchant.** A Bacchant is the party, wherever they go. Bearers are flushed with good cheer, music starts when they walk in, and their magic is giddy, warm and very hard to resist. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Revels (divine) + Feast + Mirth; Revels (divine) + Ferment + Perfume; Revels (divine) + Bell + Lullaby. It grants **Endless Feast**: Once per long rest, you can spend 10 minutes hosting a meal or celebration. Up to six creatures who share it gain temporary hit points equal to twice your level and advantage on saving throws against being frightened for 8 hours.

**What the god asks.** Celebrate the god's festivals. Share your wine and your table. Never deny a guest their joy.

**What makes the god take it back.** Forbidding pleasure to others. Swearing off joy with a vow of abstinence.

## What players know

The Church of the Revels is the church of festivals, taverns and good wine. Its priests organise the city's celebrations and run the best drinking hall in Greenstone. Nobody takes it seriously, which its priests find very useful.

## Beliefs

- Life is short. Drink deeply.
- Joy shared is joy doubled.
- Deny yourself nothing that harms no one else.

## Aspects

Revels has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Vineyard:** Revels as the patron of the harvest and the vintner's craft: the grape, the cask, and the patience of good wine.
- **Lust:** Revels as desire and indulgence, honoured in pleasure houses and by those who refuse to deny themselves anything.

## Clergy

Festival-masters, vintners, tavern-keepers and courtesans. Its high days are the city's great festivals, and its priests are always the last to leave.

## In Greenstone

A temple-tavern in the entertainment district, vineyards on the hills inland, and a festival every season.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of Trade]], [[Church of the Hearth]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Purity]]

Purity calls the Revels a corruption of the soul. The Revels call Purity a church that has forgotten how to laugh.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- The harvest festival is in three days, and the church's prize wine has been stolen.
- A festival-master needs bodyguards for a masked ball that half the great houses will attend.
- Purity preachers have started picketing the Revels temple, and both sides are looking for a fight.

## DM notes

- Revels festivals and masked balls are perfect for intrigue, assassinations and disguises. The church hears more gossip than any other, and trades it freely after a few drinks.
:::
