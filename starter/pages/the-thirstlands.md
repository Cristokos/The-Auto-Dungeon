**Region.** The great desert inland from [[Greenstone]], running east from the coast hills for weeks of travel. It has no king. Its oases are held by water-lords, its roads by caravan masters, and its open sands by nobody at all.

**What players know.** Every caravan out of Greenstone crosses the Thirstlands sooner or later. The road runs from oasis to oasis, past salt pans, red dunes and the ruins of towns that ran out of water. The heat kills more travellers than the monsters do, and the monsters kill plenty.

## The land

- **The Coast Hills.** The dry hills behind Greenstone, past [[North East Quarry Village Four]], where the inland road begins. The [[House Vane|Vane]] estate and its caravan inns lie here.
- **The Red Sea of Sand.** Towering red dunes that shift with every storm. Caravans go around them. Things live under them.
- **The Salt Pans.** Vast white flats that flood once in a generation and shimmer with mirages the rest of the time. Salt cutters work them in the cool months.
- **The Well Road.** The chain of oases that carries the caravan trade east, a few days apart. Each is a walled town around a spring.
- **The Broken Country.** Rocky canyons and mesas in the far east, full of old ruins and astral space entrances nobody has properly mapped.

## Who holds it

- **The water-lords.** Each oasis is ruled by the family that controls its spring. They charge every caravan for water, and they are rich, proud and constantly feuding. A water-lord's word is law within sight of their walls.
- **The caravan masters.** Great merchant companies that run the trade, hire the guards and know the road. The strongest of them are as powerful as any water-lord.
- **The sand clans.** Nomad families who live in the open desert, herd goats and hunt monsters for their cores. They guide caravans for a price and raid the ones that won't pay.
- **The Vanes.** The [[House Vane|Vanes]] own the inns and wells at the coast end of the road, and every caravan pays them first.

## Law and custom

- **Water law.** Stealing water is the worst crime in the desert, worse than murder. Refusing water to a dying traveller is the second worst.
- **Guest right.** A traveller who has eaten at someone's table is under their protection until they leave.
- **Slavery** is not legal under Greenstone's charter, but the far oases do not answer to Greenstone, and some of them keep slaves.

## Trade

The Thirstlands send salt, glass sand, desert herbs, monster cores and goods carried from the east. They buy grain, cloth, tools and weapons, and above all they buy guards: adventurers are always in demand on the Well Road.

## DM notes

- **Using the Thirstlands.** It is Greenstone's frontier: caravan escorts, oasis politics, desert monsters and ruins. It is also where [[The Red Table]] hunts, and the inland road is the best way to bring the party to the Vane estate without them suspecting.
- **The heat** is a constant pressure. Use exhaustion for travel in the day, and let the party learn to move at night.

**Hooks.**
- Two oases are at war over a spring that is drying up. Both are hiring adventurers, and the spring is drying up because something underneath it is drinking.
- A caravan from the far east arrived with every member asleep and could not be woken. The goods are fine.
- A sand clan has found a ruin in the Broken Country with a working astral space inside, and wants a Greenstone team to clear it, in exchange for half the cores.
- A caravan master pays a fortune for the party to escort a sealed wagon to the Vane inns, and asks no questions about the noises inside. Neither should they.
:::
