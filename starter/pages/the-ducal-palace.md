**Palace.** The seat of [[The Duke of Greenstone|the Duke of Greenstone]] on [[The Island]]: the Duke's residence, the courts, the treasury and the barracks of the household guard.

## What players know

- **The Duke's guard** is the city's real armed force. It is large, well paid and stiff with essence users, and people say it could stand up to the Adventure Society branch if it ever had to. One of the Berts (Bertram) wears its uniform.
- **The courts** sit in the palace's public wing, where magistrates hear cases in the Duke's name, alongside priests of [[Church of Justice|Justice]].
- **Bounties** issued by the Duke are posted at the palace gate and copied to the Society's board.
- **Audiences** are possible, in theory. In practice a petitioner sees a clerk, then a secretary, then nobody.

:::dm
**Hook.** The Duke is never seen in public, and some in the Old City say he hasn't been for years. The guard captain knows why, and will pay a lot to keep it that way.
:::
