#### Traits

***Weathervane (Wind essence).*** You always know which way the wind is blowing, and you can accurately predict the weather where you are for the next 24 hours.

***Kindle (Fire essence).*** At will, as an action, you can light or snuff out a nonmagical flame no larger than a campfire within 10 feet of you.

***Furnace Gust (Firestorm confluence).*** Once per long rest, as an action, you can exhale a blast of scorching wind in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the cone must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier) or be pushed 10 feet away from you. Unprotected flames in the cone flare high and go out, and loose papers and other light objects are blown away. The gust deals no damage.

**Iron rank.** Human man, a cousin on [[Rick Geller]]'s team and its magic specialist, with wind and fire.

- **Personality:** Bookish, nervous before a fight and fearless during one.
- **Looks:** Narrow-shouldered and bespectacled, with the Geller olive skin, a scorch mark on every sleeve, and hair that stands up whenever he casts.
- **Essences:** Wind, Fire and Spark, forming the **Firestorm** confluence. He throws fire and lightning and steers both with gusts of wind.
- **Ties:** Rick's cousin and Jonah's best friend since they were small. Rick trusts him to do the thinking, and Henry trusts Rick to do the shouting.

:::dm
**Offers players:** A fellow caster to compare notes with.

If [[The Builder Cult]] strikes at Rick's team, Henry is the one who dies. Introduce him early and make him likeable so it lands.
:::

## What people say

The quiet one on Rick Geller's team, always reading at the next table. People who have only seen him in a tavern are surprised to learn he is the one who sets the whole fight on fire.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **His notebook.** Henry records every monster the team fights, with sketches and what worked. He will lend it to a caster he likes, and it is genuinely useful: advantage on one check to recall a weakness of a monster in it.
- **Before a fight** he is pale, muttering and sure they will all die. Once it starts his voice goes calm and he calls targets better than Rick does.
- **If he dies.** Rick blames himself and gets reckless; Jonah stops talking. Give the party the chance to help the team through it, or to be blamed for not being there.
- **A scene.** Henry is at a corner table in the branch with three books open, trying to work out why his fire and lightning keep pulling against each other. He asks a passing caster, very politely, whether their essences ever argue.

## Playing Henry

**Nervous scholar, calm in the fire.** Worries out loud beforehand, then becomes the steadiest person on the field.

- **Speech:** Quick and apologetic, full of qualifications, until a fight starts
- **How to play:** Ask a party caster a technical question, then forget his drink while they answer
- **Tell:** Pushes his spectacles up whenever he is about to cast
- **Toward the party:** Shyly friendly, delighted by any fellow caster
- **Wants from the party:** Someone to compare notes with, and a second opinion on his confluence
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> I'm sure it's fine. It's probably fine. Is it fine?
>
> Rick, left side, now. No, your other left.
>
> Do your essences ever disagree with each other? Mine argue.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To make Bronze and write the monster guide the branch should already have
- **Driven by:** Loyalty to Rick and Jonah
- **Fears:** Freezing up when it matters, and being the reason someone dies
- **Bond:** His cousins, and his notebook
- **Flaw:** Overthinks until the moment is gone

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Wind** (common, Wind): Moving air, freedom and open skies. Grants **Weathervane**. The essence of restless air. A breeze seems to follow bearers indoors, their hair and cloaks are always stirring, and their spells arrive on a sudden gust.
- **Fire** (common, Fire): Heat, flame and fierce energy. Grants **Kindle**. The essence of living flame. Bearers run warm to the touch, ember glints flicker in their hair, and their spells crackle and smell of woodsmoke.
- **Spark** (common, Fire): Lightning, quickness and bright ideas. Grants Proficiency in Arcana or with tinker's tools. The essence of the spark, the first flash of lightning and of inspiration. Bearers' hair lifts with static, small shocks jump from their fingertips to door handles, and they are prone to sudden bursts of insight.
- **Confluence: Firestorm** (Wind + Fire + Spark): Scorching winds that feed the flames. Grants **Furnace Gust**. The firestorm, a blaze so great that it breathes its own howling wind. Bearers have ember-hot breath and singed eyebrows, loose cloth and hair stream around them, and their magic roars out like a furnace door thrown open.
