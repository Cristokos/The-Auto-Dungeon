**Landmark.** A salt lake at the lowest point of the land, so still it reflects the sky perfectly. *The eastern lands.*

## What players know

Nothing lives in the Salt Mirror and nothing sinks in it. Pilgrims float on its surface to have visions, and alchemists as far away as Vitesse prize its salt for preservation and purification.

## What mana has made of it

- The reflection in the lake shows the sky of another world: other stars, other moons.
- Salt from its shore is a reagent for purification magic and keeps any food, or body, from rotting.
- On the solstice, the reflection and the sky change places for an hour.

**Dangers.** Dehydration, salt-crusted undead from ancient battles on its shores, and visions that do not let go.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A pilgrim saw something in the reflection that saw them back, and now cannot sleep.
- **Secret:** The other sky in the mirror belongs to an astral power's home, and the lake is a window both ways.
- **On Earth this would be** the Dead Sea. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
