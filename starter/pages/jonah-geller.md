#### Traits

***Rooted (Earth essence).*** You have advantage on ability checks and saving throws made to avoid being shoved or knocked prone.

***Homing Sense (Hearth essence).*** When you absorb this essence, choose one dwelling as your home; you can change it by living somewhere else for a month. You always know the direction and rough distance to your home.

***Field Smithy (Forge confluence).*** During a short rest, you can repair one broken or damaged nonmagical metal weapon, tool or piece of armour using only a fire and a flat stone, as if you were working in a full smithy with smith's tools.

***Second Wind.*** Once per short rest, as a bonus action, regains 8 (1d10 + 3) hit points.

***Action Surge.*** Once per short rest, takes one additional action on its turn.

**Iron rank.** Human man, a cousin on [[Rick Geller]]'s team.

- **Personality:** Easygoing, loyal and a little too trusting.
- **Looks:** Big, sunburnt and smiling, with a dented shield across his back and a farmer's hands. He looks more like a delta tenant than a Geller and doesn't mind.
- **Essences:** Shield, Earth and Hearth, forming the **Forge** confluence. He is the team's defender: he plants his feet and nothing gets past.
- **Ties:** Rick's cousin and Henry's best friend. He grew up on the family's delta farms and still knows half the tenant families by name.

:::dm
**Offers players:** A friendly face on a rival team.

**A victim in waiting.** If [[The Builder Cult]] strikes at the Gellers, Jonah is separated from his team in an astral space and planted with a star seed (see [[The Builder]]). The seed can be removed, but a crude cleansing could kill him. Whether the party can save him is a strong early-campaign stake.
:::

## What people say

The friendliest Geller in the city. Jonah will buy a stranger a drink, help a carter with a stuck wheel, and believe whatever story he's told while he does it.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Trusting.** Jonah believes people. A cultist, a con artist or a Mercer agent can get information out of him simply by being nice, and he will be horrified when he finds out.
- **The farms.** He visits the tenant villages on rest days. A party that helps one of them earns his loyalty for life, and an easy route into the Geller household.
- **After the seed.** If he survives the star seed he is quieter, and sometimes stares at nothing. Something may still be listening through him.
- **A scene.** Jonah is helping a tenant family load sacks of rice onto a cart outside the Geller warehouses, in his good clothes, laughing. He waves the party over to help and asks their names before he asks their business.

## Playing Jonah

**Big-hearted shield.** Warm, simple and brave, and far too ready to believe the best of people.

- **Speech:** Slow, friendly and full of farm sayings
- **How to play:** Offer help nobody asked for, and take any answer at face value
- **Tell:** Rubs the back of his neck when he's embarrassed
- **Toward the party:** Instantly friendly, as if they were already teammates
- **Wants from the party:** Friends, honestly. And maybe a hand on the farms in surge season
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Here, grab the other end. Thanks, friend.
>
> Rick says I trust people too much. Rick doesn't trust anyone.
>
> Get behind me. No, really, get behind me.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To keep his team alive, and to farm a little when he's old
- **Driven by:** Loyalty, and a plain sense of right and wrong
- **Fears:** Being the one who lets something past his shield
- **Bond:** His cousins and the delta tenant families
- **Flaw:** Trusts anyone who smiles at him

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Shield** (common, Might): Protection and warding others. Grants Proficiency with shields or in Medicine. The essence of the shield and the one who stands in front. Bearers instinctively place themselves between danger and their companions, and a faint translucent barrier flickers around them when they cast.
- **Earth** (common, Earth): Stability, weight and endurance. Grants **Rooted**. The essence of stone and soil. Bearers gain a faint granite grain to their skin and a heavy, deliberate tread, and their magic arrives with the rumble of shifting ground.
- **Hearth** (uncommon, Fire): Home, warmth and belonging. Grants **Homing Sense**. Hearth is the warmth of home and the fire that families gather around. Bearers smell faintly of woodsmoke, their hands are always warm, and their magic glows like banked embers.
- **Confluence: Forge** (Shield + Earth + Hearth): Fire, hammer and metal made one. Grants **Field Smithy**. Forge is flame, hammer and iron joined in the endless work of the smithy. Bearers' forearms grow corded and soot-flecked, their voices ring with a hint of struck metal, and their spells land in a shower of sparks with the tang of hot iron.
:::
