*The [[Adventure Society]]'s campus in [[Greenstone]]: where adventurers are licensed, contracts are posted, ranks are judged and misconduct is answered for.*

## What players see

The branch is a walled campus in [[The Guild District]] on [[The Island]], with its own Loop terminal, a private dock, and a Trade Hall full of shops for adventurers. Its heart is the administration building on a broad square, so every cart and heidel on the Island seems to pass its doors. It is a long hall of pale green stone, three storeys high, with a slate roof and a squat tower at one corner that holds the bell. Over the main doors hangs the Society's sign: a shield crossed by a sword and a key, painted fresh every spring. Adventurers come and go at all hours. Some are fresh-faced and nervous, some are limping, and now and then one walks past glowing faintly, and everyone else steps aside.

> *The doors are open, as they always are. Inside, the noise hits you first: dozens of voices, the scrape of boots, a clerk shouting a team's name, and somewhere a laugh that goes on too long. It smells of wax, ink, wet cloaks and the faint ozone tang of magic.*

## Areas

### The Forecourt

A paved yard behind iron railings, with hitching posts for heidels, a public water trough and a stone bench that has been worn smooth by people waiting for their teammates.

> *A notice by the gate lists the branch's hours, which are "always", and the fines for brawling in the forecourt, which are steep. A boy in a Society tabard is selling yesterday's contract list for a copper.*

Hawkers, hopeful recruits and families looking for news of missing adventurers wait here. [[Dobbs]] runs messages from the gate.

### The Great Hall

The heart of the branch: a long, high hall with a gallery running around the upper floor. Benches, tables and a fire at each end. Adventurers gather here to wait for contracts, form teams and argue about both.

> *Banners of teams that have reached Gold rank hang from the rafters, faded with age, and one of them is scorched black along the edge. Below them, a dozen teams sit at long tables. Heads turn when you walk in, weigh you up, and turn away again.*

- **The contract board** fills the wall opposite the doors: a huge corkboard divided into five sections by brass rails, Iron at the bottom and Diamond at the top. The Iron section overflows. The Diamond section is usually empty, and when it isn't, a guard stands beside it. Generate its notices with the **Contracts** generator.
- **The team board** beside it carries handwritten notes from adventurers looking for teammates, offering healing, or selling gear.
- **The memorial wall** by the west fire lists adventurers of this branch who died on contract. New names are cut in brass. People touch the newest ones on their way past.

### The Counters

A row of six windows along the north side of the hall, each under a painted sign: *Registration*, *Contracts*, *Contracts*, *Assessment*, *Payments* and *Enquiries*. Brass speaking grilles, ink-stained counters, and a queue that never quite ends.

> *A tired clerk looks up as you reach the window, dips a pen, and asks for your names, your ranks, and whether you have read the conditions. Nobody has ever read the conditions.*

- **Registration:** a new adventurer's licence costs 10 iron spirit coins and a signature on the Society's charter. The applicant's name, essences and rank go on file. [[Nessa Quill]] usually takes it.
- **Contracts:** a team pulls a notice from the board and brings it here. The clerk checks every member's rank against the contract's tier, records the team, and hands over the client's details. A team may take a contract of its own rank or lower. [[Pell Hobbard]] will not be argued with on this.
- **Payments:** rewards are paid on proof of completion, less the Society's tenth.
- **Enquiries:** directions, complaints, lost property and news. The clerk on duty knows a little about everything and admits to nothing.

### The Valuation Room

A side room off the hall with a heavy door, a long table under bright lamps, and scales, lenses and warded boxes. The branch buys monster cores, quintessence and monster parts here, and sends what it buys on to the [[Magic Society]].

> *Every surface is spotless. A woman with silver-rimmed lenses pushed up on her forehead holds a monster core to the light, turns it once, and names a price before you have finished saying hello.*

Run by [[Magda Fenn]]. Prices follow the core prices in the markets, less a little for the convenience.

### The Assessment Wing

Down a corridor behind the counters: a waiting room with hard chairs, three testing rooms, and the assessment hall itself, a round chamber with a domed ceiling and a ring of scratched brass set into the floor.

> *The ring in the floor hums faintly when you step over it. Scorch marks, claw marks and one perfect handprint have been pressed into the walls over the years, and nobody has painted over them.*

Rank assessment is how an adventurer's rank becomes official. The assessor watches a candidate use their powers, questions them, and may set a sparring bout against a staff fighter or a captive monster. Passing means a new badge and access to that tier of the board. [[Ulric Stane]] runs most Iron and Bronze assessments.

### The Training Yard

An open yard behind the hall, walled and floored with packed sand. Practice dummies, archery butts, a climbing wall and a pond deep enough to drown in. Teams can book it by the hour.

> *Someone has drawn a target on the far wall in chalk, and someone else has burned a hole through the middle of it. A Leonid in a sweat-soaked shirt is teaching three teenagers how to fall over.*

### The Infirmary

A clean, bright ward on the ground floor with twelve beds, a dispensary and a chapel corner for the [[Church of the Healer]]. Adventurers back from contracts are patched up here for a small fee, or free if they were hurt on Society business.

> *The smell of herbs and boiled linen. Most of the beds are empty. One has a curtain drawn around it, and the healer's voice behind it is low and patient.*

Run by [[Brother Ilian Marsh]].

### The Records Hall

On the first floor: shelves to the ceiling, ladders on rails, and a long reading table. Every licence, contract, report and complaint the branch has ever taken is filed here, along with maps, monster accounts and reports on [[Astral Spaces|astral spaces]] in the region.

> *It is quiet up here, the noise of the hall reduced to a murmur through the floor. An old man at the far end of the table does not look up, but says your team's name before you give it.*

Adventurers in good standing may read the maps and monster reports. Team files and complaints are closed. Kept by [[Tobias Wren]].

### The Briefing Rooms

Four small rooms along the gallery with a table, a slate and a door that locks. Used for contracts that need explaining, clients who prefer not to be seen in the hall, and teams who need to shout at each other in private.

### The Silver Lounge

A quiet room at the end of the gallery, with deep chairs, a good fire and a bar. A brass plate on the door reads *Silver rank and above*. It isn't enforced by any guard; nobody below Silver has tried twice.

> *Through the half-open door: a handful of people who look no older than you, sitting very still, and a drink that refills itself.*

### The Discipline Office

Behind a plain door at the back of the ground floor: an office, a hearing room with a long table, and two holding rooms with warded walls. The Society judges misconduct by its own adventurers here, from brawling to contract fraud to worse.

> *The door is unmarked, but people lower their voices when they walk past it.*

Run by [[Captain Reyna Holt]].

### The Director's Office

On the top floor, reached by a private stair from the gallery. Wide windows over the square, a desk buried in reports, and a map of the region on the wall stuck with coloured pins: red for open astral spaces, black for surges, white for teams overdue.

> *The director does not stand when you come in. There are seven white pins on the map behind them, and their eyes go to it twice while you speak.*

[[Elspeth Arella]]'s office. [[Isolde Varrick]], the deputy, has the smaller office next door, with a door between them that is usually shut.

### The Heidel Yard and Stores

Stables for the branch's heidels and wagons, a smithy for quick repairs, and a store of rope, lamps, rations, signal flares and body bags that teams can draw on for Society contracts.

## Rules of the branch

- **Licence:** every adventurer working in Greenstone needs one. Working without one is a fine the first time and a hearing the second.
- **Contracts by rank:** a team may take a contract of its own rank or lower. A mixed team counts as its lowest member's rank.
- **The Society's tenth:** a tenth of every contract reward goes to the branch.
- **Reports:** every contract ends with a written report. Lying in one is misconduct.
- **Emergencies:** in a monster surge, every licensed adventurer in the city may be called up. Refusing is misconduct.
- **No brawling in the hall.** Sparring in the yard is fine. The difference is whether you booked it.

## DM notes

- **The branch's politics.** Arella wants the branch independent of the great houses and the Duke. Varrick thinks it should pick a side and win. The [[Noble and Adventuring Houses|houses]] each have friends on the staff, and the branch's setting on the [[Political Dials]] decides how much that matters.
- **The white pins.** Seven teams are overdue on Arella's map. Some are dead, one is hiding, and one was sent on a contract that never came through the counter. That is a ready-made [[Adventure Society|Society]] mystery.
- **Using the staff.** Pell and Nessa are the faces players will see every session. Make them consistent and let them warm to the party slowly; a clerk who bends a rule for them once is worth more than any reward.
- **Assessment as a scene.** Run a rank assessment as a short set piece: a demonstration, three hard questions, and a bout against a staff fighter or a captive monster spawned a rank above its kind.
