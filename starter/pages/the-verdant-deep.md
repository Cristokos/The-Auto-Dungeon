**Landmark.** An endless rainforest along a river so wide its far bank is out of sight. *The south-west continent.*

## What players know

The Verdant Deep grows back overnight: paths cut one day are gone the next. River traders sail its waters in armoured boats, and the people who live in its canopy villages speak to the trees.

## What mana has made of it

- Some trees walk, slowly, following the rain.
- Plants here can carry essences: a vine might hold a poison essence and a flower a healing one, and harvesters can extract them.
- The river changes course every few years, swallowing towns and revealing old ruins.

**Dangers.** Giant predators, poisonous everything, and getting lost forever.

## DM notes

- **Hook:** A canopy village asks for help: their sacred tree has started walking away, and the village is built in it.
- **Secret:** The forest is one creature, and it is beginning to notice the people who cut it.
- **On Earth this would be** the Amazon basin. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
