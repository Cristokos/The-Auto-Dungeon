**Silver rank, close to Gold.** Head of the Geller family; a pillar of Greenstone.

- **Looks:** A beautiful woman with dark olive skin and black hair, who looks perhaps forty and is a good deal older.
- **Essences:** Balance, Dimension and Swift, forming the **Time** confluence. People call her the Time Witch, and not to her face.
- **Personality:** Formidable, protective, politically sharp.
- **Family:** Her husband Keith, and their children Henrietta and [[Humphrey Geller|Humphrey]].

:::dm
**Offers players:** Patronage, protection, and obligations that come with both.
:::

- **Affiliation:** [[House Geller]]

## What people say

Head of the Geller house and one of the most powerful people in the city. Protective of her own; dangerous to her enemies.

:::dm
## DM notes

Keep her distant: she is one push from Gold, and people of her standing decide what happens to a city; they are not party members.

## Playing Danielle

**Matriarch.** Gracious, amused and always in control. She protects what is hers absolutely and treats everything else as a board to arrange.

- **Speech:** Warm, unhurried, faintly amused
- **How to play:** Be charming and generous, then make clear she already knows everything they did this week
- **Tell:** Goes very still when she is truly angry
- **Toward the party:** Interested, the way a collector is interested
- **Wants from the party:** Allies for Humphrey, information on the other houses, and loyalty she can call on later
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> Sixteen generations. We have outlasted worse than you.
>
> Do sit down. I insist, and I so rarely have to insist twice.
>
> Humphrey speaks well of you. Do not make him wrong.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To reach Gold, and to see her children surpass her
- **Driven by:** The Geller legacy, and Greenstone's safety
- **Fears:** Burying one of her children
- **Bond:** [[House Geller]], her husband Keith and their children
- **Flaw:** Believes she can manage everything, including people

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user, trained by her family. Silver rank, close to Gold.

- **Balance** (uncommon, Light): Equilibrium, poise and fair measure. Grants **Steady Footing**. Balance embodies equilibrium in body and in deed. Bearers carry a settled calm and rarely shift their weight, and their magic shows as a pair of faint scales that steady before every spell.
- **Dimension** (rare, Magic): Space folded, cut and crossed. Grants **Fold Step**. The essence of distance and the space between places. Bearers seem to arrive a moment before they should, and their magic leaves faint creases in the air like folded paper.
- **Swift** (common, Swift): Speed, reflexes and quick feet. Grants **Fleet**. The essence of speed. Bearers fidget whenever they are made to wait, leave faint afterimages when they move suddenly, and rarely seem out of breath.
- **Confluence: Time** (Balance + Dimension + Swift): Moments stretched, stopped and stolen. Grants **Stolen Second**. Time held in the hand. Bearers are never late and never hurried, and the world around them seems to pause, very briefly, when they decide something matters.
