**Wilderness.** The Mistrun Delta: waterways, swamp, farmland and monster-rich ground around [[Greenstone]], where the Mistrun River spreads out to meet the sea. Early contracts and exploration.

## What players know

Beyond the walls, the delta stretches out in channels and swamp. It is where most new adventurers earn their first contracts, and where many learn their first hard lessons. Locals will tell you which waterways to avoid. They will not always tell you why.

> *The magic in the delta has been running thicker this year. The old-timers have started counting.* (a [[Whispers|whisper]])

## The river

The **Mistrun** comes down out of the north-east, fed by a waterfall that pours out of the astral aperture near [[North East Quarry Village Four]], and turns a strip of the desert into fields on its way to the sea. The river carries a fine, cool mist that hangs over the water and the delta at dawn and rarely burns off before noon. The name is older than the city.

## The delta

- **Farms and villages.** The delta is fertile, and most of Greenstone's food grows here: rice and vegetables on raised fields, fish and eel ponds in the channels. Village life is quiet, damp and hard, and villagers rely on the Adventure Society to keep the spawns down.
- **Boats.** Delta folk build flat boats of a local green stone that floats, and pole them through channels too narrow for anything else. Green-stone boats are the cheapest way to get around and a common sight in the city's canals.
- **The deep marsh.** Away from the farms the channels turn into reed beds and black water, full of monsters. Most early contracts come from here.
- **The coast.** Where the delta meets the sea are mudflats, mangroves and small fishing towns, and the beaches south of the city.

## Dangers

Spawns in the delta are mostly Iron rank: swamp beasts, river serpents, leeches the size of dogs, and things that hide in the mist. Bronze-rank monsters appear in the deep marsh and when the magic runs thick.

## DM notes

- **The mist** is the delta's signature. It gives ambushes, lost travellers and things seen only as shapes. A creature that hunts in the mist is a fine first Bronze-rank threat.
- **Villages** are the heart of early contracts: a missing child, a farm overrun, a surge evacuation.
- **The aperture upriver** is the strangest thing in the region. Everyone knows the river comes from it; very few people have asked what else might come down.
:::
