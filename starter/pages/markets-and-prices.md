Ordinary townsfolk pay for bread, ale and rope in mundane coin: gold, silver and copper. Anything magical is priced in [[Spirit Coins|spirit coins]], and most sellers won't take anything else for it. One iron spirit coin is worth 1 gp, one bronze is worth 100 gp, and one silver is worth 10,000 gp.

These are list prices. A poor market sells about 15% cheaper and a wealthy one about 40% dearer, and every seller haggles in their own way.

What a market can sell depends on where it is: a village sells everyday goods, a town adds uncommon magic, and only the cities sell rarer things. A low-magic city like [[Greenstone]] has far fewer of them. The rarest of all are sold only at auction. Prices and descriptions for every item, and the full table of what each place sells, are in [[Items and Equipment]] and the Items browser.

## Essences

Essences are the most expensive thing most people will ever want. That's why so many poor folk take a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] instead, which costs nothing but faith. Divine essences are never for sale: only a god can grant one.

| Rarity | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Common | 25 bronze (2,500 gp) |
| Uncommon | 1 silver (10,000 gp) |
| Rare | 5 silver (50,000 gp) |
| Legendary | 25 silver (250,000 gp), only at auction |

A town's dealers sell common and uncommon essences; rare ones are sold in the cities.

## Awakening stones

A stone is priced by the level of the spell it awakens. It can be used on any essence of the same affinity. See [[Awakening Stones]].

| Spell level | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 150 iron (150 gp) |
| 2 | 6 bronze (600 gp) |
| 3 | 25 bronze (2,500 gp) |
| 4 | 1 silver (10,000 gp) |
| 5 | 4 silver (40,000 gp) |

## Monster cores

Brokers sell [[Monster Cores|cores]] at about a fifth over the price they pay: 60 iron for an Iron core, 60 bronze for a Bronze core, and 60 silver for a Silver core.

**Monster dishes.** Monster meat is ordinary fare in Pallimustus. A dish made from a monster costs more the stronger the monster was, so giant rat pie is a few coppers and red dragon steak is a noble's indulgence. A few rare dishes leave a little of the monster's magic behind: hell hound chilli keeps out the cold, and dragon makes the eater hard to frighten.

:::dm
**Running markets and taverns.** The market and tavern generators under Generators use these prices, set in the `market`, `tavern` and `food` generator lists. Add a monster to the `food` list, with the parts it gives, to put it on menus. Change them there and every new market and tavern follows. Stalls stock their everyday and magic goods from the `items` list, capped by the settlement the market is in. **Buy** in a generated market takes the coins straight off a party member's sheet. Essences go into their carried essences until they absorb them with a ritual over a long rest, cores onto their core count, and everything else into their inventory.
:::
