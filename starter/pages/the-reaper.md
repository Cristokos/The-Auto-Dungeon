The Reaper is the [[Astral Powers|great astral being]] that holds death to be final. It does not decide who dies; that belongs to the god [[Church of Death|Death]] and to the world. The Reaper cares that the dead stay dead and that souls go where souls should go. Scholars argue about how the two are related, and the churches of Death do not encourage the argument.

## What it wants

- Death kept final: no false resurrections, no stolen souls, no undying shortcuts.
- Souls released from anything that traps or trades them.

## The Order of the Reaper

Long ago an order of assassins venerated the Reaper and served its idea of a clean, final ending. The order was wiped out so thoroughly that almost every record of it is gone, and its name now lives in tavern stories. One thing survives: a martial art the Reaper gave the order, the **Way of the Reaper**, a style of evasion and precise, killing strikes. Teachers who know it are almost impossible to find.

## Velius

The Reaper's prime avatar is **Velius**, a Diamond-rank Celestine with dark skin, silver eyes and a huge mound of curly silver hair. He is relaxed and almost friendly, which unnerves people more than menace would.

:::dm
**Using the Reaper.** Treat it as neutral and absolute. It is the being most likely to grant a single precise favour in exchange for setting a wrong soul right, and the most dangerous enemy for a lich or soul-trader.

- **Shade.** The Reaper has a shadow or child called Shade: a living shadow of dark magic that may one day serve a mortal as a familiar. Keep it as a long-term reward or mystery.
- **Order hooks.** [[Emir Bahadir]] and [[Rufus Remore]] are quietly researching the lost order. A Way of the Reaper teacher is a great prize for a Swift or Dark build, and a natural link to [[Sophie Wexler]].
- **Cults.** A small cult of the Reaper exists apart from the old order. It is not evil, but it is very serious about corpses staying down.
:::
