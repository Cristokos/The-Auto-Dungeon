***Lingering Echo (Resonance essence).*** Once per long rest, you can spend 1 minute listening in silence to recall the sounds of the place you are in. You hear, faint and distant, any one minute of your choice from the last hour in that place, such as voices, footsteps or a struggle.

***Water-Finder (Water essence).*** You can sense the direction of fresh water within 300 feet of you and roughly how much there is, and by tasting a drop you know whether water is safe to drink.

***Call the Rains (Lotus confluence).*** Once per long rest, as an action, you can call down a warm, steady rain in a 30-foot radius centred on a point you can see within 60 feet of you. **Iron rank.** Elf woman, niece of [[Ephraim Cavendish]], and widely called the best Iron-ranker in Greenstone.

- **Looks:** A willowy figure, tawny skin, vivid green eyes and chestnut hair.
- **Essences:** Harmonic, Plant and Water, forming the **Lotus** confluence.
- **Fighting style:** She leads and heals her team, but her real threat is wide-area afflictions and control: thorns, spores and water that turns a battlefield against the enemy.
- **Team:** Her cousin [[Mose Cavendish]]; Emily, a Celestine scout and archer; Hudson Kettering, a defender; and Niko Tomich, a Smoulder.
- **Personality:** Gracious, competitive and very aware of her reputation. She is kind to people below her and ruthless with people beside her.
- **Ties:** Niece of [[Ephraim Cavendish]] and granddaughter of [[Isadora Cavendish]]. Her parents live abroad, and the townhouse raised her.

## What people say

The best Iron-ranker in Greenstone. Every new team gets compared to Beth Cavendish's, and every new team loses. People say she'll be Silver before she's thirty, which is very young for an elf.

## DM notes

- **The measuring stick.** Put Beth's team on the same contract board as the party. They take the best contracts first, and they are good enough that the party has to be clever to beat them to one.
- **Her one offer.** She will offer help once. A party that takes it graciously becomes a team she respects; a party that refuses it out of pride becomes a team she quietly outperforms forever.
- **Her worry.** She is afraid she is only the best because the city's training is so poor. A Remore assessment, or a fight against a properly trained team, would tell her, and she both wants and dreads it.
- **A scene.** The board is full of new contracts. Beth reads it once, takes three notices without hesitating, then pauses and hands one to the party. "You'll want this one," she says. "It's harder than it looks."

## Playing Beth

**Gracious champion.** Polite, poised and fiercely competitive, used to being the best in every room.

- **Speech:** Measured and courteous, with an edge
- **How to play:** Compliment the party on something small, and make clear she noticed everything else too
- **Tell:** Tucks her hair behind her ear before making a decision
- **Toward the party:** Courteous, appraising, and quietly competitive
- **Wants from the party:** Real rivals, and proof that she has earned her name
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> Good work. Next time, watch your left flank.
>
> I'll offer once. Take it or don't.
>
> Is that the best your team can do? Good. Then we'll both improve.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To earn her reputation, not inherit it
- **Driven by:** Pride, high standards and duty to her team
- **Fears:** Being found out as merely the best of a poor lot
- **Bond:** Her team and her grandmother
- **Flaw:** Can't bear to be second, even to a friend

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user. Her Harmonic and Plant essences use the compendium's Resonance and Growth entries, and her Lotus confluence its Monsoon entry; rename them if the compendium gains its own.

- **Resonance** (rare, Wind): Sounds that linger and return. Grants **Lingering Echo**. The essence of the voice that comes back from the canyon wall. A bearer's words repeat faintly a heartbeat after they speak, they have a habit of repeating the last word of other people's sentences, and their spells ring with a hollow, doubled sound.
- **Growth** (common, Life): Plants, nurture and green increase. Grants Proficiency in Nature or with a herbalism kit. The essence of things that grow. Moss creeps over bearers' boots if they stand still too long, flowers open where they rest, and their spells smell of fresh-cut grass.
- **Water** (common, Water): Flow, adaptation and patient force. Grants **Water-Finder**. The essence of water in all its patience. Bearers are rarely flustered, droplets bead on their skin even in dry air, and their magic sounds like distant rain.
- **Confluence: Lotus** (Resonance + Growth + Water): The rains that wake the land. Grants **Call the Rains**. Lotus is the seasonal deluge that ends the dry months and makes everything grow at once. A bearer always smells of wet earth before a storm, their hair curls in damp air, and their magic falls in warm, heavy drops.
