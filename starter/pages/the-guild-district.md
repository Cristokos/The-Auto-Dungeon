**District.** The north-west quarter of [[The Island]], packed with guild compounds. The professional heart of adventuring in [[Greenstone]].

## What players know

- **The Adventure Society campus** is the biggest compound in the district: an administration building, training yards, assessment halls, a Trade Hall full of shops for adventurers, its own Loop terminal and a private dock. See [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch]].
- **The Magic Society** has its hall and workshops a short walk away, where it buys cores, appraises essences and stones, and sells enchanting services. See [[Magic Society]].
- **Guild District Boulevard**, the wide street through the district, is lined with outfitters, armourers, potion sellers, cafés and teams arguing on the pavement.
- **The other guilds** keep their own compounds: crafters, alchemists, merchants and adventuring guilds from other cities.
- [[Arash]] works a smoothie stall near the Society campus, and probably knows your name already.

**The Trade Hall.** Inside the Society campus, a covered market for adventurers: gear, potions, monster parts and services. Gilbert Bertinelli's shop, *Gilbert's Resilient Attire for the Discerning Gentleman*, sells clothes enchanted to survive a monster fight. The prices are high. The clothes are worth it.

:::dm
**Hook.** Two guild compounds share a wall, and something has started digging through it from underneath. Each guild blames the other, and both want to hire the same team to find out.
:::
