#### Traits

***Water-Finder (Water essence).*** You can sense the direction of fresh water within 300 feet of you and roughly how much there is, and by tasting a drop you know whether water is safe to drink.

***Gentle Hands (Mercy essence).*** You automatically succeed on Wisdom (Medicine) checks made to stabilize a dying creature, and a creature you stabilize regains 1 hit point after 10 minutes rather than 1d4 hours.

***Call the Spring (Wellspring essence).*** Once per long rest, you can touch the ground and call up a spring of clean, drinkable water that flows for 1 hour, producing up to 10 gallons, before it sinks away.

***Glass Water (Shallows confluence).*** When you look into natural water in daylight or other bright light, you see through its surface as if it were clear glass, without glare or ripples, down to a depth of 60 feet.

***Turn Undead.*** Once per short rest: each undead within 30 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom save or flee for 1 minute.

***Spellcasting.*** Wisdom-based (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit). **Bronze rank.** Merfolk man, infirmary healer and priest of the Healer at the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] of the [[Adventure Society]].

- **Looks:** A broad, calm merfolk with blue-green skin, webbed hands and a voice like a slow river. Sleeves always rolled up.
- **Personality:** Gentle, patient and impossible to rattle. Has seen every kind of wound and judges nobody for how they got it.
- **Wants:** Fewer deaths, and adventurers who rest when he tells them to.
- **Essences:** Water, Mercy and Wellspring, forming the **Shallows** confluence. His healing feels like cool water, and it leaves a faint smell of the river behind.
- **Ties:** He came up the Mistrun from the delta as a young priest and still sends money to a merfolk village there.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Healing at the branch's rates, cures for monster poisons and curses, and a sympathetic ear.
:::

## What people say

Brother Ilian in the infirmary. He has patched up half the adventurers in Greenstone and lectured all of them. If he tells you to rest, rest, or he will come and find you.

:::dm
## DM notes

- He treats people the branch would rather he didn't, including adventurers under suspension and, once, a cultist. He keeps no records of those.
- **Healing at the branch.** Branch rates are fair and he gives credit to any adventurer who asks. He never chases the debt, but the branch does, and he hates it.
- **What he's seen.** Wounds that don't match the contracts they were reported on: burns from Silver-rank monsters on Iron-rankers, and once a star-shaped scar he did not recognise. He kept a sketch of it.
- **A safe place.** The infirmary is neutral ground. He will hide a hurt player character from anyone, including the discipline office, until they can walk.
- **A scene.** The party carries a wounded teammate into the infirmary. A broad merfolk with rolled-up sleeves looks at the wound, sighs, says "Iron rank, fighting Bronze things again," and starts work without waiting for an explanation.

## Playing Ilian

**Patient healer.** Unshockable, gentle and quietly stubborn about the things that matter.

- **Speech:** Slow, deep and calm, like a river
- **How to play:** Ask the party what they were fighting, then tell them what they should have done
- **Tell:** Hums low while he heals
- **Toward the party:** Kind, mildly disapproving, and fond
- **Wants from the party:** For them to rest, and to come back alive
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Sit. No, sit. That's better.
>
> I don't need to know how. I need to know what.
>
> Rest three days. You'll rest one. I know.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** Fewer deaths in his infirmary
- **Driven by:** Compassion, and the Healer's teachings
- **Fears:** A surge, and running out of beds
- **Bond:** His patients and his delta village
- **Flaw:** Bends every rule for anyone in pain

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Water** (common, Water): Flow, adaptation and patient force. Grants **Water-Finder**. The essence of water in all its patience. Bearers are rarely flustered, droplets bead on their skin even in dry air, and their magic sounds like distant rain.
- **Mercy** (uncommon, Light): Compassion and sparing the fallen. Grants **Gentle Hands**. Mercy is the choice to spare, to heal and to forgive. Bearers have a gentle touch and steady hands, and wounded creatures, even hostile ones, often grow calm when they kneel beside them.
- **Wellspring** (legendary, Water): The source of all fresh water. Grants **Call the Spring**. Wellspring is the first spring, the water every river remembers. Its bearer is never thirsty for long, their hands are always cool and damp, and desert peoples greet them with ceremony because water follows them.
- **Confluence: Shallows** (Water + Mercy + Wellspring): Sunlit water clear to the bed. Grants **Glass Water**. Shallows is sea and river at their brightest, sunlight reaching through clear water to the sand and stones below. A bearer's eyes shift colour like light on a stream bed, they wade in at every chance, and their spells ripple with dappled, wavering light.
