The church of **Death**, the god who embodies the natural end of every life, and the passage of souls beyond it.

- **Standing:** Respected everywhere, loved almost nowhere.
- **Symbol:** a closed eye, or a black candle snuffed.
- **Colours:** black and bone white.

## The god

Death is the end of every living thing made flesh, and the passage of the soul onward. Death does not kill; Death receives. Every creature that dies passes through Death's domain, and Death cares that the passage happens, not how or when.

- **What the god gives:** Peace for the suffering, an end to pain, the promise that every soul is received and nothing is lost.
- **Offended by:** Anything that stops a soul from passing on: undeath, soul theft, souls bound or trapped.

:::dm
### The dark face

Death will not save anyone. A plague, a massacre or a murder is still an ending, and Death receives those souls as calmly as any others.

- **Will accept:** Killing of any kind. Death has no quarrel with murderers, executioners or soldiers.

### Secret motives

- Death wants the cycle kept moving, not slowed. A long peace, where few die, is quietly unwelcome to Death.
- Death's church buries the world's secrets with its dead, and Death is content for them to stay buried. Its priests know who really died of what, and say nothing.
- Death tolerates the Church of Purity's burnings because they end lives, and it tolerates the Healer because healed people still die eventually.
:::

## Divine essence

Death grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers are very still, their eyes hold a faint grey light, and candles gutter when they pass.*

- **Essence trait:** Deathsight. You know whether any creature you can see is dead, undead, or dying (at 0 hit points).
- **Sense: Death's Eye.** As an action, you sense every undead creature and every creature at 0 hit points within 60 feet until the end of your next turn.
- **Channel: Final Rest.** When you hit a creature with an attack, you can deal an extra 2d8 necrotic damage (3d8 from Silver rank). An undead creature hit this way can't regain hit points until the start of your next turn.
- **Blessing: Blessing of the Threshold** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures have advantage on death saving throws, and undead creatures can't regain hit points while within 10 feet of one. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *false life*, *inflict wounds*, *protection from evil and good*; level 2: *gentle repose*, *ray of enfeeblement*, *silence*; level 3: *speak with dead*, *spirit guardians*, *bestow curse*; level 4: *death ward*, *blight*; level 5: *antilife shell*, *contagion*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Dark.

**Divine confluence: Psychopomp.** A Psychopomp walks souls to Death's door. Bearers hear the last words of the dying, the recently dead linger near them as if waiting to be led, and their magic falls like a soft grey dusk. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Death (divine) + Reaper + Requiem; Death (divine) + Gallows + Shroud; Death (divine) + Grief + Silence. It grants **Last Words**: You can speak with a creature that died within the last minute, as if it were alive, for up to one minute. It answers truthfully, but only what it knew in life.

**What the god asks.** Give the dead their rites, whoever they were. Destroy the undead wherever you find them. Never bind a soul or hold one back from passing on.

**What makes the god take it back.** Raising, binding or keeping a soul from passing on. Sparing an undead creature you could have destroyed.

## What players know

The Church of Death buries the city's dead and keeps the graves quiet. Its priests are unsettlingly calm, and they do not lie to the dying. When something will not stay buried, they are the first people anyone sends for.

## Beliefs

- Every life ends, and that ending is a door, not a theft.
- The dead belong to Death. Holding a soul back is the gravest sin there is.
- Grief is honoured. Fear is gently corrected.

## Clergy

Calm, unhurried priests who run funerals, keep the cemeteries and sit with the dying. Its **Wardens of the Threshold** hunt the restless dead and the necromancers who make them.

## In Greenstone

A quiet chapel beside the old cemetery on the edge of [[The Old City]]. Its priests are called to every death in the city, rich or poor.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of the Healer]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Undeath]], [[Church of Purity]]

Death and Purity agree that undeath is an abomination, but Death's priests find Purity's fondness for sending people early rather distasteful.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A body vanished from the chapel the night before its funeral, and the priests want it found before anyone else learns of it.
- A Warden of the Threshold asks the party to escort a dying old adventurer who wants to die in a particular place.
- Someone in Greenstone has stopped dying. They are ninety-three, sick, and very frightened, and the church wants to know why.

## DM notes

- Death is one of the oldest gods and is unusually strict about the rules that bind the gods. Its church can be a source of hard truths about how the world works, delivered only to those who have earned them.
- The Wardens keep a sealed ledger of souls that "did not arrive". The ledger is growing, and they do not know where those souls went.
