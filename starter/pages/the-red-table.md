A cannibal blood cult at the heart of [[House Vane]]. It meets on the Vane estate in the desert inland from [[Greenstone]], past [[North East Quarry Village Four]], and has hidden there for generations behind the family's money and its reputation for keeping to itself.

## Patron, want, cover, weakness

- **Patron.** The rites themselves. Shared blood and flesh keep the inner circle young and strong, and the cult believes a great enough sacrifice will open the way for an apocalypse beast, a monster that eats worlds.
- **Want.** To finish the summoning. Its leaders believe they will be spared and rewarded when the beast arrives.
- **Cover.** A reclusive noble family with a beautiful estate, desert trade and a string of caravan inns.
- **Weakness.** Too many people have gone missing on one road. The servants are terrified, not loyal, and one who escapes can bring the whole thing down.

## Members

- [[Cressida Vane]], Lady of the house, leads the cult. She is far older than she looks.
- [[Alicia Vane]], her daughter, is its hunter. She chooses the travellers from the caravan inns.
- [[Landemere Vane]], her son, sits at its edge. He venerates great astral beings, especially [[The Builder]], and has been trying summoning rituals of his own.
- **Cultists and the frightened.** Some of the estate's workers are true believers. Others are simply too afraid to leave.

## How it operates

- **Choosing victims.** The inns on the inland road see every caravan that passes. Travellers who are alone, foreign or far from home are noted, delayed, and invited to the estate.
- **The chamber.** Captives are kept and sacrificed in a chamber beneath the manor. The dead are eaten at the cult's table.
- **The hedge maze.** The estate's maze is part of the rite. Some victims are released into it and hunted.

## Signs

- Caravans on the inland road arrive one traveller short, and the innkeepers remember nothing.
- A Bronze-rank team took a church contract to look into missing people near the estate. They never came back.
- A stranger walks out of the desert, half-dressed and confused, claiming to have woken up in a hedge maze.
