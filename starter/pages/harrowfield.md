**Small town.** A farming town a day upriver from [[Greenstone]], where the Mistrun turns the desert into grain fields and heidel pastures. Magic level: low. Protector: a small [[Adventure Society]] post with one Bronze-ranker and a handful of Iron-rankers.

## What players know

Harrowfield feeds Greenstone. Its market day draws farmers from a dozen hamlets, and its granaries are the largest in the region. The magic is thin here, so monsters are rare and weak, which makes it a favorite place for Iron-rankers to take their first easy contracts.

**Export.** Grain and heidel feed, sold by the cartload to Greenstone and the coin farms.

**Quirk.** Every field has a scarecrow dressed as a famous adventurer, and the town judges them each harvest. Winning matters far more than it should. Families have stopped speaking over a badly painted Gold-ranker.

:::dm
**Hook.** Low magic means few monsters, but it also means that a spirit coin farm can't be built here. Someone has been buying up fields anyway, through agents, and no one knows why.
:::
