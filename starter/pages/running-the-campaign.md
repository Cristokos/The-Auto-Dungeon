This wiki sets the stage for a campaign on Pallimustus, run under unmodified 5e rules with essences as a flavour add-on. It covers what exists on day one: the world's rules, places, peoples, factions, notable NPCs and political fault lines. It deliberately leaves out plot, hooks and evolving storylines; those are yours to add as **Adventure** pages.

## Baseline assumptions

- **Rules:** Vanilla 5e for classes, leveling, combat, spells and items. Essences add flavour and small perks, never replace a class feature.
- **Era:** Set the campaign in a city like [[Greenstone]], at a time of relative calm, when the [[Key NPCs]] hold the ranks and roles listed. If you prefer a different era, the factions hold up but individual NPC ranks and statuses shift.
- **Tentative details:** Items marked *(tentative)* are still open and may change as the world is fleshed out. Confirm or replace them before relying on them.

## Using this app

- Click any underlined name to follow a link. Red links point to pages that don't exist yet; clicking one offers to create it.
- **Edit** (or Ctrl+E) opens a page for editing. Type `[[` to link another page, and drag images into the text or onto the portrait slot.
- Session notes that link a page show up in that page's **History**, so characters and places build a record of what happened to them.
- The **Generators** read their lists from the campaign's `data` folder. Change them under **Generator lists**.

## Day-one checklist

Before session one, settle these decisions so the world is stable when players arrive.

- [ ] Choose the era and confirm which NPCs are present
- [ ] Set each of the [[Political Dials]]
- [ ] Draw the starting region around [[Greenstone]], with magic level and protector for each town
- [ ] Finalize the essence list players can choose from at level 2
- [ ] Decide [[Spirit Coins]] rates and whether mundane coin circulates
- [ ] Create the local [[Adventure Society]] director, [[Magic Society]] appraiser and a priest
- [ ] Decide whether any player may be an [[Outworlders|outworlder]]
- [ ] Confirm or replace every *(tentative)* item

## Tentative details

Items marked *(tentative)* are still open: chiefly god and church names. NPC ranks are approximate.
