#### Traits

***Rooted (Earth essence).*** She has advantage on ability checks and saving throws to avoid being shoved or knocked prone.

***Kindle (Fire essence).*** At will, as an action, she can light or snuff out a nonmagical flame no larger than a campfire within 10 feet.

***Surge (Potent essence).*** Once per long rest, when she rolls damage, she can reroll any damage dice that rolled a 1 or 2 and must use the new rolls.

***Banked Heat (Volcano confluence).*** As an action, she can make a stone no larger than 1 foot across glow with harmless red heat for 8 hours.

***Elemental Affinity.*** She adds her Charisma modifier to the damage of her fire spells.

***Sorcery Points.*** She has 7 sorcery points. Metamagic: Quickened Spell, Twinned Spell.

***Spellcasting.*** Charisma-based (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit). **Bronze rank.** Human woman, adventurer on [[Rufus Remore]]'s team, from [[Vitesse]].

- **Looks:** Pale, with strawberry-blonde hair and pixie features, including a button nose that makes her look cute even when she is furious. This regularly misleads people.
- **Essences:** Earth, Fire and Potent, forming the **Volcano** confluence. She shapes stone, throws fire, and at full power makes the ground melt.
- **Personality:** Blunt, funny, practical and fiercely loyal. Wise when it matters and savage when crossed. She has no patience for male posturing and a strong sense that power is for protecting people who have none.
- **Family:** Her parents, Amelia and William Hurin, are ordinary people in Vitesse who saved for years to buy her the Potent essence that started her career. She means to repay them by raising them to Bronze.

:::dm
**Offers players:** A mentor who treats players as equals, and lessons in mana control and the basics of magic delivered without sympathy.
:::

## What people say

Rufus's teammate. Blunt, funny, and not someone you want to be on the wrong side of.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Team.** She met Rufus and [[Gary]] on a contract clearing a zombie plague, and the three have been inseparable since. They are in Greenstone on a contract.
- **Mentor style.** She argues with students who are naive and respects the ones who argue back. Her teaching sticks.
- **Ruthless edge.** Anyone who betrays her team should fear her far more than they fear Rufus.
- **Astral notice.** If you ever want her touched by the great astral beings, she is a natural candidate; see [[Legion]] and [[The World-Phoenix]].

## Playing Farrah

**Sharp-tongued mentor.** Treats everyone as an equal, which means she argues with them as one. Kind under the bluntness, and terrifying under the kindness.

- **Speech:** Blunt, quick and funny; swears creatively
- **How to play:** Cut through a party member's excuse in one sentence, then help them anyway
- **Tell:** The air around her gets warm when she is angry
- **Toward the party:** Respectful if they are honest; merciless if they posture
- **Wants from the party:** People who will learn, argue back and protect those weaker than them
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> Don't pout. You're not cute enough to get away with it. I am.
>
> Mana control first. Fireballs later.
>
> Power is for the people who don't have any. Remember that, or I'll remind you.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To raise her parents to Bronze and repay what they gave up for her
- **Driven by:** Loyalty to her team, and a belief that power is for protecting others
- **Fears:** Being betrayed by someone she trusted
- **Bond:** [[Rufus Remore]], [[Gary]], and her parents in [[Vitesse]]
- **Flaw:** Holds a grudge like a banked fire

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user. Her parents saved for years to buy her first essence.

- **Earth** (common, Earth): Stability, weight and endurance. Grants **Rooted**. The essence of stone and soil. Bearers gain a faint granite grain to their skin and a heavy, deliberate tread, and their magic arrives with the rumble of shifting ground.
- **Fire** (common, Fire): Heat, flame and fierce energy. Grants **Kindle**. The essence of living flame. Bearers run warm to the touch, ember glints flicker in their hair, and their spells crackle and smell of woodsmoke.
- **Potent** (uncommon, Might): Raw power and amplified force. Grants **Surge**. Potent is power pushed past its usual bounds. A pressure builds in the air before the bearer acts, and their spells and blows flare brighter and land harder than they should.
- **Confluence: Volcano** (Earth + Fire + Potent): Molten fury beneath the stone. Grants **Banked Heat**. Volcano is the earth's buried fire breaking loose with overwhelming force. Bearers feel as heavy and warm as banked coals, thin cracks of orange light show in their skin when they lose their temper, and their spells rumble and hiss like a mountain about to burst.
