**Silver rank.** Celestine woman, deputy director at the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] of the [[Adventure Society]].

- **Looks:** Tall, with pale gold skin that catches the lamplight and hair pinned up in perfect silver coils. Immaculate dark robes, a ledger always under her arm, and a smile that arrives exactly on time.
- **Personality:** Polished, warm in public, ruthless with paperwork. Remembers every name, every slight and every favour.
- **Wants:** To run the branch one day, and to turn it from a neutral referee into a power in the city.
- **Essences:** Crown, Secret and Light, forming the **Gravewarden** confluence. She is a bard of the court kind: her magic charms, commands and hides.
- **Ties:** She has a standing invitation to the Mercer music room, and is seen at Beaufort Mercer's table more than her post requires.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Introductions to the right people, fast-tracked assessments, and a patron who expects to be paid back.
:::

## What people say

Deputy Director Varrick. She knows everyone's name and remembers everyone's birthday, and she'll get your assessment moved up if you ask nicely. She's going to run the branch one day, people say, and she agrees.

:::dm
## DM notes

- She passes branch business to a great house in exchange for the backing she will need when Arella falls. She believes this is how things are done, and that she is better for the branch than Arella is. If she ever learns who Arella's father is, she will use it.
- She is not behind the missing teams, but she knows something about one of them and has decided it is not yet useful to say.
- **Her patron.** The great house is [[House Mercer]]. Beaufort feeds her information about the branch's rivals and she feeds him the branch's contract lists before they go up. The party may notice Mercer teams always taking the best contracts first.
- **What she knows.** One of the missing teams took a contract she arranged as a favour to Beaufort. She doesn't know what happened to them, and she has decided not to find out.
- **Using her.** She is useful to a party that pays her back, and a terrible enemy to one that doesn't. She will offer them a favour early. They should think about it.
- **A scene.** Isolde stops the party on the branch stairs, greets each of them by name though they've never met, congratulates them on their last contract, and mentions that she might be able to help with that assessment date, if they'd like.

## Playing Isolde

**Polished climber.** Warm, gracious and calculating, a politician in a Society uniform.

- **Speech:** Smooth, warm and precisely timed
- **How to play:** Remember a small personal detail about each party member and use it
- **Tell:** Her smile arrives exactly one beat after the joke
- **Toward the party:** Charming and generous, as an investment
- **Wants from the party:** Their loyalty, and their debt
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> I've heard such good things about you. Let me help.
>
> It's no trouble. You'll remember me, I'm sure.
>
> The director is a fine woman. She won't be director forever.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To run the branch, and make it matter in the city
- **Driven by:** Ambition, and a genuine belief she would do it better
- **Fears:** Being exposed before she has the backing to survive it
- **Bond:** Her career, and the favours she is owed
- **Flaw:** Treats every friendship as a ledger entry

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Crown** (legendary, Might): Sovereignty, rightful rule and command. Grants **Uncrowned Royalty**. Crown is rule itself, the weight of the right to command that kingdoms are built around. When the bearer speaks with authority a faint circlet of golden light seems to rest on their brow, and courtiers and commoners alike recognise them as someone born, or chosen, to lead.
- **Secret** (uncommon, Dark): Hidden things and kept confidences. Grants **Hidden Ways**. Secret is the essence of what is hidden and kept. Bearers speak softly and smile knowingly, and they feel a tug of curiosity toward locked drawers and walls that sound hollow.
- **Light** (common, Light): Illumination, clarity and revelation. Grants **Inner Lamp**. The essence of light that reveals what was hidden. Bearers carry a soft glow beneath the skin in darkness, shadows seem to shy away from them, and their magic flares bright and clean.
- **Confluence: Gravewarden** (Crown + Secret + Light): Lantern and blade among the graves. Grants **Empty Grave**. The gravewarden walks the cemetery at night with lantern and blade, keeping the dead at rest and the robbers out. Bearers have a solemn bearing and a stillness that calms mourners, lantern-light burns steadier around them, and their magic rings like iron gates swinging shut.
