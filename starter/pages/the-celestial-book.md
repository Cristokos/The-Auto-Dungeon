The Celestial Book is the [[Astral Powers|great astral being]] of understanding. It promotes knowledge of the underlying nature of magic: not spells, but the rules beneath spells, the reasons why essences combine the way they do and why mana behaves as it does.

Many people deep in magical theory venerate it, usually privately and often half jokingly. [[Clive Standish]] is one of them, and keeps a small sketch of a book with a star on its cover above his desk at the [[Magic Society]].

## What it wants

- Magic understood, recorded and taught.
- Questions asked, especially the ones nobody else thinks to ask.
- Its servants, the ones who know they are its servants, to share what they learn rather than hoard it.

## How it touches mortals

- **Insight.** A sudden understanding at the right moment, a dream of a diagram, a line in an old text that answers a question nobody else had asked. Scholars call these "pages from the Book."
- **Lost texts.** Books that should not exist turn up in libraries, written in the reader's language and answering exactly the question they were asking. Most crumble when finished. A few remain.
- **Correction.** Those who suppress or destroy knowledge sometimes find their own records failing them: ledgers smudge, memories slip, and their spells grow unreliable.

## Cultists and bargains

The Celestial Book has no church, but it has followers everywhere magic is studied: researchers, librarians, archivists and wizards who keep a star-topped book somewhere on their desk. Some of them form reading circles that meet to share what they have found. One, the Open Margin, meets in the [[Magic Society]] library in [[Greenstone]] after hours and publishes anonymous pamphlets correcting the Society's official findings.

The Book does not bargain, but it rewards. A scholar who gives away a great discovery freely may find their next one comes more easily.

:::dm
**Using the Celestial Book.** It never acts openly. Its notice shows up as a flash of insight, an old text found at exactly the right time, or a ritual that works better than it should for someone who truly wants to understand it. It is a good patron for a scholar character and a quiet reason Clive keeps being right.

**Hook.** A page has turned up in the Magic Society library, written in a hand nobody knows, describing in perfect detail how [[The Builder]] anchors stolen astral spaces. [[Lucian Lamprey]] has locked it away, and [[Clive Standish]] wants the party to help him read it before it crumbles.
:::
