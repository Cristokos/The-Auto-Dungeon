The church of **Storm**, the god who embodies weather, wind and the fury of the sky.

- **Standing:** Honoured by the crown of the Storm Kingdom.
- **Symbol:** a lightning bolt splitting a cloud.
- **Colours:** storm grey and electric blue.

## The god

Storm is the sky in motion: wind, rain, thunder and lightning. Wind is its gentler face. It cannot be bargained with, only weathered.

- **What the god gives:** Storm brings rain for crops, wind for sails, and the cleansing fury that clears the air.
- **Offended by:** Those who would chain the sky. Earth is its old rival.

:::dm
### The dark face

Storm brings hurricanes, lightning strikes, ruined harvests and ships sunk without warning.

- **Will accept:** Destruction by weather, and anyone lost to the storm.

### Secret motives

- Storm sits close to the crown of the Storm Kingdom and uses that closeness. Its priests in Greenstone send word of everything they see back to Rimaros.
- Storm resents Earth's patience and would gladly see the Earth church humbled.
:::

## Divine essence

Storm grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers' hair lifts in still air, static crackles at their fingertips, and their eyes flash like distant lightning.*

- **Essence trait:** Weather Eye. You always know what the weather will do in the next 24 hours within a mile of you.
- **Sense: Sense the Sky.** As an action, for 1 minute you sense every flying creature within 300 feet, and any storm or weather coming your way.
- **Channel: Thunder Strike.** When you hit a creature with an attack, you can deal an extra 2d8 thunder damage (3d8 from Silver rank), and it must succeed on a Strength save against your stone spell DC or be pushed 10 feet and knocked prone.
- **Blessing: Blessing of the Storm** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures have resistance to lightning and thunder damage, and ranged weapon attacks against them have disadvantage. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *thunderwave*, *witch bolt*, *feather fall*; level 2: *gust of wind*, *levitate*, *shatter*; level 3: *call lightning*, *lightning bolt*, *wind wall*; level 4: *storm sphere*, *ice storm*; level 5: *control winds*, *cone of cold*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Wind.

**Divine confluence: Thunderborn.** The Thunderborn are storms that learned to walk. Bearers' hair stands on end, thunder rolls when they lose their temper, and their magic strikes like lightning. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Storm (divine) + Wind + Spark; Storm (divine) + Firmament + Bell; Storm (divine) + Garuda + Weathervane. It grants **Living Storm**: You have resistance to lightning and thunder damage, and when a creature within 5 feet hits you with a melee attack, it takes lightning damage equal to your proficiency bonus.

**What the god asks.** Give thanks to the god on stormy nights. Never hide from a storm you were called to face. Serve the crown that bears the god's name.

**What makes the god take it back.** Cursing the god for a storm. Trying to chain the weather for your own profit.

## What players know

The Church of the Storm is close to the crown of [[The Storm Kingdom]], which takes its name from the god. Its priests read the weather for farmers and sailors, and on stormy nights they stand on the open roof of their temple, watching the sky. Nobody is sure what they are looking for.

## Beliefs

- The sky does not bargain.
- Honour the storm, and it may pass you by.
- Be swift as the wind and as hard to hold.

## Aspects

Storm has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Wind:** The Storm's gentler face: breezes that fill sails, winds that carry seeds, and the freedom of the open sky.

## Clergy

Weather-callers, rain-priests and lightning devotees. Its priests advise farmers on planting, sailors on sailing and the crown on its ceremonies.

## In Greenstone

An open-roofed temple on a hill above the city. On stormy nights its priests stand on the roof to watch.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of the Ocean]], [[Church of Dominion]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of the Earth]]

Earth and Storm have argued for centuries. The crown's favour has made the Storm church a little too pleased with itself.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A storm has been sitting over the same village for nine days without moving.
- A rain-priest needs escorts to reach a mountaintop shrine before the season turns.
- A Storm priest has arrived in Greenstone carrying a royal letter from Rimaros, and nobody knows why.

## DM notes

- The Storm church is the eyes of [[The Crown]] in Greenstone, a city the crown has no say in. Its priests report to [[Rimaros]], and the city's great houses know it.
