Monster cores drop from slain monsters, and each core takes the rank of the monster it came from. An Iron-rank core is a small, red-brown gem shaped like a teardrop; cores of higher ranks are larger, clearer and brighter. Crafters and ritualists use them up as components, and a ritual uses them to steady the ambient magic around its working. Anyone can also absorb cores to gain levels; see [[Two Paths to Rank]].

| Core | Price per core |
| --- | --- |
| Iron | 50 gp |
| Bronze | 5,000 gp |
| Silver | 500,000 gp |
| Gold | 50,000,000 gp, and almost never sold |
| Diamond | Beyond price |

## Cores needed per rank

A core user gains a level by absorbing cores of their current rank, a number equal to the new level. Climbing through a whole rank takes:

| Climb | Levels gained | Cores needed | Cost at list price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Iron to Bronze (level 2 to 5) | 3, 4, 5 | 12 Iron cores | 600 gp |
| Bronze to Silver (level 5 to 11) | 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 | 51 Bronze cores | 255,000 gp |
| Through Silver (level 11 to 16) | 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 | 70 Silver cores | 35,000,000 gp |

Cores can't make someone Iron rank: that takes three absorbed essences and their confluence (see [[Ranks]]). And soul residue stops every core user at level 16, so no one reaches Gold this way.

The [[Magic Society]] buys and appraises cores.
