#### Traits

***Mana Touch (Magic essence).*** As an action, he can touch an object to learn whether it is magical and, if it is, which school of magic it belongs to.

***Steady Stride (Staff essence).*** While holding a staff, he has advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks to keep his balance.

***Inner Stillness (Mystic confluence).*** Once per long rest, at the start of his turn, he can end one effect on himself that is causing him to be charmed or frightened.

***Cloud Flask.*** He owns a cloud flask that unfolds into a flying cloud house, his home and expedition base.

***Bardic Inspiration (5/short rest).*** As a bonus action, he gives a creature within 60 feet a d12 to add to one attack roll, ability check or saving throw in the next 10 minutes.

***Spellcasting.*** Charisma-based (spell save DC 19, +11 to hit). Cantrips: *mage hand*, *message*, *minor illusion*, *vicious mockery*. 1st (4 slots): *dissonant whispers*, *faerie fire*, *healing word*. 2nd (3 slots): *invisibility*, *suggestion*. 3rd (3 slots): *counterspell*, *hypnotic pattern*, *sending*. 4th (3 slots): *dimension door*, *freedom of movement*. 5th (3 slots): *animate objects*, *mass cure wounds*, *teleportation circle*. 6th (1 slot): *find the path*, *mass suggestion*. 7th (1 slot): *forcecage*, *teleport*. 8th (1 slot): *feeblemind*. 9th (1 slot): *foresight*.

**Gold rank.** Wealthy adventurer and explorer of [[Astral Spaces|astral spaces]].

- **Looks:** Midnight-black skin and dark woven hair threaded with colourful beads, always in neat, perfectly fitted clothes.
- **Essences:** Magic, Might and Staff, forming the **Mystic** confluence.
- **Personality:** Charming, generous, theatrical.
- **Household:** His wife Constance runs his staff and his business. He lives in a cloud flask: a flying cloud house he can carry in his pocket.
- **History:** He adventured with Gabriel and Arabelle Remore (see [[House Remore]]) before trading contracts for treasure hunting.

**Offers players:** Employment, expeditions, luxury, and debts.

## What people say

A wealthy adventurer who explores astral spaces for treasure. Charming and generous; nobody seems sure what he is really looking for.

:::dm
## DM notes

Keep him distant on day one; his generosity creates debts.

## Playing Emir

**Generous showman.** Larger than life, endlessly hospitable and always enjoying himself. Every kindness is real, and every kindness is also a ledger entry.

- **Speech:** Rich, theatrical and full of laughter
- **How to play:** Throw an arm around someone, offer them a drink and a job in the same breath
- **Tell:** Touches the beads in his hair when he is calculating
- **Toward the party:** Delighted; new talent is his favourite entertainment
- **Wants from the party:** Capable hands for an expedition into an astral space, and people who owe him
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Come, come! Nobody talks business on an empty stomach.
>
> Treasure is only half the fun. The other half is the stories.
>
> Consider it a gift. Constance will send you the details.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To find the lost astral space of the Order of the Reaper
- **Driven by:** Curiosity, wealth and a love of a good story
- **Fears:** Being bored, or being old
- **Bond:** His wife and chief of staff, Constance, and his old teammates [[House Remore|Gabriel and Arabelle Remore]]
- **Flaw:** Spends lavishly, including other people's goodwill

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user. Retired from contracts and now a treasure hunter.

- **Magic** (uncommon, Magic): Raw mana and arcane sensitivity. Grants **Mana Touch**. Magic is mana in its rawest, most adaptable form. Blue-white motes drift around the bearer's hands, and enchanted things feel faintly warm or buzzing to their touch.
- **Might** (common, Might): Raw strength and crushing force. Grants proficiency: Athletics or Intimidation. The essence of sheer physical power. Bearers' shoulders broaden and their grip grows crushing, their blows land with a dull thunderclap, and flagstones crack where they plant their feet.
- **Staff** (common, Magic): Quarterstaff, balance and channelled power. Grants **Steady Stride**. The essence of the staff, both walking stick and channelling rod. Bearers move with a wanderer's steady balance, a staff hums when they spin it, and motes of light gather at its ends when they cast.
- **Confluence: Mystic** (Magic + Might + Staff): Inner discipline and flowing power. Grants **Inner Stillness**. Mystic is the discipline of body and spirit brought into one flowing current. Bearers breathe slowly and move with unhurried grace, and their spells unfold from a single still point, like ripples spreading across a pond.
