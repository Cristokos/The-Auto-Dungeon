Nine native peoples live on Pallimustus, and all of them can take essences. Locals call them *natures*. There are no native dwarves, gnomes or halflings (see [[Outworlders]]). Cities are cosmopolitan, and rank and family matter more than species.

**Racial gifts.** Each nature has its own gifts, such as the Runics' larger mana reserves. At the table these are flavour only; the 5e species traits handle all the mechanics.

## Humans

The most widespread people of the world, found in every city, village and trade road.

## Elves

Long lives and a natural gift for spellcraft and life magic. An elf may remember a city's founding, and may mention it more often than anyone would like.

## Leonids

Large, lion-featured and proud; famed as crafters and fighters. [[Gary]] is a Leonid.

## Runics

Markings like runes that run down to the bone, and deep reserves of magic. [[Arash]] is a Runic.

## Celestines

A touch of the holy and the far-off about them; quick and gifted. Celestines have metallic hair with eyes to match, in silver, gold, copper or ruby, and skin that ranges from alabaster to deep brown.

Celestines are rare because they are not born the way other peoples are. A Celestine is always the child of two outworlder parents: when two people from other worlds have a child on Pallimustus, the child is Celestine, whatever the parents were. Celestines who have children with each other pass it on, so a few old families, the royal house of [[Rimaros]] among them, are Celestine by descent. Most Celestines in a city like [[Greenstone]] can name the outworlders they come from.

**Gifts.** Celestines recover mana quickly, resist having their magic disrupted, are faster than most, and have a natural affinity for astral and holy magic. Their essence powers lean toward special abilities more than spells.

## Draconians

Scaled, with dragon-like gifts and breath. They claim descent from dragons, and most people smile politely at the claim.

## Smoulders

Fire-touched people, warm to the touch.

## Goblins

A people with their own history and pride. Calling one a monster is a quick way to make an enemy.

## Merfolk

People of the seas and coasts, rare this far inland.
