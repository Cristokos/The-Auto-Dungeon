***Broken Vow (Oath essence).*** You always know when a creature breaks a promise it made directly to you, though not where it is or how the promise was broken.

***Letter of the Law (Justice confluence).*** After spending 1 hour in a settlement, you know its laws, who holds the authority to judge there, and the usual penalties for common crimes.

**Silver rank.** Draconian woman, discipline officer at the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] of the [[Adventure Society]].

- **Looks:** Dark grey scales, amber eyes, and a voice that never rises. A plain uniform with the Society's badge and the discipline office's black cord on the shoulder.
- **Personality:** Cold, exact and impossible to bribe. Treats everyone the same, which the nobility find very offensive.
- **Wants:** That every adventurer in Greenstone believes the rules apply to them.
- **Essences:** Verdict, Oath and Shield, forming the **Justice** confluence. Lies sit badly in her presence, and she can hold a line against anything the branch has ever faced.
- **Ties:** She answers to the branch director, [[Elspeth Arella]], whom she respects, and to nobody in the great houses, whom she does not.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Justice, slowly and thoroughly. An ally if the party is wronged; the worst enemy in the branch if they are the ones in the wrong.
:::

## What people say

Captain Holt of the discipline office. Get a summons from her and your team's contracts are frozen until she's done with you. Nobody has ever bribed her, and several people have tried.

:::dm
## DM notes

- She has a sealed warrant she is waiting to serve on a member of a great house. She needs one more witness, and the party may become it.
- **Investigations.** She is slow and thorough. If the party is accused of something, she will find the truth, and that is good news only if they're innocent.
- **The warrant.** The sealed warrant is for [[Thadwick Mercer]]. She has a witness to one of his assaults, but she needs a second before the Mercers can't make it disappear. Beaufort is already looking for her first witness.
- **An ally.** A party that brings her honest evidence gets a fair hearing and a powerful friend. A party that lies to her once never gets either.
- **A scene.** A uniformed draconian stops beside the party's table in the branch hall and says, very quietly, that she would like a word in her office. She doesn't say about what. She waits for them to stand.

## Playing Reyna

**Incorruptible officer.** Cold, exact and fair, with no interest in anyone's name.

- **Speech:** Quiet, flat and precise; never raises her voice
- **How to play:** Ask a simple question and wait in silence until someone fills it
- **Tell:** Her scales darken when she hears a lie
- **Toward the party:** Neutral, and watching
- **Wants from the party:** The truth, and a witness who won't break
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> Sit down. This won't take long, if you're honest.
>
> Your family is not my concern.
>
> The rules apply to everyone. Including you. Including him.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** For the rules to mean the same thing for everyone
- **Driven by:** Justice, and an old oath
- **Fears:** A great house buying the branch
- **Bond:** The Society's charter, and Arella
- **Flaw:** Cannot leave anything unfinished, whatever it costs

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Verdict** (rare, Light): Judgment weighed and pronounced. Grants **Weighed and Found**. The essence of the final word of a judge. Bearers grow still and level-voiced, people instinctively stand straighter when they enter a room, and their spells fall with the heavy finality of a gavel.
- **Oath** (uncommon, Might): Promises, loyalty and binding words. Grants **Broken Vow**. Oath is the force of a sworn word. Bearers speak carefully and keep promises to the letter, and when they make a vow a faint ring of light briefly circles their wrist.
- **Shield** (common, Might): Protection and warding others. Grants Proficiency with shields or in Medicine. The essence of the shield and the one who stands in front. Bearers instinctively place themselves between danger and their companions, and a faint translucent barrier flickers around them when they cast.
- **Confluence: Justice** (Verdict + Oath + Shield): Law, judgement and the sworn sword. Grants **Letter of the Law**. Justice is unbending resolve sworn to see wrongs judged and punished. Bearers stand straight and speak plainly, liars grow uneasy under their level gaze, and their spells fall like a judge's gavel, sharp and final.
