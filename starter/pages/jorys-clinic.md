**Clinic.** A small shopfront clinic on Broadstreet Boulevard in [[The Old City]], run by **Jory Tillman**, a human alchemist in his mid-twenties.

## What players know

- **Cheap healing.** Jory sells healing potions, cleaning washes and other remedies at prices the Old City can afford, and treats people who can't pay at all.
- **The research.** He funds the clinic by selling a few clever products and spends the profit on one goal: healing potions cheap enough for everyone.
- **The church problem.** The local [[Church of the Healer]] sees his prices as a threat and his methods as heresy. Its acolytes have vandalised the shop more than once.
- **The yard.** There is a walled yard behind the clinic, quiet and private, which Jory sometimes lets adventurer friends use for training.

:::dm
**Using Jory.** He is a good first friend for a new party: honest, broke, and in trouble with a church. Who he dated, and who he still has feelings for, is up to you; [[Belinda]] is a natural fit.

**Hook.** The Healer himself does not share his church's view. A priest who stands up for Jory against his own superiors might find the god agrees with him, in public, with consequences for everyone involved.
:::
