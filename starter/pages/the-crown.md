The royal family of [[The Storm Kingdom]], ruling from [[Rimaros]] with powerful adventurers of its own. The crown is a foreign power: it has no authority in [[Greenstone]], which is a city-state answering only to its Duke. It matters there only through trade, envoys, and the occasional royal adventurer who crosses the ocean on business of their own.

| Wants | Holds power through | Default view of a new Iron-ranker |

| Stability and reach of the kingdom | Law, armies, taxes and royal adventurers, at home; trade and envoys abroad | Irrelevant unless they are useful |

**Tension.** The crown would like friends among Greenstone's great houses, and some of the houses would like the crown's money. Neither side trusts the other.

**What players know.** The Storm Kingdom is rich, far away and ruled by a king. Its envoys are polite, well dressed and very interested in who is strong in Greenstone.

## The royal house

- **Soramir Rimaros**, the Diamond-rank Celestine who founded the kingdom and is still alive, holds the title Ancestral Majesty. He rarely involves himself in rule.
- **The Storm King**, a Gold-rank Celestine and a magic swordsman, reigns now. Storm Kings are chosen by contest among royal candidates, not simply by birth.
- **The royal family** is large, Celestine, and full of adventurers. Princes and princesses earn their standing in the field as well as at court.
- **The Sapphire Crown**, the royal guard, is a guild of elite adventurers sworn to the family.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Royal adventurers abroad.** A young royal on a contract in Greenstone is a perfect complication: rich, protected, overconfident and politically explosive if hurt.
- **The succession contest** gives any royal a reason to want strong allies and impressive deeds, and a reason to fear rivals.
- **The Storm church** is the crown's eyes in Greenstone. See [[Church of the Storm]].
:::
