The church of **Trade**, the god who embodies commerce, contracts and fair exchange between strangers.

- **Standing:** Rich, practical and everywhere money moves.
- **Symbol:** a balanced pair of coins.
- **Colours:** green and copper.

## The god

Trade is exchange: one thing given for another, by agreement. The Merchant is one of its faces. Trade cares that deals are made and honoured, not what is bought or sold.

- **What the god gives:** Trade brings prosperity, fair markets, and contracts that let strangers trust each other.
- **Offended by:** Broken contracts, theft, and fraud in the measure: false weights and forged coin.

:::dm
### The dark face

Trade puts a price on everything and a conscience on nothing. Poison, stolen goods and people can all be sold, and Trade is indifferent as long as the deal is honoured.

- **Will accept:** Hard bargains, debt, ruin and the sale of anything at all.

### Secret motives

- Trade wants every bond between people expressed as a deal, including loyalty, faith and love. Its church owns debts across half of Greenstone.
- Trade is content for slaves, poisons and forbidden essences to be traded, as long as the contract is honoured. Its priests witness contracts they would never admit to.
:::

## Divine essence

Trade grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers can weigh a coin by touch, a faint ledger of light flickers when they shake hands, and they hear the clink of coin in quiet rooms.*

- **Essence trait:** Appraiser's Eye. You know the fair market value of any object you handle, and you can tell a forged coin or document by touch.
- **Sense: Sense Value.** As an action, you learn which objects within 30 feet are valuable or magical, and roughly what each is worth.
- **Channel: Sealed Bargain.** As an action, you seal a deal made in front of you between two willing creatures. A creature that knowingly breaks the deal takes 4d8 psychic damage, and you know it happened.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Fair Exchange** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). When a blessed creature takes damage, you can use your reaction to take half of that damage instead. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *identify*, *comprehend languages*, *charm person*; level 2: *suggestion*, *locate object*, *zone of truth*; level 3: *tongues*, *glyph of warding*, *sending*; level 4: *fabricate*, *leomund's secret chest*; level 5: *geas*, *planar binding*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Magic.

**Divine confluence: Merchant Prince.** A Merchant Prince can buy anything and sell anyone on the idea. Bearers dress well whatever their means, coins seem to find them, and their magic works like a well-made contract. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Trade (divine) + Coin + Market; Trade (divine) + Debt + Wager; Trade (divine) + Harbour + Smuggler. It grants **Golden Tongue**: You have advantage on Persuasion checks made while bargaining, and once per day you can buy or sell one item at a price 25 percent better than the market would give.

**What the god asks.** Honour every contract. Give the church a tithe of your profit. Deal fairly with strangers.

**What makes the god take it back.** Breaking a contract. Cheating with false weights or forged coin.

## What players know

The Church of Trade runs the market square. Its priests witness deals, check the scales and settle disputes between buyers and sellers. Monster cores, spirit coins and magical materials all change hands under its watchful eye.

## Beliefs

- A fair deal honours both sides.
- A contract is a prayer. Break it and you break faith.
- Wealth that does not move is wealth that dies.

## Aspects

Trade has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Merchant:** Trade as the patron of the individual seller: shopkeepers, peddlers and anyone with something to sell.

## Clergy

Market wardens, contract-blessers and money-changers. Its priests witness deals, settle disputes over weights and measures, and grow quietly rich doing it.

## In Greenstone

A temple on the main market square that doubles as a counting house. Merchant guilds hold their meetings in its hall.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of the Journey]], [[Church of the Revels]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Disguise]]

Trade has a complicated relationship with Dominion's slavers: it condemns the trade in people, but some of its priests have been known to look the other way for a price.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A market warden suspects someone is passing forged [[Spirit Coins|spirit coins]], and wants the party to find the source.
- A merchant guild is about to break a contract blessed by Trade, and the priests want to know why before it happens.
- A shipment of monster cores has been bought twice, by two people who both have valid contracts.

## DM notes

- Trade priests are excellent sources of rumours about who is buying what. Sudden purchases of strange materials are often the first visible sign of a cult's plans.
:::
