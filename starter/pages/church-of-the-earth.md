The church of **Earth**, the god who embodies soil, stone, and the bones of the world, down to the dark places beneath.

- **Standing:** Steady, stubborn and respected.
- **Symbol:** a mountain inside a square.
- **Colours:** brown and granite grey.

## The god

Earth is the land itself: soil, stone, mountains and the deep places beneath them. Stone and Deep are its faces. It is patient, heavy and slow to change.

- **What the god gives:** Earth brings stability, fertile ground, stone to build with, and endurance.
- **Offended by:** The sky's rage tearing at the land. Storm is its old rival.

:::dm
### The dark face

Earth brings stubbornness and suffocation: the landslide, the collapsed mine, and the dark below that swallows anyone who goes too deep.

- **Will accept:** Burial, collapse and those lost in the deep.

### Secret motives

- Earth wants things to stay as they are. Its church resists every change in law, borders and leadership, and backs whoever promises stability.
- Earth knows what lies in the deep beneath Greenstone, and keeps it buried.
:::

## Divine essence

Earth grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers are heavy-footed and hard to move, their skin has a faint grain like granite, and dust settles around them.*

- **Essence trait:** Proficiency in Athletics or with mason's tools.
- **Sense: Tremorsense.** As an action, for 1 minute you gain tremorsense out to 30 feet: you know where any creature touching the ground within range is.
- **Channel: Stone Hide.** As a bonus action, for 1 minute you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage, and you can't be moved against your will.
- **Blessing: Blessing of the Mountain** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures can't be knocked prone or pushed. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *earth tremor*, *shield*, *longstrider*; level 2: *spike growth*, *shatter*, *barkskin*; level 3: *meld into stone*, *erupting earth*, *wall of sand*; level 4: *stoneskin*, *stone shape*; level 5: *wall of stone*, *passwall*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Earth.

**Divine confluence: Mountainheart.** A Mountainheart is as hard to move as the hills. Bearers are heavy and unhurried, the ground steadies under them, and their magic grinds like tectonic stone. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Earth (divine) + Earth + Quarry; Earth (divine) + Fossil + Patience; Earth (divine) + Abyss + Gravity. It grants **Rooted**: You have resistance to bludgeoning damage, can't be moved against your will while standing on earth or stone, and have tremorsense out to 15 feet.

**What the god asks.** Bless every foundation you are asked to bless. Respect the deep, and go down only with the god's leave. Hold your ground.

**What makes the god take it back.** Desecrating a shrine of the Earth. Breaking stone in spite.

## What players know

The Church of the Earth blesses every foundation in Greenstone. Its priests serve miners and masons, and its deep-delvers go into caves and mines that no one else will enter. Its temple's crypt is said to go down a very long way.

## Beliefs

- What is built on stone endures.
- Be patient as rock, and as hard to move.
- Respect the dark below. It does not respect you.

## Aspects

Earth has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Stone:** The Earth as the patron of building: masons, quarriers and anyone who shapes stone.
- **Deep:** The Earth's dark below: caverns, mines and the unknown underneath. Delvers pray to it before they go down.

## Clergy

Miners' chaplains, masons' priests and deep-delvers. Its priests bless foundations, and no sensible builder in Greenstone lays a cornerstone without them.

## In Greenstone

A squat stone temple beside the quarry road, with a crypt that goes deeper than anyone has mapped.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of Trade]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of the Storm]]

Earth and Storm have argued about the land and sky for as long as anyone remembers. Neither has ever given an inch.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- Miners have broken into an unknown cavern, and the church wants it explored before anyone else goes in.
- A building blessed by Earth priests has collapsed, and the masons are blaming the church.
- The temple's crypt has started to hum at night, and the priests swear it never did before.
