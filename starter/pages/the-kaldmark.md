**Kingdom.** A cold northern realm of fjords, pine forests and long winters on the northern continent, ruled by a high king and a ring of proud jarls. The people are tough, plain-spoken and very hard to frighten.

**What players know.** The Kaldmark is the far north: whalers, timber, iron, furs and adventurers who learned their trade fighting monsters in the snow. Northerners have a reputation for honesty, for drinking, and for settling disputes in a way that ends with someone in the healer's tent.

## The land

- **The Fjord Coast.** Deep sea inlets between steep mountains, with fishing towns clinging to every shore. Ships are the only road for half the year.
- **The Pinewood.** Endless forest in the south and east, full of wolves, bears and worse. Timber camps and iron towns along its rivers.
- **The Ice Teeth.** High mountains along the spine of the realm, snowbound most of the year. Monsters there are strong, and the passes close for months at a time.
- **The White Reach.** Tundra in the far north, where the sun does not set in summer or rise in winter. Herders follow great beasts across it, and the lights dance in the sky.
- **The Lakelands.** Thousands of lakes in the east, with towns on islands and ice roads between them in winter.

## The high king and the jarls

The Kaldmark is ruled by a **high king** chosen by the jarls at a great gathering, the Allthing, from among themselves. The crown passes to whoever can hold the jarls together, and the high king who forgets that does not stay king for long.

- **The jarls** each rule a fjord, valley or stretch of forest. Most are adventurers of Silver rank or above, because a jarl who cannot fight cannot keep the monsters off their land.
- **The Allthing** meets every summer at a sacred lake. Laws are spoken aloud, disputes are settled, and the jarls argue about everything.
- **Sworn bands.** Adventuring teams in the Kaldmark swear to a jarl rather than to a guild, and fight for them in war as well as against monsters.

## Law and custom

- **Spoken law.** The laws are memorised and recited by lawspeakers, not written. A lawspeaker's word settles most disputes.
- **Holmgang.** Serious quarrels can be settled by a formal duel. Killing is discouraged; humiliation is not.
- **Slavery** is not practised. Debts are paid in labour for a fixed term, never in a person.
- **Faith.** The churches of war, the hunt and the sea are strongest. Temples are timber halls, and priests are expected to fight.

## Trade

The Kaldmark sells iron, timber, furs, whale oil, amber and northern essences: Ice, Wolf, Bear and Frost. It buys grain, wine, cloth and southern luxuries. Its whalers range across the northern oceans.

## DM notes

- **Using the Kaldmark.** It is a place where rank matters openly: the strong lead, and visitors are judged by what they can do. A Bronze party can win a jarl's favour in a single season.
- **The Gellers' northern branch.** If you want a home for [[Rick Geller]] and [[Phoebe Geller]]'s side of the family, the Kaldmark works well: a branch that married into a jarl's line generations ago and kept the name.

**Hooks.**
- A jarl's sworn band has vanished in the Ice Teeth, and the jarl will reward any team who finds them, alive or not.
- The Allthing is about to choose a new high king, and two candidates both want a foreign team at their side to prove their reach.
- A whaling fleet has brought home a core the size of a man's head from a monster no one can name.
- Wolves of Bronze rank have begun hunting the Lakelands in packs of fifty, and the ice roads are not safe.
:::
