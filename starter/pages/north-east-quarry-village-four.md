**Village.** A quarry village in the hills inland from [[Greenstone]], between the city and the estate of [[House Vane]]. Its people cut the green marble that gives Greenstone its name. Magic level: moderate. Protector: three villagers who guard the aperture.

## What players know

High on the mountain above the village, a hole opens into an [[Astral Spaces|astral space]], and a waterfall pours out of it, down the cliffs and away into the desert. That water is the source of the Mistrun River. The village grew up under it, quarrying the green stone on either side.

- **The aperture guards.** Hiram, a short, barrel-chested man with the Might essence, leads two others, Gruff and Duggan, in keeping watch on the aperture. Things come through it now and then.
- **The inn.** Martha runs the village inn and will lend her kitchen to anyone who cooks for her too.
- **Old Murph** works the general store and knows every secret in the village.

**Export.** Green marble, sent down to Greenstone by cart and riverboat.

**Quirk.** The village is officially named by the quarry company's survey, and nobody has ever bothered to change it.

:::dm
**Shabs.** The things that come through the aperture are mostly shabs: three-metre monsters with a shark's body and head, a purple-and-red plated shell, eight crab legs, and pincers bigger than a man's head. Use a reef shark's stats with a crab's claws, at Iron rank.

**Hook.** More shabs have come through this month than in the last three years together, and Hiram has started sleeping by the aperture.
:::
