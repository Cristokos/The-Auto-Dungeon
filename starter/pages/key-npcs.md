:::dm
## Using them on day one

- Keep Gold-rankers distant: they are the people who decide what happens to a city, not party members.
- Bronze-rankers like Rufus's team make good occasional mentors and contract-givers.
- Iron-rank peers are the most useful NPCs early on, because they are on the players' level and share the same problems.
- The [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch]] page has the branch's staff, from the director to the gate runner.
- Add your own town officials, a [[Magic Society]] appraiser and a local priest. The **NPC** generator can roll them.
:::
