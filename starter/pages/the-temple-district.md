**District.** The religious quarter of [[The Island]], built around **Divine Square**. It is the city's public face of worship, where [[The Churches]] that can afford it keep grand temples within sight of each other.

## What players know

- **Divine Square.** A wide plaza of green marble with a fountain at its centre. The largest temples face it: the Healer, Purity, Knowledge, Justice and Trade. Smaller churches crowd the streets behind.
- **The temples.** Each church builds in its own style. The Temple of Knowledge has a room where petitioners may put questions to the goddess. The Healer's temple runs a hospital wing that charges what the Old City cannot pay.
- **Preachers.** On any day the square has a preacher of Purity warning about corrupt magic, a priest of the Revels giving out wine, and someone from Liberty handing out pamphlets the Duke's guard would rather they didn't.
- **Outlawed faiths** do not keep temples here. The [[Church of Undeath]] and the [[Church of Destruction]] worship in cellars, if at all.

> *Not everyone who wears a priest's robes serves the god they pray to.* (a [[Whispers|whisper]])

- An acolyte of Knowledge says her goddess told her to be present when the party registers with the Adventure Society. She does not know why, and neither does anyone else.
