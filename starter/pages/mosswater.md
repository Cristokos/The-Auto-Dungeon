**Village.** A cluster of stilt houses on the edge of [[The Delta and Marshes|the delta]], half a day's poling from [[Greenstone]]. Magic level: moderate, and rising. Protector: a pair of Iron-rank brothers on a retainer the village can barely afford.

## What players know

Mosswater stands on stilts above the shallows, its houses joined by rope walkways that sway when the wind comes off the marsh. Everyone travels by flat-bottomed boat, and children can pole one before they can swim.

**Export.** Smoked marsh eels and reed paper. The paper is rough and cheap, and half the contract notices in Greenstone are written on it.

**Quirk.** Every house has a bell hung under its eaves. When a monster is sighted, the nearest house rings, and the ringing passes from house to house until the whole village knows. The village keeps a tally of each year's alarms, painted on the meeting-house wall, and argues every spring about whether the numbers are rising.

:::dm
**Hook.** This year's tally is already past last year's, and it is barely summer. Something in the deep marsh is driving monsters toward the village, and the brothers on retainer have started quietly packing.
:::
