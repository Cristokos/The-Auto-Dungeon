**Normal.** Goblin boy, gate runner at the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] of the [[Adventure Society]].

- **Looks:** About twelve, small even for a goblin, in a Society tabard three sizes too big with the sleeves rolled up four times. Always eating something.
- **Personality:** Fast, cheeky and nosy. Knows everyone's business and sells it cheap.
- **Wants:** To be an adventurer, obviously, and to never go back to the orphanage.
- **Ties:** He grew up in the orphanage by the Old City gate and ran away at nine. [[Nessa Quill]] feeds him, and [[Pell Hobbard]] pays him out of his own pocket on slow weeks.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Messages carried anywhere in the city, rumours for a copper, and a guide to the parts of Greenstone the maps leave out.
:::

## What people say

Dobbs, the gate runner. He'll carry a message across the city for a copper, and he'll tell you three secrets on the way for free. Ask him anything.

:::dm
## DM notes

- He saw who delivered the contract that sent one of the overdue teams out of the city. He doesn't know it matters, and nobody has asked him.
- **What he saw.** The overdue team's contract was delivered by hand to the counter by a man in plain clothes with a Mercer ring. Dobbs remembers the ring because he wanted to steal it.
- **A guide.** He knows the Old City's rooftops, the dock warehouses and every back way through the slums. A party that pays him fairly gets there faster and gets told who's watching.
- **Danger.** Somebody will eventually work out what he saw. If he goes missing, the party should be the ones to notice.
- **A scene.** A small goblin in a huge tabard falls into step with the party as they leave the branch, eating an apple, and offers to tell them which contract on the board is a trap, for two coppers. Or three if they want to know why.

## Playing Dobbs

**Street-smart runner.** Fast, cheeky and nosy, braver than he should be and younger than he acts.

- **Speech:** Rapid, cheeky and full of Old City slang
- **How to play:** Offer information before he's asked and name a price
- **Tell:** Takes a bite of something whenever he's nervous
- **Toward the party:** Cheeky, testing, and secretly hopeful
- **Wants from the party:** Coin, food, and to be taken seriously
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Two coppers and I'll tell you. Three and I'll tell you true.
>
> I'm not going back there. Not ever.
>
> When I'm an adventurer, I'll do it better than you lot.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To be an adventurer, with a badge of his own
- **Driven by:** Hunger, and a fierce independence
- **Fears:** The orphanage, and being sent back
- **Bond:** Nessa, and anyone who feeds him
- **Flaw:** Can't resist poking his nose into dangerous things
