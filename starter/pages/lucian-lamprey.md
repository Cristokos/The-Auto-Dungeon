### Lucian Lamprey

*Medium humanoid (Elf), Silver rank fighter 11 (Eldritch Knight)*

**Armor Class** 20 (+1 plate, worn under tailored robes)  
**Hit Points** 104 (11d10 + 44)

#### Traits

***Mana Touch (Magic essence).*** As an action, he can touch an object to learn whether it is magical and which school it belongs to.

***Glyph Sight (Rune essence).*** He can tell at a glance whether writing or a symbol is magical, and has advantage on checks to find magical glyphs, runes and sigils.

***Item Lore (Enchanter confluence).*** Once per long rest, he can spend 10 minutes handling a magic item to learn its properties, as if he had cast *identify* on it.

***Fey Ancestry.*** He has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put him to sleep.

***Indomitable (1/long rest).*** He can reroll a saving throw he fails and must use the new roll.

***Eldritch Strike.*** When he hits a creature with a weapon attack, it has disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes against a spell he casts before the end of his next turn.

***Spellcasting.*** Intelligence-based (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit). Cantrips: *booming blade*, *mage hand*. 1st (4 slots): *absorb elements*, *shield*, *thunderwave*. 2nd (3 slots): *misty step*, *shatter*, *hold person*. 3rd (2 slots): *counterspell*, *lightning bolt*.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** He makes three maul attacks. He can replace one attack with a cantrip.

***+2 Maul.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d6 + 7) bludgeoning damage.

***Action Surge (1/short rest).*** He takes one additional action on his turn.

#### Bonus actions

***Second Wind (1/short rest).*** He regains 1d10 + 11 hit points.

#### Awakening stones

Each once per long rest. Spell save DC 15, Int-based, +7 to hit with spell attacks.

- *dispel magic* (level 3, Magic)
- *counterspell* (level 3, Magic)
- *glyph of warding* (level 3, Rune)
- *destructive wave* (level 5, Hammer)

**Silver rank.** Elf man, director of the [[Magic Society]]'s branch in [[Greenstone]].

- **Looks:** Broad and heavily muscled, which is unusual for an elf, in expensive, finely tailored clothes. His face settles into a severe, furrowed frown that can look malevolent, and he carries himself with the full weight of a Silver-ranker.
- **Essences:** Hammer, Magic and Rune, forming the **Enchanter** confluence. He is a formidable enchanter and a more formidable fighter.
- **Personality:** Imperious, vindictive, and used to getting what he wants. He enjoys the fighting pits more than a director should.
- **Wants:** Control of the Magic Society branch, influence over the Adventure Society, and a fighter he has seen in the pits.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Appraisals, research access, enchanting, and favours that come with strings.
:::

## What people say

The director of the Magic Society. Brilliant, powerful and unpleasant, and a regular in the best box at [[The Fortress]].

## DM notes

- **His network.** Lamprey runs a private web of criminal and political contacts alongside the Society: people in [[The Big Three]]'s organisations, officials, and staff in the Adventure Society branch.
- **Sophie.** He wants [[Sophie Wexler]] under his control and has leaned on Clarissa Ventress to deliver her.
- **Clive.** He has kept [[Clive Standish]] from advancing in the Society since he took over, out of spite and because Clive asks inconvenient questions.
- He is exactly the kind of powerful, ambitious person the [[The Builder Cult|Builder cult]] looks for.

## Playing Lamprey

**Petty tyrant.** Powerful, clever and used to winning. Every favour is leverage, every slight is remembered, and every beautiful thing is something to own.

- **Speech:** Low, clipped and contemptuous; never raises his voice
- **How to play:** Loom, frown, and let silence do the threatening, then name a price that is not money
- **Tell:** The frown deepens into a smile when he thinks he has won
- **Toward the party:** Dismissive, until they have something he wants
- **Wants from the party:** Rare cores, astral finds and anything that gives him a hold over the Adventure Society, or over Sophie
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> The Society will appraise it. The Society will also remember who brought it.
>
> You fight like an Iron-ranker. Pity.
>
> Clive is not available. Clive is never available.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To own the Magic Society branch outright, and the best fighter in the pits
- **Driven by:** Appetite for control
- **Fears:** Being made to look small in public
- **Bond:** His private network, which he trusts more than any person
- **Flaw:** Cannot let a slight go, however minor

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Hammer** (common, Might): Impact, smithing and breaking. Grants proficiency: warhammers or smith's tools. The essence of the hammer, maker and breaker both. Bearers' hands grow callused and sure, sparks jump when they grip metal, and their magic lands like an anvil blow.
- **Magic** (uncommon, Magic): Raw mana and arcane sensitivity. Grants **Mana Touch**. Magic is mana in its rawest, most adaptable form. Blue-white motes drift around the bearer's hands, and enchanted things feel faintly warm or buzzing to their touch.
- **Rune** (uncommon, Magic): Inscription, glyphs and structured magic. Grants **Glyph Sight**. Rune is magic given shape through writing and symbol. Faint script traces itself across the bearer's hands as they work, and their spells leave brief glowing glyphs hanging in the air.
- **Confluence: Enchanter** (Hammer + Magic + Rune): Binding magic into made things. Grants **Item Lore**. Enchanter is rune, raw magic and craft fused into the art of binding power into objects. Bearers' tools hum faintly at their touch, glowing marks crawl along anything they work on, and their spells settle into objects like heat into a horseshoe.
