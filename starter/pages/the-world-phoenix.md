The World-Phoenix is the [[Astral Powers|great astral being]] that guards the boundaries of worlds. It keeps reality whole, holding back what lies outside and mending what has torn, and its fire stands for renewal: what burns down under its care grows back. Sailors at sunrise and adventurers before a first contract sometimes say a word to it, though it has no church in Pallimustus.

## What it wants

- Worlds kept whole, their edges sealed against the things of the deep astral.
- Rifts closed, breaches burned clean, and damage to reality repaired.
- The cycle of renewal kept turning, in worlds and in people.

## Dawn, the First Sister

The World-Phoenix's prime avatar is **Dawn**, a Diamond-rank Celestine who holds the title First Sister among its servants. She has alabaster skin, ruby hair and crimson eyes, and can open wings of fire when she wishes. Her essences are Fire, Trap, Spider and Ambush: she fights with webs of metal and flame and with a sword she uses better than almost anyone alive. At full strength she can call a phoenix the size of a city.

Dawn travels between worlds and can send a projected image of herself where she cannot go in person. She carries a tiny astral vessel shaped like a snow globe with a garden cottage inside. The Second Sister, **Helsveth**, a Diamond-rank draconian, is being prepared to succeed her.

:::dm
**Using the World-Phoenix.** It is the friendliest of the great astral beings, but friendly at cosmic scale. Dawn appears only when a boundary is threatened: a Builder space anchored too deep, a Messenger incursion, a hole in the world. She is blunt, competent and short on patience for mortal politics, and she treats Iron and Bronze adventurers the way a general treats promising recruits.

- A party that seals a breach or burns out a Builder anchor may earn a small token of the World-Phoenix's notice.
- Dawn is not here to solve the party's problems. If she arrives, something is already very wrong.
:::
