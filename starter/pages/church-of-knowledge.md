The church of **Knowledge**, the god who embodies learning, memory, and the preservation of everything that is known.

- **Standing:** Influential among scholars and the wealthy.
- **Symbol:** an open book with an eye on its page.
- **Colours:** deep blue and silver.

## The god

Knowledge is everything that is known, and the act of knowing it. Knowledge wants things learned, recorded and kept.

- **What the god gives:** Knowledge is the light of learning: libraries, schools, medicine and invention, and a memory that outlasts every empire.
- **Offended by:** Destruction of knowledge: burned books, killed scholars, and secrets taken to the grave.

:::dm
### The dark face

Knowledge is hoarded secrets and unethical study. Knowledge would let a city burn if watching it burn taught something new, and treats forbidden lore as simply unread.

- **Will accept:** Experiments on the unwilling, stolen secrets, and the study of anything at all, including what is monstrous.

### Secret motives

- Knowledge wants to know everything, including every secret of the other gods and the astral beings above them. Its church spies on the other churches.
- Knowledge would happily let a disaster happen if studying it taught something new, and its Keepers have done exactly that before.
- Knowledge hoards knowledge as often as it shares it. Information is leverage, and leverage is how the church survives.
:::

## Divine essence

Knowledge grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers' eyes flick as if reading invisible text, ink never smudges under their hands, and they forget nothing.*

- **Essence trait:** Perfect Recall. You can recall anything you have seen or heard in the past month exactly, and you have proficiency in History.
- **Sense: Sense Secrets.** As an action, you learn which objects within 30 feet are magical, and whether any writing within 30 feet is hidden or encoded, until the end of your next turn.
- **Channel: Revelation.** As an action, you ask the DM one question about a creature, object or place you can see. The answer is true, but may be no more than a few words.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Insight** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures add your Wisdom modifier (minimum +1) to Intelligence checks. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *identify*, *comprehend languages*, *detect magic*; level 2: *detect thoughts*, *locate object*, *augury*; level 3: *clairvoyance*, *tongues*, *speak with dead*; level 4: *divination*, *locate creature*; level 5: *legend lore*, *scrying*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Magic.

**Divine confluence: Loremaster.** A Loremaster knows a little about everything and a lot about most things. Bearers murmur half-remembered quotations, ink appears on their fingertips when they think hard, and their magic arrives as sudden understanding. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Knowledge (divine) + Archive + Ink; Knowledge (divine) + Memory + Cipher; Knowledge (divine) + Owl + Prophecy. It grants **Font of Lore**: You add your proficiency bonus to any Intelligence check that doesn't already include it, and once per day you can ask the DM what the church's archives say about a subject.

**What the god asks.** Record what you learn, and give the church a copy. Never destroy a book or record. Answer honest questions honestly.

**What makes the god take it back.** Destroying knowledge, such as burning books or killing a scholar to silence them. Hiding what you know from the church.

## What players know

The Church of Knowledge keeps the best library in Greenstone and lets almost anyone use it. Ask a Keeper a question and you will usually get an answer, sometimes a very long one. Ask about the lower floor and the conversation ends.

## Beliefs

- Knowledge lost is a death that cannot be undone.
- Every question deserves an answer. Not every person deserves to hear it.
- Record everything. Judge later.

## Clergy

Archivists, librarians and scholar-priests. Its **Keepers** guard sealed collections of dangerous texts, and decide who may read them.

## In Greenstone

A domed library-temple open to anyone who can read, with a locked lower floor that is open to very few. It has an uneasy, competitive friendship with the [[Magic Society]].

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of Truth]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Disguise]], [[Church of Destruction]]

Knowledge guards secrets as carefully as it shares truths, which puts it at odds with the Church of Truth more often than either admits.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A Keeper hires the party to retrieve a book from a dead scholar's house before the family sells it.
- Someone has been cutting pages out of the library's oldest records about [[Outworlders|outworlders]].
- The church offers a large sum for any intact text recovered from a newly opened [[Astral Spaces|astral space]].

## DM notes

- The lower floor holds records about [[Astral Powers|astral beings]], past [[Monster Surges|surges]] and outworlders that would change how people see the world.
- Knowledge trades in secrets. A Keeper will happily swap a true answer for a piece of information the church does not yet have.
:::
