#### Traits

***Perfect Recall (Archive essence).*** You can recall, word for word, any text you have read in the past 30 days, along with any map, diagram or table it contained.

***Unhurried Hands (Patience essence).*** When you spend at least 10 minutes on an ability check using a tool you are proficient with, you can treat a d20 roll of 7 or lower as an 8.

***Glamoured Coin (Fairy Gold confluence).*** Once per long rest, as an action, you can touch up to ten pebbles, leaves, buttons or copper coins and make them look, feel and ring like gold pieces. They return to their true form at the next dawn, and detect magic reveals them as illusions.

**Bronze rank.** Human man, husband of [[Danielle Geller]] and father of [[Henrietta Geller|Henrietta]] and [[Humphrey Geller|Humphrey]].

- **Looks:** Broad, greying and genial, with ink on his fingers and a ledger never far away.
- **Role:** He married into the family and runs its business: the delta farms, the ships, the estate's household and its accounts.
- **Personality:** Patient, warm and very hard to fool. He is the one family member who laughs easily.
- **Essences:** Coin, Archive and Patience, forming the **Fairy Gold** confluence. He is a bard who fights with words, and he remembers every number he has ever read.
- **Ties:** He came to the family from the merchant houses of [[The Marinas]], and still has friends among the shipmasters there.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Introductions, fair prices, and a sympathetic ear when Danielle is being Danielle.

Keith is the quiet power behind the house's money. Anyone who wants a deal with the Gellers that Danielle would refuse should try Keith first. He will still refuse, but nicely, and he will remember who asked.
:::

## What people say

Danielle Geller's husband, and the man who actually pays for everything the Gellers do. Merchants say he is the fairest dealer in the city and the hardest to cheat, and that both are true at the same time.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **His ledgers.** Keith knows exactly where every Geller coin goes, including the money Danielle spends on things she hasn't told the family about. He has never asked her about it.
- **Danielle.** He is the one person who can make her laugh, and the one person she listens to when she is wrong. A party that needs Danielle to change her mind should talk to Keith first.
- **Work for the party.** He hires adventurers to escort ships and inspect the delta farms, pays promptly and adds a small bonus for honest reports.
- **A scene.** Keith is in the estate's counting room with a pot of tea and four ledgers open, humming. He offers the party a cup, asks after their families, and then mentions exactly how much they spent at the last market.

## Playing Keith

**Genial accountant.** Kind, patient, and a better judge of people than anyone in the house.

- **Speech:** Warm, measured and fond of small jokes
- **How to play:** Pour tea for everyone and ask a question that shows he already knows the answer
- **Tell:** Taps his pen twice before he says no
- **Toward the party:** Friendly and fatherly, and quietly assessing
- **Wants from the party:** Honest partners, and someone to watch Humphrey's back
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Tea first. Business after. Business is always better after tea.
>
> That's a generous offer. No.
>
> Danielle will say no. Let me ask her.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To see his children grow old
- **Driven by:** Love for his family, and a deep respect for a fair ledger
- **Fears:** A surge reaching the delta while his children are in the field
- **Bond:** Danielle, Henrietta and Humphrey
- **Flaw:** Will cover for the family's mistakes long after he should stop

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Coin** (common, Sin): Trade, value and the haggle. Grants Proficiency in Persuasion, or one language of your choice. The essence of coin and commerce. Bearers can weigh a purse by eye, their voices take on a trader's easy patter, and their spells glint like spinning gold.
- **Archive** (uncommon, Magic): Records, memory and preserved knowledge. Grants **Perfect Recall**. Archive is the gathered memory of everything that has been written down and kept. Bearers' thoughts become orderly and precise, and they can call up a page they once read as clearly as if it lay before them.
- **Patience** (uncommon, Earth): Calm endurance and careful work. Grants **Unhurried Hands**. Patience is the quiet strength of waiting and working without hurry. Bearers move slowly and deliberately, rarely raise their voice, and seem untroubled by delays that drive others mad.
- **Confluence: Fairy Gold** (Coin + Archive + Patience): Glamoured coin that turns to leaves. Grants **Glamoured Coin**. Fairy Gold is the gleaming payment from a stranger in the woods that turns back into leaves, pebbles and dirt by morning. A bearer's smile is just a little too charming, coins seem to wander through their fingers, and their magic sparkles gold before crumbling into dust.
:::
