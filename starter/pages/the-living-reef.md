**Landmark.** The largest reef in the world, a riot of coral, fish and colour visible from a sky ship. *The southern continent.*

## What players know

The merfolk have a great city on the Living Reef, built from coral they grow into shape. The reef sings at night, and the merfolk say it is remembering.

## What mana has made of it

- Coral here can be grown into any shape by someone with a water or life essence, given patience.
- The reef's song can be heard through the hull of any ship nearby, and sailors who listen too long forget their homes.
- Reef fish carry tiny monster cores, and merfolk use them as currency.

**Dangers.** Sharks, reef monsters, merfolk politics, and the coral's slow hunger.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** The merfolk city's queen asks for surface-dwellers to investigate a dead patch spreading through the reef.
- **Secret:** The reef is one creature, and it is old enough to remember the gods arriving.
- **On Earth this would be** the Great Barrier Reef. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
