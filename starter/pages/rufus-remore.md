#### Traits

***Steady Footing (Balance essence).*** He has advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks to keep his balance.

***Twilight Eyes (Eclipse confluence).*** Dim light doesn't impose disadvantage on his Wisdom (Perception) checks, and he can look at the sun or other bright light without discomfort.

***Combat Superiority.*** He has five superiority dice (d8), regained on a short rest. Maneuver save DC 15. Maneuvers: Disarming Attack, Parry, Riposte, Trip Attack, Commander's Strike.

***Know Your Enemy.*** After observing a creature for 1 minute, he learns whether it is his equal, superior or inferior in two characteristics of his choice.

***Instructor.*** Allies who train with him for a week gain advantage on their next check to learn a weapon or fighting technique.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** He makes two rapier attacks.

***Rapier.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (1d8 + 6) piercing damage. Includes his Dueling style.

***Action Surge (1/short rest).*** He takes one additional action on his turn.

#### Bonus actions

***Second Wind (1/short rest).*** He regains 1d10 + 7 hit points.

#### Reactions

***Riposte.*** When a creature misses him with a melee attack, he spends a superiority die to make one melee attack against it, adding the die to the damage.

#### Awakening stones

Each once per long rest. Spell save DC 12, Wis-based, +4 to hit with spell attacks.

- *daylight* (level 3, Sun)
- *blur* (level 2, Balance)

**Bronze rank.** Human man, elite adventurer and heir to the most famous training family in [[Vitesse]].

- **Looks:** Very handsome, with dark skin, a bald head and a voice like dark chocolate.
- **Essences:** Balance, Moon and Sun, forming the **Eclipse** confluence. His powers turn on the balance of light and darkness.
- **Personality:** Earnest, disciplined, a natural teacher, a little stiff. He talks about his family's school constantly, and does not notice that he does.
- **Affiliation:** [[House Remore]] and its Remore Academy.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Training, standards and an honest ally.
:::

## What people say

A rising adventurer from a family famous for training others. Disciplined, serious, and said to be very good. If you sit with him long enough, you will hear about his family's school.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Team.** He leads a team with [[Farrah Hurin]] and [[Gary]], whom he met clearing a zombie plague. They came to Greenstone on a contract and are staying a while.
- **Mentor.** A good occasional mentor and contract-giver. He judges assessments for the [[Adventure Society]] when asked, and he is a hard but fair judge.
- **Family.** His grandfather Roland founded the academy; his parents Gabriel and Arabelle are Gold-rank adventurers; his childhood friend is the crown princess of Vitesse. See [[House Remore]].
- **The drinking game.** Tables that discover the "my family's school" drinking game will not stop playing it.
- **The Reaper.** He is quietly helping [[Emir Bahadir]] research the lost Order of the Reaper (see [[The Reaper]]).

## Playing Rufus

**Born teacher.** Disciplined, kind and a little pompous. Cannot see someone doing something wrong without explaining how to do it right.

- **Speech:** Rich, measured, a lecturer's rhythm
- **How to play:** Correct a party member's stance, then mention his family's school
- **Tell:** Clasps his hands behind his back when assessing someone
- **Toward the party:** Encouraging, demanding, and secretly impressed by courage
- **Wants from the party:** Students worth teaching, and help with a contract that is bigger than it looks
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> At my family's school, we would call that a beginner's mistake.
>
> Again. And this time, watch your feet.
>
> Courage is useful. Courage with training is better.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To be a teacher his grandfather would be proud of
- **Driven by:** Duty, and a deep belief that training saves lives
- **Fears:** Losing a student, or a teammate, to his own mistake
- **Bond:** [[Farrah Hurin]], [[Gary]] and [[House Remore]]
- **Flaw:** Cannot stop talking about his family's school

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user, trained at the Remore Academy.

- **Balance** (uncommon, Light): Equilibrium, poise and fair measure. Grants **Steady Footing**. Balance embodies equilibrium in body and in deed. Bearers carry a settled calm and rarely shift their weight, and their magic shows as a pair of faint scales that steady before every spell.
- **Moon** (common, Dark): Night, cycles and silver light. Grants proficiency: navigator's tools or Insight. The essence of the moon. Bearers take on a pale silver sheen after dark, their moods seem to wax and wane with the month, and their magic glows with cool, colourless light.
- **Sun** (common, Light): Radiance, warmth and daylight. Grants proficiency: Religion or Performance. The essence of the sun. Bearers give off a gentle summer warmth, their eyes take on a burnished gold tint, and their spells shine like noon light.
- **Confluence: Eclipse** (Balance + Moon + Sun): Sun and moon held in balance. Grants **Twilight Eyes**. Eclipse is the rare moment when sun and moon stand in perfect alignment and noon turns to dusk. A bearer's irises darken at the centre inside a thin ring of gold, and their spells dim the light around them while blazing at the edges like a corona.
