**Capital city.** Seat of [[The Storm Kingdom]] and of [[The Crown]], on a warm island in a sea of islands an ocean away from [[Greenstone]]. Larger, wealthier and far more political than Greenstone, and full of magic where Greenstone has little.

**What players know.** Rimaros is larger, richer, and far more political, a city where reputations are made at court as often as in the field. Ships run between the islands all year, the harbour never sleeps, and the palace can be seen from the sea before the rest of the city.

## The royal family

The city and the kingdom are named for **Soramir Rimaros**, the Celestine who founded both. He is still alive, a Diamond-ranker given the title Ancestral Majesty, and his descendants rule as a Celestine royal house. Royal Rimaros have the metallic hair and eyes of their people, and a great many of them are adventurers.

## The Storm King

The kingdom is ruled by the **Storm King**. The crown does not pass by birth alone: when a Storm King chooses to step down, royal candidates compete in a contest, and the reigning king names the successor from what the contest shows. The current Storm King is a Gold-ranker, and his power set is a magic swordsman's: Might, Sword and Lightning.

## The Sapphire Crown

The royal guard is the **Sapphire Crown**, a guild of elite adventurers sworn to the royal family. Its members wear sapphire-blue and are among the strongest people in the kingdom.

## The city

- **The palace** stands above the harbour on the highest ground in the city.
- **The harbour** is the busiest in the region, packed with island traders, warships and cloud-flask merchants.
- **The Adventure Society and Magic Society** keep their largest local halls here, and the Society's standards are far stricter than Greenstone's.

## DM notes

- **Use Rimaros** as the place the party goes when Greenstone is too small for them: Bronze play, court politics and bigger monsters. A royal contest for the throne is a ready-made late-campaign arc.
- **Celestine royals** are the main reason most people have ever seen a Celestine. That makes a commoner Celestine like [[Sophie Wexler]] stand out, and the royal family may take an interest in her.
- **The Storm church** sits close to the crown; see [[Church of the Storm]].
:::
