#### Traits

***Quick Mending (Renewal essence).*** Once per long rest, when you spend a Hit Die to regain hit points, you regain the maximum amount for that die instead of rolling it.

***Blood Sense (Blood essence).*** You know whether any creature you can see within 30 feet of you is below half its hit point maximum.

***Gentle Hands (Mercy essence).*** You automatically succeed on Wisdom (Medicine) checks made to stabilize a dying creature, and a creature you stabilize regains 1 hit point after 10 minutes rather than 1d4 hours.

***Take Their Pain (Martyr confluence).*** Once per long rest, as an action, you can touch a willing creature and take one condition from it onto yourself: blinded, deafened, frightened or poisoned. The condition ends on that creature and affects you for its remaining duration.

***Life Drain.*** Once per turn when she hits with a spell or weapon, she can drain 3d8 necrotic damage from the target and heal herself or a creature she can see within 30 feet by that much. She rarely does.

**Gold rank.** Human woman, wife of [[Gabriel Remore]] and mother of [[Rufus Remore]] and [[Gwydion Remore]]. A retired adventurer of the [[Church of the Healer]].

- **Looks:** Composed and warm, with a healer's steady hands and eyes that notice when someone is not sleeping.
- **Personality:** Calm, perceptive and kind, with no patience at all for people who hide their wounds. She now treats the minds of adventurers as well as their bodies, and the academy's students talk to her about things they tell no one else.
- **Powers:** Healing, and life-draining powers that she rarely needs to use any more.
- **Essences:** Renewal, Blood and Mercy, forming the **Martyr** confluence. Her healing is legendary, and her life-draining power was once just as feared.
- **Ties:** Still a priest of the [[Church of the Healer]], and still close to her old team.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Healing, honest counsel, and help with the parts of adventuring nobody trains for.

Arabelle is the person in the family Rufus most wants to impress and least wants to worry. A player character who has been through something terrible will find her already asking the right questions.
:::

## What people say

Arabelle Remore, the great healer, who now listens to adventurers instead of patching them up. Students at the academy say talking to her is like being healed, and much more uncomfortable.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Her work.** She treats adventurers whose bodies are healed but whose minds are not. She will notice a player character's nightmares, flinches or drinking within an hour, and say so kindly and plainly.
- **The other power.** She can drain the life from a whole room. She stopped using it in combat after a fight she won't describe, and she will not teach it.
- **Rufus.** She knows Rufus hides his fears from her. A party member who tells her the truth about how he is doing earns her trust, and Rufus's annoyance.
- **A scene.** Arabelle sits with a cup of tea at the edge of the training yard, watching. When a party member finishes a bout and walks past, she asks them gently how long it's been since they slept properly.

## Playing Arabelle

**Calm healer.** Warm, perceptive and unflinching, with no patience for people who hide their wounds.

- **Speech:** Quiet, gentle and direct
- **How to play:** Notice something the party has been hiding and ask about it kindly
- **Tell:** Puts her hand flat on the table before she asks a hard question
- **Toward the party:** Kind and steady, and concerned
- **Wants from the party:** Honesty, and for them to look after her son
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> You're hurt. Not there. There.
>
> Brave people still need sleep.
>
> Tell me the truth about Rufus. I won't tell him you did.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To see adventurers survive what their work does to them
- **Driven by:** Compassion, and an old guilt
- **Fears:** Her own power, and what it did once
- **Bond:** Gabriel, her sons, and the Church of the Healer
- **Flaw:** Carries everyone's pain and shows none of her own

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Renewal** (common, Life): Healing, recovery and second chances. Grants **Quick Mending**. The essence of renewal and fresh starts. Bearers' cuts close with a faint green shimmer, they wake from any rest looking refreshed, and their magic leaves behind the smell of rain.
- **Blood** (common, Blood): Vitality, wounds and the pulse. Grants **Blood Sense**. The essence of blood and the life it carries. Bearers' veins glow faintly red when their heart races, they can hear their own pulse in a quiet room, and their magic leaves a coppery scent.
- **Mercy** (uncommon, Light): Compassion and sparing the fallen. Grants **Gentle Hands**. Mercy is the choice to spare, to heal and to forgive. Bearers have a gentle touch and steady hands, and wounded creatures, even hostile ones, often grow calm when they kneel beside them.
- **Confluence: Martyr** (Renewal + Blood + Mercy): Bearing others' pain as one's own. Grants **Take Their Pain**. Martyr is grief and mercy fused into the will to suffer so that others need not. Bearers' faces grow calm and a little careworn, other people's bruises sometimes bloom faintly on their own skin, and their spells glow with a soft, sorrowful rose light.
:::
