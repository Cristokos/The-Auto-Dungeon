Pallimustus is old, and its magic has been working on the land for longer than anyone has kept records. Rivers, mountains, seas and deserts that would be ordinary anywhere else have become wonders: dangerous, valuable and often alive in ways scholars argue about. These are the best known. Each has a pin on the **World map**.

## Far north

- [[The Shatterfield]]: A forest of trees flattened outward in a perfect ring, around a crater that still hums.
- [[The Deepeye]]: The oldest and deepest lake in the world, clear as glass, frozen for half the year.
- [[The Bleak Spires]]: A chain of black islands in the northern sea, north of Greenstone, crowned with natural towers of iron-grey stone.

## The north-west continent

- [[The Thunderveil]]: A vast horseshoe waterfall whose mist is so thick with magic it glows at night.
- [[The Seething Caldera]]: A great volcanic basin of hot springs, geysers and bubbling mud, with mana-rich vapour in the air.
- [[The Sundered Stair]]: A mile-deep canyon of red and gold rock layers, carved by a river that runs at its bottom.

## The south-west continent

- [[The Verdant Deep]]: An endless rainforest along a river so wide its far bank is out of sight.

## The western ocean

- [[The Changing Isles]]: Volcanic islands far out to sea, home to creatures found nowhere else in the world.
- [[The Drowning Compass]]: A stretch of open ocean north-east of the Storm Kingdom where ships and sky ships vanish.

## The south-central continent

- [[The Glass Sea]]: Endless dunes, many of them fused into rippling glass by an ancient mana storm.
- [[The Thunder Mount]]: An active volcano on the coast, south-east of Yaresh, wreathed in permanent storm cloud.
- [[The Mist Gate]]: A mile-wide curtain of falling water whose spray rises like smoke and can be seen a day's walk away.

## The eastern lands

- [[The Salt Mirror]]: A salt lake at the lowest point of the land, so still it reflects the sky perfectly.
- [[The Throne of Winds]]: The highest mountain in the world, rising above a range of white peaks into air too thin to breathe.
- [[Dragonback Bay]]: Thousands of steep limestone islands rising from emerald water, home to floating fishing villages.

## The southern continent

- [[The Living Reef]]: The largest reef in the world, a riot of coral, fish and colour visible from a sky ship.
- [[The Red Heart]]: A vast red monolith rising alone from the flat desert, glowing like an ember at sunset.

## The far south

- [[The White Silence]]: A frozen continent at the bottom of the world, with ice miles thick and a cold that kills in minutes.

## The great ocean

- [[The Abyssal Scar]]: The deepest point in any ocean, a trench so deep that sunlight never reaches the bottom.

## The northern ocean

- [[The Emberhold]]: A volcanic island of black sand, glaciers and fire in the cold northern ocean.
