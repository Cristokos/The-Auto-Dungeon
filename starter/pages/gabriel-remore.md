**Gold rank.** Human man, son of [[Roland Remore]], husband of [[Arabelle Remore]], and father of [[Rufus Remore]] and [[Gwydion Remore]]. An instructor at the Remore Academy in [[Vitesse]].

- **Looks:** Tall and grinning, with the easy balance of someone who has never once been worried about falling.
- **Personality:** Whimsical and theatrical. He jumps from a ship onto the dock before it has finished docking, and once did an unscheduled fire dance at a graduation. Underneath the showmanship he is a superb teacher and a very dangerous adventurer.
- **Powers:** Fire is his element.
- **Essences:** Fire, Swift and Mirth, forming the **Vital Spark** confluence. A dancer's footwork, a showman's flair, and enough fire to level a street.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Training, an invitation to Vitesse, and an excellent story about whoever they mention.

Gabriel adventured with Arabelle, [[Emir Bahadir]] and Callum Morse in a famous Gold-rank team. He is proud of Rufus and worried that Rufus takes himself too seriously. If the party meets him, he will test them by surprise, cheerfully and hard.
:::

## What people say

Gabriel Remore, the fire dancer. Everyone who has met him has a story: he arrived by leaping off a moving ship, he juggled flaming swords at a funeral, he turned a lich to ash and bowed.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **The surprise test.** He tests people by attacking them in the middle of a conversation, laughing. A party that holds its own gets an invitation to Vitesse; a party that runs gets a lecture.
- **Under the show.** He is a superb teacher, and the showmanship is a lesson: confidence matters, and fear is what gets adventurers killed. He will explain this to a player character who asks.
- **His old team.** If the party needs [[Emir Bahadir]], Gabriel can get them a meeting. If they need Callum Morse, he will try to talk them out of it.
- **A scene.** A ship is still twenty feet from the dock when a tall man leaps from the rail, lands in a roll, comes up grinning and asks the first person he sees, who happens to be a party member, whether they know his son.

## Playing Gabriel

**Grinning showman.** Whimsical, theatrical and alarmingly powerful, and a far better teacher than he looks.

- **Speech:** Big, warm and theatrical
- **How to play:** Do something unnecessary and spectacular, then ask what they thought of it
- **Tell:** Snaps his fingers and a small flame dances on each knuckle
- **Toward the party:** Delighted, and testing them whether they like it or not
- **Wants from the party:** To see what they're made of, and to hear all about Rufus
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> You must be Rufus's friends! Duck.
>
> Fear is a terrible teacher. Fire is a very good one.
>
> Arabelle says I mustn't set anything alight indoors. We're outdoors now.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To see his sons outshine him
- **Driven by:** Joy, pride and love of the show
- **Fears:** Outliving his children
- **Bond:** Arabelle, Rufus and Gwydion
- **Flaw:** Never knows when a joke has gone too far

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Fire** (common, Fire): Heat, flame and fierce energy. Grants **Kindle**. The essence of living flame. Bearers run warm to the touch, ember glints flicker in their hair, and their spells crackle and smell of woodsmoke.
- **Swift** (common, Swift): Speed, reflexes and quick feet. Grants **Fleet**. The essence of speed. Bearers fidget whenever they are made to wait, leave faint afterimages when they move suddenly, and rarely seem out of breath.
- **Mirth** (uncommon, Life): Laughter, joy and good humour. Grants **Crowd Pleaser**. Mirth is the bright, infectious joy of shared laughter. Bearers have laugh lines that deepen quickly and a grin that is hard not to return, and their magic sparkles like festival lanterns.
- **Confluence: Vital Spark** (Fire + Swift + Mirth): The jolt that restarts a heart. Grants **Jolt**. The vital spark is the quick flash of fire that leaps from flint to tinder and from one living heart to another, the jolt that brings the fallen back. Bearers fidget and twitch with nervous energy, their touch gives a small warm sting, and their magic crackles from hand to hand in quick golden sparks.
