The church of **Truth**, the god who embodies honesty, and the revealing of whatever has been hidden.

- **Standing:** Small, sharp-tongued and hard to bribe.
- **Symbol:** an unshuttered lantern.
- **Colours:** clear white and pale yellow.

## The god

Truth is what is real, as opposed to what is said or believed. Truth wants every hidden thing revealed and every lie undone, whatever happens afterwards.

- **What the god gives:** Truth brings honest courts, oaths that hold, and the exposure of corruption.
- **Offended by:** Lies, masks and concealment. Disguise is its great enemy.

:::dm
### The dark face

Truth has no mercy. A secret that protects a child, a kind lie at a deathbed, or a disguise that keeps a refugee alive would all be torn away, because truth matters more than the harm it causes.

- **Will accept:** Cruelty, humiliation and ruin, if they come from honesty.

### Secret motives

- Truth wants every secret exposed, including ones that would start wars or topple a great house. Truth sees no reason to wait for a good moment.
- Truth considers Disguise its true enemy and is willing to ally with almost anyone, including Purity's zealots, to hunt down its faithful.
:::

## Divine essence

Truth grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers have clear, uncomfortable gazes, illusions waver around them, and their voices carry.*

- **Essence trait:** Proficiency in Insight or Investigation.
- **Sense: Sense Lies.** As an action, for the next minute you know whenever a creature you can hear deliberately speaks a lie. Careful evasions and half-truths don't count.
- **Channel: Unveil.** As an action, everything within 30 feet is laid bare until the end of your next turn: illusions look obviously false, invisible creatures are outlined in light, and a shapechanged or disguised creature must succeed on a Charisma save against your stone spell DC or have its true form glimpsed.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Candour** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you call down the truth on one creature you can see within 30 feet for 2 rounds (12 seconds). It can't speak a deliberate lie unless it succeeds on a Charisma save against your stone spell DC, which it repeats at the start of each of its turns. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *detect magic*, *command*, *faerie fire*; level 2: *zone of truth*, *see invisibility*, *detect thoughts*; level 3: *daylight*, *clairvoyance*, *dispel magic*; level 4: *divination*, *locate creature*; level 5: *legend lore*, *dispel evil and good*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Light.

**Divine confluence: Witness.** A Witness cannot be lied to for long. Bearers' gazes are uncomfortably steady, illusions crack around them like thin ice, and their magic shows things as they are. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Truth (divine) + Lantern + Mirror; Truth (divine) + Verdict + Echo; Truth (divine) + Sun + Prism. It grants **True Witness**: You can't be deceived by illusions of 3rd level or lower, and you have advantage on Insight checks to tell whether someone is lying.

**What the god asks.** Never tell a deliberate lie. Reveal the hidden wrongs you discover. Give honest testimony when asked.

**What makes the god take it back.** Telling a deliberate lie. Knowingly protecting a falsehood.

## What players know

Priests of Truth can tell when they are being lied to, or so everyone believes. They witness oaths, verify testimony in court, and are painfully honest company. A contract sworn in their hall is about as binding as a promise gets.

## Beliefs

- A lie is a debt that someone else always pays.
- Speak plainly, even when it costs you.
- What is hidden will be found. Better that we find it first.

## Clergy

Oath-keepers, witnesses and lie-finders. Courts call its priests to verify testimony, and contracts sworn before them are rarely broken.

## In Greenstone

A plain hall near the courts. Its priests are in demand for contracts, trials and marriages, and are invited to very few parties.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of Justice]], [[Church of Knowledge]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Disguise]]

Truth regards Disguise, in all its faces, as its natural enemy, and its priests take a personal interest in unmasking its faithful.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A priest of Truth asks the party to help unmask a fraud, then admits they cannot prove anything yet.
- Two noble houses each want the same priest to witness a treaty, and both are hiding something about it.
- A witness in an Adventure Society inquiry has been threatened into silence, and the church wants them protected.

## DM notes

- A priest of Truth is the ideal NPC to threaten a disguised villain. Expect any infiltrator in Greenstone to avoid them, and use that avoidance as a clue.
:::
