The church of **Hearth**, the god who embodies home, family, sanctuary, and the growth of new life.

- **Standing:** Humble, homely, found in every kitchen.
- **Symbol:** a flame inside a ring of stones.
- **Colours:** warm orange and cream.

## The god

Hearth is home: the fire at the centre of a household, the family around it, and the life it brings into the world. Refuge, Fertility and Chastity are faces of Hearth. Its shrines are in nearly every home.

- **What the god gives:** Hearth brings warmth, family, shelter for the lost, new life and the harvest.
- **Offended by:** Broken vows, homes destroyed, and families torn apart.

:::dm
### The dark face

Hearth is the closed door. It brings suspicion of outsiders, family feuds, and the household that will do anything, however cruel, to protect its own.

- **Will accept:** Clannishness, vendettas in defence of family, and turning away a stranger to protect the household.

### Secret motives

- Hearth protects its own household and no one else's. Its priests support whatever keeps families together, including feuds, smuggling and keeping strangers out.
- Hearth wants children born and families grown, and pushes young people into marriages they have not chosen.
:::

## Divine essence

Hearth grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers are warm to stand beside, smell of woodsmoke and bread, and every room they sit in feels like a kitchen.*

- **Essence trait:** Proficiency in Animal Handling or with cook's utensils.
- **Sense: Sense Kin.** As an action, you learn whether any member of your family or household is in danger, and in which direction, if they are within a mile.
- **Channel: Sanctuary of the Hearth.** As an action, you touch a fire or doorway, and the area within 15 feet of it becomes a sanctuary for 1 hour. A hostile creature must succeed on a Wisdom save against your stone spell DC to enter it, and creatures who rest there regain an extra hit die.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Home** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures can't be moved against their will, and have advantage on checks and saves to avoid being grappled or carried off. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *sanctuary*, *goodberry*, *create or destroy water*; level 2: *warding bond*, *prayer of healing*, *arcane lock*; level 3: *leomund's tiny hut*, *plant growth*, *glyph of warding*; level 4: *mordenkainen's private sanctum*, *guardian of faith*; level 5: *hallow*, *greater restoration*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Fire.

**Divine confluence: Homeward.** Homeward is the god of the hearth carried in a person. Bearers are warm and solid company, a room becomes a home when they sleep in it, and their magic feels like a fire on a cold night. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Hearth (divine) + Hearth + Bread; Hearth (divine) + Loom + Shepherd; Hearth (divine) + Shield + Patience. It grants **Safe at Home**: When you and your companions take a long rest in a place you have warded with a small shrine, you each regain all your spent hit dice, and the first creature to approach with hostile intent wakes you.

**What the god asks.** Keep a hearth shrine in your home. Give refuge to anyone who asks for it at your fire. Keep your vows.

**What makes the god take it back.** Breaking a marriage vow or abandoning your family. Turning away someone who asked for refuge.

## What players know

The Hearth is the god of home and family, and almost every household in Greenstone keeps a small Hearth shrine by the fire. Its priests bless births and marriages and help with the harvest. Anyone who reaches a Hearth temple and asks for refuge is protected by long tradition.

## Beliefs

- Every home is a temple, and every hearth an altar.
- Turn no guest away from your fire.
- What grows from love must be tended.

## Aspects

Hearth has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Refuge:** The Hearth as sanctuary. Anyone who reaches a Hearth temple and asks for refuge is under the god's protection.
- **Fertility:** The Hearth as the source of new life: births, harvests, and the turning of the seasons.
- **Chastity:** The Hearth as keeper of vows. Its priests bless marriages and promises of faithfulness, and expect them kept.

## Clergy

Village priests, midwives, matchmakers and keepers of sanctuary. Most of its worship happens at home, in small household shrines.

## In Greenstone

A modest temple in the working districts, and a small shrine in nearly every home. A frightened person banging on the Hearth temple's door at night is a familiar sight.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of the Healer]], [[Church of Liberty]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of War]], [[Church of Destruction]]

The Hearth wants its children home. War keeps taking them away.

## Adventure hooks

- A fugitive has claimed refuge at the Hearth temple, and the people hunting them are camped outside.
- Crops across the farmland have failed in a perfect circle, and the Hearth priests are frightened.
- A Hearth priest asks the party to find a husband who broke his vows and vanished with the family savings.

## DM notes

- Refuge is a strong storytelling tool: it lets the party shelter someone, or forces them to wait outside while their quarry hides.
