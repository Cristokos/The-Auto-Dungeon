**Bronze rank.** Human woman, daughter of [[Cressida Vane]].

- **Looks:** Young, with olive skin and long dark hair.
- **Personality:** Charming in company and bored by it.
- **Essences:** Blood, Hunt and Snake, forming the **Apex** confluence. She tracks by scent, strikes from the dark, and does not stop.
- **Ties:** [[Landemere Vane]]'s younger sister. She thinks he is soft and has said so at dinner.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Very little that is safe.

Alicia is as dangerous as her mother and more impatient. She is [[The Red Table]]'s hunter: she chooses the travellers from the caravan inns and brings them home. If the party investigates the estate, Alicia is the first one to fight them.
:::

## What people say

Lady Vane's daughter. She rides the inland road on her own and is often seen at the caravan inns, talking to travellers. People find her charming, until she's bored.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **The hunter.** She chooses the cult's prey at the caravan inns: travellers with no one waiting, workers who won't be missed. She marks them with a ribbon tied to their luggage.
- **Impatient.** She hates her mother's slow ways. If the party gets close, Alicia moves first, hunts them in the desert at night, and gives the whole thing away by doing it.
- **A first fight.** She is the first Vane the party fights. Beating her should feel like winning, until they realise Cressida let it happen.
- **A scene.** At the inn's long table Alicia is laughing with a young merchant on his first caravan run. When he gets up to fetch more wine she watches him go, then turns to the party and asks, very sweetly, where they're headed and who's expecting them.

## Playing Alicia

**Bored predator.** Charming for exactly as long as she's interested, then something else entirely.

- **Speech:** Bright, teasing and quick, going flat when she's bored
- **How to play:** Flirt, then ask who's waiting for them at home
- **Tell:** Tilts her head like a hunting cat
- **Toward the party:** Playful, then predatory
- **Wants from the party:** Prey, and the party's names
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> You're far from home. Does anyone know you're out here?
>
> Mother says I'm too impatient. Mother's slow.
>
> Run if you like. I'd prefer it.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To lead the Red Table, and not wait centuries for it
- **Driven by:** Hunger, and contempt for anyone weaker
- **Fears:** Being made to wait, and growing old like the servants
- **Bond:** Her mother, whom she fears more than she admits
- **Flaw:** Can't resist showing off her power

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Blood** (common, Blood): Vitality, wounds and the pulse. Grants **Blood Sense**. The essence of blood and the life it carries. Bearers' veins glow faintly red when their heart races, they can hear their own pulse in a quiet room, and their magic leaves a coppery scent.
- **Hunt** (common, Blood): Tracking, pursuit and the predator. Grants Proficiency in Survival or with leatherworker's tools. The essence of the hunt. Bearers notice tracks and broken twigs without trying, their eyes catch the light like a cat's, and their quarry often feels watched long before they arrive.
- **Snake** (common, Blood): Venom, guile and shedding skins. Grants Proficiency in Deception or with a poisoner's kit. The essence of the snake. Bearers' eyes take on a narrow, unblinking focus, their skin flakes away in fine sheets each season, and their voices soften into a persuasive hiss.
- **Confluence: Apex** (Blood + Hunt + Snake): The predator atop every food chain. Grants **Weakness Scent**. Blood at its most concentrated: hunger, the hunt and the kill with nothing else mixed in. Bearers move with a loose, patient economy, their pupils narrow when they are hungry, and their magic leaves a coppery taste in the air.
:::
