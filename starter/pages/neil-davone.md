#### Traits

***Quick Mending (Renewal essence).*** Once per long rest, when he spends a Hit Die to regain hit points, he regains the maximum amount for that die instead of rolling it.

***Flourish (Prosperity confluence).*** Once per long rest, when a creature he can see within 30 feet regains hit points, he can use his reaction to increase the hit points it regains by 2.

***Eyes of Opportunity.*** He can sense life force, and always knows which allies within 60 feet are wounded and roughly how badly.

***Disciple of Life.*** His healing spells of 1st level or higher restore an extra 2 + the spell's level hit points.

***Fey Ancestry.*** He has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put him to sleep.

**Iron rank.** Elf man, healer on [[Humphrey Geller]]'s team, son of the minor Davone house and a member of the [[Church of the Healer]].

- **Looks:** Young for an elf, and noticeably thicker and more muscular than most of his people, which he is tired of hearing about.
- **Essences:** Growth, Renewal and Shield, forming the **Prosperity** confluence. He heals, shields, and makes his allies bigger and stronger, and he has a knack for seeing where people are hurt and where monsters are weak.
- **Personality:** Prickly, sardonic and competent. He thinks of himself as an honourable man and, when it counts, he is right.
- **Family:** His mother, Melissa Davone, keeps the family's modest name respectable among the [[Noble and Adventuring Houses|great houses]].

:::dm
**Offers players:** Healing and grumbling, in equal measure.
:::

## What people say

A healer of real promise from a family without much else. Sharp-tongued and good at his job, and he never lets anyone forget either.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Integrity under pressure.** Neil's defining moment is standing up to his own church's local leaders when they do something wrong. Give him that moment: if the party exposes a Healer priest's corruption, Neil backs them publicly, at real cost.
- **The Mercers.** Minor families like the Davones survive by attaching their sons to greater houses. The Mercers would happily take Neil, and he would hate it.
- **Personal ties.** He has history with Cassandra Mercer (see [[House Mercer]]); whether it is over is up to you.

## Playing Neil

**Reluctant conscience.** Complains about everything, fixes everything, and does the right thing when it costs him. He would hate to hear it described that way.

- **Speech:** Dry, sarcastic and precise
- **How to play:** Heal someone while explaining at length how they could have avoided needing it
- **Tell:** Sighs before saying yes
- **Toward the party:** Unimpressed until they show they can keep themselves alive
- **Wants from the party:** Fewer injuries, and to be taken seriously as more than the healer
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Yes, I'm an elf. No, I don't want to talk about it.
>
> Hold still. Or don't, and I'll enjoy this less than you will.
>
> The church is wrong about this. Somebody has to say it.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To raise the Davone name by his own merit, not a greater house's
- **Driven by:** A stubborn sense of honour
- **Fears:** Losing a patient he could have saved
- **Bond:** [[Humphrey Geller]]'s team, and the [[Church of the Healer]] at its best
- **Flaw:** Pushes people away with his tongue

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user. His family could not afford cores; he trained.

- **Growth** (common, Life): Plants, nurture and green increase. Grants proficiency: Nature or a herbalism kit. The essence of things that grow. Moss creeps over bearers' boots if they stand still too long, flowers open where they rest, and their spells smell of fresh-cut grass.
- **Renewal** (common, Life): Healing, recovery and second chances. Grants **Quick Mending**. The essence of renewal and fresh starts. Bearers' cuts close with a faint green shimmer, they wake from any rest looking refreshed, and their magic leaves behind the smell of rain.
- **Shield** (common, Might): Protection and warding others. Grants proficiency: shields or Medicine. The essence of the shield and the one who stands in front. Bearers instinctively place themselves between danger and their companions, and a faint translucent barrier flickers around them when they cast.
- **Confluence: Prosperity** (Growth + Renewal + Shield): Thriving, sheltered and well-tended life. Grants **Flourish**. Prosperity is life growing safely behind strong walls: fields green, wounds mended and households flourishing. Bearers have a healthy, well-fed glow, plants and animals thrive in their care, and their spells shine with the gold-green light of a ripening field.
