**Landmark.** A volcanic island of black sand, glaciers and fire in the cold northern ocean. *The northern ocean.*

## What players know

Smiths come to the Emberhold for the heat of its volcanoes, and alchemists come for the hot springs. Its people are hard, patient and very good with fire.

## What mana has made of it

- Fire elementals nest in its lava tubes, and the islanders trade with them for heat.
- Glaciers here hold creatures frozen in the act of fighting, from before people came.
- The island grows each year as its volcanoes pour new land into the sea, and new land sometimes comes with new ruins.

**Dangers.** Eruptions, glacier collapses, and elementals who forget their bargains.

## DM notes

- **Hook:** A new island has risen off the coast with a door in it.
- **Secret:** The island sits on a fire elemental's astral home, and the elementals are not tenants. They are the landlords.
