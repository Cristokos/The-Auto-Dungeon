**Landmark.** A stretch of open ocean north-east of the Storm Kingdom where ships and sky ships vanish. *The western ocean.*

## What players know

Every sailor in Rimaros knows the Drowning Compass. Storms come out of a clear sky there, needles spin, and ships turn up years later, crewless, in perfect condition. The Storm Kingdom's navy patrols its edge, and its priests say the Storm god keeps something there.

## What mana has made of it

- Compasses point at the nearest source of strong magic instead of north.
- Storms here are astral: lightning that strikes a ship leaves it half in another space for a minute.
- Lost ships surface with their logbooks written in their crews' handwriting, describing voyages they never made.

**Dangers.** Astral storms, ghost ships, and the things that crew them.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** The crown offers a reward for a crew lost in the Compass, including a royal cousin.
- **Secret:** There is an island at the centre of the Compass that does not exist most of the time, and the Storm Kingdom's royal family has known about it for generations.
- **On Earth this would be** the Bermuda Triangle. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
