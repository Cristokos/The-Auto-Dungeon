**Normal rank.** Celestine woman, pit fighter at [[The Fortress]] under the name **Nightingale**, and a thief when the pits do not pay enough.

- **Looks:** Early twenties, with dark skin, long silver hair in a simple ponytail and silver eyes that match it exactly, the giveaway of a [[Peoples of Pallimustus|Celestine]]. In the pits she wears fitted leather and wraps her knuckles in cloth.
- **Essences:** One, **Swift**. She is fast enough already that most people assume she has more.
- **Fighting style:** Unarmed, evasive and precise. Her father taught her an old, rare style that nobody in the pits recognises.
- **Personality:** Guarded, proud and fiercely independent. She trusts [[Belinda]] completely and almost nobody else, and she hates being owned, protected or pitied in roughly equal measure.
- **Wants:** Out from under her father's debt, and out of the Old City's hands.

:::dm
**Offers players:** A capable ally with complicated enemies.

## What people say

The Nightingale is the crowd's favourite at the Fortress: silver hair, impossible speed, and a habit of winning fights she should lose. People say she fights to pay off a debt, and that the people who hold it are not the kind you can just pay.

## DM notes

- **Background.** She came to [[Greenstone]] as a toddler and grew up in the [[The Old City|Old City]] alongside Belinda. Her father taught her the **Way of the Reaper** (see [[The Reaper]]) without telling her what it was, then left her his debt.
- **Who wants her.** Old Silva of [[The Big Three]] protects her out of his odd sense of honour. His son Cole wants to break her. When Old Silva dies she goes to Clarissa Ventress, who shelters her mainly to infuriate Cole and is quietly arranging to hand her to [[Lucian Lamprey]].
- **Her mother** is alive. **Melody Jain** is a Silver-ranker, a master of the Way of the Reaper, and the leader of the Order of Redeeming Light within the [[Church of Purity]]. Sophie does not know any of this. Keep it for a late reveal.
- **Growing.** Her natural set would add Balance and Wind to Swift and form Mystic. Whoever gives her those essences earns a loyal, very dangerous friend.
- **Ties.** She and [[Humphrey Geller]] will clash at first: his black-and-white sense of right against her life. They may end up much closer than either expects.
- **Way of the Reaper.** Emir and Rufus are researching the lost order her father's style comes from. She is the living evidence.

## Playing Sophie

**Cornered fighter.** Trusts her fists and Belinda, in that order. Proud, wary and quick to assume the worst, because the worst usually turns up.

- **Speech:** Short, flat and dry; warms up only around Belinda
- **How to play:** Stand near the exit, arms folded, and answer questions with questions
- **Tell:** Rolls her shoulders before she decides to fight
- **Toward the party:** Suspicious; assumes they were sent by someone who wants her
- **Wants from the party:** Nothing, she says. In truth, a way out of the debt that does not mean belonging to someone else
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> I don't need saving. I need paying.
>
> Whoever sent you, tell them the answer is still no.
>
> Belinda does the talking. I do the rest.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To be free of the debt and the Old City, with Belinda beside her
- **Driven by:** Her father's debt, and the people who hold it
- **Fears:** Being owned by anyone, ever
- **Bond:** [[Belinda]], her oldest and only real friend
- **Flaw:** Refuses help even when it is clearly honest

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user, not yet Iron (one of three essences).

- **Swift** (common, Swift): Speed, reflexes and quick feet. Grants **Fleet**. The essence of speed. Bearers fidget whenever they are made to wait, leave faint afterimages when they move suddenly, and rarely seem out of breath.
