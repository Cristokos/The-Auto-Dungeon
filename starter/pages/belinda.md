**Normal rank.** Human woman, [[Sophie Wexler]]'s best friend and partner in crime. Belinda Callahan is the brains of the pair; Sophie is the fist.

- **Looks:** Short, with short scraggly hair and cute features she uses to look harmless.
- **Essences:** None yet. She plans to change that, and has opinions on exactly which ones.
- **Personality:** Quick-witted, cynical and resourceful. She plans for everything, trusts nobody but Sophie, and enjoys a good con more than she admits.
- **Skills:** Pickpocketing, working crowds, forging the occasional document, and a real talent for taking cheap magic items apart and making them do things their makers never intended.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Underworld contacts, information, and a plan when the party does not have one.
:::

## What people say

You will find her at the edge of the crowd whenever the Nightingale fights at [[The Fortress]], usually holding somebody else's purse.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Background.** She grew up with Sophie in the [[The Old City|Old City]] and has spent years trying to get them both out from under the debt and [[The Big Three]].
- **Ties.** Jory Tillman of [[Jory's Clinic]] is her ex. [[Clive Standish]] would be fascinated by her tinkering with magic items if they ever met.
- **Growing.** Her natural set is Adept, Magic and Trap, forming Charlatan: a trickster who steals power, lays traps and summons spirit doubles of herself.
- She will negotiate for Sophie, and with anyone who wants Sophie, and she is better at it than they are.

## Playing Belinda

**Street planner.** Always three moves ahead, always has an exit, and always knows who owes whom. Cheerful in a way that makes people check their purses.

- **Speech:** Quick, friendly and full of questions she already knows the answers to
- **How to play:** Smile, ask what the party wants, then name a price before they finish
- **Tell:** Fiddles with something small and magical while she thinks
- **Toward the party:** Interested; sizing up whether they are marks, buyers or a way out
- **Wants from the party:** Allies strong enough to stand between Sophie and the Big Three
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> Everyone's selling something. I just admit it.
>
> Sophie hits things. I make sure they're the right things.
>
> That's not broken, that's a feature. Give me ten minutes.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** A real set of essences, and a life where she and Sophie are not running
- **Driven by:** Getting Sophie out of the debt before it kills her
- **Fears:** Planning everything perfectly and still losing Sophie
- **Bond:** [[Sophie Wexler]], the closest thing she has to family
- **Flaw:** Cannot resist a con, even when honesty would work better

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: None yet. She holds no essences and is saving for her first.
