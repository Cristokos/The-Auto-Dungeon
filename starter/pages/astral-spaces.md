---
title: "Astral Spaces"
type: "lore"
aliases: ["Astral Space"]
---

Pocket dimensions attached to the world, each with its own rules, inhabitants and rewards. They serve as the setting's dungeons. Entrances are scattered throughout the world; the suggested starting region puts two or three within a few days' travel of [[Greenstone]].

**The gods cannot reach them.** A god's power ends at the edge of the world, and an astral space is only half in it. No god can see into one or act inside it, so a dangerous space can only be cleared by people going in. Priests keep their own divine essences inside, but they are on their own.

**What players know.** Some are charted and licensed by the [[Adventure Society]]. Some are known only to whoever found them first. A few have entrances no one has managed to close, and some have doors that no one has managed to open.

[[Emir Bahadir]] is the best-known explorer of astral spaces in the city.

> *Some astral spaces were not found. They were made, and whoever made them may not be finished with them.* (a [[Whispers|whisper]])
