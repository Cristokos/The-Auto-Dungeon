#### Traits

***Planar Sense (Astral essence).*** You always know which plane of existence you are on, and you sense the direction of any portal, planar rift or place where the boundary between planes is thin within 1 mile of you.

***Dusksight (Dark essence).*** You have darkvision out to 30 feet. If you already have darkvision, its range increases by 30 feet.

***Dead Man's Voice (Charnel confluence).*** As an action, you can make a corpse within 30 feet of you twitch, sit up, open its eyes or speak one sentence of your choice in the voice it had in life. It can't leave its place or take any other action.

***Arcane Recovery.*** Once per day after a short rest, regains the use of one spent awakening stone of 3rd level or lower.

***Umbral Snake Familiar.*** A mountain snake of living shadow rides his shoulders. It can deliver his touch spells and see in magical darkness.

***Spellcasting.*** Intelligence-based (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit). Cantrips: *chill touch*, *mage hand*, *minor illusion*, *message*. 1st level (4 slots): *detect magic*, *find familiar*, *mage armor*, *shield*. 2nd level (3 slots): *darkness*, *detect thoughts*, *see invisibility*. 3rd level (3 slots): *counterspell*, *magic circle*, *summon undead*.

***Fire Bolt (Cantrip).*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d10) fire damage.

**Bronze rank.** Human man, son of [[Cressida Vane]], and a scholar of magic.

- **Looks:** Slender and olive-skinned, with dark wavy hair. An umbral mountain snake familiar rides on his shoulders.
- **Personality:** Intense, curious and lonely. He talks about great astral beings the way other people talk about lovers.
- **Essences:** Astral, Snake and Dark, forming the **Charnel** confluence. His mountain snake familiar comes from the Snake essence, and his magic draws from the deep places between worlds.
- **Ties:** He visits the [[Magic Society]] library in Greenstone several times a year, and has corresponded with half its researchers.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Arcane knowledge, especially about the astral, and an odd, awkward friendliness.

Landemere venerates great astral beings, especially [[The Builder]], and has drifted to the edge of his family's cult. He has been attempting summoning rituals of his own. He is the easiest member of [[The Builder Cult]] for the party to meet without knowing it, and the most likely to be saved, or to doom everyone.
:::

## What people say

The Vane son who reads. He comes to the Magic Society now and then and asks very odd questions about great astral beings, then thanks everyone far too warmly and goes home to the desert.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **His rituals.** He has been trying to contact The Builder in a cellar under the estate's library. He has had answers. He doesn't know they were not from the Builder.
- **Friendship.** He is lonely. A party member who talks to him about astral magic as an equal becomes his only friend, and he will tell them far more than he should.
- **Saved or damned.** If the party brings him out of the estate before the summoning, he can tell them everything about the Red Table and the Builder cult. If they don't, he is the one who finishes the ritual.
- **A scene.** In the Magic Society reading room a slender man with a snake on his shoulders is copying a diagram of an astral being. He notices a party member looking, and asks, with sudden shy delight, whether they have ever wondered what the Builder dreams about.

## Playing Landemere

**Lonely scholar.** Earnest, awkward and brilliant, half in love with things that should not be loved.

- **Speech:** Soft, fervent and a little too formal
- **How to play:** Talk about great astral beings as if they were dear friends
- **Tell:** Strokes his snake when he is nervous
- **Toward the party:** Shy and eager, desperate to be liked
- **Wants from the party:** A friend who understands, and a way out he can't name
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Have you ever wondered what it dreams about?
>
> My mother wouldn't approve of you. I think that's why I like you.
>
> I'm so close. I can hear it. Can't you hear it?

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To meet a great astral being, and be understood by it
- **Driven by:** Loneliness, curiosity and longing
- **Fears:** Becoming what his mother and sister are
- **Bond:** His snake familiar, and his research
- **Flaw:** Mistakes being answered for being loved

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Astral** (legendary, Magic): The silver sea between worlds. Grants **Planar Sense**. Astral is the silvery void between the planes, where thought itself is a road. The bearer's eyes take on a mirror-bright sheen, and anyone who watches them sleep recognises them by the thin silver thread that seems to rise from their chest and vanish into the air.
- **Snake** (common, Blood): Venom, guile and shedding skins. Grants Proficiency in Deception or with a poisoner's kit. The essence of the snake. Bearers' eyes take on a narrow, unblinking focus, their skin flakes away in fine sheets each season, and their voices soften into a persuasive hiss.
- **Dark** (common, Dark): Shadow, concealment and the unlit hour. Grants **Dusksight**. The essence of shadow and the hush of unlit places. Bearers' pupils widen until their eyes look almost black, lamplight seems to dim a little around them, and their magic trails smoky wisps of shade.
- **Confluence: Charnel** (Astral + Snake + Dark): Dead flesh stirred by stolen life. Grants **Dead Man's Voice**. Charnel is the dark art of moving dead flesh with borrowed vitality. Bearers smell faintly of grave soil and old iron, dead insects twitch in their presence, and their spells crawl outward in sickly violet threads that tug at anything lifeless.
:::
