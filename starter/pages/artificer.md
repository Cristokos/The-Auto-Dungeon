*Inventors who channel magic through tools and objects. An artificer sees magic as a craft: their spells come from tinkered devices, and they lend that magic to the gear their friends carry.*

> A quick reference for the artificer at the table, following the layout of Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, with each feature in short. The book has the full wording.

## Core Artificer Traits

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **Primary Ability** | Intelligence |
| **Hit Point Die** | D8 per Artificer level |
| **Saving Throw Proficiencies** | Constitution and Intelligence |
| **Skill Proficiencies** | Choose 2: Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception or Sleight of Hand |
| **Weapon Proficiencies** | Simple weapons |
| **Tool Proficiencies** | Thieves' Tools, Tinker's Tools and one kind of Artisan's Tools |
| **Armor Training** | Light and Medium armor and Shields |
| **Starting Equipment** | Two simple weapons, a Light Crossbow and 20 Bolts, Studded Leather Armor or Scale Mail, Thieves' Tools and a Dungeoneer's Pack |

## Becoming a Artificer

- **As a level 1 character:** gain all the traits in the Core Artificer Traits table, and the artificer's level 1 features.
- **As a multiclass character:** gain the Hit Point Die from the table, proficiency with some of its weapons and armour as the multiclassing rules allow, and the artificer's level 1 features.

### Level 1: Magical Tinkering

Touch a Tiny nonmagical object to give it one small magical property: light, a recorded message, a lasting smell or sound, or a still image. You keep as many going as your Intelligence modifier.

### Level 1: Spellcasting

You prepare artificer spells with Intelligence, using tools as your spellcasting focus. You prepare your Intelligence modifier plus half your artificer level each long rest.

### Level 2: Infuse Item

After a long rest, turn ordinary objects into magic items with the infusions you know. An infusion lasts until you replace it, and you can keep only so many infused items at once.

### Level 3: Artificer Subclass

Choose a specialism: Alchemist, Armorer, Artillerist or Battle Smith.

### Level 3: The Right Tool for the Job

With an hour's work and tinker's tools, make one set of artisan's tools you need.

### Level 6: Tool Expertise

Double your proficiency bonus on any ability check that uses a tool.

### Level 7: Flash of Genius

Reaction: add your Intelligence modifier to an ability check or saving throw made by you or someone you can see within 30 feet. Uses equal to your Intelligence modifier per long rest.

### Level 10: Magic Item Adept

Attune to up to four magic items, and craft common and uncommon items in a quarter of the time for half the cost.

### Level 11: Spell-Storing Item

Store a 1st- or 2nd-level artificer spell in a weapon or focus. Whoever holds it can cast the spell with your numbers, a number of times equal to twice your Intelligence modifier.

### Level 14: Magic Item Savant

Attune to up to five magic items, and ignore class, species, spell and level requirements on items.

### Level 18: Magic Item Master

Attune to up to six magic items.

### Level 20: Soul of Artifice

+1 to all saving throws for each item you're attuned to. If you drop to 0 hit points, end an infusion to drop to 1 instead.

### Ability Score Improvement

At the levels the table shows, take the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat you qualify for.

## Infusions known Options

*From Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. Infused items: half as many as infusions known.*

- **Enhanced Arcane Focus**: +1 spell attacks and ignore half cover (+2 at 10th).
- **Enhanced Defense**: +1 AC for armour or a shield (+2 at 10th).
- **Enhanced Weapon**: +1 attack and damage (+2 at 10th).
- **Repeating Shot**: +1 to a ranged weapon, no loading, makes its own ammunition.
- **Replicate Magic Item**: Make a common or uncommon item from the artificer list.
- **Returning Weapon**: +1 to a thrown weapon, which flies back.
- **Homunculus Servant** *(level 2+)*: A small helper that flies and delivers touch spells.
- **Boots of the Winding Path** *(level 6+)*: Teleport back 15 feet to a spot you were in.
- **Mind Sharpener** *(level 6+)*: Reaction: turn a failed concentration save into a success.
- **Radiant Weapon** *(level 6+)*: +1 weapon that sheds light and can blind an attacker.
- **Repulsion Shield** *(level 6+)*: +1 AC; reaction pushes an attacker 15 feet.
- **Resistant Armor** *(level 6+)*: Resistance to one damage type.
- **Spell-Refueling Ring** *(level 6+)*: Once a day, recover a 3rd-level or lower slot.

## Artificer Subclasses

A artificer chooses one of these at level 3.

### Alchemist

*An expert at mixing reagents into elixirs that heal, strengthen and protect.*

| Artificer Level | Spells |
|:---:|:---|
| 3 | [[Healing Word]], [[Ray of Sickness]] |
| 5 | [[Flaming Sphere]], [[Melf's Acid Arrow]] |
| 9 | [[Gaseous Form]], [[Mass Healing Word]] |
| 13 | [[Blight]], [[Death Ward]] |
| 17 | [[Cloudkill]], [[Raise Dead]] |

#### Level 3: Tool Proficiency

Proficiency with alchemist's supplies.

#### Level 3: Alchemist Spells

You always have the Alchemist spells prepared at the levels shown.

#### Level 3: Experimental Elixir

After a long rest, make a random elixir in an empty flask (more at 6th, 9th and 15th level): healing, swiftness, resilience, boldness, flight or transformation. You can also spend a spell slot to make one you choose.

#### Level 5: Alchemical Savant

Add your Intelligence modifier to one roll of a spell's acid, fire, necrotic or poison damage, or its healing, when you cast it with alchemist's supplies.

#### Level 9: Restorative Reagents

Your elixirs also give temporary hit points (2d6 + Intelligence modifier). Cast Lesser Restoration without a slot, your Intelligence modifier times per long rest.

#### Level 15: Chemical Mastery

Resistance to acid and poison, immunity to the poisoned condition. Cast Greater Restoration and Heal once each per long rest without a slot.

### Armorer

*An artificer who rebuilds their armour into a second skin of magic.*

| Artificer Level | Spells |
|:---:|:---|
| 3 | [[Magic Missile]], [[Thunderwave]] |
| 5 | [[Mirror Image]], [[Shatter]] |
| 9 | [[Hypnotic Pattern]], [[Lightning Bolt]] |
| 13 | [[Fire Shield]], [[Greater Invisibility]] |
| 17 | [[Passwall]], [[Wall of Force]] |

#### Level 3: Tools of the Trade

Heavy armor training and proficiency with smith's tools.

#### Level 3: Armorer Spells

You always have the Armorer spells prepared at the levels shown.

#### Level 3: Arcane Armor

Turn worn armour into Arcane Armor: no Strength requirement, it covers missing limbs, it doubles as a spellcasting focus, and you don and doff it as an action.

#### Level 3: Armor Model

Pick a model each long rest. Guardian: Thunder Gauntlets (1d8 thunder, the target has disadvantage against anyone else) and a Defensive Field of temporary hit points. Infiltrator: a Lightning Launcher (1d6 lightning, 90 feet, extra 1d6 once a turn), +5 speed and advantage on Stealth.

#### Level 5: Extra Attack

Attack twice when you take the Attack action.

#### Level 9: Armor Modifications

Your armour counts as separate items for infusions (armour, boots, helmet, weapon), and you can infuse two more items as long as they are parts of it.

#### Level 15: Perfected Armor

Guardian: reaction to pull a Large or smaller creature 25 feet toward you and attack it. Infiltrator: a creature hit by your launcher glows, and the next attack on it has advantage and deals 1d6 lightning more.

### Artillerist

*A specialist in magical cannons and blasting wands.*

| Artificer Level | Spells |
|:---:|:---|
| 3 | [[Shield]], [[Thunderwave]] |
| 5 | [[Scorching Ray]], [[Shatter]] |
| 9 | [[Fireball]], [[Wind Wall]] |
| 13 | [[Ice Storm]], [[Wall of Fire]] |
| 17 | [[Cone of Cold]], [[Wall of Force]] |

#### Level 3: Tool Proficiency

Proficiency with woodcarver's tools.

#### Level 3: Artillerist Spells

You always have the Artillerist spells prepared at the levels shown.

#### Level 3: Eldritch Cannon

Make a Small or Tiny cannon that lasts an hour: a flamethrower (2d8 fire in a 15-foot cone, Dex save), a force ballista (2d8 force, pushes 5 feet) or a protector (1d8 + Int temporary hit points to those near it). Activate it as a bonus action. Once per long rest, or by spending a spell slot.

#### Level 5: Arcane Firearm

Carve a wand, staff or rod into a firearm: spells cast through it deal 1d8 extra damage on one roll.

#### Level 9: Explosive Cannon

Cannon damage rises by 1d8, and you can detonate it for 3d8 force in a 20-foot radius.

#### Level 15: Fortified Position

Run two cannons at once, and you and allies near a cannon have half cover.

### Battle Smith

*A soldier-engineer who fights beside a mechanical defender of their own making.*

| Artificer Level | Spells |
|:---:|:---|
| 3 | [[Heroism]], [[Shield]] |
| 5 | [[Branding Smite]], [[Warding Bond]] |
| 9 | [[Aura of Vitality]], [[Conjure Barrage]] |
| 13 | [[Aura of Purity]], [[Fire Shield]] |
| 17 | [[Banishing Smite]], [[Mass Cure Wounds]] |

#### Level 3: Tool Proficiency

Proficiency with smith's tools.

#### Level 3: Battle Smith Spells

You always have the Battle Smith spells prepared at the levels shown.

#### Level 3: Battle Ready

Martial weapon proficiency, and you can attack with Intelligence using magic weapons.

#### Level 3: Steel Defender

A loyal construct companion that acts on your turn: it attacks (1d8 + 2 + PB force), repairs itself, and uses its reaction to give an attacker disadvantage against someone next to it.

#### Level 5: Extra Attack

Attack twice when you take the Attack action.

#### Level 9: Arcane Jolt

Once a turn when you or your defender hit with a magic weapon or its force attack, deal 2d6 force more or heal a creature 2d6. Intelligence modifier uses per long rest.

#### Level 15: Improved Defender

Arcane Jolt becomes 4d6, the defender gets +2 AC, and its deflection deals 1d4 + Int force to the attacker.
