The All-Devouring Eye is the [[Astral Powers|great astral being]] that scholars know least about and fear most. Its realm of the astral is full of things that eat, and the few texts that mention it agree on one point: it does not hand out blessings.

Its realm borders that of [[Legion]], and the border region between them is said to breed strange things of its own.

## What it wants

Nobody knows. The few scholars who have studied the Eye's realm describe it the same way: a place where everything eats everything else, and something at the centre that is always watching and always hungry. If it wants anything, it wants to consume. Whether it hungers for worlds, for magic or for meaning, nobody has survived long enough to say.

## How it touches mortals

- **Monsters.** Creatures from its realm leak into astral spaces and, rarely, into the world. They are hungry beyond reason, and they grow when they feed. The Avatars of Doom are the worst of them.
- **Hunger.** People who stare too long at its symbols, or survive the touch of one of its creatures, sometimes come away with an appetite that never goes away: for food, for power, for knowledge.
- **The border.** Where its realm meets Legion's, life is twisted by both. Creatures from that border can wear living bodies like coats.

## Cultists and bargains

No church worships the Eye, and the few who try do not last. A scattering of nihilists and the desperate leave offerings of food and blood to its sign, a ring of teeth around an open eye, asking to be eaten last.

The Eye does not bargain. Anyone who claims to have struck a deal with it has really struck one with something that lives in its realm, and that thing will want feeding.

:::dm
**Using the Eye.** Keep it as a source of monsters rather than a character. The creatures from its realm include the **Avatars of Doom**, beings of hunger and ruin that are almost impossible to kill, and smaller horrors that can be summoned, bound or, very rarely, befriended. A familiar or summon from the Eye's realm makes a great unsettling ally for a player who wants one.

**Apocalypse cults** such as [[The Red Table]] court the beasts of the Eye's realm without understanding who they belong to. The world-eating beast the Vanes are trying to summon may be one of its creatures.

**Hook.** A merchant brings a strange black core to [[Magda Fenn]] for valuation. It is warm, it pulses, and each day it is a little heavier. Anyone who holds it for long gets very, very hungry. It came from an astral space that is growing, and something in that space has noticed the core is gone.
:::
