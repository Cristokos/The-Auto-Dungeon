#### Traits

***Winter-Blooded (Ice essence).*** You automatically succeed on saving throws against extreme cold and frigid water.

***Water-Finder (Water essence).*** You can sense the direction of fresh water within 300 feet of you and roughly how much there is, and by tasting a drop you know whether water is safe to drink.

***Rising Wave (Tsunami confluence).*** Once per long rest, as an action, you can strike a body of water you are standing in or next to and send a wave rolling out in a 15-foot cone. Each Medium or smaller creature in the water within the cone must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier) or be pushed 10 feet away from you. Small boats in the area rock hard but don't capsize.

***Lay on Hands.*** A pool of 15 hit points of healing, restored on a long rest.

***Divine Smite.*** When it hits with a melee weapon, it can spend an awakening stone use to deal an extra 9 (2d8) radiant damage.

***Warhammer.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage, plus 1d8 cold damage once per turn when he spends a spell slot.

**Iron rank.** Human man, a son of the minor Kettering house, serving on [[Thadwick Mercer]]'s team because his family needs the Mercers' favour.

- **Fighting style:** A defender with ice powers.
- **Personality:** Decent, quiet and miserable in his job.
- **Family:** His cousin Hudson Kettering is a defender on Beth Cavendish's team (see [[House Cavendish]]).
- **Looks:** Stocky and fair, with frost on his gauntlets in any weather, and a shield painted with Mercer colours he does not like.
- **Essences:** Ice, Shield and Water, forming the **Tsunami** confluence. He walls off enemies with ice and drags them off their feet with water.

:::dm
**Offers players:** An inside view of Thadwick, if they earn his trust.

Dustin will leave Thadwick's team the moment he has somewhere better to go, ideally a Geller team, where [[Phoebe Geller]] already likes him.
:::

## What people say

The Kettering boy on Thadwick Mercer's team. Everyone feels sorry for him, and nobody can work out how he puts up with it.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **His family's debt.** The Ketterings owe Beaufort Mercer a great deal of money. Dustin serves Thadwick to pay down the interest, and he knows his father will lose the family house if he leaves.
- **What he knows.** Where Thadwick goes at night, who he meets, and when the strange new strength appeared. He is frightened of what it means.
- **Getting him out.** Clearing the Kettering debt, or getting the Gellers to take it on, frees him. He will join a Geller team the same day, and [[Phoebe Geller]] will make sure of it.
- **A scene.** Dustin is outside the branch in the rain, waiting for Thadwick, who is an hour late. He nods to the party, starts to say something, and stops when a Mercer carriage turns the corner.

## Playing Dustin

**Decent man in a bad job.** Quiet, loyal, and slowly reaching the end of his patience.

- **Speech:** Quiet, careful and short
- **How to play:** Apologise for Thadwick without saying why
- **Tell:** Frost spreads across his gauntlets when he is angry
- **Toward the party:** Polite and wary, warmer in private
- **Wants from the party:** A way out that doesn't ruin his family
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Sorry about him.
>
> I can't talk here.
>
> If something happens to me, tell Phoebe it wasn't her fault.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To serve on a team he believes in, and to marry Phoebe
- **Driven by:** Duty to his family, and love
- **Fears:** His family losing everything because of him
- **Bond:** Phoebe Geller, and his cousin Hudson
- **Flaw:** Endures too long before he acts

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Ice** (common, Water): Cold, stillness and frozen clarity. Grants **Winter-Blooded**. The essence of ice. Bearers' breath mists even on warm days, their touch is always cool, and frost feathers across nearby surfaces when they cast.
- **Shield** (common, Might): Protection and warding others. Grants Proficiency with shields or in Medicine. The essence of the shield and the one who stands in front. Bearers instinctively place themselves between danger and their companions, and a faint translucent barrier flickers around them when they cast.
- **Water** (common, Water): Flow, adaptation and patient force. Grants **Water-Finder**. The essence of water in all its patience. Bearers are rarely flustered, droplets bead on their skin even in dry air, and their magic sounds like distant rain.
- **Confluence: Tsunami** (Ice + Shield + Water): The sea rising with crushing force. Grants **Rising Wave**. The tsunami is the ocean's full strength, the wall of water that rises without warning and sweeps everything before it. Bearers carry a heavy, rolling stillness and the water in nearby cups trembles when they are angry, and their spells surge outward like a breaking wave.
