**Landmark.** A chain of black islands in the far northern sea, crowned with natural towers of iron-grey stone. *Far north.*

## What players know

Ships that sail the northern sea give the Spires a wide berth. On clear nights, lights move between the towers, and the wind through them sounds like a choir singing one long note.

## What mana has made of it

- The stone is naturally warded: divination and teleportation magic fail within sight of the towers.
- Lodestone veins pull iron weapons out of their sheaths when the aurora is bright.
- The towers grow, a finger's breadth a year, and nobody has found the top of the tallest.

**Dangers.** Ice storms, sea monsters in the straits, and things that nest in the towers.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A ship's captain will pay handsomely for a team to retrieve cargo from a wreck on the Spires before anyone asks what the cargo was.
- **Secret:** The towers are the antennae of something buried in the islands, and the choir note is it calling out to its own kind.
- **On Earth this would be** the islands of Novaya Zemlya. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
