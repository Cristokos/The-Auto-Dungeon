**Silver rank.** Human woman, Lady of [[House Vane]] and mother of [[Landemere Vane]] and [[Alicia Vane]].

- **Looks:** A woman in her early thirties, with olive skin and dark hair, dressed in old-fashioned elegance.
- **Personality:** Gracious, unhurried and impossible to read. She is a perfect hostess.
- **Essences:** Nobody in the city knows. She has never been seen to use a power, and she changes the subject when asked.
- **Ties:** Widow of the late Lord Vane, by her own account. Mother of [[Landemere Vane]] and [[Alicia Vane]].

:::dm
**Offers players:** Hospitality on the desert road, and an invitation to dine at the estate.

Cressida leads [[The Red Table]], the cannibal blood cult at the heart of the house. She is far older than she looks; the cult's rites keep her young. Never let her be seen doing anything wrong. Her menace is that she is so pleasant, and that the guests at her table are not always the ones eating. See [[House Vane]].
:::

## What people say

Lady Vane of the desert estate. She hardly ever comes to the city, but when she does, she gives the best dinners anyone has been to. Guests say the meat is unlike anything they've tasted.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Her powers.** Blood, Feast and Hunger, forming the **Lifeblood** confluence. She is a warlock, and her patron is the red rite itself.
- **Never caught.** Cressida does not do anything wrong where anyone can see. Every violent act on the estate is done by servants, her daughter or the cult. She does the hosting.
- **The dinner.** If the party accepts her invitation, dinner is perfect, the conversation is delightful, and one of the other guests is gone in the morning. She will be sorry to hear it.
- **Her age.** She may be Ambrose Vane's own daughter. Old caravan ledgers in the inns carry her signature in the same hand, two centuries apart.
- **A scene.** The Vane carriage stops at a caravan inn where the party is resting. Cressida steps down, asks the innkeeper after his children by name, and then invites the party to dine at the estate on Thursday. She will not take no for an answer, charmingly.

## Playing Cressida

**Perfect hostess.** Gracious, warm and utterly in control, with a cold, very old appetite behind the smile.

- **Speech:** Soft, warm and old-fashioned
- **How to play:** Make the party comfortable, then ask about someone they love
- **Tell:** Her eyes linger on throats
- **Toward the party:** Charming and generous, like a farmer with good stock
- **Wants from the party:** Guests, and the party at her table
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> You must stay for dinner. I insist.
>
> The desert is so lonely. We do so enjoy visitors.
>
> My daughter will show you to your rooms.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To see the summoning finished, and to be spared and rewarded when it is
- **Driven by:** Appetite, and the long habit of power
- **Fears:** Growing old
- **Bond:** Her children, after a fashion, and the Red Table
- **Flaw:** Sees everyone as meat, and sometimes lets it show

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Blood** (common, Blood): Vitality, wounds and the pulse. Grants **Blood Sense**. The essence of blood and the life it carries. Bearers' veins glow faintly red when their heart races, they can hear their own pulse in a quiet room, and their magic leaves a coppery scent.
- **Feast** (uncommon, Life): Celebration, nourishment and shared plenty. Grants **Hearty Meal**. Feast is the joy of a full table and good company. Bearers carry the faint smell of fresh bread and roast meat, and the meals they cook or share leave people warm, rested and in good spirits.
- **Hunger** (uncommon, Blood): Appetite that is never sated. Grants **Devour**. Hunger is endless appetite, the drive to consume and take in. Bearers have a hollow look about the eyes and a faint pull on nearby magic, and they will eat almost anything without complaint.
- **Confluence: Lifeblood** (Blood + Feast + Hunger): A strong heart with blood to spare. Grants **Share the Blood**. Vitality doubled and fed by the green strength of growing things: a heart that beats slow, deep and tireless. Bearers have ruddy cheeks and warm hands, regain their colour quickly after hardship, and their healing magic flows like a warm pulse.
