**Landmark.** A vast horseshoe waterfall whose mist is so thick with magic it glows at night. *The north-west continent.*

## What players know

Pilgrims come to the Thunderveil to stand in the spray and ask for courage. Behind the falling water is a cave the size of a cathedral, and in it a market that only opens on stormy nights.

## What mana has made of it

- At dawn, for a few minutes, the falls run upward.
- The mist carries sound for miles: a whisper behind the falls can be heard on the far shore.
- Anyone who goes over the falls and lives comes out with a single strand of white hair and a habit of hearing thunder no one else hears.

**Dangers.** The river's current, lightning-touched water elementals, and smugglers who use the storm market.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** The storm market's broker wants a monster core of a kind nobody has seen before, and is paying in favours.
- **Secret:** The cave behind the falls is the mouth of an astral space that opens only when the falls run upward.
- **On Earth this would be** Niagara Falls. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
