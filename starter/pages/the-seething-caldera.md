**Landmark.** A great volcanic basin of hot springs, geysers and bubbling mud, with mana-rich vapour in the air. *The north-west continent.*

## What players know

The Caldera is famous for its geysers of liquid mana that burst on a schedule only the local hermits seem to know. Spirit coin farms ring its edges, and so do the people who guard them.

## What mana has made of it

- Some springs bubble with liquid magic in colours that match the essences' affinities: a fire-red pool, a water-blue one, a pool of pure dark.
- Elixirs brewed with caldera water are a step stronger, and a step less stable.
- Bison here grow stone hides and walk through boiling water.

**Dangers.** Scalding pools, thin crusts over boiling mud, and the mana storms that come when too many geysers blow at once.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A spirit coin farm has started minting coins of the wrong rank, and its owners want to know why before their rivals do.
- **Secret:** The whole basin is a sleeping volcano of pure mana. If it ever wakes, it will flood a continent with monsters.
- **On Earth this would be** Yellowstone. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
