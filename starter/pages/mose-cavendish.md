#### Traits

***Kindle (Fire essence).*** At will, as an action, you can light or snuff out a nonmagical flame no larger than a campfire within 10 feet of you.

***Weathervane (Wind essence).*** You always know which way the wind is blowing, and you can accurately predict the weather where you are for the next 24 hours.

***Starburst (Nova essence).*** Once per long rest, as an action, you release a silent flash of starlight. Until the end of your next turn, every other creature within 30 feet of you is outlined in faint light and gains no benefit from being invisible or hidden.

***Furnace Gust (Firestorm confluence).*** Once per long rest, as an action, you can exhale a blast of scorching wind in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the cone must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier) or be pushed 10 feet away from you. Unprotected flames in the cone flare high and go out, and loose papers and other light objects are blown away. The gust deals no damage.

***Arcane Recovery.*** Once per day after a short rest, regains the use of one spent awakening stone of 3rd level or lower.

***Spellcasting.*** Intelligence-based (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit). Cantrips: *fire bolt*, *gust*, *mage hand*, *thunderclap*. 1st level (4 slots): *burning hands*, *mage armor*, *magic missile*, *shield*. 2nd level (3 slots): *scorching ray*, *gust of wind*, *flaming sphere*.

**Iron rank.** Elf man, cousin of [[Beth Cavendish]] and the glass cannon of her team, with fire and wind magic.

- **Personality:** Cheerful, reckless and very loud in a fight.
- **Looks:** Lanky and copper-haired for an elf, with singed eyebrows, a grin that never quite goes away, and a robe with more burn holes than cloth.
- **Essences:** Fire, Wind and Nova, forming the **Firestorm** confluence. He doesn't so much cast spells as set them off.
- **Ties:** [[Beth Cavendish]]'s cousin, and the one family member who can make [[Isadora Cavendish]] smile.

:::dm
**Offers players:** A friendly rival who likes nothing better than comparing explosions.

Mose hits harder than anyone on Beth's team and goes down faster. If the party ever needs to rescue a Cavendish, it will be Mose.
:::

## What people say

Beth Cavendish's cousin, the loud one. If there's an explosion on a contract and nobody's dead, it was probably Mose, and he will tell you about it.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Glass cannon.** He spends his biggest spell in the first round of every fight and is out of mana by the third. Beth has tried to fix this for years.
- **Rivalry.** He picks one party caster and keeps score. If they ever beat his number he buys the drinks and demands to know how.
- **Rescue.** When Mose goes down on a contract, he goes down hard: alone, out of mana and somewhere he shouldn't have run to. The party may be the only team close enough.
- **A scene.** Mose bursts into the branch, smoking slightly, and announces to the whole hall that he has killed a monster with one spell. Beth comes in behind him and corrects him: it was two spells, and he set the barn on fire.

## Playing Mose

**Happy explosion.** Cheerful, loud and reckless, and the first to cheer someone else's good hit.

- **Speech:** Loud, fast and full of noises for things exploding
- **How to play:** Ask a party caster how big their biggest spell is
- **Tell:** Little sparks pop from his fingers when he's excited
- **Toward the party:** Instantly friendly and competitive
- **Wants from the party:** A score to beat, and someone to show off to
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Did you see that? Did everyone see that?
>
> Beth says I'm not allowed to do that inside.
>
> What's your biggest one? Mine's bigger. Probably. Let's find out.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To be the strongest caster in Greenstone
- **Driven by:** Joy in his own power, and love for his cousin
- **Fears:** Being the reason Beth gets hurt
- **Bond:** Beth, and his grandmother Isadora
- **Flaw:** Never holds anything back, even when he should

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Fire** (common, Fire): Heat, flame and fierce energy. Grants **Kindle**. The essence of living flame. Bearers run warm to the touch, ember glints flicker in their hair, and their spells crackle and smell of woodsmoke.
- **Wind** (common, Wind): Moving air, freedom and open skies. Grants **Weathervane**. The essence of restless air. A breeze seems to follow bearers indoors, their hair and cloaks are always stirring, and their spells arrive on a sudden gust.
- **Nova** (legendary, Fire): A star's final, blinding brilliance. Grants **Starburst**. Nova is a star's last blinding moment, when it outshines everything around it before it fades. The bearer's skin seems lit from within whenever they are angry or afraid, and those who see that glow recognise a fire that could be spent all at once.
- **Confluence: Firestorm** (Fire + Wind + Nova): Scorching winds that feed the flames. Grants **Furnace Gust**. The firestorm, a blaze so great that it breathes its own howling wind. Bearers have ember-hot breath and singed eyebrows, loose cloth and hair stream around them, and their magic roars out like a furnace door thrown open.
