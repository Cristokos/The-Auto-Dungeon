**Great city.** The City of Flowers, capital of the kingdom of Estercost: a vast, rich magical metropolis on the northern continent, far across the sea from [[Greenstone]]. A city of flowering towers, campuses and specialist shops, where the best magical research in the known world is done, and home of the famous Remore Academy.

## What players know

To most people in Greenstone, Vitesse is half a legend: the place where the finest enchanters trained, where the rarest components can be bought, and where every tower is draped in flowers. People who have actually been there say the legend undersells it.

- **The flowering towers.** The skyline is a forest of slender towers, each one hung with living flowers from foot to crown. They bloom all year, in colours that change with the season and, some say, with the mood of the city. At dusk the petals glow.
- **The campuses.** Whole districts are given over to research: walled campuses of libraries, workshops and testing grounds, each with its own speciality and its own rivalries. The [[Magic Society]]'s branch here is one of its greatest, and scholars from Greenstone dream of a posting to Vitesse.
- **The specialist shops.** Streets of shops that each sell one thing: core-ground inks, astral glass, spell-tuned instruments, essence-grade reagents. If a component exists, someone in Vitesse sells it, at a price.
- **High magic.** The magic in Vitesse runs strong. Low-rank monsters spawn rarely, but when they do it is in huge herds, so Iron and Bronze-rankers are never sent out alone: they go in supervised groups with Silver-rank escorts. Many ambitious young adventurers leave for lower-magic cities just to be allowed to fight on their own.
- **The Remore Academy.** The most famous adventurer academy in the world, run by [[House Remore]]. Kings and queens send their children to it. See [[House Remore]].
- **The guilds.** Vitesse is home to elite adventuring guilds, the best known being the Burning Violet Guild, whose members are the elite of the elite.
- **The adventurers.** Vitesse is a magnet for high-rank adventurers, who come for gear, for research, and for the contracts the campuses post to fetch rare materials from dangerous places. Its Adventure Society branch is large, busy and used to Silver and Gold rankers walking through the door.
- **The river.** A great river runs through the city, and its gardens, terraces and canals draw on it. The farmland around Vitesse is green and rich for days in every direction.
- **The life.** Vitesse is leisurely: culture, cuisine, and money at every level. A labourer here earns what a craftsman does in Greenstone. Fashions run to earthy, sober clothes, with the odd startling exception, like the current craze for hair dyed every colour of the rainbow. The flower wine festival is the event of the year.

> *Greenstone fights monsters. Vitesse studies them.* (said in both cities, with different tones)

## Who rules it

Vitesse is the capital of **Estercost**, a kingdom with a royal family of its own. The crown princess grew up alongside the children of the great houses, [[Rufus Remore]] among them.

## Why adventurers go there

- To buy what cannot be found anywhere else: rare awakening stones, high-grade gear, and components for rituals.
- To have something identified, repaired or studied that no one at home can explain.
- To take research contracts: escorting scholars into astral spaces, fetching living samples, or testing new magic in the field.
- To meet people. Vitesse is where powerful people pass through, and where a team can get noticed.

## Places in the city

- **The Flowering Towers**, the oldest quarter, where the first towers were raised and the flowers first planted.
- **The campus quarter**, a maze of walled research grounds, including the [[Magic Society]]'s branch.
- **The Market of One Thing**, the specialist shops, street after street.
- **The Society hall**, the Adventure Society's branch, built to handle high-rank teams and big contracts.
- **The river terraces**, gardens and canals where the city's wealthy live and its festivals are held.
- **The Remore Academy**, a campus of its own, with training grounds famous across the world.
- **The Pastry Stash**, a patisserie that has nothing to do with magic and is still the most recommended shop in the city.

## Nearby

- Rich farmland, vineyards and flower fields in every direction, and the sea a few days' travel away.
- Vitesse's alchemists import salt from [[The Salt Mirror]], far to the south-east, and prize it above any other.

## DM notes

**Fixed points.** Vitesse is the City of Flowers and the capital of Estercost, a high-magic city with a royal family, the Remore Academy and the Burning Violet Guild, where low-rank adventurers are kept on a short leash. Keep these. The districts, campuses and other details are yours to change.

**Who runs it.** The Estercost crown, with the great houses, the guilds and the research campuses as loud voices. The Remores are not royalty, but a family that has trained half the world's rulers has a great deal of influence.

**Rank.** Vitesse suits Silver-rank play and above. An Iron-rank team that reaches it should feel very small.

**Hooks.**
- A campus hires the party to bring back a living monster from the delta near Greenstone, alive and unharmed, by a deadline.
- The flowers on one tower have started to wilt, and the tower's owner is offering a fortune to find out why before the rest of the city notices.
- A rival campus is buying up every core of one kind in the market, and nobody knows what they are building.
- A new way of sending messages across great distances is being developed in Vitesse, and several powers want it stopped, stolen or controlled.
