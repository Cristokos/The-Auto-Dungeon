An adventuring family of [[Vitesse]], famous across the world for training others. The Remores run the **Remore Academy**, the most renowned adventurer academy there is: great houses pay fortunes to have their children taught there, and so do kings and queens.

## What players know

- **The academy.** Founded by Roland Remore, a Diamond-ranker, and run by his family ever since. It teaches combat, magic, mana control, tactics, and above all standards.
- **The graduates.** Remore-trained adventurers are everywhere, and they recognise each other. Many of them now run teams, guilds or countries.
- **"My family's school."** Every Remore talks about the academy constantly. People who travel with one learn to make a game of it.

## Founder

The house was founded by **Roland Remore**, a Diamond-rank adventurer who decided, after a lifetime of watching promising young adventurers die of bad habits, that someone had to teach them properly. He opened the academy in [[Vitesse]] with a handful of students and a training yard, and has run it ever since. The family's standing in Vitesse came from the academy, not the other way round: the crown sent its own children there, and the Remores became a great house because everyone else's great houses owed them.

## Key people

- [[Roland Remore]] (Diamond), the founder. Very old, very strong, and very used to getting his way.
- [[Gabriel Remore]] (Gold), Roland's son. Whimsical and theatrical: he jumps from a ship onto the dock before it has finished docking, and once did an unscheduled fire dance at a graduation. Fire is his element.
- [[Arabelle Remore]] (Gold), Gabriel's wife: a retired adventurer of the [[Church of the Healer]], with healing and life-draining powers, who now treats the minds of adventurers as well as their bodies.
- [[Rufus Remore]] (Bronze), their son, adventuring abroad with his own team.
- [[Gwydion Remore]], Rufus's brother.

Gabriel and Arabelle adventured in a famous Gold-rank team with [[Emir Bahadir]] and Callum Morse, an assassination specialist.

## Interests and income

- **Tuition.** The academy's fees are enormous, and great houses, guilds and royal families pay them gladly. A single year for a noble heir costs more than most Iron-rankers earn in a decade.
- **Training contracts.** Kingdoms, churches and Adventure Society branches hire Remore instructors to assess and train their own adventurers, or send whole cohorts to Vitesse for a season.
- **Graduates' gratitude.** Successful graduates send gifts, rare materials, essences and favours back to the academy. Many leave it something in their wills.
- **Interests.** Standards. The Remores want adventurers everywhere to be better trained, and they want everyone to know where the good ones learned it.

## DM notes

- **In Greenstone.** The family has no seat in [[Greenstone]], but Rufus's team has come to the city on contract, and Greenstone's low training standards are exactly the kind of thing a Remore cannot leave alone. A Society branch that asks Rufus to run its field assessments gets more honesty than it bargained for.
- **Old friends.** Emir Bahadir will do a great deal for Gabriel and Arabelle's son. Callum Morse will do much less, and will use anyone as bait if it gets him what he wants.
:::
