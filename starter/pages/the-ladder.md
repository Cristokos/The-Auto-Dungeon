A blood and rank cult that promises Iron-rankers a fast path to Bronze.

| Wants | Holds power through | Default view of a new Iron-ranker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A way past the Silver ceiling for its leader | Ambition, real results, a respected leader | A recruit, or fuel |

## Patron, want, cover, weakness

- **Patron.** Its leader: respected, generous, and a core user who wants a way past the Silver ceiling.
- **Want.** To perfect a ritual that draws on other people's essences, and use it to climb.
- **Cover.** A mentoring circle for ambitious young adventurers. Its members really do rank up faster.
- **Weakness.** Every fast climb is paid for by someone else, and the people who paid for it are weaker afterwards in ways a healer can see.

## Structure

The Ladder is small on purpose. It has never had more than a dozen members at a time.

- **The Rungmaster.** Corwin Hale, a respected Silver-ranker who leads it.
- **The Hands.** Two or three trusted Bronze-rankers who recruit, organise the rites and keep the members in line. Imogen Slate is the senior one.
- **The Climbers.** Ambitious Iron-rankers, invited one at a time, who meet weekly for training and talk. Most of them have no idea where their progress really comes from.
- **The Rungs.** The people the rite draws on: drunk adventurers, debtors, wanderers and, increasingly, Climbers who have stopped being useful. They wake tired and weakened, and never quite recover.

## How it operates

- **Invitation.** Imogen watches the branch for talented, frustrated Iron-rankers, the kind who are passed over or held back. She becomes their friend, and introduces them to Corwin.
- **Mentoring.** Corwin trains Climbers properly, and well. This part is real. It is why his recommendations carry weight with [[Ulric Stane]] and the rank assessors.
- **The rite.** Once a month, Climbers drink a cup of wine during a meditation in which they are told to focus on their essences. The wine carries the drawn power of the Rungs. Climbers gain faster, and believe it is the training.
- **The price.** Climbers who ask questions are moved down the Ladder. They become Rungs themselves.

## Signs

- A cluster of young adventurers in the branch have ranked up unusually fast, and they all speak of the same mentor.
- [[Brother Ilian Marsh]] has treated several people with a deep, unexplained weakness, as if part of their essences had been worn thin.
- A former Climber, now oddly frail, is drinking himself to death in a dockside tavern and will not say why.
- Corwin's endorsement appears on more and more rank-up recommendations.

## Lair: the Ladder Hall

Corwin rents a former guild hall in a respectable street near the branch. The ground floor is a genuine training hall, with mats, target dummies and a library. Beneath it is the rite chamber: a round room with a carved ladder on the floor and a shallow channel running to a basin at its centre. The cellar also holds two rooms with locked doors, where Rungs who might talk are kept sedated. A secret passage leads to the next street.

## Members

- **Corwin Hale** (Silver), the Rungmaster. Late fifties, grey-bearded and kind, with a famous adventuring career behind him and a reputation as the best mentor in the city. He is a core user, stuck at Silver for twenty years. He has told himself the Ladder is temporary. It has been temporary for six years.
- **Imogen Slate** (Bronze), the recruiter. Bright, funny and attentive, the best friend a lonely Iron-ranker has ever had. She was a Climber once, and was the first to work out the truth. She chose to stay.
- **Pip Dorrance** (Bronze), the Ladder's success story: an Iron-ranker who made Bronze in a single season. He is starting to suspect, and he is terrified. He is the cult's weak point, and the party's best way in.

- **Blood** (common, Blood): Vitality, wounds and the pulse. Grants **Blood Sense**. The essence of blood and the life it carries. Bearers' veins glow faintly red when their heart races, they can hear their own pulse in a quiet room, and their magic leaves a coppery scent.
- **Soul** (legendary, Light): The undying spark within every life. Grants **Soulsight**. Soul is the self beneath the flesh, the spark that outlasts the body. A warm glow shows faintly around the bearer's heart in darkness, and priests recognise them as someone who looks past faces to what lies beneath.
- **Vessel** (rare, Magic): Containment, channelling and being filled. Grants **Held Draught**. The essence of the cup, the urn and the body that holds something greater than itself. Bearers have an echoing hollowness to their voice, and when they drink or cast a spell, a faint glow shows through their skin like light through paper.
- **Confluence: Covenant** (Blood + Soul + Vessel): Sacred pacts sealed in blood. Grants **Threshold Ward**. The covenant is a promise made holy, its words written in runes and sealed with blood so that the bond itself keeps watch. Bearers carry a thin pale line across one palm like an old cut, and their magic is written in short lines of glowing script that fade once fulfilled.

- **Crown** (legendary, Might): Sovereignty, rightful rule and command. Grants **Uncrowned Royalty**. Crown is rule itself, the weight of the right to command that kingdoms are built around.
