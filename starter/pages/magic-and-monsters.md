Magic is part of the environment. It pools in certain places, called natural arrays, and those concentrations spawn monsters. Monsters are therefore a renewable hazard of geography, not an ecology: a region with strong ambient magic always produces them.

- **[[Monster Surges|Monster surge]].** When magic builds up across a region, spawns intensify and escalate over weeks, ending in powerful creatures.
- **[[Astral Spaces]].** Pocket dimensions attached to the world, each with its own rules, inhabitants and rewards. They serve as the setting's dungeons.
- **Monster ranks.** Monsters carry the same [[Ranks]] as people, so a contract's rank tells adventurers what to expect.

## Daily life

Most people are Normal rank and live ordinary lives, protected by those who are not. Essence users age slowly, so senior figures can be centuries old while looking middle-aged. Power and politics are entangled: a Gold-rank adventurer is a political actor whether they want to be or not.

Magic is industrialized in cities. Expect enchanted infrastructure, magical lighting, cloud-based constructions (buildings and furniture made of shaped, solid cloud substance), and skilled crafters who turn monster parts into goods.
