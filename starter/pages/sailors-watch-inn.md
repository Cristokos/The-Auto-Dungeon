**Inn.** A comfortable inn at the very edge of [[The Island]], with exceptional ocean views. Visiting adventurers who can afford it rent suites here by the month.

## What players know

- **The rooms.** Suites of two or three rooms with balconies over the sea. A three-room suite costs about what an Iron-rank team earns in a good month.
- **The guests.** Bronze-rank teams on long contracts, merchants, and the occasional foreign envoy. [[Rufus Remore]]'s team keeps a suite here while it is in the city.
- **The bar.** A quiet, expensive bar where Society officials drink after work and talk more than they should.

:::dm
**Hook.** A guest in the next suite has not come out for three days, and the staff are afraid to open the door because of the heavy, cold pressure they feel through it.
:::
