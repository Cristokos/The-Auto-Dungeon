The setting's best-known source of zealotry: its faithful see some magic, such as necromancy or dark affinities, as corruption to be purged. A character with [[Dark essence|Dark]], [[Blood essence|Blood]] or [[Sin essence|Sin]] essences is a natural target of its suspicion. It also treats the soul residue of [[Two Paths to Rank|core users]] as a mark of weakness or corruption, and can always tell.

- **Standing:** Loud, powerful and rising.
- **Symbol:** a white flame inside a ring.
- **Colours:** white and gold.

## The god

Purity is the state of being unmixed and uncorrupted. That applies to a body free of disease, a soul free of taint, and magic free of anything that does not belong in it. Purity makes no distinction between cleaning a wound and cleansing a village.

- **What the god gives:** Purity cures poison and disease, burns out corruption, and protects the world from things that would rot it from within.
- **Offended by:** Corruption, mixture and compromise. Necromancy and the undead most of all.

:::dm
### The dark face

Purity is intolerance made divine. Whatever is "impure" (a mixed bloodline, a dark affinity, the residue cores leave on a soul) can be scoured away, and the person attached to it often goes too.

- **Will accept:** Purges, inquisitions and burnings, as long as the result is cleaner than what came before.

### Secret motives

- Purity's idea of a clean world has no room for anything mixed: not mixed bloodlines, not core residue, not outworlders who do not belong here.
- Purity is content for its church to gain political power, because power lets it decide who is impure.
- Purity regards most other churches as compromised, and would see several of them cleansed if it ever had the chance.
:::

## Divine essence

Purity grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers are spotless whatever they have been through, smell faintly of clean linen, and their magic burns white.*

- **Essence trait:** Pure Blood. You are immune to disease and have advantage on saving throws against poison.
- **Sense: Sense Taint.** As an action, you sense undead, fiends, curses, poison, disease, and the soul residue of core users within 60 feet until the end of your next turn.
- **Channel: Cleansing Fire.** When you hit a creature with an attack, you can deal an extra 2d8 radiant damage (3d8 against undead, fiends, and creatures with a Dark, Blood or Sin essence). Alternatively, as an action, you touch a creature and end one poison, disease or curse on it.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Purity** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures have advantage on saving throws against poison, disease, being charmed and being possessed. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *detect poison and disease*, *protection from evil and good*, *searing smite*; level 2: *lesser restoration*, *branding smite*, *see invisibility*; level 3: *remove curse*, *dispel magic*, *blinding smite*; level 4: *banishment*, *aura of purity*; level 5: *dispel evil and good*, *flame strike*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Light.

**Divine confluence: Inquisitor.** An Inquisitor sees taint the way others see dirt. Bearers' eyes burn white when they look at the corrupted, nothing impure can hide in their presence, and their magic leaves the air scrubbed clean. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Purity (divine) + Light + Verdict; Purity (divine) + Fire + Crucible; Purity (divine) + Vigil + Glass. It grants **Purging Gaze**: You can always see the soul residue of core users and the taint of Dark, Blood and Sin essences on anyone within 30 feet, and creatures with them have disadvantage on Deception checks against you.

**What the god asks.** Cleanse the corruption you find. Never absorb a monster core. Never take a Dark, Blood or Sin essence.

**What makes the god take it back.** Absorbing a monster core, or a Dark, Blood or Sin essence. Showing mercy to corruption.

## What players know

The Church of Purity is louder than the other churches. It has also changed: about five hundred years ago a disaster wiped out most of its clergy, and the church that rebuilt itself afterwards slowly turned away from many of its old ways. Its faithful believe some kinds of magic, such as darkness, blood and death, are a corruption that should be cleansed. Those with such essences are wise to know where the Church's people gather, and how warmly they are likely to be received.

## Beliefs

- Corruption spreads. What is tainted must be cleansed before it touches others.
- Some magic is a sickness of the soul: darkness, blood, death, and the residue that cores leave behind.
- Mercy for the corrupted is cruelty to everyone else.

## Clergy

Preachers, confessors and inquisitors. Its **Cleansers** are trained to read the soul, and they can always spot the residue left on [[Two Paths to Rank|core users]].

## In Greenstone

A gleaming white temple in the [[The Temple District|Temple District]] on the Island, and preachers in the market most days. Its congregation is growing.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of Justice]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Undeath]], [[Church of Liberty]], [[Church of the Revels]]

Purity regards the Revels as decadent and Liberty as naive. Liberty, in turn, regards Purity as the most dangerous church in Greenstone.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A Cleanser has taken an interest in one of the party and starts turning up wherever they go.
- The Church offers a generous contract to clear a "nest of corruption" in the marshes. The nest turns out to be a village.
- A young acolyte of Purity asks the party, in secret, whether everything the Church teaches is true.

## DM notes

- **Tension.** Zealots want dark, blood and death-themed powers restricted or purged; their users want to be left alone. See the *Church of Purity* and *Dark essences* settings in [[Political Dials]] (recommended: rising, and distrusted).
- Purity is central to the campaign. Its true aims and how far its zealots will go are yours to set.
