Each god has a church with real divine backing, so religious politics carry teeth. The [[Church of Purity]] is the setting's best-known source of zealotry. The [[Church of the Healer]] is the most trusted, running clinics and sanctuaries everywhere. The [[Church of Undeath]] is outlawed in [[The Storm Kingdom]]. Slavery is not: it is legal there, and [[Church of Dominion|Dominion]] claims it as one of his aspects.

| Wants | Holds power through | Default view of a new Iron-ranker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faith spread, their god's interests served | Healing, sanctuaries, congregations, divine favor | Soul to win or heretic to watch |

**What players know.** Every god has its faithful in Greenstone. The healing churches run clinics and are generally welcome everywhere. Some churches keep grand temples, others only a shrine in a kitchen or a coin behind a loose brick.
