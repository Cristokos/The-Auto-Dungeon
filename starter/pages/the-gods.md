Each god is the living embodiment of a single concept, and each has a church that speaks for it. The gods act through priests, churches and occasionally avatars. They are bound by rules among themselves and rarely act directly.

**What players know.** The gods of Pallimustus are real, and they are not distant. Priests heal wounds, bless ventures, and sometimes receive answers. The gods are said to be bound by rules among themselves, which is perhaps why they act through mortals so often.

## What a god is

A god is the physical embodiment of a single concept. Each god simply *is* its concept, and its church speaks for it.

:::dm
Gods are not good or evil. Each god is its concept with both of the faces that concept has, and the dark face is rarely spoken of. Players should discover it through play.

- **The Healer** wants wounds healed, and is just as content for wounds to be made, because without wounds there is no healing.
- **Dominion** is rule and the right to command, so the ownership of people sits comfortably within his concept.
- **Liberty** frees the slave and the murderer alike. **Truth** will expose a lie that was keeping someone alive.

A god supports anything that serves its concept, and opposes whatever denies it. Gods clash not because one is good and the other evil, but because their concepts collide: Liberty and Dominion, Death and Undeath, Truth and Disguise.

Churches are mortal interpretations. Most preach the bright face of their god and play down the dark one, but the god accepts both. A priest who serves the dark face is no less faithful than one who serves the bright face. Each church page has the god's dark face and secret motives in DM mode.
:::

## Old rivalries

- **Death and Undeath**: the end, and the refusal to accept it.
- **Liberty and Dominion**: the open hand against the crown and the chain.
- **Truth and Disguise**: the lantern against the mask and the lie.
- **Purity and the Revels**: denial against indulgence.
- **Earth and Storm**: land and sky, endlessly at odds.
- **Destruction** against everything that builds, heals or protects.

## DM notes

- Disguise, Purity, the Healer, the Hero, Knowledge, Dominion, Liberty, Death and Undeath are the plot gods. Their pages keep the player-facing story thin so you can decide their true roles.
- The gods sit below the [[Astral Powers]] in the cosmic order. See [[Cosmology]].
- **Gods cannot act inside astral spaces.** A god's reach ends at the edge of its world, and an astral space is not fully part of it. Priests keep their divine essences there, but the god itself cannot see in or intervene, which is why astral spaces need adventurers to clear them.
:::
