A charity in [[The Old City]] that feeds the poor every night. It is a front for the outlawed [[Church of Undeath]].

## Patron, want, cover, weakness

- **Patron.** The god of the [[Church of Undeath]].
- **Want.** To grow a congregation in Greenstone without drawing the [[Church of Purity]].
- **Cover.** A well-run soup kitchen with friendly volunteers, praised by people who should know better.
- **Weakness.** Some of the poor stop coming home. Their friends notice, even when nobody else does.

## How it operates

The Supper takes in the dying no one will miss, eases their last days, and raises them afterwards as quiet servants who work its cellars and carry its messages. Its volunteers mostly believe they are doing good, and on most nights they are.

## Structure

- **The Mother.** Mother Hesper Gale runs the kitchen and the congregation. Everyone in the Old City knows her as a saint.
- **The Cellarer.** Absalom Crane, a necromancer, lives beneath the kitchen and tends the risen. He is the real priest of the Supper, and Hesper is not entirely sure she is in charge.
- **The Inner Table.** About a dozen true believers: volunteers, a gravedigger, an apothecary and two city guards, who know what the Supper is and worship there in secret.
- **The volunteers.** Forty or so kind people who ladle soup, wash dishes and believe they are doing good. They are.
- **The quiet servants.** The risen: perhaps thirty, working in the cellars, carrying messages at night and standing very still in the dark until they are needed.

## A night at the Supper

- **The kitchen.** Every night the Supper feeds two hundred people in a long hall near the old port. The food is good and free and nobody asks questions.
- **The dying.** Hesper sits with those who are dying alone. She eases their pain and offers them the Supper's blessing. Some of them accept it knowing what it means. Most don't.
- **The rising.** Absalom raises the dead in the cellars. They are fed, clothed and kept, and they work: digging tunnels, carrying goods, guarding the congregation and, sometimes, removing people who ask too much.
- **The congregation.** The Inner Table meets after the kitchen closes, in a chapel under the hall, to pray to the god of the [[Church of Undeath]] for an end to the fear of death.

## Signs

- Some of the poor stop coming home. Their friends notice, even when nobody else does.
- Old City beggars say the Supper's back alley is never empty at night, and that the people in it never speak.
- The undertakers' trade has dropped, and the paupers' graves have fewer bodies in them than the records say.
- The [[Church of Purity]] has a rumour of necromancy in the Old City. It hasn't found it yet. If it does, it will burn the wrong people first.
- A volunteer saw her late grandfather carrying flour sacks in the cellar. She has convinced herself she imagined it.

## Lair: the kitchen and the cellars

The Supper occupies an old warehouse near the port: a long hall with trestle tables, huge cauldrons and a hearth that never goes out. Under it, cellars run into older vaults, and from there into tunnels the risen have dug into the Old City's forgotten crypts. The chapel is a round crypt hung with grey cloth, with an altar of bones. Absalom's workroom is beyond it, full of bodies in various states of readiness. The risen sleep standing up in the deepest vault.

## Members

- **Mother Hesper Gale** (Bronze), the Mother. A round, warm woman of sixty in a flour-dusted apron, who remembers everyone's name and has held more dying hands than anyone in Greenstone. She believes completely that she is giving the poor peace and purpose.
- **Absalom Crane** (Silver), the Cellarer. A gaunt, quiet man who lost his wife and children to a plague twenty years ago and never let them go. They work in his cellar still. He does not care about the poor, the city or Hesper. He cares about learning to bring them back properly.
- **Brother Lowe**, the Supper's friendliest volunteer and a member of the Inner Table, who acts as its contact with the outside world and its eyes in the [[Church of the Healer]].

#### Traits

***Homing Sense (Hearth essence).*** When you absorb this essence, choose one dwelling as your home; you can change it by living somewhere else for a month. You always know the direction and rough distance to your home.

***Lay to Rest (Requiem essence).*** By singing over a corpse for 10 minutes, you lay its spirit to rest: the body can never be raised as undead, and you learn the name the creature bore in life. Magic that restores the dead to life still works on it.

***Purging Flame (Pyre confluence).*** Once per long rest, as an action, you can burn a poison or sickness out of your body. You take 1d4 fire damage, which can't be reduced in any way, and you end either the poisoned condition or one disease affecting you.

***Turn Undead.*** Once per short rest: each undead within 30 feet must make a DC 14 Wisdom save or flee for 1 minute.

***The Last Supper.*** She can raise a creature that died within the last day and was fed at her table as a zombie under her command. She can control up to six at once.

***Spellcasting.*** Wisdom-based (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit). Cantrips: *chill touch*, *spare the dying*, *toll the dead*. 1st level (4 slots): *bane*, *create or destroy water*, *cure wounds*, *disguise self*. 2nd level (3 slots): *gentle repose*, *pass without trace*, *silence*. 3rd level (3 slots): *animate dead*, *create food and water*, *speak with dead*. 4th level (1 slot): *dimension door*.

#### Actions

***Mace.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage.

***Sacred Flame (Cantrip).*** One creature within 60 ft. must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity save or take 9 (2d8) radiant damage.

#### Awakening stones

Each once per long rest. Spell save DC 14, Wis-based, +6 to hit with spell attacks.

- *hellish rebuke* (level 1, Hearth)
- *daylight* (level 3, Hearth)
- *freedom of movement* (level 4, Bread)

</div>

- **Bread** (common, Life): Home cooking, warmth and hospitality. Grants Proficiency with cook's utensils or with brewer's supplies. The essence of the hearth and the meal shared around it. Bearers always smell faintly of fresh bread, strangers relax in their company, and their magic glows like banked coals.
- **Hearth** (uncommon, Fire): Home, warmth and belonging. Grants **Homing Sense**. Hearth is the warmth of home and the fire that families gather around. Bearers smell faintly of woodsmoke, their hands are always warm, and their magic glows like banked embers.
- **Requiem** (legendary, Dark): The mourning song that grants rest. Grants **Lay to Rest**. Requiem is the last song sung for the dead, the grief that lets a spirit go. The bearer's voice takes on a low, resonant sorrow and candles gutter when they hum, and mourners across the land recognise them as someone whose singing at a graveside means the dead will truly rest.
- **Confluence: Pyre** (Bread + Hearth + Requiem): Cleansing fire that ends and begins. Grants **Purging Flame**. Pyre is the funeral flame that consumes what has ended so that something new can begin. Bearers smell of cedar smoke and ash, their gaze is steady and solemn, and their spells burn white at the heart and leave clean ash behind.

#### Traits

***Breathless (Lich essence).*** You can stop breathing for up to 1 hour at a time. While you do, you can't be suffocated or drowned, and inhaled poisons and gases have no effect on you.

***Deathlike Trance (Shroud essence).*** Once per long rest, as an action, you can fall into a deathlike trance for up to 1 hour. You are incapacitated but can still hear, and you appear dead to all outward inspection; a creature that examines you can tell you are alive only with a successful Wisdom (Medicine) check against a DC of 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier. You can end the trance at any time (no action required).

***Mourner's Touch (Grief essence).*** By touching a corpse for 1 minute, you learn roughly how long ago it died and whether it died from violence, sickness, poison or old age.

***Pronounce Doom (Doom confluence).*** Once per long rest, as a bonus action, you can speak the doom of one creature you can see within 30 feet of you that can hear you. The next time it makes a saving throw within the next minute, it subtracts 1d4 from the roll.

***Arcane Recovery.*** Once per day after a short rest, regains the use of one spent awakening stone of 3rd level or lower.

***Undead Master.*** He commands up to twelve zombies and four ghouls. As a bonus action, he can see through the eyes of any undead he raised.

- **Lich** (legendary, Dark): Undeath seized by forbidden will. Grants **Breathless**. Lich is the sorcerer who cheated death through sheer, terrible will. The bearer's heartbeat slows to a crawl and a cold blue glow kindles behind their eyes in darkness, and anyone versed in the arcane recognises them and quietly wonders what they are hiding.
- **Shroud** (rare, Dark): Burial wrappings and deathly stillness. Grants **Deathlike Trance**. The essence of the cloth wound around the dead. Bearers grow pale and quiet, favour layered, wrapped clothing, and their magic hangs about them like a faint grey veil that muffles sound.
- **Grief** (uncommon, Dark): Mourning, loss and remembrance. Grants **Mourner's Touch**. Grief is the ache that love leaves behind when someone is gone. Bearers have quiet, heavy eyes and a gentle patience with the dying, and a soft grey haze hangs about their magic like the smoke of a pyre.
- **Confluence: Doom** (Lich + Shroud + Grief): Inescapable ruin and the coming reckoning. Grants **Pronounce Doom**. Doom is the certainty of ruin closing in, a debt of blood and darkness that will be paid in full. A bearer's shadow pools a shade too deep and faintly red, and people who meet their eyes feel a brief, cold certainty that something terrible is on its way.

**The risen** use the zombie and ghoul stat blocks from the *Monster Manual*. Three or four of the oldest are Absalom's own family, and are ghasts.
