**City-state.** A great free city on the coast of the southern continent, and a regional power in its own right. Yaresh answers to no larger realm. It sits between the world's powers, trades with all of them, and has grown rich and crowded doing it.

## What players know

- **A crossroads of the world.** Ships from the Storm Kingdom, caravans from the east, river barges from the interior and sky ships from everywhere meet in Yaresh. If something is for sale anywhere, someone in Yaresh can get it.
- **More people than anywhere.** Yaresh is one of the most populous cities in the world. Every people of Pallimustus lives here in numbers, and its districts sprawl along the coast and up the hills behind it.
- **A power of its own.** Yaresh is not a kingdom, but it controls the towns, farms and roads around it for days in every direction, and the larger realms court it rather than threaten it. Its wealth buys high-rank adventurers, and its harbour can close to anyone it chooses.
- **Neutral ground.** Envoys of rival powers meet in Yaresh because neither side owns it. Deals, treaties and betrayals are made in its tea houses.
- **The Thunder Mount** smokes on the horizon to the south-east, and its smiths and glassworkers are famous. See [[The Thunder Mount]].

> *In Yaresh, everyone is a foreigner, so nobody is.* (a saying on the docks)

## How it is run

Like [[Greenstone]], Yaresh stands on its [[Adventure Society]] branch, its great houses and its churches, under a city government. Unlike Greenstone, its merchant families are a fourth force as strong as any of them, and its houses are richer, older and more numerous. Politics here are faster, louder and more expensive.

## Why adventurers go there

- To buy and sell: Yaresh has the biggest markets and auction houses the party is likely to see.
- To take the contracts that come with a trade city: caravan and ship escorts, guarding warehouses, and clearing the roads.
- To meet powerful people from every realm without travelling to each one.
- To disappear. A city this big and this busy is a good place to hide.

## DM notes

**Rank.** Yaresh suits Bronze-rank play and above: a bigger stage than Greenstone, with Silver and Gold-rankers in plain sight.

**Use it as a hub.** Anything from anywhere can turn up in Yaresh, so it is the natural place for a lead from far away to surface, or for the party to meet a faction they have only heard about.

**Hooks.**
- A merchant family hires the party to escort a cargo that three different powers want, and none of them can be seen taking it by force.
- Two foreign envoys are negotiating in Yaresh, and one of them is going to be killed before the talks end.
- The harbour has closed to all ships from one realm, overnight and without explanation.
:::
