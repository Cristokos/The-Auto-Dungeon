**Landmark.** Volcanic islands far out to sea, home to creatures found nowhere else in the world. *The western ocean.*

## What players know

Naturalists from the Magic Society keep a station on the largest island. The animals here are tame, strange and, the naturalists swear, different every time someone visits.

## What mana has made of it

- Creatures change between generations, adapting to whoever visits: after a team of fire-users came, the iguanas began breathing sparks.
- Monster cores taken here carry traits of more than one kind of creature.
- Time runs a little slower on the islands; a month's stay is three weeks back home.

**Dangers.** Creatures that have adapted to adventurers, and the sea between the islands.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** The naturalists need a breeding pair of a monster that has only existed for a year.
- **Secret:** The islands are an experiment, and whoever started it is still taking notes.
- **On Earth this would be** the Galápagos Islands. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
