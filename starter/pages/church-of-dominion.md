The church of **Dominion**, the god who embodies rule, command and the right of the strong to govern, up to and including the ownership of people.

- **Standing:** Favoured by nobles, crowns and conquerors.
- **Symbol:** a crown above a closed fist.
- **Colours:** purple and gold.

## The god

Dominion is power over others: rule, command and ownership. He is the authority of a king over a kingdom, a general over an army, and an owner over a slave. To Dominion these are the same thing at different scales.

- **What the god gives:** Dominion brings order, stability and strong rule. Roads are built, laws are kept, and people are protected by someone strong enough to protect them.
- **Offended by:** Rebellion, anarchy and the breaking of a rightful hold. Liberty is his oldest enemy.

:::dm
### The dark face

Dominion brings tyranny, conquest and slavery. He sees no wrong in a person owned as property, so long as the owner holds them.

- **Will accept:** Slavery, conquest, tyranny and any hierarchy enforced by strength.

### Secret motives

- Dominion wants the whole world under a single strong rule. Which crown it is matters little; what matters is that the hold is total.
- Dominion's priests in Greenstone bless the slave trade that moves people through the region, and advise noble houses on turning debtors into property.
- Dominion sees Liberty as the root of all disorder, and its priests work behind the scenes to have the Liberty church restricted.
:::

## Divine essence

Dominion grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers' voices carry an edge of command, people step aside without knowing why, and a faint crown of light shows when they give orders.*

- **Essence trait:** Proficiency in Persuasion or Intimidation.
- **Sense: Sense Authority.** As an action, you learn which creatures within 60 feet hold command over others, and which are bound to another's will (slaves, the charmed, and those under a magical contract), until the end of your next turn.
- **Channel: Command.** As an action, you speak a one-word command to a creature within 30 feet that can hear you. It must succeed on a Wisdom save against your stone spell DC or obey on its next turn, as the *command* spell.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Command** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures add your Charisma modifier (minimum +1) to Wisdom saving throws. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *command*, *compelled duel*, *heroism*; level 2: *hold person*, *enthrall*, *suggestion*; level 3: *fear*, *crusader's mantle*, *bestow curse*; level 4: *compulsion*, *dominate beast*; level 5: *dominate person*, *geas*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Might.

**Divine confluence: Overlord.** An Overlord commands, and is obeyed. Bearers' voices land like orders even when they ask questions, lesser people find themselves kneeling, and their magic settles over others like a collar. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Dominion (divine) + Crown + Pride; Dominion (divine) + Marionette + Debt; Dominion (divine) + Whip + Oath. It grants **Word of Command**: Once per short rest, you can cast *command* without a spell slot, targeting up to three creatures, using your stone spell DC.

**What the god asks.** Obey those above you, and command those below. Keep what you hold, whether land, people or position. Serve the house or crown you are sworn to.

**What makes the god take it back.** Rebelling against a rightful master. Freeing what you own, or breaking an oath of service.

## What players know

Dominion is the god of rulers. His priests crown kings, bless noble heirs and advise the powerful on how to stay that way. Ordinary folk rarely visit his temple, but they feel his influence whenever the great houses make a decision. Everyone knows that in other lands Dominion is also the god of slavers, and the temple in Greenstone would rather nobody brought it up.

## Beliefs

- Order comes from rule, and rule comes from those strong enough to hold it.
- Everyone serves something. Choose well what you serve.
- A ruler who will not command is not a ruler.
- To own is to protect. To be owned is to be kept.

## Aspects

Dominion has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Slavery:** Dominion over people taken to its end: the chain, the collar and the bond that cannot be broken. Slavery is legal in the Storm Kingdom, and this face of Dominion is worshipped openly there and abroad: its priests bless slave markets and seal ownership with magic.

## Clergy

Advisors to the powerful, and the priests who bless a new head of house. Many noble houses keep a priest of Dominion, and some are rumoured to answer to the church before the family. Slavers and debt-binders, in the kingdom and beyond it, serve the god's Slavery aspect.

## In Greenstone

A small, wealthy temple in the noble quarter. Its influence is felt more in the great houses than in the streets. See [[Noble and Adventuring Houses]].

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of War]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Liberty]], [[Church of Justice]]

Dominion and Liberty are old enemies. Their argument about who has the right to rule, and whether anyone may own another person, runs through every political quarrel in Greenstone.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A priest of Dominion offers the party patronage in exchange for an oath of service to a noble house.
- A noble heir is about to be blessed by Dominion, and a rival house will pay well to make sure the ceremony fails.
- Slaves taken by raiders are being moved through Greenstone, and the trail leads to a house with a Dominion chaplain.
- A magically sealed contract has bound a Greenstone family to serve a stranger, and no healer or priest can break it.

## DM notes

- Dominion is central to the campaign's politics. Keep his true goals open until you decide them; the church is more useful as a question than an answer early on.
- The church teaches that slavery is just one form of rule, and in the Storm Kingdom it can say so openly. Some of its priests disagree in private. How far the Greenstone temple goes beyond the law, binding the free and the indebted alike, is one of its best-kept tensions.
- Slavers' magic deals in bonds and contracts. A bound NPC who cannot speak of their bond is a natural "compromised" twist in the NPC generator.
:::
