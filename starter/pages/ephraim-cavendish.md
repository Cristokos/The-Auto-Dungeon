**Silver rank.** Elf man, an elder of [[House Cavendish]] and uncle of [[Beth Cavendish]].

- **Looks:** Tall and severe, always in grey, with a ledger in one hand and a walking cane he does not need in the other.
- **Role:** He manages the family's properties, rents and loans, and speaks for the house in public while [[Isadora Cavendish|Isadora]] stays home.
- **Personality:** Formal, careful and hard to surprise. He is proud of Beth and would never say so to her face.
- **Essences:** Archive, Key and Patience, forming the **Compass** confluence. Wards, locks and records: there is no door in the Old City he can't open and no lease he can't recite.
- **Ties:** Isadora's son. He has run the family's business for two centuries and expects to run it for two more.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Property, loans and Old City contacts.

Ephraim is the one who keeps the family's understanding with [[The Big Three]] in good repair. He meets Adris Dorgan once a season.
:::

## What people say

The Cavendish elder in grey. Every landlord's agent in the Old City answers to him, and he collects the rent from people nobody else dares to bill.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **The Big Three.** He meets Adris Dorgan once a season in a back room of the music hall. They agree on what the rent is, and what neither side will do. He knows exactly what the Big Three are, and he has decided peace is worth it.
- **Loans.** He lends to adventurers at fair rates, secured against future loot. Default, and the family takes the debt in contracts: the party does a job for the Cavendishes, no questions.
- **Beth.** He has kept every newspaper notice about her in a folder in his desk. If the party ever tells her this, he will never forgive them.
- **A scene.** Ephraim is in the Cavendish rent office with a cane across his knees, going through a ledger line by line while a frightened tenant waits. He finishes the page before he looks up at the party.

## Playing Ephraim

**Severe steward.** Formal, careful and unflappable, and much softer than he lets anyone see.

- **Speech:** Clipped, formal and exact
- **How to play:** Answer a question with the relevant clause of a lease
- **Tell:** Leans on the cane he doesn't need when he is pleased
- **Toward the party:** Courteous and businesslike
- **Wants from the party:** Quiet, rent paid, and the Old City kept peaceful
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> The rent is due on the first. It is always due on the first.
>
> My mother will see you. Not today.
>
> You will find our terms are fair. You will find our memory is long.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To hand the house over intact when Isadora finally lets go
- **Driven by:** Duty, order and old family pride
- **Fears:** His mother dying before the family is ready
- **Bond:** Isadora, Beth and the Old City
- **Flaw:** Will tolerate any evil that pays its rent quietly

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Archive** (uncommon, Magic): Records, memory and preserved knowledge. Grants **Perfect Recall**. Archive is the gathered memory of everything that has been written down and kept. Bearers' thoughts become orderly and precise, and they can call up a page they once read as clearly as if it lay before them.
- **Key** (rare, Swift): Locks, openings and secret access. Grants **Matching Lock**. The essence of the small thing that opens great doors. Bearers fidget with locks and latches without noticing, doors seem to swing a little easier for them, and their magic turns in the air with a faint metallic click.
- **Patience** (uncommon, Earth): Calm endurance and careful work. Grants **Unhurried Hands**. Patience is the quiet strength of waiting and working without hurry. Bearers move slowly and deliberately, rarely raise their voice, and seem untroubled by delays that drive others mad.
- **Confluence: Compass** (Archive + Key + Patience): The needle that finds the way. Grants **Seeking Needle**. Compass is the magnetised needle that turns to a hidden pull, guiding travellers along roads and across trackless land. A bearer tilts their head slightly before choosing a direction, iron filings cling to their sleeves, and their magic swings and settles like a needle coming to rest.
