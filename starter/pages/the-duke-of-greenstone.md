The Duke rules [[Greenstone]]. The city is a state of its own, with no king above it, so the Duke's word is the law: the courts, the household guard, the walls and gates, the roads and bridges, the markets and the tariffs all answer to the [[The Ducal Palace|Ducal Palace]]. The title belongs to [[House Mercer]].

| --- | --- | --- |
| A city that stays calm, rich and his | The guard, the courts, the treasury, and the Mercer name | One more licence holder; trouble if they make noise |

## The Duke and the three pillars

The Duke rules the city but not its three pillars, and each of them pushes back.

- **The [[Adventure Society]]** keeps the monsters away from the walls and answers to its own continental council, not to the Duke. It expects the palace to stay out of its business.
- **The [[Noble and Adventuring Houses|great houses]]** pay the most tariffs and own the most land. The Gellers helped found the city and do not like being reminded who holds the title.
- **[[The Churches]]** speak for most of the city's people and can fill Divine Square with angry worshippers in an afternoon.

A wise Duke keeps all three just unhappy enough that none of them moves against the palace. A foolish one picks a side.

## What players know

- The Duke's seal is on every licence, permit, bounty and fine in the city.
- The Duke is rarely seen. His authority is felt through his guard, his clerks and his bounties.
- The Duke's guard is large, well armed and full of essence users. People say it could match the Adventure Society branch.
- The Duke's brother, Beaufort Mercer, and his family live on the Island. Lady Thalia Mercer, a Silver-ranker, is the face of the family in society.

## DM notes

**Who the Duke is.** Left unnamed on purpose. He is a Mercer, older brother of Beaufort, and the uncle of Cassandra and Thadwick Mercer. Name him when the players first need to.

**What he wants.** Order, revenue, and the title kept in the family. He would like to rein in the Adventure Society branch and has friends in it.

**Hooks.**
- The Duke posts a bounty on thieves who robbed a great house, and the thieves are people the party likes.
- A Mercer offers the party a contract that is really a way to embarrass the Gellers.
- The Church of Purity petitions the Duke to ban a dark essence from the city, and the Duke needs a reason to say no.
