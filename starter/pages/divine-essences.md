A god can place a small piece of its own power inside a worshipper: a **divine essence**. It sits in one of the three essence slots like any other [[Essences|essence]], but it can only be granted by a god, and the god can take it back at any time.

## Who gets one

- **Every priest** of every church holds a divine essence from their god. It is how the faithful know a priest is real.
- **Ordinary worshippers** can be granted one too, if they are adults and their devotion is sincere. Children cannot hold essences, so they cannot receive one.
- **Poor folk** most of all. A normal essence costs more than a villager earns in a lifetime. A divine essence costs nothing but faith, which is why so many farmers, guards and labourers carry one. Holding it makes them Iron rank like any other essence.

A person can hold only one divine essence. Gods do not share their worshippers.

## What it gives

A divine essence works like a paladin's gifts: a sliver of the god's real power.

- **Essence trait.** A skill or tool proficiency, or a small quirk, like any essence.
- **Sense.** A way to perceive what the god cares about. Uses equal to 1 + your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1) per long rest.
- **Channel.** The god's power used directly: a smite, healing, a command. Uses equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest, unless it says otherwise.
- **Blessing.** From Bronze rank, as a bonus action, you bless yourself and nearby allies, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Once per short rest, or twice from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones.** A divine essence takes [[Awakening Stones|awakening stones]] from its own spell list, shown on each church page.
- **Confluence.** It counts toward a [[Confluence|confluence]] using its affinity, like any other essence.
- **Divine confluence.** Each god also has a confluence of its own, which forms only when the divine essence is joined by the two essences of one of its exact recipes. Each church page lists its recipes.

Where a Channel or Blessing calls for a saving throw, the DC is your stone spell DC.

## The price

- **The god's demands.** Each god asks something of those who carry its power. Each church page lists what its god asks.
- **The god's mark.** Anyone with a soul-reading ability, such as a priest's Sense or a Purity Cleanser's training, can tell which god you serve. That matters when you walk into a temple of a rival god, or past a [[Church of Purity]] inquisitor.
- **Service.** The church expects its essence-bearers to answer when called, to tithe, and to serve its interests.
- **It can be taken away.** A god can revoke its essence at any time, for breaking its demands, for losing faith, or for no reason a mortal can see.

## When a god takes it back

- The essence fades from its slot, and the slot is empty.
- You lose its trait, Sense, Channel and Blessing.
- **You lose every awakening stone ability on that essence.** The stones are spent, and the spells are gone.
- Your confluence goes dormant until you hold three essences again. When it reforms, it may be a different confluence.
- Your level and rank do not change.

:::dm
## DM notes

- A god may give the essence back after atonement, or never. That is a story beat, not a rule.
- Revocation is the god's strongest leash on its church. It makes every priest, and every villager with a divine essence, someone who can be pressured.
- A divine essence is stronger than a normal essence. The price is obedience, and that is the trade the players should feel.
- Worshippers of [[Church of Undeath|Undeath]] and [[Church of Disguise|Disguise]] carry divine essences too, and keep them hidden. Their mark is the clue.
:::
