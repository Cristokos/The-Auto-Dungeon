**Landmark.** Thousands of steep limestone islands rising from emerald water, home to floating fishing villages. *The eastern lands.*

## What players know

The fishers of Dragonback Bay say the islands are the spine of a dragon that lay down in the sea to sleep. Their villages float, roped together, and move with the tides. Caves in the islands hold shrines, smugglers' stores and stranger things.

## What mana has made of it

- The islands shift position, very slowly: charts of the bay are only good for a few years.
- Water in the deepest caves is warm, and the walls of those caves breathe.
- Pearls from the bay are monster cores of a creature no one has ever seen.

**Dangers.** Sea monsters, cave predators and the occasional tremor when the dragon dreams.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A floating village has drifted into a cave and not come out.
- **Secret:** The fishers are right. The dragon is a Diamond-rank creature that has slept for an age, and someone is trying to wake it.
- **On Earth this would be** Ha Long Bay. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
