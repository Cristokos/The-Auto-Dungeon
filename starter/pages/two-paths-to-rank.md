**The natural path** is growth through hard use of every ability in real danger. In 5e terms, this is ordinary XP or milestone advancement.

**The core path** is absorbing [[Monster Cores]]. It is fast, safe, and open to anyone with money, which is why nobles, officials and other non-adventurers favor it. With enough coin, a person can reach Bronze without ever facing a monster. Most people in [[Greenstone]] who hold rank got it this way. Adventurers tend to look down on core users as powerful but unskilled.

## Core advancement

Instead of earning XP, a character gains a level by absorbing monster cores of their current rank, a number equal to the new level: five Iron cores to go from level 4 to 5, for example. [[Monster Cores]] totals how many cores and how much coin each rank takes. The costs are what slow core users down at Silver.

## The drawbacks

- **Closing door.** Each level gained through cores permanently reduces the XP the character earns by 25%. After a fourth core level, the character can no longer gain XP at all and can advance only with cores.
- **Unpracticed.** A character who has gained any level through cores makes [[Awakening Stones|awakening-stone]] spell attacks at −2 and sets their awakening-stone spell save DCs at −2. Core users have the power without the practice.
- **Soul residue.** Every core absorbed leaves residue on the soul. Anyone with a soul-reading ability can detect it, such as a Purity Cleanser or certain essence powers, and the [[Church of Purity]] treats it as a mark of weakness or corruption. Residue also sets a hard ceiling: **a core user can never rise above Silver rank (level 16)**, no matter how many cores they absorb or how they advance afterward.

:::dm
**DM secret:** whether residue can ever be cleansed is a mystery for the campaign to decide. Players know only of the ceiling, and hear rumors.
:::
