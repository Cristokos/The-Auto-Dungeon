This app holds the whole campaign: the world wiki, the party's sheets, session notes, generators and a battle tracker. You don't need all of it on day one. Start with the wiki and the sheets, and pick up the rest when you want it.

## Finding your way

The sidebar has four groups. Click a group's name to fold it away.

- **Play:** the home page, the **Party** (everyone's sheet and the shared purse), **Sessions** (one page of notes per session) and the **Battle** tracker.
- **World:** the **Calendar** with the world's date and chronicle, **News** the party has heard, **Legends**, and the **World map**.
- **Reference:** essences, items, spells and the bestiary.
- **Prep:** the **Generators** and your **Saved hooks**.

Below them is the wiki itself, every page grouped by kind. Underlined names are links, and red ones are pages nobody has written yet. **Search** (Ctrl+K) finds any page.

The **?** in the top right corner (or F1) says what the screen in front of you is for.

## For players

- Your character lives under **Party**. Change anything on the sheet and it saves by itself. The stat block at the top is worked out from it.
- Spirit coins are shared: the **Party purse** holds everyone's coins.
- **News** is what your characters have heard about the world. **Legends** is what the world remembers.
- Click any spell, item or essence name to read about it.

## For the DM

### Your first session

1. Read [[Running the Campaign]] and the [[Player Primer]].
2. Add each player character under **Party**, or import a sheet a player sends you.
3. Press **New session** under **Sessions** and keep notes as you play. Link names with `[[ ]]`, and each one gets the session in its **History**.
4. Need someone on the spot? **Generators → NPC**, then **Save to wiki**.
5. A fight? Add the party and some monsters on the **Battle** screen, roll initiative and press **Start**.

### DM mode and player view

The switch at the bottom of the sidebar flips between **DM mode** and **Player view**. Player view hides DM-only pages, secrets inside DM blocks and the DM tools, so you can turn the screen to the table.

### Turning systems on and off

**Settings → Systems in this campaign** turns whole systems on or off. **Simple** keeps the wiki, sheets, sessions, reference pages, the map, the everyday generators and a plain battle tracker. **Everything** turns it all on. A system you switch off is only put away: its data stays, and it comes back when you switch it on again.

A good order to add them in:

1. **Town building buttons:** add taverns, markets and temples to a town with one click.
2. **The living world:** a calendar, Advance time, and a chronicle of what happens. News and Legends come with it.
3. **Storyteller and small events:** ideas for the next session, and small events that happen by themselves as time passes.
4. **Reputation**, **NPC lives** and **Faction aims**: the party's standing, NPCs' goals and ties, and factions that act on their own.
5. **Trade**, **Travel**, **Contracts** and **AI monster turns** when your table wants them.

### The DM tools strip

In DM mode, place, NPC, faction and session pages have a **DM tools** strip under their text. Its tabs (Add here, Trade goods, Standing, Their story, Its aims and so on) open one panel at a time, and the strip remembers which one you had open for that kind of page. Players never see it.
