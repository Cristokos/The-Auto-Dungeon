The church of **Undeath**, the god who embodies existence that refuses to end; the dead made to walk, think and serve.

- **Standing:** Outlawed in the Storm Kingdom.
- **Symbol:** a skull crowned with a living flower.
- **Colours:** grey and violet.

## The god

Undeath is existence that continues after death: the corpse that rises, the spirit that lingers, the will that refuses to stop. Undeath is not the enemy of life. It is the enemy of endings.

- **What the god gives:** Undeath is defiance of the inevitable. It is the grieving mother who will not let go, and the scholar who needs one more century to finish their work.
- **Offended by:** Final, willing rest, and above all Death, who takes back what Undeath has kept.

:::dm
### The dark face

Undeath feeds on the living to persist, and turns the dead into tools. It treats a soul's rest as a waste of a good body.

- **Will accept:** Necromancy, soul-binding, the theft of corpses, and any bargain that buys more time.

### Secret motives

- Undeath wants a world where nothing ends, which means a world ruled by those who no longer die. It patiently cultivates the old, the powerful and the grieving.
- Undeath sees Death's rules as a cage built by the other gods, and wants them broken.
:::

## Divine essence

Undeath grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers are cold to the touch, their wounds bleed slowly and darkly, and animals shy away from them.*

- **Essence trait:** Deathless. You have advantage on saving throws against poison and disease, and you don't need to sleep, only to rest.
- **Sense: Sense the Grave.** As an action, you sense every corpse and every undead creature within 60 feet until the end of your next turn.
- **Channel: Raise.** As an action, you touch a corpse and it rises as a zombie or skeleton under your command until the next dawn. You can control one raised creature at a time (two from Silver rank).
- **Blessing: Blessing of the Unending** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). When a blessed creature drops to 0 hit points, it can drop to 1 hit point instead. Each creature can benefit from this once per long rest. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *false life*, *ray of sickness*, *cause fear*; level 2: *gentle repose*, *ray of enfeeblement*, *blindness/deafness*; level 3: *animate dead*, *vampiric touch*, *feign death*; level 4: *blight*, *death ward*; level 5: *danse macabre*, *enervation*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Dark.

**Divine confluence: Undying.** The Undying have stepped halfway out of death and stayed there. Bearers do not age, their hearts beat slowly or not at all, and their magic tastes of grave dirt. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Undeath (divine) + Lich + Revenant; Undeath (divine) + Wraith + Hunger; Undeath (divine) + Rot + Eternity. It grants **Not Yet**: Once per long rest, when you drop to 0 hit points, you drop to 1 hit point instead and gain temporary hit points equal to your level.

**What the god asks.** Never let a useful body go to waste. Keep the faith hidden from the law and the other churches. Preserve yourself, whatever it costs others.

**What makes the god take it back.** Destroying the god's undead servants. Choosing final rest for someone you could have kept.

## What players know

The Church of Undeath is outlawed, and mostly spoken of as a warning: grave-robbers, necromancers, and the dead who walk. Most people in Greenstone believe its worshippers are gone. Most people would rather believe that.

## Beliefs

- Death is a cage, not a door. The wise refuse to walk through it.
- Flesh is a tool, and a tool does not stop being useful when its owner is done with it.
- What is feared becomes free.

## Clergy

There are no open priests in the Storm Kingdom. Worship happens in hidden cells: necromancers, mourners who could not let go, and the very old who are running out of time.

## In Greenstone

Officially none. In practice, a few cells meet in cellars and crypts, and the [[The Delta and Marshes|marshes]] hide older shrines that predate the city.

## Friends and rivals

- **At odds with:** [[Church of Death]], [[Church of Purity]], [[Church of the Healer]]

Every other church treats Undeath as a threat. Death's church hates it most of all.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- Fresh graves in a village outside Greenstone are being dug up from below.
- A grieving widow hires the party to find her husband's body, which was stolen, and she seems strangely calm about it.
- The Church of Purity is about to burn a "necromancer" in the market square, and the accused swears they only spoke to their dead daughter once.

## DM notes

- Undeath's worshippers are not all monsters. Many are desperate people who were offered one more day with someone they loved. That makes them easy to recruit and hard to fight with a clear conscience.
- A Dark, Blood or Death-themed adventurer is exactly who a cell would try to recruit, and exactly who the Church of Purity would blame for it.
:::
