**Village.** A village at the river crossing on the south road out of [[Greenstone]], built around the ford and a toll bridge that is always being repaired. Magic level: moderate. Protector: the toll keepers, who are better armed than toll keepers need to be.

## What players know

Caravans stop at Lantern Ford to water their heidels and pay the bridge toll, and the village lives off them: two inns, a smithy, a stable and more stalls than a place this size should support.

**Export.** Glass made from marsh sand, faintly green and prized in Greenstone for lamps and windows.

**Quirk.** No one crosses the ford after dark without a lit lantern, ever. Every house keeps spares to lend, and the youngest child in each family lights the village's lamps at dusk. Locals can't agree on why. Most say it's for the river.

:::dm
**Hook.** The custom is older than the village. Something under the ford takes travelers who cross in the dark, and the lanterns are a bargain that was struck long ago. The terms are coming due.
:::
