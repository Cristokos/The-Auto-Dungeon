# Pallimustus

A high-magic world where monsters rise from the land itself, the gods answer prayers, and every person's power can be measured. This campaign begins in [[Greenstone]], a frontier city at the edge of delta country, at a time of relative calm.

**Rules:** the 2024 rules (the 2024 Player's Handbook) for classes, leveling, combat, spells and items. [[Essences]] add flavour and small perks on top, never replacing a class feature.
