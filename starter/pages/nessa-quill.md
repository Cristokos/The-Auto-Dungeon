**Normal rank.** Human woman, registration clerk at the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] of the [[Adventure Society]].

- **Looks:** Early twenties, freckled, with ink-stained fingers and a mess of red hair held back by two pencils. Wears a staff badge she is very proud of, and stares at every Iron-rank badge that crosses the counter.
- **Personality:** Cheerful, curious and talkative. Asks about every essence, remembers every team and tells everyone everything.
- **Wants:** To be a real adventurer, not just the girl at the counter. She has one essence and is saving for a second; she will not be Iron rank until all her slots are filled.
- **Essences:** Ink. Her one essence lets her write fast and copy anything perfectly, which is why the branch hired her.
- **Ties:** Best friends with [[Dobbs]], whom she feeds, and terrified of [[Pell Hobbard]], whom she adores.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Gossip, early word of new contracts, and a friend inside the branch.
:::

## What people say

Nessa at the registration desk. She'll register your team, tell you everyone else's business, and ask about your essences until you leave. Lovely girl. Never tell her a secret.

:::dm
## DM notes

- She is the easiest leak in the building, and three different people use her without her knowing it.
- If the party ever needs a fifth for a team, she will volunteer before anyone finishes the sentence.
- **The leak.** Isolde Varrick, a Mercer agent and a man who works for [[The Big Three]] all pump her for gossip. Each thinks he is the only one. A party that works this out can feed false information to all three at once.
- **Her second essence.** She is saving for it, and her savings are very small. A party that gives her one, or helps her earn one, has a friend for life, and a new Iron-ranker shortly after.
- **Volunteering.** If she joins the party, she is brave, keen and badly underpowered. Protect her, or she becomes a tragedy in the session notes.
- **A scene.** The party walks up to registration. A freckled clerk with pencils in her hair is already reaching for the form, and asks which essences they have before they've finished saying their names.

## Playing Nessa

**Eager clerk.** Cheerful, curious and unstoppable, a would-be adventurer stuck behind a counter.

- **Speech:** Fast, bubbly and full of questions
- **How to play:** Ask about the party's essences and then tell them about three other teams
- **Tell:** Pulls a pencil out of her hair whenever she gets excited
- **Toward the party:** Adoring and nosy
- **Wants from the party:** Stories, and one day a spot on a team
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> Ooh, what's that one? Is that a rare one? Can I see?
>
> Don't tell anyone, but Beth Cavendish's team took the swamp contract.
>
> When I'm an adventurer, I'm going to be just like you.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To be a real adventurer
- **Driven by:** Excitement, and a love of stories
- **Fears:** Being a clerk forever
- **Bond:** Dobbs, and every team that's nice to her
- **Flaw:** Can't keep a secret to save her life
