**Landmark.** A vast red monolith rising alone from the flat desert, glowing like an ember at sunset. *The southern continent.*

## What players know

The desert peoples hold the Red Heart sacred and guard it fiercely. Travellers who are welcome may touch it and hear the land's stories. Unwelcome ones find the desert never lets them leave.

## What mana has made of it

- Touching the stone shows memories of the land: whoever and whatever has passed through it.
- The monolith changes colour with the danger in the region: red at peace, purple when monsters gather, black when a surge is coming.
- Water appears in its shadow for anyone the guardians allow, and nobody else.

**Dangers.** Heat, the guardians, and the desert's patience.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** The guardians ask for help: the Red Heart has turned black, and no surge has come.
- **Secret:** The monolith is a record keeper set down by a god, and it is filling up.
- **On Earth this would be** Uluru, in the central desert of the southern continent. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
