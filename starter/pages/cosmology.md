Pallimustus is one world among countless others, all of them floating in the astral. Who holds power over them depends on where you stand.

## The layers

- **The physical world** is Pallimustus itself: the land, the sea, and everyone living on it. It is soaked in magic, though some places, like [[Greenstone]], hold much less of it than others.
- **[[Astral Spaces]]** are pockets that sit halfway between the world and the astral. Each has its own rules, inhabitants and rewards, and they are the setting's dungeons.
- **The astral** is the vast space between worlds. It is home to astral beings of enormous power, some of whom view mortal worlds as resources or property. See [[Astral Powers]].

## The powers

- **The gods** each embody a concept, such as Purity, Death, Healing or Knowledge, and act through priests, churches and divine essences. A god's power in its own world is enormous, but it ends at the edge of that world. **Gods cannot act inside astral spaces**, which is why a dangerous astral space has to be cleared by adventurers rather than by prayer. See [[The Gods]].
- **Great astral beings** sit far above the gods in scale. Each governs one part of how reality works across every world at once, from the boundaries of worlds to the finality of death to the flow of time. They cannot touch a physical world directly, so they act through prime avatars, servants and cults. On day one they are rumour and scripture rather than presence. See [[Astral Powers]].
- **Astral kings and Messengers** are lesser astral powers that want worlds for themselves. They are the astral threat most likely to arrive.

## People and power

Rank changes more than strength. A Normal-rank person lives about as long as anyone in a world without magic. An Iron-ranker lives a little longer, a Bronze-ranker noticeably so, a Silver-ranker for centuries, and a Gold-ranker for longer still. Diamond-rankers are rare enough that most people never meet one, and they live so long that nobody is sure how long. See [[Ranks]].

## Visitors

**[[Outworlders]]** are people who arrive from other worlds. They are rare, recognisable to those who know what to look for (to a sensitive nose they smell faintly of springtime), and treated with curiosity, suspicion or opportunism.

:::dm
**Why gods stay out of astral spaces.** An astral space is not fully part of any world, and a god's reach is bounded by its world. Priests can still use their own divine essence inside one, because that power belongs to them once given. The god cannot step in, see in or intervene. This keeps astral spaces as places where mortals have to do the work, and it makes them ideal hiding places for anything a god is looking for, such as a cult, a prisoner or a stolen relic.
:::
