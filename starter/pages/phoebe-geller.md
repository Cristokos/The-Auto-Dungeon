***Fleet (Swift essence).*** Once per short rest, you can take the Dash action as a bonus action.

**Iron rank.** Human woman, sister of [[Rick Geller]], of the family's northern branch. She was raised far from Greenstone and came to the city to train with her cousins.

- **Personality:** Steady, practical and quietly fierce. She is the one who keeps Rick from doing the stupidest version of every plan.
- **Skills:** A strong martial trainer. She teaches better than she brags.
- **Looks:** Lean and weathered from the northern winds, with her dark hair in a tight plait, bare feet in the training yard and wrapped hands everywhere else.
- **Essences:** Swift, Wind and Balance, forming the **Mystic** confluence. She fights unarmed, faster than most people can follow.
- **Ties:** [[Rick Geller]]'s sister and keeper. She trains in the Geller yard most mornings and will happily take on a student who shows up early.

:::dm
**Offers players:** A trainer for a Swift or martial character, and a sensible voice inside the family.

Phoebe has an understanding with Dustin Kettering, a son of the minor Kettering house who serves the Mercers. Both families would object, which is half the appeal.
:::

## What people say

Rick Geller's sister, the one who actually knows what she's doing. She runs the early session in the Geller yard, and the young Gellers are more afraid of her than of Danielle.

## DM notes

- **Teacher.** A Swift or unarmed character who trains with her for a few weeks can learn one of her moves: once per long rest, after taking the Dash action, they can make one unarmed strike as a bonus action.
- **Dustin.** She meets [[Dustin Kettering]] in the delta, away from both their families. If the party carries a message between them, they are trusted with something neither family knows.
- **Cooling Rick down.** If the party has a feud with Rick, Phoebe is the one who can end it, and she will ask them for a favour in return: get her brother onto a contract that teaches him something.
- **A scene.** It is barely light. Phoebe is running the Geller yard alone, and doesn't stop when the party arrives. She asks which of them is fastest and then sets off around the wall without waiting for an answer.

## Playing Phoebe

**Quiet trainer.** Says little, sees everything, and keeps her brother alive mostly by standing next to him.

- **Speech:** Calm, dry and brief, with northern vowels
- **How to play:** Correct a party member's stance without being asked
- **Tell:** Rolls her shoulders before she says something that matters
- **Toward the party:** Coolly interested, warmer to anyone who trains
- **Wants from the party:** Good students, and a safe place for her and Dustin to be seen together
- **Pronouns:** she/her

**Things to say:**

> Your weight's on the wrong foot. Again.
>
> Rick, no.
>
> Up early? Good. Run.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** A life with Dustin that neither family can forbid
- **Driven by:** Discipline and a fierce, private love
- **Fears:** Rick getting himself killed out of pride
- **Bond:** Rick, and Dustin
- **Flaw:** Hides what she wants until it's almost too late

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Swift** (common, Swift): Speed, reflexes and quick feet. Grants **Fleet**. The essence of speed. Bearers fidget whenever they are made to wait, leave faint afterimages when they move suddenly, and rarely seem out of breath.
- **Wind** (common, Wind): Moving air, freedom and open skies. Grants **Weathervane**. The essence of restless air. A breeze seems to follow bearers indoors, their hair and cloaks are always stirring, and their spells arrive on a sudden gust.
- **Balance** (uncommon, Light): Equilibrium, poise and fair measure. Grants **Steady Footing**. Balance embodies equilibrium in body and in deed. Bearers carry a settled calm and rarely shift their weight, and their magic shows as a pair of faint scales that steady before every spell.
- **Confluence: Mystic** (Swift + Wind + Balance): Inner discipline and flowing power. Grants **Inner Stillness**. Mystic is the discipline of body and spirit brought into one flowing current. Bearers breathe slowly and move with unhurried grace, and their spells unfold from a single still point, like ripples spreading across a pond.
:::
