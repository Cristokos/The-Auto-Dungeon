Spirit coins are magic made solid: small, glassy coins with a metallic sheen, usually embossed, that feel like glass to the touch. They are the world's currency and a consumable commodity at the same time. Every coin spent in a ritual, burned in a magic item, or eaten is gone from circulation.

A coin's value comes from the rank and purity of the magic in it. Iron coins are an impure distillation, cloudy and pale blue. Higher ranks are refined further, and each is worth 100 coins of the rank below:

| Coin | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Iron spirit coin | 1 gp |
| Bronze spirit coin | 100 gp |
| Silver spirit coin | 10,000 gp |
| Gold spirit coin | 1,000,000 gp; rarely traded outside national treasuries and the greatest houses |
| Diamond spirit coin | 100,000,000 gp; almost never seen |

Ordinary townsfolk still use mundane coin for everyday purchases.

## Where coins come from

Spirit coins are made in **spirit coin farms**: mints built inside high-magic areas to draw on the dense magic there. Monsters and spirit coins are born from the same magic, so the richest farm sites are also the most monster-ridden. Every farm needs constant protection, which makes guarding farms a steady source of contracts, and makes control of farm sites a constant prize for houses, guilds and the crown.

## Uses at the table

- **Currency.** Use the rates above.
- **Fuel.** Any spell or ritual with a costly material component can use spirit coins of equal value instead. Some magic items need a coin of their rank to recharge.
- **Food.** An essence user can eat one coin of their rank in place of a day's food and water. Familiars, summons and other magical beings can be fed the same way.
- **Surge.** As a bonus action, eating a coin one rank above your own grants advantage on Strength, Dexterity and Constitution checks and saving throws for 1 minute. When it ends, you gain one level of exhaustion. Coins two or more ranks higher are too potent to eat safely.
- **Low-magic regions.** Where ambient magic is thin, a character who does not eat a coin of their rank each day regains no Hit Dice on a long rest.
