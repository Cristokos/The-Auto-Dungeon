**Port.** [[Greenstone]]'s waterfront: the working port along the [[The Old City|Old City]], the north marina with its transit terminal, and the private docks of the Adventure Society and the great houses.

## What players know

- **The Old City port** runs the length of the Old City, crammed with trade ships from every coast. Cranes, warehouses, chandlers, and the smell of tar and eels.
- **The North Marina Transit Terminal** handles passenger traffic: ferries along the coast, ships to [[Rimaros]] and beyond, and the odd sky ship.
- **The marina district** holds pleasure craft, yachts and floating cloud houses, magical homes that can unfold from a flask, moored on the water by people rich enough to own one.
- **The Adventure Society dock** is private, for Society boats and teams heading out on delta contracts.
- **River traffic.** Green-stone riverboats and barges come down the Mistrun all day, loaded with marble, grain and eels.

:::dm
**Hook.** A cloud house breaks its moorings in the night and drifts out to sea with its owner asleep inside. The owner's family will pay well to get it back before anyone notices what is kept in the cellar.
:::
