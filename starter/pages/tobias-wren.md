**Normal.** Human man, archivist at the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] of the [[Adventure Society]].

- **Looks:** Very old, very thin and very still, with white hair to his collar and fingers like dry twigs. Wears a shawl in all weathers.
- **Personality:** Quiet and precise. Speaks rarely, and always to the point. Knows every file in the building and most of what isn't in them.
- **Wants:** Order in his records, and to be left alone.
- **Ties:** He has been archivist under five directors. He trained [[Nessa Quill]] to copy files and is the only person in the branch who can make Isolde Varrick nervous.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Maps, monster accounts, old astral space reports and, for those he likes, a look at a file they should not see.
:::

## What people say

Old Wren in the archive. He's been there forever and knows where everything is. He doesn't like visitors, but if he likes you, he'll find you anything.

:::dm
## DM notes

- He keeps a second copy of every report that has ever been altered after filing, in a locked case only he can open. The case has been getting fuller.
- **The locked case.** The altered reports in his case include contracts on the missing teams, surveys of land the Mercers later bought, and a branch death report from twenty years ago that Isolde Varrick signed as a junior clerk.
- **Earning his trust.** Return a file in perfect order, ask a precise question, and don't waste his time. He tests visitors by giving them the wrong file and seeing if they notice.
- **His secret.** He has a weak heart and knows he will not last many more winters. He is looking for someone to give the key to.
- **A scene.** The party asks the archive for a map. A thin old man in a shawl listens without moving, walks into the stacks without a word, and comes back ten minutes later with the map, a monster report they didn't know they needed, and a raised eyebrow.

## Playing Tobias

**Silent archivist.** Quiet, precise and watchful, keeper of what the branch would rather forget.

- **Speech:** Rare, dry and exact
- **How to play:** Answer with a file instead of a sentence
- **Tell:** Taps the key at his neck when someone lies near him
- **Toward the party:** Cool, then quietly approving
- **Wants from the party:** Order, and someone worthy of the key
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Shelf nine. Left. Bring it back.
>
> That isn't the right file. Good. You noticed.
>
> Some records are altered. I keep the originals.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To pass on the truth before he dies
- **Driven by:** Order, duty, and a quiet anger at liars
- **Fears:** Dying with the case still locked
- **Bond:** The archive
- **Flaw:** Trusts paper more than people
