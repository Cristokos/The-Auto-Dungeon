The scholarly and crafting counterpart to the [[Adventure Society]], and one of the great organisations of the world alongside it and the Alchemy Association. It researches magic, trains enchanters and ritualists, and buys and appraises [[Monster Cores|monster cores]], [[Awakening Stones|awakening stones]] and [[Essences|essences]]. It cooperates with the Adventure Society out of necessity and competes with it for influence.

| Wants | Holds power through | Default view of a new Iron-ranker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge, crafting monopoly, research access | Scholarship, enchanting, appraisal of cores and stones | A potential customer or test subject |

**What players know.** Scholars, enchanters and ritualists. The Magic Society buys monster cores, appraises essences and awakening stones, and researches magic no one else understands. The two societies are said to tolerate each other the way neighbours do when they share a wall.

## In Greenstone

The branch keeps a hall and workshops in [[The Guild District]] on [[The Island]], a short walk from the Adventure Society campus. Low ambient magic makes Greenstone a modest posting, and ambitious members tend to move on to [[Vitesse]] or [[Rimaros]].

- **The front hall** buys monster cores and quintessence, appraises essences and awakening stones, and sells common enchantments.
- **The workshops** take commissions for enchanted gear, ritual work and magical analysis.
- **The ritual hall** handles anything too big or too dangerous for a workshop, such as sealing a dimensional breach or reading what an astral space wants.

## DM notes

- **Lamprey's branch.** The director uses the Society's access to cores and appraisals for his own network. A party that sells to the Magic Society is telling Lamprey what they found.
- **Clive is the way in.** He will help the party out of curiosity and frustration, and he is right far more often than his director admits.
- **Surges.** In a [[Monster Surges|surge]] the Society's ritualists are as important as its fighters, and the branch's readings are the first sign one is coming.
:::
