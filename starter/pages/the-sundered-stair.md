**Landmark.** A mile-deep canyon of red and gold rock layers, carved by a river that runs at its bottom. *The north-west continent.*

## What players know

Climbers say each layer of rock in the Stair is a different age of the world, and that the deeper you go the older the magic. The river at the bottom is said to run backward.

## What mana has made of it

- Gravity shifts at each layer: on some ledges, the canyon wall is the floor.
- Fossils in the deepest layers are monster cores turned to stone, still faintly charged.
- Echoes come back in other voices, sometimes answering questions nobody asked.

**Dangers.** Falls, shifting gravity, and the canyon's harpy flocks.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A scholar needs an escort down to the oldest layer to read an inscription only visible at noon.
- **Secret:** The bottom layer is older than the world itself: a piece of whatever the world was built on.
- **On Earth this would be** the Grand Canyon. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
