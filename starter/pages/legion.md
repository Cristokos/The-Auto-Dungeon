Legion, also called the Limitless Legion, is the [[Astral Powers|great astral being]] that administers the cosmic rules that govern life itself: what living things are, how they grow, and what they may become. Its realm of the astral borders that of [[The All-Devouring Eye]].

Like the Eye, Legion is not known for handing out blessings. When it does, the blessing tends to touch the body itself.

## What it wants

Legion wants life to keep its shape. It keeps the rules of living things in order: that a body grows, heals and ages in the ways its kind should, that creatures breed true, and that change, when it comes, follows a pattern. It is not cruel and not kind. It is a gardener who prunes.

What troubles Legion is life that breaks its rules: undeath, which keeps a body without its life; abominations stitched together by flesh crafters; and things from the Eye's realm that have learned to wear living bodies. Where the border between their realms frays, Legion's attention sharpens.

## How it touches mortals

- **Transformation.** Its rare blessings change the body: a new form, a lost limb regrown into something better, a lineage reshaped. They are always permanent and never quite what the receiver expected.
- **Strange births.** Twins born with one mind, animals born speaking, children with the traits of a people neither parent belongs to. Midwives in old villages call these "Legion's marks" and leave the child a gift.
- **Monsters.** Its realm seeds creatures that are many bodies acting as one: swarms, hive beasts and colonies. Astral spaces near its influence fill with them.

## Cultists and bargains

Legion has no church and wants none. A few flesh crafters, druids and alchemists revere it as the patron of their work, and some of them call themselves the Many. They study how bodies change and dream of the perfect form. Most are harmless scholars. A few experiment on people.

Bargains with Legion are almost unheard of. When they happen, the price is change: the bargainer is altered to serve its purpose, and their body becomes a tool for keeping life in order somewhere it has gone wrong.
