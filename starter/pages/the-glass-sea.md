**Landmark.** Endless dunes, many of them fused into rippling glass by an ancient mana storm. *The south-central continent.*

## What players know

Caravans cross the Glass Sea by night, when the glass is cool and the stars show the way. By day it blinds, and the mirages are dangerous: some of them are real for a little while.

## What mana has made of it

- Mirages solidify for a minute or two: an oasis you can drink from, a city you can walk into, a monster that can bite.
- Sand-glass from deep in the sea holds images of the storm that made it, and seers pay well for it.
- Sandstorms here scour away enchantments as well as skin.

**Dangers.** Heat, thirst, glass-sharp ground, and the things that live in the mirages.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A caravan master swears a mirage city has appeared on the same spot three nights running, and wants someone to go inside.
- **Secret:** The storm that made the Glass Sea never ended. It is still out there, sleeping in the deep desert, and the mirages are its dreams.
- **On Earth this would be** the central Sahara. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
