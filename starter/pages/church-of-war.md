The church of **War**, the god who embodies organised conflict: armies, campaigns, discipline and the strength to win.

- **Standing:** Respected by soldiers and rulers, feared by everyone else.
- **Symbol:** crossed spears over a shield.
- **Colours:** iron grey and blood red.

## The god

War is armed conflict between peoples: armies, campaigns, battles and the skill to win them. The soldier, the warrior, the strong and the champion are all faces of War. War does not care who wins or why, only that the fight is fought.

- **What the god gives:** War brings discipline, courage and comradeship, and the defence of home against invaders and monsters.
- **Offended by:** Cowardice, desertion, and peace bought by surrender.

:::dm
### The dark face

War brings conquest, massacre and endless conflict. A war that never ends pleases War well enough.

- **Will accept:** Slaughter, sieges, mercenaries who change sides, and wars begun for no reason at all.

### Secret motives

- War wants conflict to continue. A war that ends too quickly, or a peace that lasts too long, is a failure in War's eyes.
- War's chaplains sell their training and their blessings to both sides of a conflict, and sometimes help start one.
:::

## Divine essence

War grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers carry old scars that never fade, their weapons hum before a fight, and they stand as if always on parade.*

- **Essence trait:** Proficiency with martial weapons or with heavy armour.
- **Sense: Battle Sense.** As an action, you learn the number and positions of hostile creatures within 60 feet, even behind cover, until the end of your next turn.
- **Channel: Divine Smite.** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can deal an extra 2d8 radiant damage (3d8 from Silver rank).
- **Blessing: Blessing of Courage** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures can't be frightened, and a blessed creature that is already frightened stops being frightened. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *divine favor*, *searing smite*, *heroism*; level 2: *magic weapon*, *enhance ability*, *branding smite*; level 3: *crusader's mantle*, *haste*, *spirit guardians*; level 4: *staggering smite*, *stoneskin*; level 5: *banishing smite*, *circle of power*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Might.

**Divine confluence: Crusader.** A Crusader is a soldier of War itself. Bearers wear their scars like medals, banners seem to snap in a wind around them, and their magic lands like a cavalry charge. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: War (divine) + Sword + Valour; War (divine) + Shield + Oath; War (divine) + Onslaught + Fury. It grants **Holy Charge**: If you move at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and hit it with a weapon attack on the same turn, the attack deals an extra 2d8 radiant damage and the creature must succeed on a Strength save against your stone spell DC or be knocked prone.

**What the god asks.** Answer a call to arms. Never desert your company. Honour a worthy enemy.

**What makes the god take it back.** Fleeing a battle you swore to fight. Betraying your own company.

## What players know

The Church of War trains soldiers and blesses armies. Its training yard by the walls is open to anyone who wants to spar, and it is a fine place to learn to fight, or to find out you cannot. Mercenary companies hire its priests as chaplains.

## Beliefs

- War is a craft. Treat it with discipline and respect.
- Victory belongs to the prepared, and to the strong.
- Never abandon a comrade on the field.

## Aspects

War has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Soldier:** The face of War for the rank and file: drill, loyalty and the comrade beside you.
- **Warrior:** War for those who fight alone, for the joy of the fight itself.
- **Strength:** War as raw might, worshipped by brawlers, labourers and those who rely on their own body.
- **Champion:** War as the one who steps forward to fight for others. Its champions stand in duels and trials on behalf of those who cannot fight.

## Clergy

Chaplains to armies and mercenary companies, drill-masters and war priests. Its fighting orders are among the best-trained soldiers in Greenstone.

## In Greenstone

A barracks-temple near the city walls with a training yard open to anyone who will spar. Many Iron-rank adventurers learn their trade there.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of Dominion]], [[Church of the Hero]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Liberty]], [[Church of the Hearth]]

War respects the Hero and serves Dominion. It has little patience for the Hearth's sermons about staying home.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A War priest is recruiting adventurers for a "short campaign" against a monster nest that has grown far too large.
- Two mercenary companies are about to fight in the street, and the only thing stopping them is a War priest who has lost their voice.
- The training yard's champion has been beaten for the first time, by a stranger who will not give a name.

## DM notes

- Its four aspects make it easy to place a War priest in any NPC: a disciplined Soldier, a reckless Warrior, a brawling devotee of Strength, or a Champion who fights other people's duels.
