Three crime lords run [[Greenstone]]'s underworld from [[The Old City]]: gambling, smuggling, protection, loans and the [[The Fortress|Fortress]] pits. Each runs their own organisation, and they keep a wary peace with each other and with the Duke's guard.

| Wants | Holds power through | Default view of a new Iron-ranker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money, territory, and each other's weakness | Debt, fear, bribes, and people in useful places | Muscle to hire, or trouble to price |

## The three

- **The Silva family.** The oldest and loudest of the three. Old Silva built it on fear and an odd sense of honour, and once protected people the others would have sold. His son and heir, **Cole Silva**, is arrogant, impulsive and short-sighted. He reached Bronze rank by soaking himself in bought monster cores, and he has never been in a real fight.
- **Clarissa Ventress.** A Bronze-ranker with dark, cascading hair, satin dresses and a serpentine smile. She collects people: fighters, informants, debtors. She protects them in public and spends them in private.
- **Adris Dorgan.** The quietest of the three, and the most careful. His organisation keeps its hands clean, mostly, and he keeps his family's connections even more private than his business.

**What players know.** Everyone in the Old City knows the three names and knows whose street is whose. Locals pay for protection and call it rent. The watch takes its share and calls it something else.

## DM notes

- **Cole Silva wants [[Sophie Wexler]].** Old Silva protected her; when he dies, Cole will come for her. Ventress shelters her mainly to infuriate him.
- **Ventress is playing both sides.** She protects Sophie in public while quietly moving her into the hands of [[Lucian Lamprey]], who wants her too.
- **Dorgan's secret.** [[Elspeth Arella]], director of the Adventure Society branch, is his daughter. Neither of them can afford for that to be known.
- **When Old Silva dies,** the peace breaks. Use that moment to start a gang war the party can ride, stop or profit from.
:::
