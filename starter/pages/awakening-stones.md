Awakening stones are loot that unlock specific essence abilities. In 5e terms, when absorbed into a matching essence, a stone grants one spell from a short list tied to that essence, castable once per long rest without a slot. The spell's level can be no higher than half the character's level (rounded up), to a maximum of 5th.

A character can hold a number of stone abilities equal to their proficiency bonus. Absorbing a new one past that limit replaces an old one. Stones are the main way to make [[Essences]] feel distinctive without inflating power.

Each essence page lists its stone spells, and the party sheets suggest spells that fit a character's level.

**Core users** (see [[Two Paths to Rank]]) make awakening-stone spell attacks at −2 and set their stone spell save DCs at −2.

The [[Magic Society]] buys and appraises stones.

## What a stone is

An awakening stone looks like a smooth, palm-sized pebble with a faint inner light, the colour of the affinity it belongs to: red-gold for Fire, deep green for Life, silver-blue for Magic. Each holds one spell from its affinity's list, and it awakens on any essence of that affinity. A Fire stone holding *scorching ray* can awaken on Fire, Smoke or Spark, whichever the character chooses.

Absorbing a stone takes a short rest spent holding it against the chosen essence's mark. The stone crumbles to dust, and the spell is now part of that essence. It can be cast once per long rest without a spell slot, using the character's spellcasting ability, or their class's main ability if they have none (Strength for most fighters).

## Grades and rank

Stones are graded by the level of their spell, and each grade is linked to a rank: a level 1 stone is an Iron-rank stone, level 2 Bronze, level 3 Silver, level 4 Gold and level 5 Diamond. Monsters of a given rank drop stones of about that grade, and markets sell stones up to the rank they deal in. A character still needs to be high enough level to use what they buy: the spell's level can be no higher than half their level, rounded up.

## Finding and buying

- **Loot.** Monsters drop stones less often than cores and more often than essences. Astral space bosses are the most reliable source.
- **Contracts.** Some contracts pay in stones, especially those posted by the [[Magic Society]] or a church.
- **Markets.** Awakening stone dealers sell them openly in any city. A typical price for a stone is:

| Grade | Spell level | Typical price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron | 1 | 150 gp |
| Bronze | 2 | 600 gp |
| Silver | 3 | 2,500 gp |
| Gold | 4 | 10,000 gp |
| Diamond | 5 | 40,000 gp |

Auction houses in great cities sell rarer stones for about a quarter more. Prices rise where magic runs low and the affinity is rare.

## Swapping and replacing

- A character can hold a number of stone abilities equal to their proficiency bonus.
- At the limit, absorbing a new stone replaces an old one of the character's choosing. The old ability is lost for good: the stone it came from is already dust.
- Stone abilities stay with the essence they awakened on. They cannot be moved to a different essence, sold, or given away.

## Worked example

Iris is a level 5 Iron-ranker with the Fire, Swift and Shield essences. Her proficiency bonus is +3, so she can hold three stone abilities, and her level lets her use stones up to 3rd level.

1. She buys a Fire stone holding *scorching ray* (600 gp) and absorbs it on her Fire essence.
2. A Silver-rank contract pays her in a Swift stone holding *haste*. It is 3rd level, which she can use, so she absorbs it on her Swift essence.
3. She finds a Shield stone holding *shield of faith* in an astral space and absorbs it. She now has three.
4. Later she buys a Fire stone holding *fireball*. To absorb it she must give up one of the three. She drops *shield of faith*.

## Questions and answers

- **Can I absorb the same spell twice?** No. A second stone with the same spell does nothing and is wasted.
- **Do divine essences take stones?** Only their own god's stones, not the usual affinity stones.
- **What if I don't have an essence of that affinity?** The stone does nothing for you. Sell it, or keep it until you do.
- **Do core users get anything worse?** Their stone spell attacks and save DCs are at −2.
- **Do stone spells need components?** Only components with a gold price, which must be supplied as usual.

:::dm
- **Loot.** Roll on the essence's stone list for a monster's drop, or choose one that suits the party. The Market and Auction generators already stock stones at these prices.
- **Pacing.** One stone per astral space or significant contract is about right for Iron play. Let players save up for the stone they want instead of handing it over.
- **Rare stones.** A stone of a kind nobody has seen, with a spell from outside the usual list, makes a fine heart reward or plot hook.
:::
