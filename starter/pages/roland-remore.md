**Diamond rank.** Human man, founder and head of [[House Remore]] and of the Remore Academy in [[Vitesse]]. Father of [[Gabriel Remore]] and grandfather of [[Rufus Remore]] and [[Gwydion Remore]].

- **Looks:** Old in the way only very powerful people get old: unhurried, unbent, and with a presence that makes the room feel smaller.
- **Personality:** Exacting, proud and certain. He built the best adventurer academy in the world by refusing to accept "good enough", and he applies that rule to his family too.
- **Ties:** Every Remore in the world reports to him eventually. So do a surprising number of kings.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Almost nothing directly. A word from him opens doors on any continent; a word against someone closes them.

Roland is far beyond anything the party can fight or bargain with. Use him as a distant presence: a letter, a name, a standard Rufus measures himself against. He takes a keen interest in anyone who trains alongside his grandson.
:::

## What people say

Roland Remore, the Diamond-ranker who founded the academy. People in Vitesse say he has trained more Gold-rankers than any living person, and that he has never once said "well done".

:::dm
## DM notes

- **At a distance.** Roland appears as letters, rumours and the way other Remores talk about him. A letter from him to the party, polite and three lines long, is a major event in the campaign.
- **What he wants from them.** If the party trains with Rufus, Roland wants a report. He will send someone, probably Gwydion, to get one.
- **Standards.** He believes most adventurers die because they were badly trained. He is right, and he is willing to be cruel to prove it.
- **A scene.** A courier in academy colours delivers a sealed letter to the party at the branch. It says, in a precise old hand, that Roland Remore has heard of them, that he expects better of his grandson's friends, and that there is a place at the academy if they wish to earn it.

## Playing Roland

**Distant patriarch.** Proud, exacting and certain, a figure more often felt than met.

- **Speech:** Few words, carefully chosen, never repeated
- **How to play:** Ask a question the party can't answer, and wait for them to admit it
- **Tell:** Never sits down unless he means to stay
- **Toward the party:** Coolly assessing
- **Wants from the party:** To know whether they are good enough for his grandson
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Good enough is how adventurers die.
>
> My grandson speaks of you. Prove him right.
>
> Again.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** That no adventurer he trained dies of a bad habit
- **Driven by:** Pride, and the long memory of every student he lost
- **Fears:** His family name outliving its standards
- **Bond:** The academy, and his family, in that order
- **Flaw:** Cannot say well done, even when he means it
