**Kingdom.** A rich, high-magic kingdom on the northern continent, ruled from [[Vitesse]], the City of Flowers. Estercost is green, settled and old, a land of river valleys, vineyards, forests and walled towns, where magic runs strong and the monsters come in herds.

**What players know.** Estercost is where the world's best mages are trained and its finest magic goods are made. Its people are cultured, comfortable and convinced that the rest of the world is a little behind. To most of Greenstone it is a place of legend, an ocean away.

## The land

- **The Heartland.** The wide river valley around Vitesse: wheat, vineyards and orchards for days in every direction, dotted with manor houses and market towns. The richest farmland in the known world.
- **The Western Bocage.** Hedged fields, apple country and rainy coasts facing the western ocean. Fishing towns, cider houses and smugglers.
- **The Forest of Haut-Ver.** A great old forest in the east, kept as a royal hunting ground. Its deep parts are full of astral space entrances, and the crown licenses adventuring there by the season.
- **The Southern Slopes.** Hills and mountains running down to a warm southern sea. Lavender, olives, perfume houses and monasteries cut into the rock.
- **The Marches.** Wooded hills on the eastern border, where the kingdom's old fortresses still stand.

## The crown and the houses

Estercost is ruled by a royal family that has held the throne for centuries. The crown princess grew up alongside the children of the great houses, [[Rufus Remore]] among them, and is expected to rule within a generation.

- **The great houses** hold the provinces for the crown. Many of them are adventuring families, and most of them sent their children to the Remore Academy.
- **[[House Remore]]** is not the oldest house in the kingdom, but it may be the most influential: the crown, the great houses and half the kingdom's officials were trained by it.
- **The campuses** of Vitesse act almost as houses of their own. Each has its own charter, lands and politics, and the crown treats their heads with the respect it gives a duke.

## Law and custom

- **Slavery is illegal** in Estercost, and has been for centuries. Slave ships are seized, and their captains hanged.
- **Magic is regulated.** Dangerous research needs a licence from the crown, and the campuses police their own. Unlicensed necromancy is a hanging offence.
- **Adventurers** are honoured, and supervised. Iron and Bronze-rankers fight in groups with a Silver-rank escort, because the high magic spawns monsters in herds.
- **Faith.** The churches are many and mostly at peace with each other. The [[Church of Knowledge]] is powerful in Vitesse; the Church of the Healer runs the kingdom's hospitals.

## Trade

Estercost sells magic goods, awakening stones, enchanted instruments, wine, perfume and scholarship. It buys rare monster parts, exotic essences and cores from lower-magic lands, which is why its merchants turn up in places like [[Greenstone]].

## DM notes

- **Using Estercost.** It is the high-magic destination of the campaign: bigger monsters, better shops and a society that looks down on Greenstone's standards. A party from Greenstone will be treated as rustic, and has every chance to prove otherwise.
- **The supervision rule** is a good source of friction. A Bronze party that wants to fight alone has to talk its way past an escort, or slip away from one.

**Hooks.**
- A campus in Vitesse needs living samples from a Greenstone astral space, and will pay for the party's passage north to deliver them.
- The crown princess wants to know whether Rufus Remore's friends are as good as he says. Her invitation comes with a test.
- A herd of Bronze-rank monsters has broken out of Haut-Ver, and every adventurer in the east is being called up, including foreigners.
- A perfume house in the Southern Slopes is using an outlawed essence in its scents, and its customers are starting to change.
:::
