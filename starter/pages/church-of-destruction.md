The church of **Destruction**, the god who embodies ruin and endings by force, and the wasteland left behind.

- **Standing:** Feared and mostly shunned.
- **Symbol:** a cracked circle.
- **Colours:** ash grey and black.

## The god

Destruction is ending by force: what is broken, burned, torn down or torn apart. Desolate, Discord and Pain are its faces: the wasteland afterwards, the breaking of bonds between people, and the suffering that breaking causes. It cares that things break, not what grows in their place.

- **What the god gives:** Destruction clears the way. It brings an end to rotten institutions and the wildfire that renews a forest, and makes room for something new.
- **Offended by:** Things that endure unchanged forever.

:::dm
### The dark face

Destruction is ruin for its own sake: cities razed, alliances shattered, and pain inflicted because it can be.

- **Will accept:** Arson, demolition, riots, torture and the fall of anything at all.

### Secret motives

- Destruction wants the old order to fall: the Adventure Society, the churches and the noble houses. It does not care what comes next.
- Destruction's faithful stir up riots and feuds, then let the other churches take the blame.
:::

## Divine essence

Destruction grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers leave hairline cracks where they lean, food spoils a little faster near them, and their magic tears rather than burns.*

- **Essence trait:** Ruinous Touch. Your weapon attacks and spells deal double damage to objects and structures.
- **Sense: Sense Weakness.** As an action, you learn the weakest point of a structure, and which creature within 60 feet is closest to breaking, until the end of your next turn.
- **Channel: Unmaking.** When you hit a creature or object with an attack, you can deal an extra 2d8 force damage (3d8 from Silver rank). A nonmagical object or a 5-foot section of wall hit this way is destroyed.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Ruin** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). When a blessed creature hits with an attack, the target takes extra force damage equal to your proficiency bonus. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *thunderwave*, *burning hands*, *cause fear*; level 2: *shatter*, *scorching ray*, *heat metal*; level 3: *fireball*, *erupting earth*, *fear*; level 4: *blight*, *wall of fire*; level 5: *destructive wave*, *contagion*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Fire.

**Divine confluence: Ruinbringer.** A Ruinbringer breaks everything they touch, eventually. Bearers leave cracks in stone and rust on iron, people quarrel when they are near, and their magic tears and shatters. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Destruction (divine) + Cataclysm + Fury; Destruction (divine) + Desolate + Rust; Destruction (divine) + Hammer + Nova. It grants **Unmake**: Your attacks and spells ignore resistance to force, fire and thunder damage, and deal double damage to constructs and structures.

**What the god asks.** Tear down what is rotten. Never rebuild what you have destroyed. Let what must fall, fall.

**What makes the god take it back.** Saving something the god wanted to fall. Rebuilding what you destroyed.

## What players know

Destruction is the god of endings, ruin and the wasteland. Most people only meet its followers as doomsayers in the street, or as hermits living in places no one else would. Its cracked-circle symbol appears on burnt-out buildings, and nobody likes seeing it.

## Beliefs

- All things end. Some must be helped along.
- From ash comes clear ground.
- What breaks you shows what you were.

## Aspects

Destruction has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Desolate:** Destruction as the barren place after ruin: deserts, wastelands and ash fields. Hermits and ascetics seek it out.
- **Discord:** Destruction among people: strife, division, and the breaking of every bond.
- **Pain:** Destruction as suffering. Some of its devotees seek pain to purify themselves; others inflict it.

## Clergy

Doomsayers, demolishers, ascetics and agitators. Some are harmless hermits; others are dangerous fanatics. The church itself has no single voice.

## In Greenstone

No temple. Ruined buildings sometimes bear its cracked circle in charcoal, and a few street preachers announce that the end is coming, as they have for years.

## Friends and rivals

- **At odds with:** [[Church of the Healer]], [[Church of the Hearth]], [[Church of the Journey]]

Almost every church that builds, protects or heals sees Destruction as its opposite.

## Adventure hooks

- Cracked circles have been chalked on the doors of six houses on the same street.
- A hermit of the Desolate who lives out in the marshes has warned the Adventure Society that a [[Monster Surges|surge]] is coming.
- A riot breaks out in the market for no clear reason, and the party notices a calm stranger watching from a rooftop.

## DM notes

- Destruction worshippers are useful as a red herring for a real threat. People blame "the doomsayers" for everything, which gives the actual villains cover.
