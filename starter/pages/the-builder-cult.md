The most dangerous of the [[Cults]]. Its members serve [[The Builder]], the great astral being that is building a world of its own in the deep astral (see [[Astral Powers]]). On day one it is a whisper: missing people, strange rituals, and officials who seem a little too accommodating.

> *Some astral spaces were not found. They were made, and whoever made them may not be finished with them.* (a [[Whispers|whisper]])

| Wants | Holds power through | Default view of a new Iron-ranker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Astral spaces carved off the world and handed to the Builder | Infiltration, constructs, star seeds, ritual knowledge | A recruit, a sacrifice, or nothing at all |

## What the cult does

The Builder cannot act in the world, so its cult does the work. Cultists find astral spaces near a world, or make the conditions for one, and feed them power, people and monster cores until the space can sustain itself. Once it can, the Builder tears it free and anchors it to the world it is building. For the region left behind, that can mean anything from a strange empty hole to a disaster.

## How it operates

- **Recruitment.** It looks for powerful, ambitious people who feel they are owed more: frustrated scholars, nobles in decline, officials who want reach. It promises power, and often delivers some.
- **Cells.** Members know only their own cell and a contact above it. Most of them believe they serve a cause, and only half understand what it is.
- **Constructs.** Its guardians are machines built around clockwork cores, which the cult gets from beings called clockwork kings. They are strong, ugly and relentless, and their cores are proof the Society will pay for.
- **Star seeds.** The Builder gives the cult star seeds to plant in chosen people. A seeded person slowly becomes a puppet, and their body may grow mechanical limbs ending in spear points. Ordinary cleansing magic cannot remove a seed.
- **Made spaces.** The cult opens and maintains its own astral spaces. They are well built, full of loot and riddled with traps.

## In Greenstone

- The cult's hand is behind a string of missing people in the delta villages and the outer city.
- It has members in at least one great house. In [[House Vane]], Landemere Vane already venerates great astral beings, and the family's own secrets make it easy to lean on.
- An astral space in the region is being fed. When it is ready, the cult will need a crowd of people inside it.

## Running the cult

- **Pacing.** Iron play: missing people, a strange core, a construct that should not exist. Bronze play: a seeded victim, a cell exposed, a made astral space. Silver play: the full plan and the Builder's prime avatar, Shako.
- **Seeded NPCs.** Pick one or two people the party knows and seed them. Their slow change is the cult's best horror.
- **Why it matters.** The cult's big play is an astral space fed by a [[Monster Surges|surge]] and filled with people. Use it as the climax of the Greenstone arc.
- The astral space generator sometimes rolls a space whose workmanship points to the Builder cult.
:::
