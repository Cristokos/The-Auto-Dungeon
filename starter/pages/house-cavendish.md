An old Greenstone family with a whole quarter of [[The Old City]] named after them: the Cavendish quarter, desert-stone streets with painted walls around the family townhouse. Wealthy, well connected, and quieter than the Gellers or the Mercers.

## Founders

The house was founded by **Isadora Cavendish**, an elf who came to the delta with the first settlers as a Silver-rank adventurer and bought cheap land beside the old port when nobody else wanted it. The port became the Old City, and the Cavendishes became its landlords. She is a Gold-ranker now, very old even for an elf, and still alive. She rarely leaves the townhouse, and the family still does nothing important without her approval.

Most of the family are elves, which is why its generations run so long: Isadora's grandchildren are still young adventurers.

## Key people

- [[Isadora Cavendish]] (Gold), founder and head of the house.
- [[Ephraim Cavendish]], an elder of the family who manages its properties and speaks for it in public.
- [[Beth Cavendish]] (Iron), his niece, widely called the best Iron-ranker in Greenstone.
- [[Mose Cavendish]] (Iron), Beth's cousin, a fire and wind glass cannon on her team.

**Beth's team:** Beth; Mose; Emily, a Celestine scout and archer; Hudson Kettering, a human defender with the Fortress essence; and Niko Tomich, a Smoulder.

## Interests and income

- **Rents.** The family owns much of the Old City: the Cavendish quarter, whole streets of tenements and shops, and more warehouses by the old port than it admits. Almost everyone in the Old City pays a Cavendish, directly or not.
- **Entertainment.** Shares in the Old City's music hall and taverns, and in the box seats at [[The Fortress]].
- **Lending.** Loans to merchants and lesser houses, at rates that are fair until they aren't.
- **Interests.** Keeping the Old City profitable and quiet, which means keeping an understanding with [[The Big Three]], and raising a new generation strong enough to protect the family when Isadora finally isn't there.

## DM notes

- **Using them.** Beth's team is the measuring stick for every other Iron-rank team in the city. Put them on the same contract board as the party and let the rivalry write itself.
- **The landlords of the Old City** know exactly how [[The Big Three]] operate, because they collect rent from them. The Cavendishes look the other way in return for peace. A party that hurts the Big Three's business may find the Cavendishes unexpectedly cold.
- **Isadora** knows a great deal about the city's early history, including things the Gellers and Mercers would rather nobody remembered.
:::
