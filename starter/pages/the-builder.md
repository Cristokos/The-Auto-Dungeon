The Builder is the [[Astral Powers|great astral being]] of making. Where the others tend some part of every world, the Builder wants a world of its own. It is assembling one, piece by piece, in the deep astral far from any reality.

## What it wants

The Builder is obsessed with physical reality but cannot exist in it, so it builds with borrowed material. Astral spaces, the pockets that sit halfway between a world and the astral, are its raw stock. If an astral space is given enough power to sustain itself, even briefly, the Builder can tear it free and anchor it to the world it is making.

Since it cannot act in a world itself, scholars reason that it must recruit people to do the carving for it.

## Its tools

- **Servants** who open and feed astral spaces until they can be taken.
- **Constructs** built around clockwork cores, ugly and relentless machines that guard its work.
- **Star seeds** planted in chosen people. They change the host, sometimes into something with extra mechanical limbs ending in spear points, and make them the Builder's puppets.

## Shako

The Builder's prime avatar is **Shako**, a Diamond-ranker with pale freckled skin, wild red hair and glowing green eyes. On his home world he founded an empire, and his descendants still fill it. The Builder also burns through lesser, disposable vessels that it uses up and throws away.

:::dm
**Its cult.** [[The Builder Cult]] does the carving: it finds astral spaces, feeds them until they can sustain themselves, and hands them over.

**The Builder's origin.** The current Builder is not the original. It was a Messenger named **Zithis Carrow Vayel** who rose to the office after the first Builder was removed for breaking the rules great astral beings live under. No one knows what became of the first Builder, since astral beings cannot die. A Messenger's contempt for mortals now sits behind a cosmic power, which explains why the Builder treats people as tools in a way the other great astral beings do not.

- **Pacing.** On day one the party meets the cult's symptoms: missing people, strange cores, astral spaces that feel made. By Bronze they meet constructs and a seeded victim. Shako belongs at Silver or later.
- **Star seeds** cannot be removed by ordinary cleansing. Removing one early does little harm; removing one late is a crisis and a quest.
- **Clockwork cores** come from beings called clockwork kings. A party that finds one has found evidence the Society will pay for.
- **Enemies.** The World-Phoenix opposes the Builder's damage to world boundaries; use Dawn as distant backup, never a rescue.
:::
