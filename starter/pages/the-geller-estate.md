**Estate.** The seat of [[House Geller]], outside the walls of [[Greenstone]]: walled grounds, gardens kept green by magic, a household of guards and retainers, and one of the few mirage chambers in the city.

## What players know

- **The mirage chamber.** A huge dome shaped like an insect's eye, rising from the gardens. Inside, magic stages illusions solid enough to fight: monsters, terrain, whole battles. Control and viewing rooms look down into it. Training in a mirage chamber is a privilege money can buy, and the Gellers rarely sell it.
- **The family.** Bronze and Silver-rank Gellers live and train here, so the estate is better defended than most towns.
- **Heidel paddocks**, stables, a private astral space entrance rumoured in the cellars, and very good food.

:::dm
**Hooks.**
- [[Danielle Geller]] lends a visiting Bronze-ranker the mirage chamber to train a promising Iron-ranker, and asks a favour in return: a fair assessment of her son.
- A recording of a fight in the mirage chamber gets out, and suddenly the whole city has seen how the party fights.
