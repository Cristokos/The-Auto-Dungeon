**Bronze rank.** [[Peoples of Pallimustus|Leonid]] man, smith and [[Rufus Remore]]'s teammate. His full name is Gareth Xandier, and nobody uses it.

- **Looks:** Huge, hairy and covered in fur, with a glorious mane and a lion's head over oddly human, enormous hands.
- **Essences:** Hammer, Iron and Fire, forming the **Forge** confluence. He fights with a big hammer and makes better gear than most people can buy.
- **Personality:** Warm, big-hearted and easygoing, right up until training starts. As a movement coach he is a merciless taskmaster. He loves meat, smithing and hard work, and he is proud of all three.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Crafting, gear advice, movement training and a friendly face.
:::

## What people say

A leonid craftsman who adventures with Rufus. Big, warm, and fond of his food and his work in equal measure. His smithing is already earning him a name.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Gear advice.** Gary's rule is that it is smarter to spend money than to lose a life. He will talk the party out of cheap armour and into good armour they cannot quite afford.
- **Commissions.** He takes smithing work between contracts. A weapon made by Gary is a fine reward, and he will happily make one from a monster part the party brings him.
- **Team.** He met Rufus and [[Farrah Hurin]] clearing a zombie plague. He is the heart of the team and the one most likely to like the party first.

## Playing Gary

**Gentle giant.** Warm, generous and easygoing, right up until he is teaching. Then he is the hardest taskmaster in the city.

- **Speech:** Deep, warm, booming laugh; says "mate" a lot
- **How to play:** Clap someone on the shoulder hard enough to stagger them, then ask what they ate today
- **Tell:** His mane bristles when someone is cheap about armour
- **Toward the party:** Friendly from the start; adopts the youngest one
- **Wants from the party:** Good monster parts to work with, and students who keep moving when they are tired
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Spend the money. Gold's easier to get back than a leg.
>
> Again! Feet first, then the hammer. Your feet are lying to you.
>
> Is there meat? There should be meat.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To make something truly great at his forge
- **Driven by:** Love of the craft, his team and a good meal
- **Fears:** Gear he made failing someone who trusted it
- **Bond:** [[Rufus Remore]] and [[Farrah Hurin]]
- **Flaw:** Too trusting of people who seem friendly

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Hammer** (common, Might): Impact, smithing and breaking. Grants proficiency: warhammers or smith's tools. The essence of the hammer, maker and breaker both. Bearers' hands grow callused and sure, sparks jump when they grip metal, and their magic lands like an anvil blow.
- **Iron** (common, Earth): Metal, toughness and unbending will. Grants **Unbending**. The essence of iron and a will that does not bend. Bearers' skin takes on a dull metallic sheen, their voices pick up a faint ring, and their resolve hardens like quenched steel.
- **Fire** (common, Fire): Heat, flame and fierce energy. Grants **Kindle**. The essence of living flame. Bearers run warm to the touch, ember glints flicker in their hair, and their spells crackle and smell of woodsmoke.
- **Confluence: Forge** (Fire + Hammer + Iron): Fire, hammer and metal made one. Grants **Field Smithy**. Forge is flame, hammer and iron joined in the endless work of the smithy. Bearers' forearms grow corded and soot-flecked, their voices ring with a hint of struck metal, and their spells land in a shower of sparks with the tang of hot iron.
