#### Traits

***Mana Touch (Magic essence).*** As an action, he can touch an object to learn whether it is magical and, if it is, which school of magic it belongs to.

***Steady Footing (Balance essence).*** He has advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks to keep his balance, and can tell at a glance whether a surface is perfectly level.

***Glyph Sight (Rune essence).*** He can tell at a glance whether writing or a drawn symbol is magical, and has advantage on checks to find magical glyphs, runes and sigils.

***Returned in Kind (Karmic confluence).*** Once per long rest, when a creature he can see damages him, he can use his reaction to mark it. His next attack roll or ability check against it before the end of his next turn has advantage.

***Ritualist.*** He can cast any wizard ritual he knows without expending a slot, and leads ritual work for the Magic Society.

***Spellcasting.*** Intelligence-based (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit). Cantrips: *fire bolt*, *light*, *mage hand*, *prestidigitation*. 1st level (4 slots): *detect magic*, *identify*, *mage armor*, *magic missile*, *shield*. 2nd level (2 slots): *arcane lock*, *detect thoughts*, *misty step*.

**Iron rank.** Human man, researcher at the [[Magic Society]] in [[Greenstone]], with the very long title of adjunct assistant to the deputy director.

- **Looks:** Late twenties, thin and untidy, with ink on his cuffs and the distracted look of someone halfway through a calculation.
- **Essences:** Magic, Balance and Rune, forming the **Karmic** confluence. He is a ritualist and spellcaster, best at astral magic and runework, and his Rune essence gives him a rune tortoise familiar.
- **Personality:** Brilliant, obsessive and socially oblivious. He gets excited about magic the way other people get excited about a horse race, and he will happily explain any of it at length.
- **Faith:** He venerates [[The Celestial Book]], the great astral being of understanding, and makes no secret of it.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Lore, appraisal, ritual magic, and a researcher who will follow any strange thing they find.
:::

## What people say

A Magic Society researcher who knows more about magic than he does about people. If something odd turns up in the city, the Society usually sends Clive, and Clive usually finds out more than they wanted.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Background.** He grew up on an eel farm in the delta (see [[The Delta and Marshes]]). The Magic Society's director at the time spotted him and became his mentor, and at sixteen Clive also joined the Adventure Society, just in time for the last [[Monster Surges|surge]]. His mentor died in it. Clive threw himself into research and never really went back to adventuring.
- **Lamprey.** The new director, [[Lucian Lamprey]], has kept him from advancing ever since. Clive knows it and has stopped pretending not to.
- **Using him.** Clive is the party's best route to understanding astral spaces, odd cores and anything the Builder cult leaves behind. He is braver than he looks once he is curious.
- **Rejoining the field.** A party that gives him a reason to adventure again gets a loyal friend and, in time, a formidable caster.

## Playing Clive

**Enthusiastic scholar.** Delighted by every strange thing, oblivious to every social cue, and braver than he looks once he is curious.

- **Speech:** Fast, technical and full of tangents
- **How to play:** Get distracted by a party member's essence mid-conversation and ask to examine it
- **Tell:** Starts sketching runes on whatever is nearest
- **Toward the party:** Fascinated, especially by anything unusual about them
- **Wants from the party:** Samples, observations, and someone to take him into the field again
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Fascinating. Do that again. Slower.
>
> The director would say no. The director isn't here.
>
> It's astral magic. Well, mostly. Probably. Let me check.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To understand the deep rules of magic, and to be recognised for it
- **Driven by:** Curiosity, and the memory of his mentor
- **Fears:** Another surge, and losing someone again
- **Bond:** [[The Celestial Book]], and his mentor's unfinished research
- **Flaw:** Forgets people while chasing a question

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Magic** (uncommon, Magic): Raw mana and arcane sensitivity. Grants **Mana Touch**. Magic is mana in its rawest, most adaptable form. Blue-white motes drift around the bearer's hands, and enchanted things feel faintly warm or buzzing to their touch.
- **Balance** (uncommon, Light): Equilibrium, poise and fair measure. Grants **Steady Footing**. Balance embodies equilibrium in body and in deed. Bearers carry a settled calm and rarely shift their weight, and their magic shows as a pair of faint scales that steady before every spell.
- **Rune** (uncommon, Magic): Inscription, glyphs and structured magic. Grants **Glyph Sight**. Rune is magic given shape through writing and symbol. Faint script traces itself across the bearer's hands as they work, and their spells leave brief glowing glyphs hanging in the air.
- **Confluence: Karmic** (Magic + Balance + Rune): Every deed weighed and returned. Grants **Returned in Kind**. Karmic is the world's ledger written in glyphs of power, where every deed is weighed and paid back in kind. Bearers feel a small, satisfied tug whenever a wrong is repaid, and their spells show as interlocking runes that tip like scales before they settle.
