***Surge (Potent essence).*** Once per long rest, when you roll damage for an attack or a spell, you can reroll any of the damage dice that rolled a 1 or 2. You must use the new rolls.

**Iron rank.** Human man, son of [[Beaufort Mercer]] and [[Thalia Mercer]], and nephew of [[The Duke of Greenstone|the Duke]].

- **Looks:** Slicked-back hair, expensive clothes, and an expression of permanent offence.
- **Essences:** Lightning, Potent and Wind, forming the **Storm** confluence.
- **Personality:** Arrogant, petty and not very bright, with a very high opinion of himself. Anyone who embarrasses him is an enemy for life.
- **Team:** [[Dustin Kettering]] and whichever minor-house sons his family can attach to him.
- **Ties:** He blames the Gellers for every embarrassment of his life, and [[Humphrey Geller]] most of all.

:::dm
**Offers players:** A rival to humiliate, at a price.

**His fall.** Thadwick chases power, and [[The Builder Cult]] can give it to him. Plant a star seed in him; if it is removed, he goes looking for another. In the end he may join the cult willingly and become a vessel for [[The Builder]]. Use him as the face of the cult's reach into the great houses.
:::

## What people say

The Duke's nephew. Avoid him, people say, and if you can't, let him win. Several Iron-rankers who didn't have found their contracts mysteriously cancelled.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Power and the seed.** Once the star seed is in him, Thadwick gets stronger very quickly. He believes it is his own talent finally showing, and anyone who suggests otherwise becomes his enemy.
- **Dustin.** Thadwick treats Dustin like a servant. If Dustin leaves, Thadwick takes it as a public insult and tries to ruin the Kettering family over it.
- **Redemption.** It is possible, but only if he loses badly in public, survives, and finds someone who takes him seriously. That someone could be a player character, and it will not be easy.
- **A scene.** Thadwick's team blocks the contract board while he reads every notice aloud and sneers at it. When the party reaches past him, he asks if they know who he is, and then tells them anyway.

## Playing Thadwick

**Petty heir.** Arrogant, insecure and dangerous, a small man with access to a great deal of power.

- **Speech:** Loud, sneering and full of his family's name
- **How to play:** Take something small from the party and dare them to object
- **Tell:** Glances at Dustin to check whether he's being laughed at
- **Toward the party:** Hostile and condescending, then obsessed if embarrassed
- **Wants from the party:** To be feared and to win, preferably both
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Do you know who my uncle is?
>
> You'll regret that. You'll regret that for years.
>
> Finally. Finally someone sees what I'm capable of.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To be respected, and to be stronger than the Gellers
- **Driven by:** Envy, wounded pride and hunger for power
- **Fears:** Being laughed at, and being ordinary
- **Bond:** His mother, though he would never say so
- **Flaw:** Will sell anything, including himself, to anyone who flatters him

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user. His Lightning essence uses the compendium's Spark entry, and his Storm confluence its Dragon entry; rename them if the compendium gains its own.

- **Spark** (common, Fire): Lightning, quickness and bright ideas. Grants Proficiency in Arcana or with tinker's tools. The essence of the spark, the first flash of lightning and of inspiration. Bearers' hair lifts with static, small shocks jump from their fingertips to door handles, and they are prone to sudden bursts of insight.
- **Potent** (uncommon, Might): Raw power and amplified force. Grants **Surge**. Potent is power pushed past its usual bounds. A pressure builds in the air before the bearer acts, and their spells and blows flare brighter and land harder than they should.
- **Wind** (common, Wind): Moving air, freedom and open skies. Grants **Weathervane**. The essence of restless air. A breeze seems to follow bearers indoors, their hair and cloaks are always stirring, and their spells arrive on a sudden gust.
- **Confluence: Storm** (Spark + Potent + Wind): Wyrm majesty, scale and hoard. Grants **Hoard Sense**. Storm is power, pride and the open sky fused into the shape of the great wyrms. A faint sheen of scales shows along the bearer's cheekbones and the backs of their hands, their pupils narrow to slits when they are angry, and their spells arrive with the rush of vast wings.
