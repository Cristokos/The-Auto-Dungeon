The church of **Liberty**, the god who embodies freedom, self-determination and the breaking of chains.

- **Standing:** Loved by the common folk, distrusted by the powerful.
- **Symbol:** a broken shackle.
- **Colours:** sky blue and white.

## The god

Liberty is freedom: the absence of chains, masters and limits. Liberty wants every being free to choose, and does not care what they choose.

- **What the god gives:** Liberty frees slaves and tears down tyrants. It gives hope to the oppressed and the right of every person to their own life.
- **Offended by:** Chains, cages and ownership of people. Dominion is its oldest enemy.

:::dm
### The dark face

Liberty frees the murderer as readily as the slave. It tears down good laws with bad ones, and leaves chaos where order used to be.

- **Will accept:** Rebellion, riots, broken oaths and the escape of criminals, all in the name of freedom.

### Secret motives

- Liberty wants every chain broken, including laws, oaths, contracts and crowns. Some of its priests are revolutionaries who want the Storm Kingdom itself overthrown.
- Liberty will help anyone escape. Its safe houses have sheltered murderers, cultists and spies alongside slaves, and the church knows it.
:::

## Divine essence

Liberty grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers are restless, locks click open when they lean on doors, and a breeze follows them indoors.*

- **Essence trait:** Unbound. You have advantage on checks and saving throws to escape a grapple, restraints or manacles.
- **Sense: Sense Chains.** As an action, you sense every creature within 60 feet that is restrained, imprisoned, charmed or enslaved, until the end of your next turn.
- **Channel: Break the Chain.** As an action, you touch a creature and end one of the grappled, restrained, paralysed or charmed conditions on it, and any lock or manacle holding it opens.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Freedom** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures can't be charmed, and their speed can't be reduced by magic. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *expeditious retreat*, *sanctuary*, *feather fall*; level 2: *knock*, *misty step*, *calm emotions*; level 3: *haste*, *remove curse*, *dispel magic*; level 4: *freedom of movement*, *dimension door*; level 5: *passwall*, *dispel evil and good*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Wind.

**Divine confluence: Liberator.** A Liberator cannot be held. Bearers slip bonds without thinking, doors tend to stand open around them, and their magic blows through like a wind that cannot be caught. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Liberty (divine) + Key + Wind; Liberty (divine) + Wing + Rope; Liberty (divine) + Swift + Herald. It grants **Unchained**: You are under the effect of *freedom of movement* at all times, and once per long rest you can free every creature you choose within 30 feet from being grappled, restrained or charmed.

**What the god asks.** Free those held against their will. Never own or sell another person. Help anyone who flees.

**What makes the god take it back.** Owning, buying or selling a person. Handing a fugitive back to their master.

## What players know

The Church of Liberty preaches freedom for everyone, and means it. It is popular in the working districts and unpopular in the great houses. Anyone running from something can usually find a priest of Liberty willing to listen.

## Beliefs

- No one is born to be owned.
- Freedom given by a master is still a leash. Freedom must be taken.
- Help any who flee, and ask nothing in return.

## Clergy

Street preachers, abolitionists and smugglers of the escaped. Its **Unchained** are former slaves who have sworn to free others.

## In Greenstone

A crowded temple in the working districts, and a network of safe houses that the church insists does not exist.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of the Hearth]], [[Church of the Journey]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Dominion]], [[Church of Purity]]

Liberty's great enemy is Dominion, and above all the slavers who serve him. Its most dangerous quarrel is with Purity, whose zealots it accuses of building cages of their own.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- A runaway slave from a foreign land is hiding in the Liberty temple, and their former owner has hired adventurers to bring them back.
- The Unchained ask the party to scout a marsh camp where they believe slavers are holding captives.
- A noble house accuses the Church of Liberty of inciting its servants to leave, and demands the other houses help close the temple.

## DM notes

- Liberty is central to the campaign. Its fight against Dominion, and against slavery above all, can drive whole arcs.
- The church's safe houses are perfect cover for infiltrators. Not everyone it shelters is a victim.
:::
