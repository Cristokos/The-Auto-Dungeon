The church of **Justice**, the god who embodies law, fairness and rightful judgement, including retribution when the law fails.

- **Standing:** Formal, respected, and close to the courts.
- **Symbol:** a sword laid across a set of scales.
- **Colours:** slate grey and red.

## The god

Justice is the balance between deed and consequence: every wrong answered, every debt paid. It is not the same as law, and not the same as mercy. Vengeance is one of its faces, justice taken by the wronged when no one else will deliver it.

- **What the god gives:** Justice brings fair judgement, protection for the powerless, and the certainty that the wicked will not escape forever.
- **Offended by:** Wrongs that go unanswered, rigged judgements, and those who escape what they are owed.

:::dm
### The dark face

Justice does not forgive. It answers blood with blood, punishes the desperate as harshly as the cruel, and will follow a feud down the generations.

- **Will accept:** Executions, vendettas and harsh sentences, if they are deserved.

### Secret motives

- Justice cares that wrongs are answered, not that the law is kept. When the courts fail, Justice backs the Avengers, even against the great houses.
- Justice keeps a long memory. Old wrongs committed by noble houses, churches and even the Adventure Society are recorded, and Justice intends to see every one answered eventually.
:::

## Divine essence

Justice grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers stand very straight, a faint scale-shaped mark glows on their palm when they are wronged, and their gaze weighs people.*

- **Essence trait:** Proficiency in Insight or Intimidation.
- **Sense: Sense Guilt.** As an action, you learn whether any creature you can see within 30 feet has killed a person in the past week.
- **Channel: Judgement.** As a bonus action, you mark a creature that has harmed you or an ally within the past minute. For 1 minute, your attacks against it deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage, and it has disadvantage on attacks against anyone but you.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Reckoning** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). When a creature hits a blessed creature with an attack, you can use your reaction to make one weapon attack against the attacker if it is within your reach. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *wrathful smite*, *hunter's mark*, *command*; level 2: *zone of truth*, *hold person*, *branding smite*; level 3: *bestow curse*, *blinding smite*, *crusader's mantle*; level 4: *banishment*, *compulsion*; level 5: *geas*, *hold monster*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Light.

**Divine confluence: Arbiter.** An Arbiter weighs every deed and answers it. Bearers carry an air of quiet authority, scales of light hover behind them when they pass judgement, and their magic strikes the guilty harder. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Justice (divine) + Balance + Verdict; Justice (divine) + Sword + Oath; Justice (divine) + Gallows + Debt. It grants **Scales of Judgement**: Your attacks deal an extra 1d6 radiant damage to a creature that has harmed you or an ally within the past minute.

**What the god asks.** See wrongs answered. Keep your word and your lawful oaths. Never punish someone you know to be innocent.

**What makes the god take it back.** Letting a guilty party escape in exchange for gain. Punishing someone you know to be innocent.

## What players know

The Church of Justice stands behind the courts. Its priests judge disputes and advise magistrates, and they are thought to be fair, if not always kind. There are stories of other servants of Justice, called Avengers, who settle what the courts cannot.

## Beliefs

- Every wrong has a weight, and the scales must balance.
- The law serves justice, not the other way around.
- Those who escape the courts do not escape the god.

## Aspects

Justice has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Vengeance:** The face of Justice for those the law has failed. Its order of **Avengers** pursues wrongs the courts will not touch, and the formal church is never quite comfortable with them.

## Clergy

Judges, magistrates and priest-advocates on one side; the Avengers, who answer only to their own oaths, on the other.

## In Greenstone

Its priests sit in the city courts, where the magistrates answer to the Duke, and the Duke answers to everyone. Avengers come and go quietly, usually following someone.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of Truth]], [[Church of War]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Disguise]], [[Church of Dominion]]

Justice and its own Avengers disagree about method more than about goals, which makes for interesting family arguments.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- An Avenger is in Greenstone hunting a man the courts acquitted, and asks the party to help find him.
- A Justice priest is to judge a dispute between the [[Adventure Society]] and a noble house, and someone wants the verdict decided in advance.
- Somebody is murdering criminals who escaped sentence, and leaving the god's scales painted at each scene.

## DM notes

- The church is split between the court-priests, who want order, and the Avengers, who want outcomes. A party can be drawn into that split from either side.
