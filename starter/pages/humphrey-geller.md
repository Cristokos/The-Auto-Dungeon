**Iron rank.** [[Danielle Geller]]'s son, leading his own young team.

- **Looks:** Tall, broad-shouldered and handsome, with the olive skin and dark hair of old Greenstone families.
- **Essences:** Magic, Might and Wing, forming the **Dragon** confluence. He fights in heavy armour with a huge sword and can sprout dragon wings.
- **Personality:** Honorable, earnest, a bit sheltered. He sees the world in black and white, and his family has been adventurers for sixteen generations.

:::dm
**Offers players:** A rival or allied team, and a path into the Geller orbit.
:::

- **Affiliation:** [[House Geller]]

## What people say

Danielle's son, leading a young team of his own. Earnest and honorable, if a little sheltered, by most accounts.

:::dm
## DM notes

His team includes the healer [[Neil Davone]]. He meets new adventurers at their Society assessment and is a natural rival who can become a close friend.

## Playing Humphrey

**Noble champion.** Raised on sixteen generations of family heroes. Honest, brave and sure of what is right, until the world gives him a harder question.

- **Speech:** Formal, earnest and a little stiff; introduces himself with his full name
- **How to play:** Stand straight, offer a hand, and assume everyone is as honest as he is
- **Tell:** Squares his shoulders when he disapproves
- **Toward the party:** Friendly rivalry; wants to see if they are as good as they claim
- **Wants from the party:** Worthy rivals, and maybe friends who see the world differently
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> My name is Humphrey Geller. My family have been adventurers for sixteen generations.
>
> If it's wrong, it's wrong. I don't see the complication.
>
> Shall we see whose team clears it first?

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To live up to his family name, and his mother
- **Driven by:** Duty to Greenstone and the Geller legacy
- **Fears:** Failing his team because he trusted the wrong person
- **Bond:** [[Danielle Geller]] and [[House Geller]]
- **Flaw:** Sees the world in black and white

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user. Trained by his family; he has never absorbed a core.

- **Magic** (uncommon, Magic): Raw mana and arcane sensitivity. Grants **Mana Touch**. Magic is mana in its rawest, most adaptable form. Blue-white motes drift around the bearer's hands, and enchanted things feel faintly warm or buzzing to their touch.
- **Might** (common, Might): Raw strength and crushing force. Grants proficiency: Athletics or Intimidation. The essence of sheer physical power. Bearers' shoulders broaden and their grip grows crushing, their blows land with a dull thunderclap, and flagstones crack where they plant their feet.
- **Wing** (common, Wind): Feathers, flight and open sky. Grants **Featherfall**. The essence of the wing. Bearers feel lighter on their feet, stray feathers drift down where they have stood, and spectral wings briefly flare behind them when they cast.
- **Confluence: Dragon** (Magic + Might + Wing): Wyrm majesty, scale and hoard. Grants **Hoard Sense**. Dragon is power, pride and the open sky fused into the shape of the great wyrms. A faint sheen of scales shows along the bearer's cheekbones and the backs of their hands, their pupils narrow to slits when they are angry, and their spells arrive with the rush of vast wings.
