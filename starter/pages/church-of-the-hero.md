The church of **the Hero**, the god who embodies great deeds, courage and the glory that follows them.

- **Standing:** The adventurers' favourite.
- **Symbol:** a raised sword wreathed in a crown of leaves.
- **Colours:** gold and crimson.

## The god

The Hero is great deeds: the moment someone stands where others run and does what should be impossible. Every legend told in a tavern belongs to the Hero.

- **What the god gives:** The Hero brings courage, self-sacrifice, and legends that inspire generations.
- **Offended by:** Cowardice, and victories won by lies or in secret, where no one sees the deed.

:::dm
### The dark face

Heroes need monsters, villains and disasters to rise against. The Hero's faithful chase glory into needless danger, and some quietly arrange the crises they mean to solve.

- **Will accept:** Reckless risks, glory-seeking and crises that give heroes a stage.

### Secret motives

- The Hero needs crises to make heroes. Its church has been known to let a threat grow, or even arrange one, so that the right champion can rise to meet it.
- The Hero cares about the legend, not the truth of it. Its saga-keepers polish the stories of celebrated heroes and bury the ugly parts.
:::

## Divine essence

The Hero grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers seem a little taller than they are, light catches them at the right moments, and people remember their names.*

- **Essence trait:** Heroic Presence. Once per long rest, when you or a friendly creature within 30 feet fails a saving throw against being frightened, you can turn it into a success.
- **Sense: Sense Peril.** As an action, for the next minute you know the direction of the most dangerous creature within a mile, and of any person within 300 feet who is in mortal danger.
- **Channel: Heroic Stand.** As a bonus action, for 1 minute you have advantage on saving throws and deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage once on each of your turns. It ends early if no ally or innocent within 30 feet is in danger.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Inspiration** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). At the start of each of their turns, blessed creatures gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *heroism*, *bless*, *compelled duel*; level 2: *aid*, *enhance ability*, *warding bond*; level 3: *crusader's mantle*, *beacon of hope*, *haste*; level 4: *freedom of movement*, *death ward*; level 5: *circle of power*, *holy weapon*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Light.

**Divine confluence: Paragon.** A Paragon is what every child imagines when they hear the word hero. Bearers draw eyes in any room, light finds them at the decisive moment, and their magic rises with the stakes. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Hero (divine) + Legend + Valour; Hero (divine) + Star + Sword; Hero (divine) + Avatar + Dawn. It grants **Against All Odds**: When you make an attack roll or saving throw while you are below half your hit points, you can add 1d6 to it. You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

**What the god asks.** Stand where others run. Never refuse a worthy challenge. Let your deeds be known.

**What makes the god take it back.** Abandoning someone you could have saved. Taking credit for another's deed.

## What players know

The Church of the Hero is the adventurers' church. Its priests bless parties before dangerous contracts and record the deeds of those who do something worth remembering. Its hall is full of trophies, and every adventurer secretly hopes to see their own name on the wall.

## Beliefs

- The world needs heroes. Be one.
- A great deed is worth more than a long life.
- Stand where others run.

## Clergy

Battle-blessers and saga-keepers. Its priests bless departing parties, record great deeds and honour the fallen. Famous adventurers are sometimes honoured in its halls while still alive.

## In Greenstone

A hall full of trophies and banners close to the [[Adventure Society]] offices. Adventurers drop in before a hard contract for luck.

## Friends and rivals

- **Friendly with:** [[Church of War]]
- **At odds with:** [[Church of Disguise]], [[Church of Destruction]]

The Hero's church admires courage wherever it finds it, and despises those who win by lies.

:::dm
## Adventure hooks

- The church wants a party to recover a famous hero's lost weapon from an astral space, so it can hang it in the hall.
- A young adventurer is getting themselves killed trying to earn a place on the wall, and their mother begs the party to stop them.
- A celebrated hero honoured by the church is not who everyone thinks they are.

## DM notes

- The Hero is central to the campaign. The church's attention is a reward the party can earn, and a pressure: a church that celebrates heroes also expects them to behave like heroes.
- A disguised or compromised hero is a strong twist. Use the NPC generator's twists on any celebrated figure the church honours.
:::
