**District.** The original [[Greenstone]], on the shore of the delta: desert-stone tenements, canals, warehouses, markets and the working port. Most of the city's people live here, and most of its poor. The Duke's watch keeps the main roads. Many of the side streets belong to [[The Big Three]].

> *Everyone in Greenstone knows the rules. Very few know who wrote them.* (a saying in the Old City)

## What players know

- **The oldest streets.** Desert stone, sun-bleached and patched, with narrow lanes, canals spanned by arched bridges, and washing strung between balconies. Magic lamps light the main roads and very little else.
- **The people.** Labourers, dockhands, boat crews, eel-sellers, craftspeople, retired Iron-rankers who never made Bronze, and families who have lived on the same street for generations. Nearly all of them are Normal rank, and they can pick out an adventurer the moment one walks in.
- **The port.** The Old City's waterfront runs the length of the district, crammed with trade ships, cranes and warehouses. See [[The Marinas]].
- **The warehouse district.** Behind the docks, streets of warehouses and counting houses surround [[The Fortress]], a stone stronghold older than the city walls, which now holds the city's fighting pits.
- **Broadstreet Boulevard.** In spite of its name, a narrow street of cramped shopfronts and market stalls, home to [[Jory's Clinic]].
- **The Cavendish quarter.** Desert-stone streets with painted walls around the townhouse of [[House Cavendish]], who own more of the Old City than they admit.
- **The music hall.** The one place where the Old City and the Island meet on equal terms. Good musicians draw crowds from every district, adventurers included.
- **Clinics, not temples.** The temples are on the Island, so most people here go to small clinics and alchemists who charge what the neighbourhood can pay. The [[Church of the Healer]] does not approve.
- **The Big Three.** Three crime lords divide the underworld between them: gambling, smuggling, protection and loans. On most streets they keep a firmer order than the watch does. Everyone knows their names. Nobody says them loudly.

## The Berts

Eight identical brothers, the Bertinelli octuplets, all named Bert, are a Greenstone institution. Players will meet them everywhere and will be confused every time.

| Bert | Where | What he does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Albert | Adventure Society | Front desk attendant |
| Bertram | The Ducal Palace | One of the Duke's guards |
| Bertrand | Anywhere | Silver-rank adventurer, and "the handsome one", according to Gilbert |
| Filbert | Old City | Filbert's Fine Leather Emporium |
| Gilbert | The Island, Society Trade Hall | Gilbert's Resilient Attire for the Discerning Gentleman |

## DM notes

**Fixed points.** The Old City is the original city: working-class, on the water, run underneath by the Big Three, with the Fortress pits and Jory's clinic. Everything else is yours to change.

**Hooks.**
- Cole Silva offers the party good money to "persuade" a pit fighter who refused him. The fighter is Sophie.
- Jory's clinic is vandalised by two acolytes of the Healer, and the party walks in while it is happening.
- A Fortress promoter wants an Iron-rank adventurer for an exhibition bout, and the odds he is offering make no sense.
- The Night Supper charity (see [[Cults]]) feeds people on a Ventress street, and Ventress wants to know who gave them permission.
- Herbert and Robert have discovered that someone is reselling their fruit on a third street, and they want the party to find out which of their brothers it is.
:::
