**Landmark.** An active volcano on the coast, south-east of Yaresh, wreathed in permanent storm cloud. *The south-central continent.*

## What players know

The people of Yaresh call it the Thunder Mount and say that a god once forged weapons in its crater. Its slopes are rich in volcanic glass and lightning-struck stone, and smiths climb it to quench blades in its springs.

## What mana has made of it

- Lightning strikes the summit every minute of every day.
- Blades quenched in its summit springs can hold a lightning enchantment without a smith's ritual.
- The forest on its lower slopes grows in rings, each ring a different season.

**Dangers.** Lightning, lava, and the storm drakes that nest on its ridges.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A Yaresh smith needs a storm drake's core to finish a commission for a noble house, by the next new moon.
- **Secret:** The god's forge is still there, under the crater, and it is still lit.
- **On Earth this would be** Mount Cameroon. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
