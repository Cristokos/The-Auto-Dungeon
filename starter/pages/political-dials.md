Greenstone's politics come down to one question: who gets to decide how power is used. Pick one option on each dial below to set the tone of your world on day one.

## Standing tensions

- **[[Adventure Society]] versus the houses.** The Society claims authority over all adventurers; elite families expect their children to be exempt.
- **[[Church of Purity]] versus "corrupt" magic.** Zealots want dark, blood and death-themed powers restricted or purged; their users want to be left alone.
- **Adventure Society versus [[Magic Society]].** One controls the fighters, the other the knowledge and crafting; each needs the other and resents it.
- **Old money versus new power.** Rank can be earned by anyone with essences, which lets commoners rise fast and threatens old families.
- **The Duke in the middle.** The Duke rules the city but none of the pillars, and each of them pushes the palace to serve its interests. The Duke is also a Mercer, so the other houses never quite trust him.
- **House against house.** No crown sits above the houses, so they settle things through deals, marriages and alliances, and sometimes through feuds.
- **Foreign money.** [[The Storm Kingdom]] and other realms have no authority in Greenstone, but they court the houses with gold and favours.

## Dials

| Dial | Option A | Option B | Option C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adventure Society | **Principled:** fair and independent | **Captured:** quietly serves the great houses | **Overstretched:** honest but too thin to enforce its rules |
| Church of Purity | **Marginal:** a loud fringe | **Rising:** gaining converts and officials | **Dominant:** shapes local law and suspicion |
| Great houses | **Cooperative:** compete politely | **Feuding:** an open rivalry splits the city | **Consolidated:** one family effectively rules |
| Foreign powers | **Distant:** trade and little else | **Courting:** envoys buy friends among the houses | **Meddling:** a foreign power backs one house against the others |
| Dark essences | **Accepted:** a power like any other | **Distrusted:** legal but socially costly | **Restricted:** registration and scrutiny |
| Outworlders | **Unknown:** most people have never heard of them | **Curiosity:** sought by scholars and collectors | **Threat:** watched by churches and the great houses |

## Recommended starting configuration

Principled Society, rising Church of Purity, feuding houses, distant foreign powers, distrusted dark essences, and outworlders as curiosities. This keeps the institutions players rely on trustworthy while giving every faction something to push against.
