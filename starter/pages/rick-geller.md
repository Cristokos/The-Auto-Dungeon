**Iron rank.** Human man, a distant cousin of [[Humphrey Geller]] from the family's northern branch, leading his own team.

- **Essences:** Might, Iron and Fury, forming the **Forge** confluence. He is focused on getting stronger above everything else.
- **Personality:** Competitive, blunt and hungry. He respects strength, and he respects people who beat him even more.
- **Team:** His cousins [[Jonah Geller]] and [[Henry Geller]], and the Adeah twins: Hannah, a ranged scout with a bow, and Claire, the team's healer.
- **Looks:** Tall, heavy-shouldered and restless, with the Geller olive skin, a broken nose he refused to have healed, and a greatsword older than he is.
- **Ties:** Brother of [[Phoebe Geller]]; rival of his cousin [[Humphrey Geller]], whom he has never quite beaten and never stops trying to.

:::dm
**Offers players:** A rival team that will take the same contracts and push them to be better.

Rick is the cocky rival who becomes a friend after a hard fight, ideally a training bout in the Geller mirage chamber. He has a soft spot for Hannah Adeah that the whole team can see.
:::

## What people say

Rick Geller from the northern branch, who leads his own Iron team and challenges anyone who looks strong. He is loud, rude and very good, and he's never yet backed down from a fight he was offered.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **The rivalry.** Rick and Humphrey were measured against each other from childhood. Rick came out second every time, and he will push his team into contracts it can't handle just to get ahead of Humphrey's.
- **Winning him over.** Beat him fairly, or save his team, and he becomes the loudest ally the party has. He tells the story of the fight he lost to them at every table in the city.
- **Hannah.** He has never said a word to Hannah Adeah about how he feels. Claire Adeah has noticed and does not approve.
- **A scene.** Rick bangs his tankard down beside a party member's at the branch, says he's heard they're good, and asks when they want to find out who's better. He means the Geller mirage chamber, tonight.

## Playing Rick

**Hungry rival.** Cocky, blunt and honest, and much easier to like after he has lost to you.

- **Speech:** Loud, short sentences and constant challenges
- **How to play:** Size up the strongest party member and say so out loud
- **Tell:** Cracks his knuckles before every argument
- **Toward the party:** Competitive until they beat him, then loyal
- **Wants from the party:** A worthy fight, and someone to measure himself against who isn't Humphrey
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> You're the new team? You don't look like much. Prove me wrong.
>
> Again. One more round.
>
> Fine. You won. Drinks are on me, and I want a rematch.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To be the strongest Geller of his generation
- **Driven by:** Being second best all his life
- **Fears:** Being ordinary
- **Bond:** His sister and his team
- **Flaw:** Picks fights he can't win to prove he isn't afraid of them

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Might** (common, Might): Raw strength and crushing force. Grants Proficiency in Athletics or Intimidation. The essence of sheer physical power. Bearers' shoulders broaden and their grip grows crushing, their blows land with a dull thunderclap, and flagstones crack where they plant their feet.
- **Iron** (common, Earth): Metal, toughness and unbending will. Grants **Unbending**. The essence of iron and a will that does not bend. Bearers' skin takes on a dull metallic sheen, their voices pick up a faint ring, and their resolve hardens like quenched steel.
- **Fury** (uncommon, Fire): Anger, wrath and wild strength. Grants **Seething Strength**. Fury is anger hot enough to bend iron. Bearers flush red when roused, their knuckles crack and pop, and their magic arrives with a low growl like a stoked forge.
- **Confluence: Forge** (Might + Iron + Fury): Fire, hammer and metal made one. Grants **Field Smithy**. Forge is flame, hammer and iron joined in the endless work of the smithy. Bearers' forearms grow corded and soot-flecked, their voices ring with a hint of struck metal, and their spells land in a shower of sparks with the tang of hot iron.
:::
