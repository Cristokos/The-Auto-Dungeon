**Normal.** Human man, head clerk at the contract counter at the [[Greenstone Adventure Society Branch|Greenstone branch]] of the [[Adventure Society]].

- **Looks:** Small, round and balding, with spectacles on a chain, sleeve garters, and a pen behind each ear. His counter is the tidiest surface in Greenstone.
- **Personality:** Precise and unbending. Will not hand a Silver contract to a Bronze team if the Director himself asks. Secretly kind: the one who notices a team has not eaten and leaves a bread roll on the counter.
- **Wants:** That the forms are filled in correctly, and that nobody he signs out comes back in a box.
- **Ties:** He has signed out every contract in the branch for three decades. [[Ulric Stane]] and [[Brother Ilian Marsh]] are his only friends, and they play cards every Tenthday.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Honest advice on which contracts are worse than they look, if the party has earned his respect.
:::

## What people say

Old Pell at the contract counter. He'll make you fill the form in twice and he'll never bend a rule for you. But if he tells you not to take a contract, don't take it.

:::dm
## DM notes

- Pell has worked the counter for thirty-one years and keeps his own private ledger of every contract he thought was wrong. Several of the overdue teams are in it.
- **His ledger.** Pell's private ledger lists every contract he thought was wrong, with notes: the client who paid too much, the place that doesn't exist, the rank that didn't match. Several of the overdue teams' contracts were arranged by the same deputy.
- **Advice.** A party he respects gets a quiet warning when a contract smells wrong, written on the back of the receipt.
- **Pressure.** He is under pressure from upstairs to sign contracts without questions. If he refuses one more time he may lose his post, and he is about to.
- **A scene.** The party slaps a contract notice on the counter. A small round man looks at it over his spectacles, looks at their badges, looks at the notice again, and says, "No. This one is not for you. Try this one."

## Playing Pell

**Strict, kind clerk.** Precise, unbending and secretly caring, the conscience of the contract counter.

- **Speech:** Prim, slow and precise
- **How to play:** Point out an error on their form and fix it for them
- **Tell:** Polishes his spectacles before he gives bad news
- **Toward the party:** Stern, and quietly protective
- **Wants from the party:** Correct forms, and every team back alive
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> Initial here. And here. No, here.
>
> That contract is above your rank. I don't care who sent you.
>
> Eat something. You look terrible. Here.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** That nobody he signs out comes back in a box
- **Driven by:** Duty and a quiet, stubborn decency
- **Fears:** Signing out a team to its death
- **Bond:** His counter, his ledger and his card nights
- **Flaw:** Follows the rules even when the rules are wrong
