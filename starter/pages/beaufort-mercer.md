#### Traits

***Merchant's Eye (Market essence).*** By handling a nonmagical object for 1 minute, you learn its fair market value, and you can tell when someone is asking more than twice that price for it.

***Uncrowned Royalty (Crown essence).*** Guards, servants and common folk treat you as a person of noble rank unless shown clear proof otherwise, and you can always secure an audience with a local lord or magistrate within a day.

***Sellsword (Mercenary confluence).*** Within 1 hour of arriving in a settlement, you learn who there is hiring guards, escorts or soldiers and what they pay. When you spend downtime working as a guard, bodyguard or soldier, you earn twice the normal wage.

**Bronze rank.** Human man, brother of [[The Duke of Greenstone|the Duke]], husband of [[Thalia Mercer]], and father of [[Cassandra Mercer|Cassandra]] and [[Thadwick Mercer|Thadwick]].

- **Looks:** Sleek, well fed and immaculately dressed, with the Mercer olive skin and a smile that never quite reaches his eyes.
- **Role:** He runs the family's business while his brother runs the city: the quarries, the warehouses, the land purchases.
- **Personality:** Smooth, calculating and patient. He never raises his voice, because he never needs to.
- **Essences:** Coin, Market and Crown, forming the **Mercenary** confluence. Every power he has is for persuasion, and most of them are invisible.
- **Ties:** Brother and heir presumptive of [[The Duke of Greenstone|the Duke]], who has no children of his own.

:::dm
**Offers players:** Money, contracts, and doors opened, all with an invoice attached.

Beaufort is behind the family's land schemes. He is also the one who ends Cassandra's relationships when they become inconvenient, and the one who keeps covering for Thadwick long after he should stop.
:::

## What people say

The Duke's brother, who runs the money. Everyone in Greenstone who owns a warehouse, a permit or a field has met Beaufort Mercer's agents, and most of them have sold to him.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **The land.** His agents buy fields near astral space entrances before anyone knows the entrances are there. Someone is telling him, and the party may find out who.
- **Invoices.** Every favour he does is written down. He calls them in at the worst possible time, politely, with interest.
- **Covering for Thadwick.** He pays off victims, buries reports and leans on the [[Adventure Society]] for his son. A party that gets one of those reports to Captain Reyna Holt makes a dangerous enemy.
- **A scene.** A clerk brings the party to a private room above a Mercer warehouse. Beaufort pours wine, says he's heard good things about them, and slides a sealed contract across the table that pays three times the board rate.

## Playing Beaufort

**Smooth fixer.** Never raises his voice, never forgets a debt, and never does his own dirty work.

- **Speech:** Soft, pleasant and very precise
- **How to play:** Offer the party something they want before they ask, and make sure they notice it was a favour
- **Tell:** Smiles wider the less he means it
- **Toward the party:** Warmly transactional
- **Wants from the party:** Useful debts, and a team that will do quiet work without questions
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> I'd consider it a personal favour.
>
> Everyone has a price. It's rude to make me guess yours.
>
> My son is young. Young men make mistakes. Mistakes can be settled.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** For his line to inherit the dukedom with its fortune intact
- **Driven by:** Ambition, patience and pride in the family name
- **Fears:** Scandal, and his son becoming one
- **Bond:** The house, his brother, and his money
- **Flaw:** Believes anything can be bought, including his son's soul

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Coin** (common, Sin): Trade, value and the haggle. Grants Proficiency in Persuasion, or one language of your choice. The essence of coin and commerce. Bearers can weigh a purse by eye, their voices take on a trader's easy patter, and their spells glint like spinning gold.
- **Market** (uncommon, Sin): Trade, haggling and worth. Grants **Merchant's Eye**. Market is the noisy energy of buying and selling. Bearers develop a quick, assessing glance and a merchant's patter, and a faint chink of coins sounds when they work magic.
- **Crown** (legendary, Might): Sovereignty, rightful rule and command. Grants **Uncrowned Royalty**. Crown is rule itself, the weight of the right to command that kingdoms are built around. When the bearer speaks with authority a faint circlet of golden light seems to rest on their brow, and courtiers and commoners alike recognise them as someone born, or chosen, to lead.
- **Confluence: Mercenary** (Coin + Market + Crown): A sword for sale to anyone. Grants **Sellsword**. The mercenary fights for whoever pays best, blade and bargain fused into a single trade. Bearers count coins without looking and keep their gear well oiled but plain, and their magic flares with the dull gleam of gold on steel.
:::
