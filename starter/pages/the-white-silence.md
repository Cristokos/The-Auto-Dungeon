**Landmark.** A frozen continent at the bottom of the world, with ice miles thick and a cold that kills in minutes. *The far south.*

## What players know

Few have crossed the White Silence and fewer have returned. Expeditions report black towers under the ice, ruins of a city older than any kingdom, and an aurora that moves like it is looking for something.

## What mana has made of it

- Sound does not carry: two people shouting a foot apart cannot hear each other.
- Under the ice lies a city with streets, towers and doors, perfectly preserved.
- The aurora drifts toward light and warmth, and anything it touches is frozen solid.

**Dangers.** The cold, the silence, the aurora, and whatever kept the city's people inside.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A wealthy explorer is mounting an expedition to the buried city and needs a team that can survive it.
- **Secret:** The city's people are still there, frozen mid-step, and they are waiting for the ice to melt.
- **On Earth this would be** Antarctica. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
