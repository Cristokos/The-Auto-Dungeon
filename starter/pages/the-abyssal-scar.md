**Landmark.** The deepest point in any ocean, a trench so deep that sunlight never reaches the bottom. *The great ocean.*

## What players know

Sailors crossing the great ocean avoid the Scar. They say leviathans rise from it to feed, and that its bottom opens onto somewhere else entirely.

## What mana has made of it

- Pressure magic is the only way down, and it fails at the halfway mark for anyone below Silver rank.
- Leviathans that rise from the Scar carry Gold and Diamond-rank cores.
- Things that sink into the Scar sometimes float up years later in other oceans.

**Dangers.** Leviathans, crushing depths, and the dark.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Hook:** A whaling fleet from the Storm Kingdom wants protection for a hunt over the Scar, and wants it quietly.
- **Secret:** The trench is the scar of a wound in the world, from the war that brought the gods here, and it has never healed.
- **On Earth this would be** the Mariana Trench. Use the real place for scale and look, and let the magic do the rest.
:::
