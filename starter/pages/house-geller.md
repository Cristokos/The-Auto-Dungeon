One of the founding families of [[Greenstone]], and its most famous adventuring house. The Gellers were already rich and influential when the city was founded, and they have produced adventurers for sixteen generations since. They are prominent, protective of their own, and deeply tied to the [[Adventure Society]].

## What players know

- **Sixteen generations.** The Gellers have done one thing for centuries: produce adventurers to protect the world. Their children are trained from childhood and given every advantage money can buy, and are then expected to earn their rank the hard way.
- **The look.** Like most old Greenstone families, they have olive skin and dark hair.
- **The estate.** The family seat outside the walls has its own household guard of Bronze and Silver-rankers, and one of the few mirage chambers in the city. See [[The Geller Estate]].
- **Old rivals.** The Gellers helped found the city, but the title of Duke went to [[House Mercer]]. The two houses are friendly in society and rivals in everything else.

## Founders

The house was founded by **Aldric Geller**, a Gold-rank adventurer and caravan master who made his fortune guarding the desert trade roads before Greenstone existed. When the first settlers built a port on the Mistrun delta, Aldric paid for half its walls and put his own family on them. His motto is still carved over the estate gate: *We stand on the wall.*

His wife **Mara Geller**, a healer, founded the family's habit of marrying talent rather than titles. Every generation since has brought in at least one strong adventurer from outside the city.

## Key people

- [[Danielle Geller]] (Silver, close to Gold), head of the house. Called the Time Witch, and not to her face.
- [[Keith Geller]], her husband, who runs the family's business so that she doesn't have to.
- [[Henrietta Geller]] (Bronze), their daughter, a summoner with a bonded phoenix.
- [[Humphrey Geller]] (Iron), their son, leading his own young team with [[Neil Davone]].
- [[Rick Geller]] (Iron), a cousin from the northern branch, leading a rival family team.
- [[Phoebe Geller]], Rick's sister.
- [[Jonah Geller]] and [[Henry Geller]] (Iron), cousins on Rick's team.

**Rick's team:** Rick, Jonah, Henry, and the Adeah twins, Hannah (a ranged scout with a bow) and Claire (the team's healer, fiercely protective of her sister).

## Interests and income

- **Adventuring.** The core of the family's money and fame. Geller teams take the dangerous contracts, and the house keeps a share of the rewards, cores and loot their members bring home.
- **Land.** Rice and vegetable farms across the Mistrun delta, worked by tenant villages the Gellers protect in return. In a surge, those villages shelter at the estate.
- **Training.** Places in the estate's mirage chamber are the best training in the city. The Gellers rarely sell time in it, but they trade it for favours.
- **Shipping.** Shares in several trading ships out of [[The Marinas]], managed by Keith.
- **Interests.** Keeping the delta safe, keeping the [[Adventure Society]] honest, and keeping the Mercers from forgetting who built the walls.

## DM notes

- **Two Geller teams.** Humphrey's team and Rick's team give the party two very different Iron-rank rivals from the same family: one earnest and principled, one hungry and competitive.
- **Fair assessments.** Danielle knows the branch is influenced by local powers, so she goes out of her way to get her son assessed by outsiders who won't show favour.
- **Rick's team is in danger.** If you run [[The Builder Cult]], Rick's team is the natural victim of its first big strike: Henry is killed in an astral space, and Jonah is seeded with a star seed. A clumsy cleansing by the [[Church of Purity]] could kill him. That gives the Gellers a personal reason to hunt the cult, and the party a reason to work with them.
- **Danielle's lost sister.** Danielle had a sister who is believed dead. Whether she is, and what happened, is yours to decide.
:::
