The church of **Disguise**, the god who embodies concealment: masks, false identities, lies, and the unseen hand.

- **Standing:** Hidden, misunderstood, and older than it looks.
- **Symbol:** a blank mask.
- **Colours:** grey.

## The god

Disguise is concealment: the false face, the hidden name, the lie and the unseen hand. Deceit and Subtlety are faces of Disguise. It cares that what is true stays hidden, not why.

- **What the god gives:** Disguise brings escape for the hunted, freedom to become someone new, and the protection of secrets that keep people alive.
- **Offended by:** Exposure. Truth is its great enemy.

:::dm
### The dark face

Disguise brings spies, traitors, impostors and murderers who walk unseen. Every infiltrator and every replaced person serves Disguise.

- **Will accept:** Lies, betrayal, impersonation and any deception at all.

### Secret motives

- Disguise wants everything that is true to stay hidden. Its faithful are already inside the other churches, the noble houses and the Adventure Society.
- Disguise's true aims are yours to set. This god is central to the plot, so these notes stay deliberately open.
:::

## Divine essence

Disguise grants a [[Divine Essences|divine essence]] to adults who worship sincerely. Every priest of the church holds one. *Bearers are hard to describe afterwards, their faces seem slightly different in every light, and mirrors show them a heartbeat late.*

- **Essence trait:** Proficiency in Deception or with disguise kits.
- **Sense: Sense Masks.** As an action, you learn whether any creature within 30 feet is disguised, shapechanged, possessed or invisible, though not who they really are.
- **Channel: Borrowed Face.** As an action, you take on the appearance and voice of a creature you have seen, for 1 hour. The disguise holds up to touch, and seeing through it takes an Investigation check against your stone spell DC.
- **Blessing: Blessing of Obscurity** (from Bronze rank). As a bonus action, you bless yourself and friendly creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your proficiency bonus in number, for 2 rounds (12 seconds). Blessed creatures can't be targeted by divination magic or perceived through magical scrying, and have advantage on Stealth checks. You can do this once per short rest, or twice per short rest from Gold rank.
- **Awakening stones:** level 1: *disguise self*, *silent image*, *charm person*; level 2: *invisibility*, *alter self*, *pass without trace*; level 3: *nondetection*, *major image*, *clairvoyance*; level 4: *greater invisibility*, *polymorph*; level 5: *mislead*, *seeming*.
- **Affinity for confluences:** Dark.

**Divine confluence: Faceless.** The Faceless have no single face. Bearers are forgotten as soon as they leave a room, their features never settle, and their magic hides more than it reveals. It forms only from one of these exact recipes: Disguise (divine) + Mask + Mimic; Disguise (divine) + Secret + Mirage; Disguise (divine) + Kitsune + Silk. It grants **No True Face**: You can cast *disguise self* at will without a spell slot, and divination magic that would reveal your identity or true form fails unless its caster succeeds on a check against your stone spell DC.

**What the god asks.** Keep every secret entrusted to you. Never ask who is behind a mask. Never reveal the faith.

**What makes the god take it back.** Unmasking another of the faithful. Betraying a secret the god entrusted to you.

## What players know

Disguise is the god of masks and hidden faces. Actors make offerings to it before a performance, and people who need to vanish pray to it. Liars, thieves and spies pray to it too, under the names Deceit and Subtlety, though nobody admits to it. Some say its priests have no faces of their own. Most people think that is just a story.

## Beliefs

- A face is a choice.
- To be unseen is to be free.
- Never ask who is behind the mask.
- Never be seen to win.

## Aspects

Disguise has other faces, each with its own shrines and devotees.

- **Deceit:** Disguise as the lie: cons, false promises and hidden agendas. Con artists and swindlers leave coins at little two-faced shrines that turn up in gambling dens.
- **Subtlety:** Disguise as the unseen hand: patience, secrets, and moving things without ever being seen. Spies, assassins and court whisperers favour it.

## Clergy

Actors, infiltrators, spies, con artists, the hunted, and anyone who has needed to become someone else. Its priests are rumoured never to show their true faces, even to each other.

## In Greenstone

No known temple. A theatre company in the entertainment district is said to keep a shrine backstage, and the actors laugh when asked about it.

## Friends and rivals

- **At odds with:** [[Church of Truth]], [[Church of Purity]], [[Church of Justice]]

Truth wants every mask removed. Purity distrusts anything that hides what lies beneath. Justice has hanged more than one of its faithful.

## Adventure hooks

- A theatre troupe hires the party to guard its masks, which are worth far more than any theatre should own.
- Someone is wearing the face of a dead Adventure Society official, and doing a very good job of it.
- A frightened stranger begs the party to help them hide, then walks out of the inn wearing a different face.
- A two-faced coin is found in the pocket of a murdered courier.

## DM notes

- Disguise pairs naturally with the NPC generator's twists: body doubles, mimics, traitors, double agents and people who are not who they say they are.
