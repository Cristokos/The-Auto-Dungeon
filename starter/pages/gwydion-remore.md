**Bronze rank.** Human man, brother of [[Rufus Remore]] and son of [[Gabriel Remore]] and [[Arabelle Remore]]. Raised at the Remore Academy in [[Vitesse]] like the rest of the family.

- **Personality:** Less serious than his brother and less theatrical than his father; the family member most likely to tell Rufus he is being ridiculous.
- **Looks:** Lean and easy-going, with his father's grin, his mother's watchful eyes, and a sword worn the way other people wear a hat.
- **Essences:** Sword, Swift and Mirth, forming the **Centaur** confluence. A duellist who laughs while he fights, and hits harder when he does.
- **Ties:** He teaches junior sword classes at the academy, and writes to Rufus more often than Rufus writes back.

:::dm
Gwydion is lightly sketched on purpose. Set his rank and his story to suit the campaign: a younger brother still at the academy, a rival who chose a different path, or a loss that Rufus does not talk about.
:::

## What people say

The other Remore son, the one who stayed home. Students at the academy say he's the most fun instructor there and the only one who'll tell you when a Remore is wrong.

:::dm
## DM notes

- **Default.** Unless the campaign needs otherwise, Gwydion is a Bronze-ranker who stayed at the academy to teach. He chose it, and he is tired of being asked why he didn't follow Rufus abroad.
- **Using him.** He is the easiest Remore to meet: a visitor from the academy assessing branch training, a duellist passing through, or the brother who arrives to check on Rufus. He is the friendly face that makes the rest of the family make sense.
- **What he hides.** He was offered a place on a famous team and turned it down, because his grandfather made it plain he was expected to. He has never told Rufus.
- **A scene.** A stranger in academy colours takes a practice sword from the rack in the branch yard, and asks Ulric Stane, cheerfully, if anyone wants a lesson. He beats three Iron-rankers in a row while chatting about the weather.

## Playing Gwydion

**Cheerful duellist.** Funny, relaxed and very good, the Remore who doesn't take the family name too seriously.

- **Speech:** Easy, joking and quick
- **How to play:** Make fun of Rufus fondly, then defend him fiercely
- **Tell:** Spins his sword when he's thinking
- **Toward the party:** Friendly, teasing and genuinely interested
- **Wants from the party:** News of his brother, and a good sparring partner
- **Pronouns:** he/him

**Things to say:**

> You've met my brother? I'm sorry. He's better than he seems.
>
> Again, but this time, don't telegraph it.
>
> My family's school. Yes. Drink.

## Hopes, drives and fears

- **Hopes:** To be his own man without disappointing anyone
- **Driven by:** Humour, and love for his brother
- **Fears:** Becoming Roland, or failing him
- **Bond:** Rufus, and his students
- **Flaw:** Makes a joke of everything, including the things that hurt

## Essences and powers

Path to rank: Essence user.

- **Sword** (common, Might): Blades, swordplay and the duel. Grants Proficiency with longswords or with rapiers. The essence of the sword. Bearers stand with a duellist's poise, any blade they hold hums faintly as it moves, and their magic carries a cold edge of light.
- **Swift** (common, Swift): Speed, reflexes and quick feet. Grants **Fleet**. The essence of speed. Bearers fidget whenever they are made to wait, leave faint afterimages when they move suddenly, and rarely seem out of breath.
- **Mirth** (uncommon, Life): Laughter, joy and good humour. Grants **Crowd Pleaser**. Mirth is the bright, infectious joy of shared laughter. Bearers have laugh lines that deepen quickly and a grin that is hard not to return, and their magic sparkles like festival lanterns.
- **Confluence: Centaur** (Sword + Swift + Mirth): Horse strength and the open plains. Grants **Born in the Saddle**. Centaur is beast and warrior made one, the speed of the wild herd joined to a fighter's strength. A bearer has a long, powerful stride and an ease with horses that borders on kinship, and their spells come with a drumming of hooves.
