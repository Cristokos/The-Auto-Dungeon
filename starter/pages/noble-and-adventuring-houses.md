A noble house in Pallimustus is not a title handed down by a king. It is the family of someone who became powerful, usually an adventurer who climbed to Gold or Diamond rank and got rich doing it. That person has the money and influence to do as they please, and they use it to protect their own: large estates, guards, the best gear and schooling for their children, and a name that makes people careful.

The houses are one of the three pillars of Greenstone, alongside the [[Adventure Society]] and [[The Churches]]. No king sits above them, only [[The Duke of Greenstone|the Duke]], who is the head of one of them. Each house answers to its founder or current head, and to whatever deals it has made with the others.

## How a house works

- **The founder.** Most houses begin with one very strong person. While they live, the house is as strong as they are, and a Diamond-ranker can live a very long time.
- **The family.** Children and relatives get what money can buy: training, essences, awakening stones, the best teachers, and a team of retainers to keep them alive on their first contracts.
- **The estate.** Large walled grounds outside or inside the city, with its own guards, staff and sometimes its own astral space entrance.
- **The deals.** Houses trade constantly: marriages, contracts, loans, shared expeditions, and favours owed. Alliances form and break, and every house keeps track of who owes it what.

## The houses

- [[House Mercer]]: the ducal family. The head of the house is [[The Duke of Greenstone|the Duke]], and his heirs are [[Beaufort Mercer]]'s line.
- [[House Geller]]: one of the city's founding families, and its most famous adventuring house.
- [[House Cavendish]]: an old family with an Old City quarter named after them, and the best Iron-ranker in the city.
- [[House Vane]]: an old family on a desert estate, who keep to themselves.

**Lesser houses.** The Davones, whose son [[Neil Davone]] is a healer of promise. The Ketterings, a minor family whose sons take service with greater houses, like [[Dustin Kettering]] on a Mercer team. The Lindovers, who made their money in lumber. The Sparnows, a once-noble family now better known for their debts.

**Houses abroad.** [[House Remore]] of [[Vitesse]] runs the most famous adventurer academy in the world, and its children sometimes come to Greenstone on contract.

**Tensions.** New power versus old: anyone with essences can rise, so a new Gold-ranker can found a house in one lifetime and threaten families who took three. Versus the Adventure Society over who disciplines their children. Versus each other, always, through deals as much as feuds.

**What players know.** A few families hold more power in Greenstone than anyone else, because someone in each of them is terrifyingly strong. Doing a great house a favour can open doors for years. Crossing one can close them for longer.
